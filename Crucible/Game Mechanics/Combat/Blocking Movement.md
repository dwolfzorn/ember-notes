# Blocking Movement

A creature's space normally cannot be entered or crossed by another creature - ordinary movement is halted by anyone in its path. Some abilities let a creature force its way through, resolved by comparing the moving creature's **Movement Strength** against the **Blocker Strength** of whoever stands in the way.

A movement passes through a blocker only when its Movement Strength is *greater* than the blocker's Blocker Strength; on a tie, the blocker holds. Both ordinary creatures and ordinary movement have a strength of **None**, so by default no one passes through anyone. Forceful movements - **Bull Rush**, **Shield Charge**, **Overrun**, **Tumble Through**, and the like - carry a **Powerful** strength that pushes through ordinary creatures. A few defenders, most notably a Guarded **Bastion**, project an **Unstoppable** Blocker Strength that even Powerful movement cannot pass.

The same comparison governs forced displacement: a creature may be pushed, pulled, or thrown only if the strength of the effect moving it exceeds its Blocker Strength. An Unstoppable creature therefore cannot be shoved from its position.

## Exceptions

Two kinds of movement disregard Blocker Strength entirely, because they do not pass *through* a creature at all:

- **Jump.** A leaping creature clears the top of anyone in its path and is never blocked.
- **Blink.** Teleporting movement - such as the **Step** spell gesture - reappears at its destination and is likewise unobstructed.
