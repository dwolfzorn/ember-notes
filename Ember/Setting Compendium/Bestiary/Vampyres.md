# Vampyres

- Keywords: Blood, Death, Immortal
- Ember Type: Monster
- Type: Undead

Mortal ancestries on the [[World of Ember]] know a great deal about Vampyres from the tales and stories of Moiran refugees who fled the Evernight and from the continuing experience of the [[Lumek]], who regularly encounter them during the cycles of Fire and Death. This shared knowledge contains many truths but also a few misconceptions and exaggerations.

A Vampyre retains its appearance from life. It may appear extraordinarily hale and healthy, almost pristine and without marks, while signs of aging become graceful and firm. This does not mean all Vampyres are youthful-looking, and their appearances range across the age ranges of every ancestry. This serene appearance can change dramatically on a whim, however, and when a Vampyre is hunting, angry, or acting upon command, its facial features twist unnaturally. Its regular teeth extend and grow sharp, especially the canines, which elongate dramatically. Often, this means a slight distension of the entire jaw to accommodate the more prominent, sharper teeth.

A Vampyre is said to have no heartbeat and requires blood from mortals to survive. This is primarily true by most accounts; Vampyres are undead in every sense of the word, but they do not require blood to survive and do eat normal food types like bread and fruit when needed. Unfortunately, the transformation into a Vampyre has a dramatic mental effect and causes the vast majority of Vampyres to become extremely predatory. This results in them hunting, feeding upon, and consuming mortals. It's said that a Vampyre has an unending hunger for blood alone, but in reality, some Vampyres feel only the need to kill or the thrill of the hunt, while others seek to consume flesh instead.

It is said that Vampyres are created by allowing a mortal to feed from them, offering their own tainted blood to transform the mortal into an immortal monster. This is partly true, but the process is more complex and requires the original Vampyre to imbue the act with a measure of their own power, draining them for days or even months. Thus, despite what many believe, Vampyres do not regularly create more of their kind unless a situation requires it.

Vampyrism is thought to be a curse upon the living and a curse specifically created to target and punish the Moiran people for some ancient folly or injustice. Surviving Moiran families believe this wholeheartedly, despite some evidence suggesting that Vampyrism may be a magical process created by some ancient being. Regardless of the origin, Vampyres are undead, immortal, and among the most powerful undead. They can move incredibly quickly, leading some mortals to believe they have an innate ability to teleport and move instantly through space. Vampyres possess greater physical strength than most mortals, and their skin cannot be pierced by ordinary weapons.
