# Redrak Fields

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Biome is located in the northcentral region of the [[Arctus Plateau]].
>
> - Travel through this Biome is generally [[Region Exploration]].
> - This Biome can be explored within the [[Redrak Fields]] Area Map.

The region's land is naturally rich, and is further enhanced by the efforts of both [[House Lilifeld]] and the [[Agrimage Circle]] headquartered in the city of [[Rortwark]].

These rural and rustic mages combine the soil and silt drawn out of [[Lake Orial]] with the waste of livestock, and use magical processes to transform it into incredible topsoil capable of growing all manner of vibrant crops and foodstuffs.

Farms in the Redrak Fields are typically owned and operated collectively by labor councils comprised of all those who work the land in a given locality. By longstanding mutual agreement, these labor councils supply the agrimages of Rortwark and the dredgers of Lake Orial's [[Ushna Dredging]] with food and other necessities, in exchange for the magically enriched topsoil. In a balance both symbiotic and delicate, it is this soil that enables the excess agricultural production through which the councils are able to feed the agrimages and dredgers, and trade with House Lilifeld in Ordain for goods not available locally in the Redrak Fields.

### Environment

Stretching from the coast of the [[Reaver Ocean]] to the chaotic curves of the Tarca River and the shores of Lake Orial, the Redrak Fields are a gently rolling landscape awash in broad seas of grain, vineyards, and grass that sway rhythmically with the wind. Travel across this land is easy, and what dangers exist have traditionally been few and far between.

### Ecology

This bountiful terrain is adorned with ubiquitous red grass, its hue ascribed to the rich soil derived from the nearby Lake Orial, and nourished by the Tarcas River running along the northern [[Bloodwoods]]. Apart from this omnipresent grass, the Redrak Fields is devoid of any significant tree population, except for the ones deliberately planted by the local residents.

The Redrak Fields are home to various creatures found elsewhere in the Arctus Plateau region, such as Weaver Bugs, whose domed nests dot the grasses, and Tiller Birds, who make a nuisance of themselves on farms. Meanwhile, both wild and domesticated [[Grassrunners]], Lodoxeros, and Docktail Lizards can be encountered here. Around Lake Orial, one can also find mighty [[Yarnac]] imported from distant [[Bejak]].

### Lore

The Redrak Fields are a region shot through with magic that flows from the massive Lake Orial up north and the endless earth magic that flows from the moon of [[Cora]] thanks to an age-old pact with the Korvathi sage [[Terrane]], made a millenia ago by the first agrimages of the [[Agrimage Circle]].

Unknown even to the agrimages, Terrane turned Lake Orial into the site of an elemental prison holding [[Slaith]], a corrupted Coran earth elemental. It is Slaith's very body that provides the silt above its prison with the magical properties the mud is known for. The folk of [[Ushna Dredging]] unwittingly draw up the infused water and soil, and spread it across the land; this has made the region immensely productive in terms of agriculture, but leaves it vulnerable to a growing influence from the trapped earth sage under the lake — and its prison is not as sturdy as it once was.
