# Amerasp Grove

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This location exists at ⬢ s.3194.2905 in the [[Bloodwoods]], west of [[Lake Yrthain]].
>
> - This location can be explored within the [[Amerasp Grove]] Area Map.
> - This location is featured in [[The Primordial Grove]].

Amerasp Grove is a dense stand of black aspens built around a large, freestanding vespiary maintained by the ruby collectors of Talei. [[Amerasps]] gather materials from the surrounding trees, while collectors use the scaffolding to inspect the colonies and harvest its ruby galls. Like other vespiaries scattered throughout the Bloodwoods, the area is dangerous, but manageable if the proper precautions are taken.

### Environment

The grove is dense even by the standards of the Bloodwoods. Black aspens crowd around the vespiary on all sides, their trunks close enough in places to force travelers into single file. Thick resin clings to bark, roots, and timber scaffolding alike, leaving the underbrush gummy underfoot. The air is warm and humid, filled with the saccharine smell of sap and the pheromonal musk of the amerasps.

### Ecology

The amerasp wasps of the Bloodwoods are dangerous creatures, but the ruby collectors of Talei have learned to manage them through a careful and tenuous form of vespiculture. Collectors build rows of artificial nests from timber, woven fiber, and treated resin, giving the [[Amerasp Queen]] a durable structure to colonize. These nests last much longer than natural ones thanks to their reinforced frames and regular maintenance, ensuring that the amerasp colonies do not migrate from their appointed sites. The wasps gather sap and fibrous materials from the surrounding black aspens — hollowing them out in the process — then pack the vespiary with galls of russet resin. These galls are then harvested and brought back to Talei, where they are processed into ruby.

### Lore

This vespiary has served the people of Talei as a reliable source of ruby galls for nearly a hundred years, though its scaffolding has begun to show signs of strain. Like any healthy vespiary, its colony depends on the pheromonal orders of its queen. During the events of [[Ruby Grove]], Kyrban and his Doomsayers have corrupted this particular vespiary to suit their own schemes.
