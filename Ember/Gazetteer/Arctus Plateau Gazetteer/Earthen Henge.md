# Earthen Henge

The Earthen Henge is a weathered ritual site established a millennia ago by Arcturian mages seeking to strengthen their connection to Cora. Though unused for centuries, the henge still holds a lingering concentration of elemental power preserving the site in its bare, undisturbed state.

The henge was once used as a place of communion with elemental sages of Cora but over time its use declined, and the site passed from active tradition into old records, eventually becoming little more than a seldom-mentioned entry in Agrimage Circle archives, and secret tricentennial meetings by the head of the Agrimage Circle and trusted subordinates.

### Environment

The henge stands in an open, quiet cliffside where the soil is dry, compact, and clean. As creatures approach the circle, they feel subtly lighter, with each step carrying a little farther than expected and loose clothing or hair seeming to lift in the still air.

Small pebbles, flakes of cracked stone, and particles of earth periodically rise from the ground and drift in slow, spiraling patterns around the standing stones before falling back into place. The air hums with a strange energy, and the stones are known to emit an unearthly green light.

### Ecology

Natural growth avoids the henge entirely. Plants flourish normally beyond the outer edge of the cleared ground, but grasses, mosses, and weeds dare not cross into the circle. Animals are reluctant to linger here, though birds may perch briefly on the taller stones before taking flight again.

### Lore

The Earthen Henge was established a millenia ago by Arcturian mages who sought to make a stable ritual connection to Cora, sculpted by the Korvathi — earth elementals that the agrimages summoned from Cora. At its height, the henge was often used as a site of communion with great Korvathi sages, and became a central site for the development of agrimagic.

As magical practices changed and the agrimagic culture become less focused on the sages, the henge was visited less often, and is now left unused between the secret tricentennial meetings with the Korvathi sage [[Terrane]] known only to the head of the Agrimage Circle and trusted subordinates.

Today, it is mostly known through [[Agrimage Circle]] records as an archaic site of stellar communion, noted more as a curiosity of past practice than as a place anyone still expects to serve a practical purpose, though occasional, particularly knowledgeable travelers — and their copycats — are known to construct stone cairns along the ledge of the henge, to beseech the blessing of Cora.
