# Mistwalk

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This settlement exists at ⬢ s.3192.2894 in the [[Barrows]], northwest of [[Brevin]].
>
> - This settlement is visually depicted by the Mistwalk Vista.
> - This settlement is featured in `[[/eventState cindaric3Mistwalk]]`.

Situated near the eastern shores of the Barrows and constructed on a foundation of ancient [[Shent]] ruins, Mistwalk is a fledgling settlement marked by the constant presence of a murky fog that shrouds its crumbling yet stalwart battlements. Mistwalk is primarily known for two things: its proximity to the shadow-haunted tombs of the long-dead [[Varún]] Giants, and its congregation of multiple faiths that have rallied together against this lurking, unknowable threat. Verily, the more superstitious citizens of Mistwalk even believe the stony foundations of the settlement are haunted (a rumor that isn't true, even if it's a source of countless ghost stories).

Mistwalk has recently become a bulwark against the rising threat of undead incursions emanating from the surrounding Barrows and [[Wedgelands]] beyond, providing an element of ignorant relief for the citizens of [[Ordain]] and other people of the [[Arctus Plateau]]. As such, it’s also become the fateful meeting place of a stoic congregation of warriors, priests, and spellswords known as the Mistwalk Sodality — all of whom are drawn to this bleak border town for their own reasons, many of them mystically or divinely appointed.

## Landmarks

**The All-ShrineCenter of Worship**

This candlelit plaza in the center of town features altars to most of the deities worshipped by denizens of Mistwalk, with a particular focus on the various sects that comprise the Mistwalk Sodality. Eldest among the shrines are the altars to Volta (the Dawnbringer), Lumé (the Warrior Queen), and Thayloc (the Silver Bowman), whose adherents were the first warriors called to congregate here.

**The CraterTraining Field**

A concave pit marked by the smell of earth, sweat, and blood, this circular training field caters to the Sodality and other warriors of Mistwalk, who hone their skills in constant preparation for the settlement's ceaseless strife. The wry Kivahr fighter known as Weaponmaster Khad holds court over the Crater, and provides individualized training for those who can afford his time (and weather his insults).

**Handal's LodgeLodging**

Although its timber walls are constantly damp and its cloistered rooms are perpetually cold, this large boarding house on the southeastern edge of town is one of the more welcoming spots in Mistwalk, and provides both temporary and long-term housing to newcomers and frequenters alike.

## Characters of Interest

**Sister Aera StreddLawful Good, Arcturian Aster, she/her**

This unflappable cleric of [[Volta]] serves as the even-handed overseer of the All-Shrine. Despite being known for her love of secret lore and decadent [[Ruby]] cakes, Sister Stredd may very well be the most level-headed denizen of Mistwalk, if not the entire Barrows. Aera Stredd's [[Aster]] nature is exhibited by her innate ability to shapeshift into a [[Casir Cat]] made of arcane light.

**Handal KrestNeutral Good, Arcturian Human, he/him**

Mr. Krest is the humorously cantankerous owner of the eponymous Handal's Lodge, and is highly regarded for his ability to turn a dour mood into an agreeable one (whether he intends to or not). Rumor has it that Handal Krest was once a talented explorer of the [[Pathways]], and that his irascible nature is the result of years of lightless solitude; but Handal is reluctant to confirm the truth of this tale to newcomers.

**Weaponmaster KhadNeutral, Arcturian Kivahr, he/him**

This sharp-tongued fighter offers expert tutelage in the arts of martial and exotic weaponry, and works alongside other warriors as the head trainer of The Crater, where hopeful students can pay a handsome price to learn (and master) the intricacies of Khad's worldly techniques. A hard-earned friendship with Khad is said to last more than a lifetime, because his wisdom and guidance are as timeless as Ember itself.

**Bolton ShentstoneChaotic Good, Arcturian Hul'grun, he/they**

Proud of spirit and sharp of mind, the roguish scholar known as Bolton Shentstone is an enterprising young agent of the [[Anachraenum]] who volunteered for assignment to Mistwalk in an effort to learn more about his [[Giant]] progenitors.

**Rowena MontrestLawful Evil, Ordani Human, she/her**

Masquerading as a harmless [[Ordani Aristocrat]] antiquarian, Rowena Montrest is actually a would-be acolyte of the Cult of [[Nethehepticas]], covertly appointed to spy on Mistwalk for her dark masters in the Grimhold.

## Life in Mistwalk

Life in Mistwalk is markedly dangerous compared to other settlements of the Arctus Plateau, thanks to the town's remote location and proximity to ancient tombs of the restless dead. Mistwalk's very existence is predicated on the exploration of these graves, and most of the town's people have come to terms with the existential challenges that life in the Barrows presents. The founders of Mistwalk settled into this forlorn area because the ancient ruins that comprise the town's substructure — salted with latent magic of the ancient Shent — were thought to be a hopeful boon against the biome's ghostly threats. Furthermore, the eponymous mists obfuscate the town from the malevolent eyes of would-be predators and invaders … Until now.

### Culture

Despite the danger of existence here, the citizens of Mistwalk are largely unfazed by the town’s “cursed” nature and tend to exhibit a blunt kind of gallows humor in their personalities and way of life. Some of the despair that comes with living in the Barrows is mitigated by the presence of The All-Shrine, a plaza in the center of town that caters to a wide array of Ember's religions, and duly serves as a fount of hope and inspiration for the dour and downtrodden.

### Economy

While many citizens of Mistwalk are itinerant members of the Mistwalk Sodality (that come and go according to their needs), other locals make their living through an assortment of occupations, including: domestication of regional livestock, extraction of regional ores and precious metals, white (and black) market export of rare Varùn artifacts, and the protection of hapless outlanders in need of secure escort.

### Politics

The Council of Mistwalk is a group of locally-elected officials tasked with overseeing the day-to-day administration of governance. When it comes to managing the ongoing collection of archaeological goods, [[House Wandren]] of Ordain controls the village's purse strings. However, the recent undead incursion has caused them to withdraw their House members, leaving Mistwalk economically isolated. In Wandren's absence, leaders of the Mistwalk Sodality have appointed a handful of Council members to serve the town's political needs in their stead.

### Defense

As a settlement founded by and for a warrior class, Mistwalk has been an overtly defensible town since its inception. Shared anticipation of an undead incursion resulted in the timely construction of a timber palisade around the town's perimeter, which bears notable signs of a recent assault. Nearly every citizen of Mistwalk is prepared for the worst, and these stalwart frontier people are prepared to take up arms whenever necessary; in addition to the skilled warriors of the Mistwalk Sodality, the town can always muster a sizable militia in times of crisis.

### Lore

Long before the Council of Mistwalk would send out their last raven bearing their call for aid from the recent undead attack, an order of champions from all across Ember began to gather here in preparation for some fated event — a preordained happening that only few of them claim to comprehend. Some of these champions dreamt of this moment; others received what they consider a divine vision. The precise reason for this gathering is unknown, even to the most learned of Arcturian sages, but the recent appearance of animated Varùn corpses confirms the alliance's mutual suspicions of a greater unseen threat. Made of priests, paladins, warriors, rangers, and spellcasters from all walks of life, this order of sentinels — known as the Mistwalk Sodality — stands ready to face whatever threat comes their way. Naturally, religion has become an important part of daily life here. Faiths of Mistwalk may not share the same tenets or ideologies, but they share a remarkable amount of respect for each other.
