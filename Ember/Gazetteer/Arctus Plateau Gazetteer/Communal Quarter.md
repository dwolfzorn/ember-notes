### Culture

The way of Tajar's Communal Quarter exemplifoes the district's name: whatever is to be done, Tajarins do together. Tending animals and farmland, repairing infrastructure, preparing meals, raising children, and more are all seen as communal responsibilities. While there are always skilled specialists compensated for their work, community members regularly work voluntarily alongside these skilled laborers in order to make the Tajarin way of life possible. At the end of the day, the merrymaking is shared by all, whether that be in large outdoor parties out on the Savanna grasses, or — during the Savanna's many storms, fires, and other natural disasters — in torchlit underground shelters. For a Tajarin, the celebration of life's joys continues through rain, shine, and even flame.

### Landmarks

**Hall of the Weathered**

This long hall was one of the first buildings constructed by contemporary Arcturians, and now serves as the meeting place of Tajar's Wise-Women, where all major decisions about the city are made. The cavernous chamber easily fits the entire assembly, and its edifice resembles the women themselves — craggy and worn, but nevertheless standing despite countless years of hardship.

**The People's Pasture**

Just before the Bridge of the Eons — which spans the river between Tajar's Communal Quarter and the islanded Historic Quarter — lies this massive building. It is the only Cinderspar Sedyri structure in the contemporary Communal Quarter — or at least, the only one that remains — and most believe it once functioned as a defensive outpost to protect the Sedyri city across the bridge from invasion by the Aedir. Now, the old fort serves as a fireproof headquarters for the Flamewardens, who in turn protect the rest of Tajar from the Caryx Savanna's harsh fire season.

**Flamewardens' Keep**

These wide grassy fields on the outskirts of the city are open not just to Tajar's citizens, but to all travelers as well. Paid for by the Tajar Farming Cooperative, the pasture nevertheless encourage both monetary donations and volunteer labor to help maintain this communal space.

**The Buried Warehouse**

This warehouse stores a massive supply of construction materials, kept constantly stocked in order to effect immediate repairs on the frequent occasion that the Savanna's natural forces damage or even obliterate a building in Tajar. The warehouse itself is partially underground, in order to protect such emergency supplies from the elements.

### Locals

**[[Vekaris Ossa]] (Lawful Good, Arcturian Ashka, pronouns)**

Vekaris Ossa is an elderly Ashka who serves as the matriarch who sits at the head of the council of Wise-Women that lead Tajar. In her younger years, she was known for her bardic tales and beautiful singing voice, but in old age, her reputation instead comes from her ability to weave those many stories together into one. Informed by the customs of the ancient Sedyri, and her deep knowledge of and personal connections to near-everyone in Tajar, she leads the city with careful deliberation and an invitation to all to make their voice heard through her.

**[[Flamewarden Tiga]] (Lawful Good, Arcturian Drakon, she/her)**

Tiga is a Drakon in her early thirties and a member of the Caryx Savanna's Flamewardens, who bear the responsibility of protecting the people of the Savanna from its dangerous fire season. Having been inducted as a full Flamewarden in her late teenage years, she is known as one of the youngest ever to join their ranks — a result of Tiga's well-known and widely-demonstrated patience, caution, and oneness with the natural world. Tiga is known to bring out the best in that which lies around her — both people and the environments they live in.

**Flamesentinel Syral Paran (Lawful Good, Arcturian Ashka, she/her)**

Flamesentinel Syral Paran is a brave, charismatic, and hands-on leader who serves as both the head of the Flamewardens, and a member of Tajar's Wise-Women. This is a rare feat, as few who bear the dangerous responsibilities of a Flamewarden survive long enough to serve on Tajar's civil council. Nevertheless, her countless years of protecting the city have left her well-loved, and rumors say the Wise-Women may suspend the traditional rule preventing a Flamewarden from also serving as the council's head matriarch, in order to allow Syral to ascend to that rank when the current matriarch passes.

**Augus Karn (Neutral, Arcturian Ashka, he/him)**

Augus Karn is the elected head administrator of the Tajar Farming Cooperative — the city's largest employer and the source of most of its food. Karn is notable as the first administrator to court trade with the great houses of Ordain, bringing a variety of new luxuries to the quite traditional city of Tajar. Opinions on this are mixed, and Augus' nickname of "the most powerful man in Tajar" is somewhat tongue-in-cheek; he lacks much competition for the title.

### Lore

The story of Tajar's Communal Quarter is one of expansion — while the original Arcturian settlers founded Tajar in the ruins of the ancient Sedyri city that once stood in Tajar's Historic Quarter, they very quickly outgrew the small island, and the Communal Quarter became the basis of what Tajar *would* be, rather than what it *was*. As preservation efforts caused Tajarins to leave the Historic Quarter, the Communal Quarter struggled to keep up — especially given the intensive construction techniques necessary to withstand the Savanna's weather — and some credit the increasingly crowded nature for the highly communal and collaborative atmosphere of modern Tajar. This process was accelerated by the Great Burning and subsequent abandonment of the Flooded Quarter. However, some contemporary currents threaten to take Tajar in a different direction; increasing interest from tourists and Ordani traders threatens to has introduced a newly mercantile environment, and a few rare but powerful Tajarins are quite interested in capitalizing on this budding change.
