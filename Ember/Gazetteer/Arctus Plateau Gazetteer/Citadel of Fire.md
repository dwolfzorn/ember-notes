# Citadel of Fire

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This location exists at ⬢ s.3234.2889 in the [[Caryx Savanna]], atop Mount Sitara, just north of Tajar.

> - This location is featured in [[Burning Hearts]].

### Environment

Though constructed by the divine power of [[Fiyara]], the citadel's edifice is a perfect recreation of [[Sedyri]] Cinderspar — an arcane echo of the long-destroyed Sedyri fire temple that once stood at the same location. The citadel's design, however, betrays its divergent origin. Runes of terrible power adorn its walls, and the bastion's architecture betrays not even the slightest hint of Sedyri duality. Within this great monument to [[Ragen]], veneration is reserved for flame alone.

### Ecology

The Citadel of Fire begets no life but flame. The air shimmers with overbearing heat at all hours of the day, driving off all but the most fire-resistant fauna, and the citadel's superheated surfaces permit no flora to take root in its crevices. The citadel is inhabited primarily by creatures for whom flame is their nature: elementals of pure fire. As long as Fiyara reigns within the citadel, even these great beings cannot escape her power, and even the most peaceful elementals are driven slowly but inevitably to hatred and heartless violence.

### Lore

The citadel's location atop Mount Sitara is no accident of fate. When the joint powers of Ragen and Fiyara blazed in through the crack opened by the Spark of the Savanna, their gazes were drawn irresistibly to the fiery peak; not just due to the volcano beneath it, but so too the lingering magical energy of the Sedyri fire temple that once sat atop the mountain. Though a catastrophic eruption obliterated the abandoned temple nearly two millennia ago, the location still resonates with pure elemental essence of fire. It is this same well of energy that fuels the great forge in the citadel's core, capable of smithing materials which would refuse to give way before even the most unimaginably powerful of mundane fires.
