# Orthinal Bridge

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This location exists at ⬢ s.3231.2870 in the [[Golden Flats]], in the lower region to the west of the town of Arcturel.

Today, the Orthinal Bridge is used on a weekly, if not daily, basis, as caravans from Toraga make their way to Ordain, and caravans from Ordain likewise make their way across on their way to Toraga. The main span of the bridge is maintained by these passing caravans to the best of their ability, with the bridge floor cleared of plants and vines. However, the rest of the structure remains a living ecosystem, as plants, bushes, and vines crawl across most of its surface, even trailing deep into the Splinter Canyons below.

### Environment

The Orthinal Bridge is considered a local wonder by many, as its longevity is seen as evidence of some of the last remaining Aedir magic left in the world. Not only is the bridge in good condition, but it has also remained strong in the face of local catastrophes and even an attempt to destroy it during the Mourning War several centuries ago. Even the near-constant use by the massive and extremely heavy caravans of the various Arcturian merchants that pass across it does not seem to be wearing down the white stone it is made of.

### Ecology

Although the Bridge itself is covered by a vast collection of local plants, bushes, and vines. It seems to repel local fauna. No birds or small animals are seen among the foliage, and while this may have something to do with the many caravans passing across its length on a regular basis, several researchers have noted that even during quiet periods, no animals have been observed passing across the bridge or emerging from the foliage that covers it. Further study has suggested that the bridge emits a very subtle magical aura, which is likely what has kept it in such pristine condition, but that aura may also be driving local wildlife away.

In fact, the Orthinal Bridge is a major reason why the more aggressive wildlife to the west does not traditionally cross into the more peaceful areas around Arcturel. At least until the massive earthquake ten years ago, after that, several of the region's predators somehow found their way across.

### Lore

The Aedir settled on the Arctus Plateau, mainly along its coastlines thousands of years ago, and for a long time, they avoided land travel. It was often more convenient and cheaper to sail around obstacles. During this period, the Arctus Plateau was also much more treacherous, which is why the Bridge was built later in their expansion, during the rise of the Aedir Sunfire Empire, mainly to control the local area but also to facilitate local travel. The Aedir used the construction of the bridge to study the extremely deadly Spliter Canyons, which were inhabited by large groups of [[Juvenile Garganthus]]. They eventually found ways to harvest and control the creatures. Additionally, the bridge acted as a barrier, preventing hostile creatures from easily traveling between different parts of the Plateau, a strategy they used elsewhere as well.
