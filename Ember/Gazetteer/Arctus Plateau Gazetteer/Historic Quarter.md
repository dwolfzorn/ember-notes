# Historic Quarter

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This district exists at ⬢ s.3246.2893. It is located in the northeastern hex of the city of Tajar, which itself lies along the southern coast of the [[Caryx Savanna]].
>
> - This district is indirectly featured in [[The Flood and the Flame]] Side Quest.

> [!info] Social
> #### At a Glance
>
> - **District Type:** Historic (Largely uninhabited ruins and protected land)
> - **Size:** 10 - 25 (Guards and researchers)
> - **Services:** Historical research, environmental maintenance.
> - **Architecture**: Ruins (Eroded ancient Sedyri Cinderspar buildings)

### Culture

Those who work in Tajar's Historic Quarter share a single common purpose: to see that the winds of time — and the fire and floods of the present — never take what Tajar *was* away from what Tajar *is.* The historians, archaeologists, antiquarians, guards, and Flamewardens who devote their lives to the investigation and preservation of the Historic Quarter believe that history always has something more to teach the present, and it is only by their efforts that the Cinderspar buildings of the ancient Sedyri — and more fragile other constructions — stand unmarred by contemporary use and destructive weather of the Caryx Savanna. To the irritation of some, this mission does make for a rather unwelcoming environment for those outside of it; anyone who does not share preservation as their first and foremost priority are regarded as an unwelcome presence (if not outright disallowed from entry) by the Historic Quarter's cadre of preservationists.

### Landmarks

**Bridge of the Eons**

Constructed of pure Cinderspar by the ancient Sedyri and named by modern Tajarins for its seemingly timeless nature, the Bridge of the Eons is the only way to travel between the islanded Historic Quarter once inhabited by the ancient Sedyri, and the landward Communal Quarter where Tajarins of today reside. Its towering arches rise hundreds of meters out of the water without a single seam to suggest they were constructed of multiple blocks or pieces. Scholars today believe the monumental arches were transported whole — or even formed in place — via magical means known only to the Sedyri.

**Firewarden Emergency Shelter**

Though the building's original function has been lost to time, this partially underground and entirely windowless building — with unusually thick walls of nigh-invulnerable Cinderspar — makes for the perfect shelter from particularly dangerous storms or wildfires. It is for this reason that Tajar's Wise-Women and the Historic Quarter's caretakers have collectively agreed to make an exception to the typical usage restriction on Historic Quarter ruins, and granted the Flamewardens permission to stock it with rations and emergency supplies to make use on during especially devastating natural disasters.

**The Sunken Tower**

While exactly what degree of archaeological excavation constitutes preservation rather than destruction is a constant source of tension between those who research in the Historic Quarter and those who preserve it, the Sunken Tower serves as a useful and unusual point of mutual agreement. Having been constructed at the bottom of a small depression, millennia worth of stormwater has caused the foundation to sink into the earth, collapsing the tower into rubble that archaeologists can dig through for Sedyri artifacts, without fear of damaging an intact structure.

**The Great Dial**

In the center of the Historic Quarter is a wide pavilion with a sundial of near-incomprehensible scale; the gnomon itself rises far enough into the air that its shadow could provide shade to an entire trade caravan, were such caravans allowed to travel through the Historic Quarter.

### Locals

**Asara Kier (Neutral Good, Arcturian Kiska, they/them)**

Asara Kier is an incredibly skilled archaeologist and a foremost expert in the history of the ancient Sedyri. Though she was an inseparable childhood friend of famed Anachraenum archeologist [[Fernis Ossa]], when Fernis decided to leave for Ordain, Asara found themself unable to join her — she fervently believes that the Sedyri ruins of Tajar's Historic Quarter contain more secrets yet to be uncovered than any one scholar could dig up in their lifetime.

**Flamewarden Yyra Rhen (Lawful Good, Arcturian Ashka, she/her)**

Flamewarden Yyra Rhen works tirelessly Tajar's Historic Quarter from the Caryx Savanna's infamous elements. While her fellow Flamewardens primarily protect the Savanna's people, Yyra and her few subordinates are instead tasked to protect its *past*. Day in and day out, Yyra devises strategies to keep the remains of the ancient Sedyri buildings from wearing away completely — despite unrelenting bombardment by the Savanna's natural elements.

**Ikra Tanir (Lawful Neutral, Arcturian Ashka, he/him)**

Where Flamewardens like Yyra Rhen protect the Historic Quarter's ruins from threats monumental, the elderly caretaker Ikra Tanir rather preserves them in the face of threats mundane. Ikra delicately cleans grit, dries water, and identifies structural issues for others to address, and more. The old caretaker has a reputation among some for being somewhat irritating, but those who understand the delicacy required to preserve such history are more understanding of the chiding admonitions he foists on the district's more reckless tourists.

**Stevin Venn (Chaotic Neutral, Arcturian Ashka, he/him)**

When the citizens of Tajar began to gradually, over centuries, abandon the Historic Quarter out of the collective desire to preserve the city's history, the Venn family said "You can't make us." The old and childless Stevin Venn is the last in a centuries-long line of Venn who have refused to leave their family home and have tried — at great personal cost — to maintain their residential life in the Historic Quarter. He is much despised by the caretaker Ikra Tanir, and vice versa.

### Lore

While some question the utility of the preservation and research effort in the Historic Quarter — which entailed a centuries-long effort to expand the Communal Quarter and move Tajarins off of the island — fewer still understand the benefits such efforts have brought to Tajar.

While modern Tajarins have yet to crack the secret of forging Cinderspar, investigation into Sedyri construction techniques has uncovered vital methods of designing buildings for the Communal Quarter that can withstand the threat of the Savanna's weather. Translations of Sedyri etchings and inscriptions have woven themselves into Tajar's songs and stories, and become a uniting rallying cry for defiance in the face of the Savanna's punishing elements. Magical artifacts dug up by the Historic Quarter's archaeologists have equipped the Flamewardens with supernatural abilities vital to protecting Tajarins from the fire and flood that assaults them season after season. While the sight of empty buildings may look — to the eyes of some in the crowded Communal Quarter — like untapped potential, those with a deeper understanding of Tajar's legacy know that it is precisely the Historic Quarter's emptiness that has allowed the Communal Quarter to flourish.
