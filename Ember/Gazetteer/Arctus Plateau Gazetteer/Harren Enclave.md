# Harren Enclave

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This location exists at ⬢ s.3214.2879 in the [[Golden Flats]], in the lower region near Arcturel.

Harren Enclave is a large Agrimage building situated among a once well-maintained grove of golden trees. Now abandoned, the building's contents have mostly been removed or left to collect dust. The interior features a spacious central common area, several smaller sleeping quarters, a kitchen, and storage rooms. In the common area, the Agrimages gathered local rural knowledge in a small library built into the walls, while several sturdy planters were used for experiments or simply growing local plants for study or meals. The central space was often lit by numerous windows overhead.

### Environment

Although the interiors have been cleared and abandoned, there is no sense of violence or harm to the inhabitants. Instead, a thin layer of dust covers the furniture and some of the wooden beams and flooring, which, under open windows, shows small patches of moss where rainwater has seeped in.

### Ecology

Outside Harren Enclave, the surrounding grove of golden trees is wild and growing with vigor, as thick bushes and hardy plants have sprung up among trees that may once have been tended with care. Various small animal noises can be heard in the underbrush, and the wildlife here seems to be thriving now that the previous caretakers are long gone. Several Tiller Birds seem to have made the grove their home, enjoying the dense foliage in order to hide from predators while venturing out to gather food from the nearby grasslands and satellite farms during the day.

### Lore

Harren Enclave was an Agrimage Enclave for nearly three centuries, manned by a small group led by Juri Conlan until just two years ago. It was always slightly isolated from the rest of the Arctus Plateau, located several miles east of the main road. It wasn't until a passing Anachraenum adventurer visited the enclave and reported its abandonment that anyone noticed that the Agrimages stationed there had vanished. There were no signs of struggle, combat, or bloodshed, only that some possessions were missing, including Juri Conlan and the other Agrimages. They have yet to be recovered, and the enclave remains suspicious among locals, despite there being no evidence of foul play. Many believe an evil force or curse has settled over the place, with rumors and stories of an unknown force supposedly whisking away its inhabitants in the dead of night.
