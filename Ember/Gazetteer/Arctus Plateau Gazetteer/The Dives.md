Located nearly a mile below Arcturel, The Dives is both the heart of the city's mining community and the core of its operations. A collection of mines, businesses, and homes comprises the area that rarely sees the light cast by Lantyr, except when the occasional alignment and positioning lines up perfectly. Many buildings are cut into the rock of the sinkhole itself or are suspended in the air by massive columns and hanging chains. Thanks to its traditionally unsavory reputation, The Dives isn't exactly renowned as a place for tourists or visiting dignitaries; this industrial area of Arcturel is home to true locals, those who actually live and work within the city or even deeper within the Pathways themselves.

**## Landmarks**

Many of the buildings within The Dives are hardy, squat, industrial, and practical, with few luxuries or places of relaxation. Many miners and workers live here in the Dives, making their way downwards to Rock Bottom below and further into the Sinkhole depths to gather and mine ore, before hauling it back either by hand or more often with the help of various machines and construction helpers.

**[[Hob Korell's Unconventional Steeds]]Stables**

Modest stables owned and operated by the stilt-wearing frog herder named Hob Korell. Featuring a range of rare mounts, including frogs, lizards, and dragonflies of the giant variety.

**[[Zodi Trask's Apartment]]Home**

The private residence of Zodi Trask, the current foreman of the Arcturel Mining Concern and chief contact in the area for House Cevher.

**[[Arvoda's Elixirs]]Alchemical Shop**

The residence, workshop, and storefront of Emelyn Arvoda, a matronly water mage who crafts scrolls, potions, and other magic items for travelers of the lightless depths.

**[[Wrestful Repairs]]Blacksmith**

A repair shop owned by Rennerie Wrest, former miner and current metal artist; the mining operations often rely on him for big jobs, but he always tries to make time for individuals and odds jobs. The shop changes its slogan frequently, but none have managed to catch on. The current iteration, "Not At Its Best? Come Get Some Wrest!" does not appear likely to change this track record.

**[[Waterborne Distillery]]Brewery**

The Waterborne Distillery, which is run by a family of trained distillers, primarily makes brews from water found in the Inkaro Pools, which improves the taste and is thought to put people in a good mood. The family is currently under the guidance of elder member Sevali Waterborne.

**## Life In The Dives**

Life in The Dives is a ceaseless, echoing symphony of clanging pickaxes, grinding gears, and the heavy thud of creaking lifts transporting raw ore from the depths of the Pathways. Residents navigate their days in near-total darkness, illuminated only by the artificial, amber glow of torches, lamps, or bioluminescent fungi. Time is often measured by shift whistles rather than the rising of the sun. It is a harsh existence where every citizen and worker has a layer of rock dust on their skin, yet there is an undeniable, survivalist pride among these true locals who look down on the soft, pampered lives of those living on the surface.

### Culture

The culture of this subterranean district is forged in the shared peril of mining the Pathways' extreme depths, breeding a fiercely loyal, close-knit community where a neighbor's reliability can mean the difference between life and death. Because they rarely catch a glimpse of the light cast by Lantyr, the rare moments of perfect alignment when the light penetrates the deep are treated as brief moments of respite, where many take time to rest and recover before the darkness envolps them once more. Superstition among the locals runs rampant in The Dives, with miners leaving small offerings at tunnel thresholds to appease elementals or creatures they fear lurk in the darkness.

### Economy

The economy of the Dives is entirely driven by the sweat and blood of heavy industry, which serves as the primary source of all the mineral wealth, precious metals, and raw arcane components that fuel Arcturel’s prosperity. While the Arcturel proper above hoards the ultimate profits, the Dives thrives on a massive internal market of tool-smithing, heavy-machinery repair, and workers’ taverns.

### Politics

Politics in the Dives is led by three dominant groups: House Cevher, the Silver Beam Consortium, and the local miners who call The Dives their home. Both House Cevher and the Silver Beam Consortium skillfully manage the miners, understanding that a strike would immediately halt the city's productivity. As a result, a tense truce persists, where miners maintain order and meet production quotas in return for full autonomy.

### Lore

The Dives began centuries ago as a chaotic, temporary web of scaffolding and rope ladders constructed by the very first pioneers who dared to follow the rich mineral veins into the heart of the sinkhole. As the excavations grew deeper and eventually discovered that a section of Pathways existed below the Sinkhole, the scaffolding slowly evolved into permanent structures and suspended walkways capable of housing working families. Its history is marred by cave-ins, industrial sabotage from competing businesses, and whispered tales of horrifying things that have crawled up from the darkness.
