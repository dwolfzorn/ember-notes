# Talei

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This settlement exists at ⬢ s.3199.2908 in the [[Bloodwoods]], north of [[Ordain Gazetteer]].
>
> - This settlement is visually depicted by the [**Talei]** Vista.
> - This settlement is featured in the [[Ruby Grove]] Side Quest.

Talei is best known for ruby: a red, jam-like substance harvested from the galls of amerasp wasps and refined in the Riverside Ruby Works. When ingested, ruby strengthens a person's connection to the Inner Realm of Primordis, allowing them to better withstand the Bloodwoods' mercurial magic. Without ruby, long travel through the forest would be difficult, if not impossible. The substance also acts as an analgesic, which eventually attracted the attention of the Cindaric Sages.

Ruby became an industry out of necessity. Over generations, the people of Talei developed a form of vespiculture around the amerasp wasps, guiding colonies into artificial vespiaries where their galls could be regularly harvested. The work remains dangerous, but it is central to the town's survival. In recent years, it has also become profitable: the socialites of Ordain discovered that large doses of ruby can produce fleeting euphoric episodes, turning Talei's means of survival into a fashionable vice.

The generational exposure to ruby has left its mark on the people of Talei. Most residents have a reddish cast to their hair, eyes, and nails, ranging from dark russet to bright crimson. Their customs show older marks, too: though Talei appears Acturian to most outsiders, the town was shaped by the Waerd, Maziran, and Kithil families who first settled the forest's edge.

## Landmarks

**Mayor's HomeGovernment Lodge**

A sturdy, two-story dwelling made from very old marble oak taken from Talei's founding tree. It sits on a small rise just north of the town's heart. The wood has blanched with age, but faint red veining still shows beneath the grain.

**The General StoreStore / Trading Post**

A large, single-story structure that serves as both storehouse and trading post. The back half of the building is dedicated to bulk storage: sacks of grain, spare tools, bolts of cloth, preserved food, lantern oil, rope, and other necessities. The front is crowded with shelves and bins where residents trade goods and leave items for neighbors who need them.

**Riverside Ruby WorksIndustrial Shop**

This riverside factory is where amerasp galls are sorted, opened, boiled, and strained into the jam-like substance known as ruby. Waterwheels turn slowly beside the building, powering simple machinery within. Outsiders are not permitted inside unless under the mayor's personal supervision.

**The Oakmark LodgeSocial Lodge**

The Oakmark Lodge stands on the northern edge of town, adjacent to the mayor's home and constructed of the same marble oak taken from Talei's founding tree. It is a large, austere building where Talei's hunters — the Oakmark — gather before entering the forest. Its walls are hung with old trophies, weatherproof cloaks, and maps that resemble star charts, but are actually records of marked marble oak trees. The black aspens often move, but the marble oaks remain stationary, making them reliable guideposts.

**The Founding TreeHistorical Site**

Near the center of Talei stands the hollow stump of an enormous marble oak that was used to construct the town's first homes. Its interior has been filled with rich, dark soil and turned into a communal garden.

## Characters of Interest

**ProctusNeutral Good, Arcturian Human, he/him**

Proctus is the mayor of Talei, a title his family has held in one form or another since the town's founding. He governs from the old marble oak home north of the town's center, and is accustomed to solving the kinds of disputes that can be hashed out over a drink on the front porch. Proctus is a warm but practical person who is more concerned with keeping the locals safe than attracting outsiders.

**Aximillian PordenLawful Good, Arcturian Human, he/him**

Aximillian Porden runs the General Store, which serves as Talei's storehouse, trading post, and gossip mill. Most residents prefer to trade amongst themselves, and Aximillian is trusted to arbitrate those exchanges fairly. When Ordani merchants arrive with outside coin, he is also one of the few people in town with the patience and business sense to deal with them.

**KivvoNeutral Good, Arcturian Keth, they/them**

Kivvo is Talei's only formal teacher and its closest equivalent to a smith. A skilled transmuter even by Ordani standards, they oversee the continued operation of the Riverside Ruby Works. Kivvo teaches the town's children three times a week — or four, if they behave badly.

**[[Maevren]]Neutral Good, Arcturian Human, she/her**

Maevren is a veteran Bloodwoods tracker and one of the Oakmark's most trusted guides. She leads ruby harvesters through shifting black aspen paths to their amerasp vespiaries and fends off any beast that wanders too close to town.

## Life in Talei

Life in Talei is communal, practical, and shaped by its proximity to the Bloodwoods. Most residents are intimately familiar with one another's troubles and family histories; if ruby is Talei's first industry, gossip is its second. Days are spent tending gardens, repairing tools, refining amerasp galls, mingling at the General Store, and preparing the next group of Oakmark and ruby harvesters to enter the forest. Strangers are treated with cold courtesy. Talei is not used to visitors, but it is well accustomed to visitors leaving.

### Culture

Talei's culture is Arcturian on the surface, but older Waerd, Maziran, and Kithil traditions remain visible to those who know what they are looking for. The first Oakmark were Waerd, and the organization still carries a longstanding tradition of trophy collecting and adornment; the Oakmark Lodge is decorated with the hides and horns of dangerous beasts. Maziran influence shows in the town's attachment to lineage and ancestral claim, especially in the mayoral office, which has remained with Proctus' family since Talei's founding. Kithil tradition survives through the art of tattooing, which Talei's people still practice to mark life events.

### Economy

Talei's economy depends on vespiculture: the stewardship of amerasp colonies, gall harvesting, and refinement into ruby. Nearly every household has some connection to the ruby cycle, even if they never enter the Bloodwoods proper. Harvesting tools must be crafted, vespiaries must be woven, and Oakmark must be stitched up after a bad run.

Beyond ruby, Talei survives on practical local trades. Woodcarvers shape black aspen and old marble oak into tools and toys, while fishers work the river upstream, hunters trap small game along the forest edge, and foragers gather mushrooms and herbs. Coin is useful when dealing with outsiders, but most daily trade between locals runs on barter and favors.

### Politics

Talei is governed by a mayor, though the position is less formal than the title suggests. Proctus may have the authority to settle disputes and receive outsiders, but his legitimacy depends on the trust of Talei's entwined institutions: the ruby harvesters and the Oakmark. None of Proctus' ancestors were ever ousted from office, and he does not intend to be the first.

### Defense

Due to its relative isolation, Talei has no standing militia. In rare instances where armed defense is needed — such as when a beast begins nesting nearby — the town relies on the Oakmark to protect it.

### Lore

Talei's own story begins with the founding tree. The first settlers came to the edge of the Bloodwoods following famine and hard travel from the north, and built their earliest homes from the marble oak that still stands as a hollow stump near the town's center. The stump was transformed into a communal garden and remains a civic landmark, a reminder that the things most worth tending are often born of dire circumstances.

Darik Melsbow, founder of the Sanguinaries, had close ties to Talei through his frequent visits to the Bloodwoods. It was during one such visit that he encountered Nimaelle, a Casia of Primordis, and the two formed a deep friendship that lasted until Darik's death. Nimaelle entrusted Darik with a dire truth, that the Heart of Ember was slowly dying.

When Darik brought this startling revelation to his fellow Cindaric Sages, he was summarily dismissed by the Holy Speaker. The resulting schism led to the formation of the Sanguinaries, whose flight from Ordain eventually brought them to Talei. From there, Nimaelle led Darik and his followers to the northernmost reaches of the Bloodwoods, to the great tree that she planted many centuries ago. Within that tree, the Sanguinaries carved their sanctuary: the Arbore Sanctorus.
