# Lilifeld Vineyard

Lilifeld Vineyard is both a highly visible symbol of House Lilifeld’s wealth and refinement, but also a demonstration of Krafton Lilifeld's method: only the best, executed at the absolute highest quality. While much of the Redrak Fields is dedicated to the mass production of food meant to feed masses, the vineyard presents itself as a carefully controlled island of imported excellence, where all the finest grapes of Ember can be cultivated and blended into the perfect wine.

Krafton Lilifeld’s private estate sits within the vineyard grounds, serving as both family residence and political stage. Guests of status may be invited to private tastings, dinners, or negotiations here, while ordinary travelers are more likely to encounter the estate through its public-facing wine halls, delivery yards, or the workers tending the vines.

### Environment

Lilifeld Vineyard occupies a broad stretch of cultivated land in the Redrak Fields, where the terrain is carefully managed with equal measures of physical labor and mystical enhancement. The neat rows of grapevines trained along trellises grow along stone-lined irrigation channels, which are themselves surrounded by gravel paths, allowing plants, water, and workers all the space they need to work.

Beyond the vineyard rows one can fine, wine sheds, covered pressing yards, and barrel houses, each one kept in fine order. Watching over all of this is the manor estate which stands slightly elevated above the rows, giving it a commanding view over the vines and surrounding fields.

Every part of this estate is clean and prestigious, with well-maintained tools, painted doors, polished fixtures, and House Lilifeld markings placed where visitors are sure to see them.

### Ecology

The grapes of Lilifeld Vineyard are not native to the Redrak Fields, and their presence requires constant tending and careful management of the souls. The estate grows several varieties, some prized for deep color and heavy sweetness, others for sharper notes or delicate fragrance. The rich black earth provides excellent nutrients, but the vines must be protected from local grasses, insects, molds, and weather patterns to which they are poorly adapted.

Gardeners, vintners, and agrimagic consultants keep careful watch over the crop, encouraging the vines while suppressing native growth. Beyond the vineyard walls, red grasses quickly reclaim any unattended ground, making the green rows seem even more deliberate and unnatural against the surrounding land.

### Lore

Krafton Lilifeld established this estate both to have a place near Ordain and Rortwark to entertain dignitaries and guest, but to demonstrate his personal desire to find the pinnacle of quality, believing that it above all else is what makes life worth living.

Establishing this vineyard, sourcing the grapes, establishing favorable growing conditions, and hiring a staff up to the task of making everything run smoothly has been a costly, time consuming endeavor that Krafton has overseen himself.
