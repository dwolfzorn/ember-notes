# Carena Tree

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This location exists at ⬢ s.3214.2879 in the [[Golden Flats]], in the lower region near Arcturel.

The Carena Tree is mind-bogglingly massive. Its trunk is wide enough to house a small village, and its canopy casts a permanent, sprawling shadow over the surrounding Golden Flats. The bark resembles tightly woven, petrified vines rather than traditional wood, glowing with a faint, pulsing amber light at night. The area immediately surrounding the tree hums with a low-level transmutation magic, and water drawn from nearby springs tastes faintly of sweet nectar.

### Environment

The Carena tree is so large that it generates its own localized weather system. The air beneath the leaves is cool, and the temperature drops significantly as you approach its trunk, smelling of ozone and crushed herbs. Dew often coalesces on the massive leaves and rains down in a gentle, refreshing mist until midday, creating a lush, verdant oasis in a ring around the trunk.

### Ecology

Many predators of the Golden Flats instinctively avoid the tree's shadow, and it's been reported that several different kinds of grazing herd animals, and smaller plains-dwelling creatures like Tiller Birds often use the base of the Carena Tree as a safe haven to rest. The canopy above is home to a unique species of bioluminescent winged insects called Glowka Moths, which feed on the tree's ambient magic and illuminate the canopy at night.

### Lore

According to the oldest texts, the Carena Tree was planted by the Shent Shard Goddess Sha'lune as a peace offering to the Aedir. It was meant to be a living bridge between their peoples, but it was unfortunately never taken seriously by the Aedir, who viewed the gift as provincial and quaint, dismissing it entirely. Anachraenum scholars who have studied the tree fiercely debate its origins. They argue that its biology is too alien to be a simple gift, theorizing it is an invasive species plucked from the chaotic, primordial forests of Primordis. They believe ancient magic was used to stunt its growth, implying that without these magical bindings, the plant would eventually have consumed the entire Golden Flats.

Local Arcturians often speak of figures emerging from the base, and some say the tree's deep taproots actually pierce the veil between realms, acting as a one-way gateway from Primordis. This has some truth to it, as the tree is magically linked to several other massive Primordis-descended trees in the area, such as Arbore Sanctorus and the Baohim Tree, and this network is often used by the Sanguinaries.
