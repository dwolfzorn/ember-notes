# Khar'koridax

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This location exists at ⬢ s.3192.2889 in the [[Barrows]], three miles north of [[Mythspire Observatory]].
>
> - This location can be explored within the Varun Tomb Area Map.
> - This location is featured in `[[/eventState cindaric3TombHunting]]`.

Long forgotten until its recent discovery, the [[Varún]] tomb known as Khar'koridax is situated beneath one of the biome's larger tumuli, and serves as the eternal resting place of a group of warriors and priests who pledged fealty to the [[Abyssal Horrors]] named [[Nethehepticas]] and his vile cohorts. Known as the Striven, these Varún adherents of Nethehepticas were considered to be some of the most fearsome slayers of their time, and their eternal remains lay in wait for the day when The Bleak Seed or his minions will call them forth for battle once more. In life, these marauders were promised a power everlasting; and in death, this power is made manifest by unholy necromancy. Verily, the Striven continue to serve Nethehepticas and his minions as [[Varún Gravewalkers]] — loathsome undead creatures who wield accursed magic as well as their ancient weapons.

Khar'koridax itself is a gloomy three-story structure, featuring a two-tiered primary vault and a secret ritual chamber beneath, which can only be accessed via interaction with the archaic Varún artifice that binds this place. Upstairs, the main chamber is decorated with an array of Varún hunting trophies and dusty sarcophagi. Meanwhile, the ritual chamber is a gruesome display of bloody arcane liturgy — with four stony altars surrounding a central summoning circle, and a dais hosting a radiant gemstone relic that seethes with eerie crimson light.

### Environment

Khar'koridax was constructed by ancient Varún stonemasons who served the Moonwalker known as Kiroh, the Varún High Priest of [[Cora]]. As such, the archaic stone here is expertly crafted, and laced throughout with subtle protection magic that binds and fortifies it from normal damage and weathering. The air within Khar'koridax has stood relatively still for centuries, and is musty with the odor of long-gone decay.

### Ecology

Other than the arcane luminance of the crystals in the dungeon, light has failed to penetrate the shadows of this place for thousands of years, resulting in a stark lack of plant life. Various species of mushrooms and other fungi have managed to eke out an existence in this lightless vault, and insectoid vermin can sometimes be found prowling the tomb's web-shrouded nooks and crannies. But the true denizens of Khar'koridax are the Varún Gravewalkers that reside in the upstairs sarcophagi. These deathless spellswords slumber in ceaseless anticipation of the day they will rise from their coffins and march against the living.

### Lore

The Varún Gravewalkers that were crafted from the moldering remains of the Striven who were buried here have only recently been animated, thanks to the machinations of [[Amelia Naxan]] — a [[Ossarchate]] of ill repute who also serves as a warlock of Nethehepticas. Furthermore, necromancers of Nethehepticas known as [[Bleak Seekers]] have been assigned the ongoing task of studying the dungeon's curious altars and other accoutrements (including, foremost, the eerie crystalline artifact on the eastern dais). And the most forgotten of legends holds true: the graves within Khar'koridax are laden with ancient Varún treasure, worth a handsome reward to those who seek their secrets.
