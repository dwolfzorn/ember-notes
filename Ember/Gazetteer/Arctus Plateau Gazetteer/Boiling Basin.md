# Boiling Basin

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This location exists at ⬢ s.3231.2870 in the [[Golden Flats]], in the lower region to the south of the town of Arcturel.

The Boiling Basins' deep fissures are directly connected to the Pathways below, much like the surrounding area, only they are more concentrated here. The air itself is hazardous, and a deep, permanent mist hangs over the area, blanketing it so heavily that it's often difficult to see even a few feet ahead. Travelers in the Basin must not only watch the ground for geysers, pools of bubbling sulfuric water, and jagged rocks but also the sky. The sheer force of the eruptions from the Geysers continuously launches basketball-sized shards of rock into the air, creating a constant peril of localized, unpredictable rock rain.

### Environment

The air here is thick, humid, and yellowish-white. It is heavily saturated with sulfuric steam that can scald exposed skin. More dangerously, heavy, invisible pockets of toxic, lethal vapors pool in the lower trenches and craters, capable of suffocating an unprotected traveler in minutes.

### Ecology

No normal life can survive the acidic, superheated environment. Instead, the Basin is home to only a few hardy species of tough, leathery yellow moss that grows near the geysers and feeds directly on the sulfur and minerals blasted from the earth. According to the local Arcturians, the only other inhabitant is the ancient Urkon, Variclaw, who sits at the absolute top of the surrounding food chain. He uses the deafening roar of the geysers to mask his heavy footsteps and massive bulk. While he seldom hunts around his lair, using it instead as a refuge to sleep, he often leaves the Boiling Basin to hunt in the nearby Golden Flats, particularly at night or when the geysers have spread a thick mist that can help hide him.

### Lore

The Boiling Basin and the surrounding Geysers are not ancient themselves, appearing ten years after the great earthquake that rocked the Arctus Plateau. The quake tore open the earth, releasing the pressures of the Pathways and reshaping the Golden Flats overnight. Shortly after the Basin's eruptions began, local Arcturians noticed a grim pattern: livestock went missing, followed by incredibly violent scenes of outlying farmsteads being ripped apart. A hunting party tracking the devastation finally spotted a colossal shape in the sulfuric mists, an Urkon from the old stories. According to ancient myths, Variclaw is the very same beast driven deep into the subterranean Pathways eons ago by a Shent deity. The legends say that although Variclaw survived the clash, his right tooth was broken and his face was horrifically scarred, giving the already terrifying Urkon a Brustish sneer. After a few foolhardy hunters and adventurers tried to kill Variclaw by tracking him to his lair in the Boiling Basin, they were never seen again, and locals now avoid the area.
