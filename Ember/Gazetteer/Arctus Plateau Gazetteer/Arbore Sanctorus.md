# Arbore Sanctorus

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>

> - This location is featured in [[Local Experts]].

The Arbore Sanctorus is the living stronghold and holy refuge of the [[Sanguinaries]], a schismatic order of former Cindaric Sages who believe that the Heart of Ember is dying. Hidden in the northern reaches of the [[Bloodwoods]], the Sanctuary gives the order a place to pursue their research without submitting to the Cindaric's dogma.

The sanctuary is raised within a colossal tree that originated in the Inner Realm of [[Primordis]]. It was planted in the aftermath of the [[Abyssal Shear]] by [[Nimaelle]], a [[Casia]] of Primordis who sought to heal the region's deepest scar.

The Arbore Sanctorus is not merely built into the tree, but carved and pruned from the tree's living body. Its chambers serve the order as places of rest, meditation, and archives of knowledge for the Sanguinaries who dwell there; visitors are rarely permitted beyond the receiving rooms.

### Environment

The Arbore Sanctorus stands deep within the northern Bloodwoods, surrounded by black aspens of remarkable age and size. The tree's bark is white, ridged, and scarred, with a circuitous footpath carved into the exterior that spirals from the forest floor to the sanctuary's upper chambers. The lower roots form natural archways and shaded hollows where small animals nest. Old charms, woven from fallen branches, hang on strings of fraying twine.

Inside, the tree is warm, humid, and smells of sap. Its chambers are shaped from the living wood and lit in soft amber color by magical crystal.

### Ecology

The Arbore Sanctorus is the oldest and most stable expression of the Bloodwoods. Its roots run deep beneath the region — and, some suspect, deep into the Pathways — shepherding the influence of Primordis into all it touches. Plants near the sanctuary tend to be less violently unstable than those elsewhere in the Bloodwoods, though no less strange.

The sanctuary's canopy hosts a variety of creatures that would ordinarily never cohabitate. Vermillion finches, small birds with red-feathered throats and black beaks, nest beside rival hook-billed jays and share resources without contest. Rootshrews — small, pale, and blunt-snouted beasts that burrow through loose soil around tree roots — sleep in the same shaded hollows as the insects they would normally eat. Even amerasp wasps sometimes pass through the upper branches without disturbing anything around them. Most visible of all are the [[Casir Cat]], who move through and around the Arbore Sanctorus without an inkling of predation.

### Lore

In the age of the Abyssal Shear, the final battle between the Shard God Kyra and an Abyssal horror of Sha-Xotha left the northeastern region of the Arctus Plateau decimated and rent apart. Abyssal energies lingered beneath the earth long after the battle was over, poisoning the land. It remained so until an unlikely creature — Nimaelle, a Casia of Primordis — came upon the destruction and took pity on the life struggling to take root there. To eradicate the Abyssal energies tainting the earth and revitalize the region, she thought to plant a seed taken directly from Primordis. From that seeding sprang the Arbore Sanctorus, and from the Arbore Sanctorus sprang the Bloodwoods itself.

But the insidiousness of the Abyss is such that Nimaelle only succeeded in sealing the wound without curing it. The Abyss still festers beneath the Bloodwoods, despite Nimaelle's best efforts. This fact is entrusted by Nimaelle only to the head of the order — at present, [[Avwynn Taol]]. A path beneath the Arbore Sanctorus leads directly to the scar, and this, too, is known only to Avwynn and Nimaelle herself.

In the aftermath of the Cindaric schism, Nimaelle gifted the refuge to Darik Melsbow, the Sanguinaries' founder. Darik and his followers were former Cindaric Sages who believed that the [[Heart of Ember]] was dying, and that the Cindaric order had become too rigid and politically compromised to face the truth. Within the Arbore Sanctorus, they built a life around that forbidden conviction: studying the natural world for signs of the Heart's decline, and searching for some way to reverse it.
