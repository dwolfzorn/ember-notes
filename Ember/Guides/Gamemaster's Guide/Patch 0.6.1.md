# Patch 0.6.1

**Welcome to Ember Early Access!** This update (version `0.6.1`) is a major content update that begins a series of Ember releases focused on **Chapter 4** of our game story. Along with some brand new game content in this update there are a host of bugfixes and quality-of-life improvements that should benefit your ongoing Early Access playtests!

#### Compatibility

Ember patch `0.6.1` requires core Foundry VTT version `14.366` or higher, and one of:

- D&D5E system pre-release version `5.3.3` or higher.
- Crucible system version `0.10.2` or higher.

## Actors

- Added several new characters! Nimaelle, Proctus Caylas, Maevren, Kryban, Verno Kreed.
- Updated the Thayloc Courser, adding a Silver Sword and tweaking the disabling shot to reduce speed instead of knocking characters prone.
- Updated characters with new abilities: Avwynn Taol.
- Added several new adversaries: Doomsayer Shadewright, Doomsayer Acolyte, Vespinoth, Amerasp, Amersap Queen, Amerasp Swarm, Grayling, Shadebranch, Sanguinary Warden, Casir Cats.
- Crumbling Sanctuary: Evesso now has Eldritch Blast and is not afraid to use it! He also now has Summon Undead instead of Animate Dead, and Sleet Storm instead of Bestow Curse.
- The Gore Bird has been reworked somewhat to ensure that it's features work in a more narratively appropriate way.

## Audio

- Added a new combat music theme for Primordis and fey adversaries.

## Area Maps

- The Bloodwoods Biome map has been redesigned with new compositions that match the long-term vision for the biome. The Scene has also been converted to use V14 levels.
- Added a beautiful new area map "Kadra-Zann" that features a very large multi-level dungeon to explore as part of the conclusion of the Ruby Grove quest.

## Crucible

- Fixed an issue with "turns"-unit durations, leading to them never applying
- Fixed textual issues in Yakoshta Mine Area Walkthrough.
- Fixed Blast Flask links in Crucible.
- Fixed Suarrok Juvenile tactics blocks regarding usage of Searing Stare.

## D&D5e

- Attunements: Cora 4 Compressed & Cora 5 Monolith have been buffed so that when you push or pull a target, you move them 10ft instead of 5 ft.
- Attunements: Orbis 5 has been buffed - Once per short rest, as an action, you release a vicious blast of Psychic energy at all creatures of your choice within a 30-foot emanation. Rather than a 20 ft emanation.
- Attunements: Ragen 3, 4 and 5 have been buffed - When you use a Hit Die to regain Hit Points during a Short Rest, you regain an additional 1d8 + Proficiency Bonus as your internal fire mends your wounds. (Removed the once-per-day limit and increased from d6 to d8.

## Quests and Events

- Implement an entirely new Chapter 4 quest called "Ruby Grove", the second side quest added for Chapter 4, which takes place in the Bloodwoods biome north of Ordain (including 10+ brand new Items and a few map-related keys).
- Added two new Standalone Events for the Redrak Fields Biome.
- Added two new Standalone Events for the Bloodwoods Biome.
- Added clarifying text in Old Friend and Glitter and the Dark quests, within Unhappy Accidents and A Troubled Tradeway, to make it clear that both events can happen concurrently.
- Added clarifying text in Brush with Death on what happens if you continue to hold the Finger of Nethehepticas.
- Copy/Line Edit improvements for Disturbed Earth and related gazetteer entries and Actors.
- Serethus is now properly introduced as a Cor'ak.
- Fixed textual issues with 'Time for a Swim."
- Fixed textual issues with "Shattered Expectations."
- Fixed textual issues with "A Sticky Situation."
- Fixed textual issues with "In the Behemoth's Wake."
- Fixed textual issues with Aedir Signalpost Area Walkthrough.
- Updated "Glint of Gossamer" to better reflect the Scene.

## Region Map

- The Bloodwoods Biome has been slightly expanded and tweaked with 4 new illustrated points of interest: Talei, Amerasp Grove, Arbore Sanctorus, and Kadra-Zann.
- Massively expanded the southern section of the Megaregion, with a fully illustrated Caryx Savannah and Elenain Delta!

## Setting Compendium

- Tweaked the Otherhood of Fortune and the Mutagist player-facing content to remove spoilers.
- Copyedited Vampyre bestiary entry.

## Tokens

- Improvements to the Fej token maker pieces, a beautiful and complete visual overhaul.
- Updated many humanoid token actors to include updated helms and clothing that match either their organization or culture, including Otherhood Raiders, Arcturians, and Anachraenum members.
- Added several new token maker parts for various Ruby Grove enemies, including Doomsayers Masks and Robes and Sanguinary Masks and clothing.
- Fixed an issue with eyebrows using two color options; they now only use hair roots.
- Changed the naming conventions for token colors, instead of "Hair1", "Hair2" we now use "Hair roots" and "Hair Highlight" etc.
- Fixed an issue where the party token would render black squares after returning to the Megaregion.

## Vistas

- The Bloodwoods Biome Vista has been upgraded, with tons of new and updated assets. There are 2 available template Levels and 4 new pre-composed Levels: Clearing, Clearing - Camp, Mangled Grove, and Primordian Monument.
- Added a fully illustrated Location Vista for the settlement of Talei.
- Vistas now feature a fully dynamic sky that follows the time of day, from dawn through dusk to full night.
- All six moons now appear overhead, each following its own lunar orbit.
- Moons visually advance through phases, shaded according to their orientation with respect to Lantyr.
- At night, the moons cast colored light across the Vista. Clouds passing overhead dim that light as they drift by.
- Solar eclipses occur on their own when a moon passes in front of the sun, dimming the daylight and revealing the corona around it.
- Cloud cover now follows the local weather, from light high wisps in fair conditions to a heavy overcast ahead of rain. Clouds drift with the wind and take on the colors of sunrise, sunset, and night.
- When the sun sits low on the horizon, its light breaks through the gaps between clouds.
- Shooting stars occasionally cross clear night skies.
- The sky is synchronized for everyone at the table, so all players see the same moons, clouds, and events at the same time.
- Heavy storms are now accompanied by periodic lightning which flashes to fill the scene.
- The Vista Configuration Tool has received further development! In addition to bug-fixes and UX improvements, several new features have been added, such as: expanded Placement and View controls, asset list filtering, additional asset options, and a new "Effects" tab for managing full-scene shaders and FX.
