# Guardian of Myth

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration and Combat Event occurs when [[Ankarist]] and the party visit [[Mythspire Observatory]] in pursuit of a clandestine entrance to the [[Pathways]] below [[World of Ember]]. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Explore the aged ruins of Mythspire Observatory.
> - Survive a dangerous encounter with the ancient [[Mythspire Guardian]] in the observatory's [[Central Rotunda]], whether via diplomacy or combat.
> - Discover the untimely fate of another group of adventurers who previously came this way.
> - Locate the [[Pathways Elevator]] on the observatory's lower level, which leads to the subterranean Pathways.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Mythspire Observatory]] where exploration begins at the [[Mythspire Approach]].

When the Event begins, the party arrives at the [[Mythspire Approach]] outside the exterior doors of the ruined tower. Take note of the narrative moments presented in this event, but otherwise follow the Area Walkthrough for details on exploration of Mythspire Observatory.

#### Aura Attunement: Mythspire Discovery

If the party manages to discover Mythspire Observatory independently without receiving directions from Liestra Grann and without having reunited with Ankarist in Skybrush, each character advances their **Attunement: Aura (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### Exploring the Observatory

> [!abstract] Ankarist
> **[[Ankarist]]**
>
> Level 2 · Drakon Veiled Chain Investigator
>
> ![Ankarist](../../../assets/image120.webp)
>
> You observe a stern Drakon warrior with a determined expression and piercing golden eyes. Clad in leather armor reinforced with steel, his martial prowess is immediately apparent in the way he handles the hefty greatsword at his side — a hulking blade with a wide, flared tip. This two-handed brand is obviously venerated by the Drakon, who regards the blade with marked discipline. A cloak pin on his breast bears the symbol of the Veiled Chain, the city of Ordain's noble protectorate.

Ankarist shares his initial thoughts on the observatory with the party before they enter.

First ArrivalIf the party traveled to Mythspire Observatory together with Ankarist, either via Skybrush and [[Mapping the Myth]] or by discovering the location of Mythspire independently, read the following aloud as Ankarist considers the ruin for the first time.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Ankarist takes a deep breath and steels himself.
>
> > What an impressive ruin. Legend tells that the ancient Shent used to study the heavens from this place. Who knows what secret knowledge they gleaned, and what we have lost from their passing.
> >
> > Let's head inside. With any luck, we'll be descending into the Pathways in no time. Here's hoping our luck hasn't run out just yet …

Return VisitIf Ankarist originally explored Mythspire Observatory on his own, only for the party to rejoin him later in [[Reuniting with Ankarist]], read the following aloud as Ankarist returns to the ruins with the party to aid him.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Back again, good. I'm still licking my wounds from my previous encounter here and eager to prevail this time around with strong allies at my side. Be cautious, the guardian prowls the lowest chambers, we should craft a plan for how to approach. We may also want to spend time investigating the upper levels for any clues I may have missed.

### Confronting the Guardian

Within the [[Central Rotunda]] of Mythspire's lower chambers, the party must confront and survive an encounter with the dangerous and enigmatic [[Mythspire Guardian]]. Depending on how they resolve that interaction, they may earn certain attunement progression.

#### Akon Attunement: Befriend the Mythspire Guardian

If the party chose to befriend the Mythspire Guardian, each character advances their **Attunement: Akon (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

#### Abyss Attunement: Destroy the Mythspire Guardian

If the party chose to fight and kill the Mythspire Guardian, each character advances their **Attunement: The Abyss (+2)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### Unlocking the Ancient Lift

In order to descend to the [[Pathways]], the party must solve the puzzle of how to unlock the [[Pathways Elevator]] which is initially sealed. Once they have resolved its puzzle and enabled the lift to be used, the following outcome will be automatically marked as completed and an attunement progression is earned by all members of the party.

`[[/outcome unlocked]]`

#### Signara Attunement: Ancient Lift Restored

If the party manages to restore the Ancient Lift to the Pathways, each character advances their **Attunement: Signara (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### The Investigator's Mission

With the lift now enabled, the party must choose whether to accompany Ankarist on his mission into the Pathways or allow the Drakon to forge ahead on his own. Mark the appropriate Event Outcome after the characters make their decision and Ankarist has had a moment to respond.

`[[/outcome ankaristJoined]]`

If the party decides to join Ankarist, he says:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Ankarist appears pleased that he'll have backup going into the Pathways.
>
> > Excellent! I'm glad to have you along. You've been worthy allies so far, and I'm grateful you'll be at my side on this little expedition of ours. From what I understand of the Pathways, it's a rather massive region, with more dangers than one can count. Not the place for lone adventure, by any means.
> >
> > Let's get moving as soon as you're ready.

`[[/outcome ankaristLeft]]`

If the party decides to leave Ankarist, he says:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Ankarist seems a touch disappointed that he won't have backup going into the Pathways, but he doesn't let it get the better of him.
>
> > I'll be honest with you, I was hoping that I wouldn't have to go into the Pathways on my own, but I'm grateful for your help getting us this far. I'm determined to see this through and get to the bottom of where these malevolent creatures are coming from.
> >
> > Once I've found the evidence I'm after, I'll take it to the Hallows in Ordain. If you make your way to the city, look for me there and we can compare notes on what we've learned.
> >
> > Safe and swift travels, friends. Until we meet again.

### Concluding the Event

At the conclusion of this event, the characters will either descend the ancient Pathways Elevator into the depths of the Pathways, or they will return to their exploration of the overland Arctus Plateau.
