# Guarded Neighbors

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration Event occurs if the party investigates a specific section of the [[Kaleidoscope Caverns]] a few miles northeast of [[Yakoshta]]. By exploring this cavern, the characters can:
>
> - Discern fragmented clues about how this place was used by the ancient [[Shent]] culture.
> - Discover lost recordings that describe an unlikely relationship between a Shent carver and an Aedir researcher stationed nearby.
> - Translate a sequence of murals describing the calamitous events of [[The Shattering]].
> - Locate a hidden cache containing some curious antiquities.

The inscribed reliefs upon the cavern walls are magically shrouded such that they do not reveal their full picture unless the central obelisk has been activated by speaking the words [[Guarded Neighbors]] Inspection of the murals through other sources of light will clearly reveal their presence, but observers are unable to discern what they depict. The party must engage with the central obelisk in order to fully illuminate them.

### Investigating the Obelisk

Investigation of this ancient site will likely begin with inspection of the crystal obelisk in the center of the cavern.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The trilateral obelisk gleams when exposed to light and reveals script, etched as intricate lettering into each flat face of the pillar. Each face depicts a different style of glyphs, some runic, others more flowing. One side is recognizable as an antiquated variant of modern common writing.
>
> At the base of the obelisk is a small twelve-faceted crystal with a large topmost face mounted into an ornate metal sconce.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Reading the Tryptych
>
> The three sides of the pedestal feature script written in **Language: Pathward**, **Language: Towyr**, and **Language: Common**. Each side bears the same message, reproduced in multiple languages.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 12)** check can confirm that one side uses an early version of the language that became the Common Coin. It is readable by anyone who speaks **Language: Common** but distinct from the modern language.
>
> Other languages can be translated through use of special skills or knowledge:
>
> - Characters can read any language they know, or translate another language through use of the **Talent: Versatile Translator** talent.
> - **Language: Pathward** can be recognized by anyone with **Knowledge: Shent** or with a successful **Society (DC 14)** check.
> - **Language: Towyr** can be recognized by anyone with **Knowledge: Aedir** or with a successful **Society (DC 14)** check.

Each set of symbols on the pedestal translates to the same phrase:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Our journey ends here. We cannot foresee what will yet come, so we leave these words for whomever comes after. Illuminate the past by speaking our words and telling our tale. Begin with the beginning.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Beginning Words
>
> Characters who make a successful **Deception (DC 13)** check can guess that they should speak the words "the beginning."

When the pedestal is activated:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A glow emanates from within the purple depths of the crystal obelisk, illuminating the entire cavern with a soft lavender light. Beneath this emanating light, the reliefs carved into the cavern's upper walls stand out clearly. You behold four separate pictographic murals, each on a different surface of the cavern walls.

The inscribed reliefs upon the cavern walls are magically shrouded such that they do not reveal their full picture unless the central obelisk has been activated in this way. Inspection of the murals through other sources of light will clearly discover their presence, but observers are unable to discern what they depict.

### Preserved Messages

The peculiar twelve-faceted crystal is a Recording Crystal, a magical gemstone that can store spoken words and replay them in a language that is familiar to the listener. Only advanced mages are capable of using the technique required to create such crystals, but they can be activated by anyone regardless of magical talent. This message, and others in the cavern, were left behind over two thousand years ago by a young Shent carver named Chiseqi.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Recording Crystal
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 13)** check can identify the crystal as a Recording Crystal and understand that the relic should function when someone maintains physical contact with it and concentrates on a desire to receive the message.
>
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Previously Identified Crystal:** The character automatically succeeds on this check if they identified a similar crystal in the [[Multifaceted]] Event.
> - **Critical Success**: You understand that the recording is magically translated into a language that you intuitively understand, and is not a pure reflection of its original speaker.

If a character touches the crystal while concentrating on the intention for the crystal to replay its recording, a voice is audible only to that character, speaking in whatever language they speak most fluently.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A voice rings out from the crystal, young-sounding and bright, speaking in an exaggerated whisper.
>
> > I know. This is not a time to dawdle. This is a time to work. But my hands have made these same motions so many times and there's something to be learned from hiding a message in plain sight.
> >
> > If you discover this small rebellion, feel free to assign me an extra task. Write "Chiseqi" down on your list as many times as you wish. As I have been told more than once when struggling toward a new goal, the accomplishment is generally worth the consequence.
> >
> > I shall leave these notes as annotations to my carving, perhaps a small hope that our work will endure and someone may yet hear them.

When the party investigates and discovers a Recording Crystal, the messages should be revealed in the following order, regardless of the order in which the murals are investigated.

Recorded Message 1> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > I am better at erasing my tracks than I thought — so far as I can tell, no one has read my last message. I quite like the idea of being able to speak into the void with no idea of who might listen — it's freeing, almost like speaking to the moons.
> >
> > I can tell **you** that I am growing weary of the chisel, or that these crystals sometimes make my head throb, or that I'm probably more curious than I should be about the tower the Aedir Empire is trying to hide up in the cliffs. I wouldn't admit it to anyone else, but the tower fascinates me. I wish there was some way to say hello.

Recorded Message 2> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > I have to tell someone, and you're all that I have, so here's my small confession: I went up to the cliffs today, to try to get a glimpse of what the Aedir do on that tower. I'm not sure why I'm so intrigued. Maybe it's that the Aedir are a bit of a mystery to me — they're arrogant and powerful and they appear to be everywhere, but we Shent don't interact much with the Empire. After all, we're fairly sure they're going to break the world one day. So why do I want to get closer?
> >
> > Perhaps it is curiosity, perhaps boredom, or perhaps it is an intuition that we must connect with those we distrust in order to survive this breaking world.

Recorded Message 3> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > I saw someone. Up on the cliffs. Orbis only knows why I've taken to going up there so frequently. Taking "walks," I say. In truth just staring at where I think the tower is, even though I lack the arcana to perceive it. There's probably a lesson in that, though I can't quite puzzle it out.
> >
> > All I know is that staring is why I saw her, running through the cliffs for just a moment, glancing back over her shoulder. As if she shouldn't be there. We have that, at least, in common.

Recorded Message 4> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > The woman I saw? A researcher in the invisible tower, which they call the "signalpost" — more likely a "spy post," but I digress. Her name is Golarne.
> >
> > We have talked together each evening beneath Orbis' shimmer. Perhaps we both know better, and yet the spirit moon has willed us forward. Golarne swears that she is only there for research, but the Empire doesn't waste an opportunity. She doesn't appear to enjoy alchemy more than I enjoy chiseling, but at least we can talk to each other and I can stop bothering you.
> >
> > She even gifted me a few trinkets that I've buried near the northern mural. The world is a shaky place these days. It never hurts to be ready.

### The Four Murals

On each face of the cavern walls overhead — roughly aligned to cardinal directions relative to the cavern center — is a work of pictographic line art that incorporates both visual diagram and etched runic symbols that work together to craft a cohesive depiction of some historical scene. The meaning of each mural can be deciphered by examining its picture and its runes together. Each mural is keyed by a single short phrase which, if spoken aloud, causes latent enchantment upon the mural to activate.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Interpreting the Murals
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 13)** check recognizes these as glyphs written by the ancient Shent giants in their runic language.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Language: Pathward**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> - The runes can only be exactly read by someone who speaks **Language: Pathward** or makes use of some magical translation technique. Without this language, the meaning of each mural can be deciphered as described below.
> - Some runes are reused across multiple murals. Once a rune has been deciphered, it is automatically known when it appears in other murals.
>
> #### Replaying Ancient Messages
>
> On the cavern wall at the base of each mural there is a Recording Crystal similar to that described in [[Guarded Neighbors]]. Each stone replays one of the recorded messages left by the Shent carver, Chiseqi.

#### Signara Attunement: Decode a Mural

Any character who helps decode one or more of the murals advances their **Attunement: Signara (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### The Eastern Mural

The eastern mural depicts a scene of the ancient Shent traveling a great distance into a rocky landscape. On the cavern wall at its base is one of the Recording Crystals left by Chiseqi the carver.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A swirling line drawing chiseled into the cavern wall depicts a group of statuesque humanoids standing around an empty vehicle, their backs to an unfamiliar line of rocky cliffs. A few of the cliffs appear to have shallow puddles on them. The cliffs are steep, but have lines that seem out of place, as if something has been traced onto them.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Decoding the Eastern Mural
>
> This mural contains three runes: **Shent**, **Stone**, and **Travel**. Its approximate translation is:
>
> > The Shent travel to the stone.
>
> Each rune can be learned through the following checks:
>
> **Shent:** Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 13)** check can recognize the depiction of the giant humanoids as ancient Shent, identifying the **Shent** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> **Stone:** Any character who makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 13)** check recognizes the landscape as the Forest of Stone and the depicted puddles as representative of waterfalls and ooze pools, identifying the **Stone** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Subterranea**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> **Travel:** Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 13)** check identifies the strange vehicle in the mural as some method of arcane transportation, identifying the **Travel** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Machines**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> If these three runes are spoken in a sentence that is close to the original meaning, the enchantment of the mural is activated:
>
> > The mural begins to emit a soft glow, and the runes that make up the humanoids appear to shift slightly on the wall, as if they were waving out at whoever watches them.

### The Southern Mural

The southern mural depicts a scene of the Shent gazing at the sky and venerating the moons. On the cavern wall at its base is one of the Speaking Stokes left by Chiseqi the carver.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The lines of this drawing depict a group of humanoids standing on a rocky plain, staring at an object in the sky above them. It appears as if rays or beams are coming down from the object, touching both the humanoids and a pool in the distance. Each ray looks like a slightly different symbol.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Decoding the Southern Mural
>
> This mural contains three runes: **Shent**, **Honor**, and **Mayis**. It's approximate translation is:
>
> > The Shent honor Mayis and her creatures.
>
> Each rune can be learned through the following checks:
>
> **Shent:** Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 13)** check can recognize the depiction of the giant humanoids as ancient Shent, identifying the **Shent** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> **Honor:** Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check recognizes the posture of worship presented by the humanoid figures, identifying the **Honor** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> **Mayis:** Any character who makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 13)** check identifies the depicted celestial object as the water moon, Mayis.
>
> - **Knowledge: Cosmology**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Attunement: Mayis**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> If these three runes are spoken in a sentence that is close to the original meaning, the enchantment of the mural is activated:
>
> > The rays on the mural appear to glisten and glow, and light shines from the depiction of the moon above, creating moonbeam-like light on the cavern around it.

### The Western Mural

The western mural depicts the arrival of the Aedir and the construction of their tower on the bluffs high above this area. On the cavern wall at its base is one of the Speaking Stokes left by Chiseqi the carver.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> In this mural, a large and imposing tower on a cliff blocks the orb in the sky. The tower is surrounded by a group of faceless humanoids made of sharp, angled strokes, different from those on the other stones. They stare down at the humanoids seen in the previous mural.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Decoding the Western Mural
>
> This mural contains three runes: **Others**, **Stone**, and **Tower**. It's approximate translation is:
>
> > Others watch the stone from their tower.
>
> Each rune can be learned through the following checks:
>
> **Others:** Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 13)** check can recognize the depiction of the angular humanoids as soldiers of the ancient Aedir Empire — expressed by the Shent as strange foreigners — identifying the **Others** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Aedir**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> **Stone:** Any character who makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 13)** check recognizes the landscape below the tower as the Forest of Stone, identifying the **Stone** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Subterranea**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> **Tower:** Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check identifies the tower as being a fortified or military installation, commanding a superior vantage point, identifying the **Tower** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Warfare**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> If these three runes are spoken in a sentence that is close to the original meaning, the enchantment of the mural is activated:
>
> > The glow in this mural seems to emanate from the tower depicted in it. The tower flickers almost like a fire, as do the faces of the humanoids surrounding it, almost as if there is something dangerous and out of control in their very presence. The crystals near the mural seem to flicker in response, casting a glowing light on the ground.

### The Northern Mural

The northern mural depicts the calamitous consequences of the Shattering and the destruction of the Sun Tower. On the cavern wall at its base is one of the Speaking Stokes left by Chiseqi the carver.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The land beneath the tower in this mural is splintered and shattered. Glyphs appear to have been created with the jagged lines in the land. In the midst of the splintered ground, the faceless humanoids flee the tower.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Decoding the Northern Mural
>
> This mural contains three runes: **Tower**, **Break**, and **Others**. It's approximate translation is:
>
> > The tower breaks and the others go.
>
> Each rune can be learned through the following checks:
>
> **Tower:** Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check identifies the tower as being a fortified or military installation, commanding a superior vantage point, identifying the **Tower** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Warfare**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> **Break:** Any character can identify, without a check, that this rune references the Shattering, a calamitous event in which the Aedir Empire is said to have broken the world.
>
> **Others:** Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 13)** check can recognize the depiction of the angular humanoids as soldiers of the ancient the Aedir Empire — expressed by the Shent as strange foreigners — identifying the **Others** rune.
>
> - **Knowledge: Aedir**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> If these three runes are spoken in a sentence that is close to the original meaning, the enchantment of the mural is activated:
>
> > The jagged lines splitting the ground flare with pale light, spreading outward from the tower in branching fractures. The tower itself flickers, then seems to break apart across the stone as the faceless figures below turn and flee. Nearby crystals answer with a violent shimmer before settling back into darkness.

### A Hidden Cache

As revealed in his fourth and final message, the Shent carver Chiseqi stashed some supplies and trinkets near the northern mural in a crevice between large stalagmites.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Locating the Cache
>
> Any character who searches the cavern for items of interest and makes a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check discovers the hidden cache.
>
> - **Listened to Chiseqi's Message:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they learned of the cache's existence from listening to Chiseqi's fourth recorded message.
>
> Once the cache has been located, it can be easily removed from the ground. It contains:
>
> - Antique coins worth  **2**.
> - 1 [[Healing Elixir]] and 1 [[Rallying Elixir]] sealed within unbroken glass vials.
> - A carved charm bracelet; it is a [[Amulet]].
> - A carefully folded note, [[Chiseqi's Letter]], tucked neatly at the bottom of the cache.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The ancient note is written in a scribbled and uneven hand on ancient vellum, which has evidently been preserved against decay through some minor enchantment. Beneath the signature is a sketched diagram of a spiral with three diagonal lines of differing lengths cutting across its curvature.

The note is written in **Language: Towyr**. If inspected by someone who knows the language or has the means to translate it, they learn the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Chiseqi,
> >
> > I wanted you to know how glad I am that we met. I remember your words and will use them to focus my task during the days ahead. It won't make the days go any faster, but I will see you beneath the stars once my work is done and I promise we will go exploring soon.
> >
> > As thanks for your friendship, I invite you to see our work as I do. Trace this glyphsign upon the lens of the clarion shrine I showed you. I will hear it when you do and meet you there.
> >
> > Golarne

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Recognizing the Glyphsign
>
> The depicted glyph is unique, and cannot be recognized as belonging to any particular language or known spellcraft. In the absence of translation, it is possible for a character to infer that this style of symbol is some mechanism for authentication or a passphrase with a successful **Glyphweaving (DC 13)** check.
>
> - **Knowledge: Aedir**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Intrigue**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.

Golarne's glyphsign may be used later in [[The Tower Revealed]] to cause the [[Aedir Signalpost]] to become revealed to the party. If the party learns of the glyphsign's purpose, mark the following outcome as completed.

`[[/outcome glyph]]`

### Concluding the Event

Once the party has concluded their inspection of the cavern and its four murals, they may conclude the event and return to their travels.
