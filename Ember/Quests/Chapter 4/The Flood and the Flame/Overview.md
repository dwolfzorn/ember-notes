# Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Requirements
>
> This Side Quest occurs during Chapter 4, and begins in any major city of Location that could presumably have a library or bookstore, though the majority of the Quest occurs while the party is traveling through the [[Caryx Savanna]] Biome. There are no specific prerequisites for this Side Quest to occur.
>
> #### Difficulty
>
> "The Flood and the Flame" is designed for a party of Level 7-8 characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
>
> This is a Side Quest that awards 2 [[Milestone Progression]]: 1 Milestone Point each upon completion of [[Flash of Insight]] and [[Burning Hearts]].

Over two millennia ago, when the [[Caryx Savanna]] was inhabited by a lingering pocket of the [[Sedyri]] diaspora, Sedyri mage-priests known as Unifiers wielded two powerful magical entities: the "Spark of the Savanna" and the "Ripple of the Rivers." By summoning these opposing entities into themself, a Unifier gained unparalleled control over the Savanna's intense fire and flood seasons. This allowed the Unifiers to balance and temper the Savanna's dangerous weather — at least, until Sedyri civilization dissolved, and their techniques were lost. In order to protect her hometown of Tajar from the fires that threaten it, [[Kess the Firemage]] is determined to revive the practice. However, the hotheaded young Kiska's affinity for fire has caused her to overlook the delicate balance maintained by the Unifiers; she seeks to summon *only* the Spark of the Savanna.

This Quest begins as the party encounters Kess, in any Location that might reasonably contain a library where she could be researching. Afterward, the party must travel to the Caryx Savanna, in order to aid the Kiska by delving into the [[Tomb of the Unifiers]] and retrieving an artifact crucial to summoning the Spark. Unbeknownst to Kess, taking the power of the Spark into herself (without the Ripple to balance it) will open the door for an even more dangerous entity: [[Fiyara]], the Elemental Mistress of Hatred. Fiyara once ruled [[Ragen]] as its Elemental Sovereign, before being corrupted by [[The Abyss]], deposed by her siblings [[Lorgi]] and [[Ravela]], and secretly imprisoned on Ember's surface. Fiyara's newfound influence fabricates the [[Citadel of Fire]] — a foreboding, flaming temple that appears suddenly atop Mount Sitara after Kess's misbegotten ritual. After Kess is swallowed by Fiyara's Voidfire, she is transported to the Citadel, from within which the corrupted Kess begins spreading Fiyara's flames throughout the Savanna.

"The Flood and the Flame" follows the party as they investigate Kess's disappearance and attempt to find a way through the nigh-impenetrable dome of flame that protects the Citadel of Fire. To do so, the party will have to seek the aid of powerful allies, such as[[Flamewarden Tiga]] of the Savanna's guardian Flamewardens, along with the matriarch of Tajar [[Vekaris Ossa]], and [[Sorak the Stormheart]], champion of [[Katu]] (a Shard God of storms). Along the way, they will battle enraged water elementals and Abyss-corrupted fire elementals, delve into the [[Temple of the Tides]] (an ancient Sedyri water temple), and potentially discover the secrets to summoning the Ripple of the Rivers (which would permit them to balance the magical forces within Kess). Once the party finally penetrates the citadel and bests the corrupted Kess in a climactic battle, they will face a final and difficult decision: attempt the challenging task of summoning the Ripple into the young mage and balancing the powers she wrought, or resort to slaying Kess entirely, thus banishing the Spark from Ember's surface and restoring the status quo.
