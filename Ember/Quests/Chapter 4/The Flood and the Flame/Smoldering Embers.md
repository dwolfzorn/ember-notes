# Smoldering Embers

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event is guaranteed to occur when the party visits the [[Communal Quarter]] of Tajar after completing [[Burning Hearts]]. By debriefing with the various inhabitants of the Savanna that they have met throughout this Quest, the characters can:
>
> - Reunite with [[Kess the Firemage]] and receive her blessing, if the party saved her life.
> - Reunite with [[Vekaris Ossa]] and receive payment for aiding Tajar.
> - Reunite with [[Sorak the Stormheart]] and reflect on a task well done.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Vista: Tajar]] Vista.

### Reuniting with Kess

If the party [[Burning Hearts]], saving the life of [[Kess the Firemage]], they find her on the outskirts of Tajar, where she is helping put out the remnants of wildfires that the Flamewardens beat back to the edge of the city.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> In the distance, you spot Kess standing amidst a crowd of Flamewardens. Water curls around the Kiska's arms, and springs forth from her outstretched palms as she douses lingering wildfires that the Flamewardens already drove away from Tajar. When Kess catches sight of you, she bounces excitedly, and the floating waters fall to the ground.
>
> > Hiya!! So glad you made it!! Things are already doing **so** much better, and thanks to you, I've been able to help the Flamewardens with putting out the last of the wildfires!! I could probably do more on my own, but … I'm taking it slow this time.
>
> She takes a big deep breath.
>
> > If you'd let me though, I'd like to give you all a gift, as a thank you for everything you've done for me. It involves a little magic— or, well, maybe some big magic … but I'm really sure I know what I'm doing now! Y'see, I'm **REALLY** connected to Ragen and Mayis now, and I can share a bit of that connection with you all, if you'd like?

If one or more party members agree, Kess summons gentle streams of water and fire that twirl around those party members, and those party members each gain Attunement with both Ragen and Mayis. Kess is willing to repeat the process for any party members nervous to go first.

#### Ragen Attunement: Blessing of the Spark

Any character who lets Kess share her connection to Ragen with them advances their **Attunement: Ragen (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

#### Mayis Attunement: Blessing of the Ripple

Any character who lets Kess share her connection to Mayis with them advances their **Attunement: Mayis (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

Afterwards, Kess thanks the party again, and returns to firefighting efforts alongside the Flamewardens.

### Reuniting with Vekaris

The party encounters [[Vekaris Ossa]] in front of the Hall of the Weathered, where Vekaris has just finished meeting with Tajar's governing council of Wise-Women. Her dialogue depends on which decisions the party made during [[Burning Hearts]]:

Kess Killed and Spark BanishedIf Kess was killed — intentionally or unintentionally — and the party [[Burning Hearts]], Vekaris is grateful, but still grieving the loss. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Vekaris dismisses the Wise-Women who surrounded her, and offers you a nod of gratitude.
>
> > Heroes — thank you. The fires have already begun to fade, and the Flamewardens are smothering their last vestiges.
>
> She pauses, and her face turns solemn.
>
> > I can't help but notice, however, that young Kess is not with you. Did you have to … ?

After listening to the party's explanation, Vekaris comments, and proffers the promised payment. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Vekaris shakes her head — not in disapproval, but mourning.
>
> > What a tragic shame. She will be missed. I wish there was more we could have done, but … please know: your actions have saved countless lives, here and across the Savanna — perhaps across Ember as a whole. You did what needed to be done, and for that, I thank you. As promised, here is payment — in my personal coin, and the city's collection of Sedyri artifacts.

She provides the gold and items listed in [[Smoldering Embers]] below.

Ripple Summoned and Spark BalancedIf the party [[Burning Hearts]] by summoning the Ripple of the Ripples — saving Kess's life — Vekaris is delighted, grateful, and curious about the process. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Vekaris dismisses the Wise-Women who surrounded her, and offers you a nod of gratitude.
>
> > Heroes — thank you. The fires have already begun to fade, and Kess and the Flamewardens are smothering their last vestiges. You have my gratitude not just for saving the Savanna, but for Kess as well. It is yet to be seen exactly what her newfound abilities will mean for the Savanna and its seasons, but more than anything, I'm overjoyed to see her not just alive, but truly feeling that she has a role and a home here in Tajar.
> >
> > She told me you all summoned the Ripple of the Rivers into her — just how did you do it?

After listening to the party's explanation, Vekaris comments, and proffers the promised payment. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Impressive work, adventurers. You've changed this place, and we won't soon forget it. Perhaps I'll even compose a song of your great deeds. For now, as promised, here is your payment — in my personal coin, and the city's collection of Sedyri artifacts. I hope they serve you well in future heroism!

She provides the gold and items listed in [[Smoldering Embers]] below.

### Reuniting with Sorak

The party finds [[Sorak the Stormheart]] being tended to by Flamewarden medics, injured and ornery. However, he is impressed by the party's deeds, and makes it clear they have a friend in him. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Under a tent, Flamewarden medics still tend to the injured. Among them is Sorak the Stormheart, bruised and battered, with lightning blisters zig-zagging down his fur. As you approach, he waves off a Flamewarden medic, and pushes himself painfully upright. It clearly pains him even to speak — but he is no stranger to discomfort.
>
> > We meet again, adventurers. I must admit, for all my doubts … you did well. You confronted no small power in that forsaken place. For saving my home here in the Savanna … you have my thanks. And while holding that passage open took quite a toll on my body, your deeds made it more than worth a few burnt hairs.
> >
> > Call upon me any time, and I will lend my strength — Katu's too, if the damned fool ever decides to unblock his ears.
