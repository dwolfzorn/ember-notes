# Firewalled

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration and Social Event occurs when the party walks up the precarious mountain pass leading to the [[Citadel of Fire]] at the top of Mount Sitara. This Event recurs any time the party enters that tile, until they complete [[Tidebound Temple]]. By attempting to approach the Citadel, the characters can:
>
> - Witness a cloud of smog and an impenetrable wall of fire preventing access to the Citadel.
> - If the party has not yet encountered [[Flamewarden Tiga]], they do so here, and learn from her about the city of Tajar and its matriarch [[Vekaris Ossa]], who Tiga believes can assist the party in their efforts.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Citadel Flamewall]] Level of the [[Vista: Caryx Savanna]] Vista.

### Navigating the Smog

To make their way closer to the Citadel of Fire — and the impassible wall of flame that awaits — party members must first navigate the wave of smog that greets them.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Choking Hazard
>
> Any party members who attempts to move towards the citadel and makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** or **Awareness (DC 14)** check may exit the fog on the side nearest the citadel. After facing the checks listed in this block below, they continue at [[Firewalled]].
>
> If party members maintain physical contact, one party member's success allows them to guide the rest; otherwise, those who fail their check become separated, and find themselves exiting the smog downslope, as if pushed in that direction by the smog. Characters may attempt the check to navigate the smog any number of times.
>
> After each check attempt (including the first) regardless of the result:
>
> - The party member must make a successful Crucible text TODO or else take `[[/damage 1d6]]` damage and gain @Condition[Exhausted] due to smoke inhalation along the way.
> - The party member hears a whisper from the [[Kess the Corrupted Whispers]] Table. Party members who stay together in a group all hear the same whisper. They are obviously in Kess's voice, but attempts to speak back garner no response.
>
> Characters who hear a whisper and make a successful **Arcana (DC 13)** check determine the whispers are magical in nature, and therefore do not necessarily entail that Kess is physically nearby.

### Confronting the Flame Wall

Party members who exit the smog on the side of the citadel are greeted by the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Though the smog seemed endless, as you wander closer to the cloud's edge, you feel an intense heat, then a red glow that guides the remainder of your passage. As you exit the smoke completely, you find yourself in a narrow no mans land — just a few feet of empty air between the choking smog and a wall of fire that stretches as far as you can see in every direction. The fire burns cleanly and curves at its edges, suggesting a dome that envelops the entire citadel. The flames react to your presence, juddering each time you move and growing as you approach. There is no telling just how thick the wall extends.

Once the party has finished navigating the smoke and decides to test the wall of flame, they are faced with an extraordinarily difficult (and functionally impassable) obstacle — though they may prod its boundaries via the means farther below.

If the party has not yet met [[Flamewarden Tiga]] (either in this Event or in [[Trial by Fire]]) then — at the time of your choosing — Tiga trudges out of the smoke and interrupts them, triggering [[Firewalled]] below.

Optionally, if some party members failed to navigate the smoke and opted to remain behind rather than make further attempts, you may instead introduce Tiga later, having her trudge out of the smoke downslope (after the characters near the flame wall have retreated and reunited with their compatriots). See [[Firewalled]] below for more advice on when to introduce Tiga.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Narrating an Impass
>
> Functionally, the wall of fire exists as an impassable obstacle, preventing access to the Citadel of Fire until the players beseech the help of [[Sorak the Stormheart]] in [[Eye of the Storm]], and meet him back at this tile during [[Dousing the Flame]]. Moments of impossibility can be frustrating for players. Keep the following in mind:
>
> - It is important that regardless of the strategy employed, something about the wall always thwarts attempts to pass through it. For cases not covered in this Event, you will need to improvise a reason the effort fails.
> - [[Firewalled]] provides a way to have a character *within the fiction* convey the impossibility of the task, at the moment of your choosing. She can also signal to players how else they might progress this storyline. **It is recommended you introduce Tiga when investigation of the barrier reaches a natural lull, or when players grow frustrated.**
> - The investigation opportunities and exploration hazards below exist to make engagement with the impassable wall of flame interesting, and (somewhat) subtly convey the futility of the task. However, at some point, if players are growing frustrated, you can explicitly inform them that this barrier cannot be passed without progressing further into the Quest.
> - The [[Kess the Corrupted Whispers]] Table provides players a consolation prize for attempting to delve into the wall, even if they cannot fully pass through it.
>
> Note that this is a repeatable Event, which blocks the party from accessing the Citadel of Fire until later in the Quest — it is up to you to decide how many of the provided mechanics you want to repeat if the party makes additional attempts to pierce the barrier.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Whispered Knowledge
>
> If players manage to piece together hints from the [[Kess the Corrupted Whispers]] that feel relevant to the investigatory checks below, you are encouraged to grant them **+2 Boons** in recognition of this, or allow access to checks or results they might not otherwise qualify for.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Shining Light on the Fire
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 14)** check determines the fire is the result of divine magic — a source that could sustain this wall indefinitely, despite how wide (and presumably, how thick) the flame is. However, this task would be much easier were the deity to be present somewhere on Ember's surface.
>
> - **Knowledge: Abyssals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also detects an Abyssal taint to the divine magic.
>
> If the party determines the divine origin, any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 13)** check can recall a list of fire-related gods who could supply this magic: [[Lumé]], [[Steelsong]], [[Lorgi]], [[Ravela]], [[Fiyara]], and Soots.
>
> - **Knowledge: Gods**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character reasons that of all the gods known for fire, Fiyara — who fell to Abyssal corruption — is by far the most hostile and dangerous.
> - **Party also identified the Abyssal taint in the divine magic:** The character also recalls that Fiyara fell to Abyssal corruption, and is therefore the most likely candidate.
>
> If (here or in a previous Event) the party identified Fiyara as a possible source, any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 20)** recalls a piece of arcane lore indicating that Fiyara, the Abyss-corrupted Wild God now known as the Elemental Mistress of Hatred, was deposed as a Elemental Sovereign of Ragen by her siblings Lorgi and Ravela, who imprisoned her on Ember's surface.
>
> - **Knowledge: Gods**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Fighting Fire with Magic
>
> A character whoattempts to use the **Talent: Counterspell** Talent manages to carve a small hole in the fire that extends a few feet into it, but this only reveals the wall is at least that thick, if not significantly more.
>
> Short-distance teleportation magic Crucible text TODO is partially blocked, and only able take the caster a few feet into the wall — triggering the hazards of [[Firewalled]] below.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Fire Walk With Me
>
> Any character who ventures into the wall of fire takes ever-increasing amounts of damage. If a character attempts to venture through the wall of flame:
>
> - They take `[[/damage 4d6 fire]]` damage and an additional `[[/damage 1d6 fire]]` damage for every time they have already taken fire damage during this delve into the wall of flame.
> - They hear a whisper from the [[Kess the Corrupted Whispers]] Table (though Kess herself is not nearby, and cannot hear replies).
> - They are given a choice to forge ahead, or turn back and immediately exit the wall — at no point do they receive any indication they are near the opposite edge. In fact, the fire only gets hotter and brighter as they move ahead.
>
> While Resistance to Fire damage can allow the character to spend longer in the wall (and gather more whispers) the unimaginable intensity of the divine fire is so overpowering that characters with Immunity to Fire damage are instead treated as having Resistance to Fire damage — there is no way to prevent the damage completely.

#### Ragen Attunement: Firewalking

Any character who braves the fires and long enough to take damage advances their **Attunement: Ragen (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### Meeting Tiga

If the party has not met Flamewarden Tiga during this Event or [[Trial by Fire]], then when investigation of the wall of flame reaches a natural lull (or players grow frustrated) Tiga exits the smoke and speaks with them. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A tall Drakon slinks quietly out of the smoke, removing a mask from her face and hanging it from the quiver on her back. She gives you a calm nod of greeting.
>
> > I take it you've also discovered this unfortunate impasse? My comrades in the Flamewardens sent me here to investigate the citadel beyond, but for all my training, no strategy I've employed has allowed me to pass through the flames and reach their other side; I'm beginning to believe that such an effort may be beyond mortal means.
>
> She glances thoughtfully in the direction of the wall of flame, then back to you.
>
> > My name is Tiga, Flamewarden of Tajar. Glad to make your acquaintance, but if you'll permit me to ask: what brings you to this place of danger?

> [!abstract] Flamewarden Tiga
> **[[Flamewarden Tiga]]**
>
> Level 1 · Unknown Unknown
>
> ![Flamewarden Tiga](../../../assets/image126.webp)

After the party has finished telling their story — presuming speaking honestly about their time with Kess and their desire to investigate the citadel — Tiga urges them to come with her to Tajar and tell their story to the matriarch, [[Vekaris Ossa]].

If players opt not to share details about Kess and the ritual, either have Tiga ask leading followup questions, or modify the following narration accordingly.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The Drakon nods, deep in thought, then speaks with firm assurance.
>
> > I'm glad we stumbled upon each other. I believe what you know may be of great importance to figuring out how we might penetrate this barrier, and uncover what sort of threat this strange citadel may be to the Caryx Savanna and its people.
>
> She unfurls a map, tracing a line from your location to a city that sits on the Savanna's southern coast.
>
> > If you'd like to continue the investigation, join me at the Communal Quarter of the city of Tajar. There, we can consult with the other Flamewardens, and Tajar's matriarch, Vekaris Ossa. She is as wise and weathered as they come, and if anyone will know how to make use of our combined knowledge, it's most certainly her. If you'd like, I can travel there with you.

> [!info] Social
> #### A Warden's Responsibility
>
> Tiga wishes the party to join her in the [[Communal Quarter]] of Tajar and present what they know — about Kess, the ritual, and anything they have learned investigating the wall of flame — to matriarch [[Vekaris Ossa]], who leads the council of Wise-Women that govern Tajar. To that end, Tiga will also readily discuss:
>
> - The Flamewardens are an organization of skilled rangers who train in traditional techniques for protecting the people of the Caryx Savanna from its many natural hazards.
> - Vekaris Ossa leads the Savanna's largest city, and is a former bard, with unparalleled knowledge of this place and its history, dating back to the ancient [[Sedyri]].
> - Tajar is Caryx Savanna's largest city, and despite the disasters that have befallen it, through the work of the Flamewardens, its people persist.
>
> Specific dialogue for Tiga on the above topics can be found immediately below this block.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 11)** check determines Tiga is being as open and earnest as she can, for the good of the Savanna's people.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About the Flamewardens?
>
> **A:**
>
> > We are a small organization of rangers dating all the way back to the first Arcturians that settled the Caryx Savanna. All Flamewardens train in traditional techniques for protecting the Savanna's people from its difficult environment — the fire season first and foremost, though some have deemed our name a misnomer, as we also oversee efforts like evacuations and rescues during the flooding season.
> >
> > In the end, Flamewardens are those who recognize that people are but small in the face of the great Savanna, and take responsibility for adapting our way of life to be compatible with this place's awesome nature.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About Vekaris Ossa?
>
> **A:**
>
> > The head matriarch of the elderly Wise-Women that lead Tajar.
> >
> > In her younger years, Vekaris was a renowned bard, and her encyclopedic knowledge of the Savanna's songs and stories makes her one of the most knowledgeable sources about Tajar's history and traditions. Her tales retell our past all the way back to the Sedyri who once inhabited these lands. You may know her daughter Fernis, famed scholar of the Anachraenum — it's said Vekaris' stories are what inspired her daughter's scholarship.
> >
> > If anyone in the Savanna can shed light on what magics are at work here, it's her — not to mention, she'll want to know anything that can better equip her to protect Tajar's citizens.
> >
> > Given you're here investigating, she may also be able to answer **your** questions, if you have any?

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About Tajar?
>
> **A:**
>
> > The Savanna's largest city, and my home. Originally built on top of ancient Sedyri ruins, and now on our own ruins, I suppose, after the Great Burning brought down half the city a decade ago. We are a tenacious people — if you'll permit me a moment of pride. As a Flamewarden, I've dedicated my life to protecting that legacy, and carrying on the traditions that have long allowed us to weather this land.

If the party assents to having Tiga join them, she will accompany the party through the Caryx Savanna as long as they stay reasonably focused on reaching Tajar's Communal Quarter, and she will fight alongside the party in combat encounters.

Regardless, before departing, Tiga asks the party about the whispers in the smoke and flame, if the topic has not already arisen. Tiga herself heard a whisper of (at least) the following:*"I tried to tell you, but none of you listened. You'll listen now, I'll make **sure** of it!"*

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Tiga pauses for a moment, looking unsure of herself for practically the first time:
>
> > One last question … did any of you hear … whispers? While in the smoke, or the flame? I believe I did, and they were quite concerning. Angry. Hateful. A bad sign about whoever is behind this, surely?

Tiga encourages the party to report what they heard — and what they think it means — to Vekaris.

### Concluding the Event

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Next Steps
>
> Once the party has investigated the wall of flame and spoken with Tiga, they have multiple avenues for continuing their investigation:
>
> - Travel to Tajar's Communal Quarter and meet with Vekaris Ossa in [[Flash of Insight]].
> - Randomly encounter a flash fire in [[Trial by Fire]].
> - Stumble into an attack by enraged fire elementals in [[Spontaneous Combustion]].
>
> If the party opted to have Tiga travel with them, add [[Flamewarden Tiga]] to the [[DreamTeam]].
