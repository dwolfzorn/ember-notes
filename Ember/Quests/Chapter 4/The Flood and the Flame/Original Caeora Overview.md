# Original Caeora Overview

Seasons upon Ember have always been influenced by the moons above, but within the Arctus Plateau, nowhere is that more evident than the Burning Savanna. Every year fires spread across the tall dry summer grasses, wiping the slate clean and rejuvenating the land so that when the rains arrive during the height of Mayis during the winter, life springs forth once more. Migratory herds and animals are the savanna's mainstay, and the yearly fires' intensity, the raging monsoons, and the dangerous massive wildlife make it all but impossible for local Arcturians to build permanent settlements. Instead, the area is home to wanderers, hunters, researchers, and the occasional druid who revel in a strong connection to life energy. In such an isolated and ignored area of the region a small hidden village of Sedyri Ashka, still clinging to their ancient traditions lives in relative peace and are supported by the nearby godly family of Katu, Verkalla, and Raineka.

Six months ago after the arrival of the rains and the flooding of the savanna, a Kiska named Kess arrived in the area, intent on studying the Burning Savanna's deep connection to fire Moon Ragen. Unfortunately, as she delved deeper into the connection she encountered the hidden power of Friyara, the Elemental Mistress of Hatred, and was enslaved to her will. Kess soon began exploiting the area's connection to fire and constructed a fortress upon a central savanna plateau, naming it the Citadel of Fire. Since then the area around the Citadel has begun to smolder and burn kept at bay by the efforts of the nearby hidden settlement of Seceriss and their ancient traditions of balance between fire and water.

This quest involves the massive area of the Burning Savanna and the deep connection between the Cycles of Fire and Water in the area. It also involves many different lore connections and elements of other stories, such as hinting at the small family of Shard Gods that live upon the Isle of Storms, the Crumbling Spire looming out of the Plateau, and the first touchstone with the last remnants of the ancient Sedyri culture.
