# Burning Hearts

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event is guaranteed to occur when the party visits the [[Citadel of Fire]] after completing [[Dousing the Flame]]. By braving the burning citadel, the characters can:
>
> - Battle [[Fiyaran Cultist]], Voidfire elementals, and enraged fire elementals.
> - Meet Sathyr, an [[Ignarch]] magically imprisoned within the citadel's caldera.
> - Forge [[Cinderbreaker]], a weapon capable of freeing Sathyr, thereby robbing Kess of a source of power.
> - Confront Kess in her corrupted form at the top of the citadel.
> - Make a crucial decision about whether to banish the Spark by killing Kess, or attempt a dangerous ritual to balance it by summoning the Ripple of the Rivers.
>
> #### Area Walkthrough
>
> Exploration of this Event takes place on [[Citadel of Fire]] Area Map. A complete description of its contents can be found in the Area Walkthrough, [[Citadel of Fire]].
>
> The party begins their exploration at the [[Entrance]].

### Kess and the Spark

After the party has defeated Kess the Corrupted — knocking the empowered firemage unconscious — they are faced with a difficult decision about how to save the Savanna from the havoc wreaked by the Spark contained within the young Kiska. The party has two options: banish the Spark from the surface of Ember by killing Kess entirely, or summon the Ripple of the Rivers into her to balance the Spark's power. In the end, one of these two outcomes will happen; how each be reached — and which are even possible — depend on the party's prior decisions and current capabilities.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Conveying the Impossible
>
> Importantly, there is no way to banish the Spark (or resolve this Quest) without either Kess's death or the successful summoning of the Ripple. This Event contains checks that attempt to convey this to the party within the fiction; however, if players attempt and exhaustive search for alternatives and begin to grow frustrated, it can be worth communicating this inevitability out of character.
>
> You can also go off book and allow the party a clever and original alternative, but if you do so, the remaining text of the Event will not support it.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### No Way Out
>
> Any character with **Arcana (DC 12, Passive)** can feel an absolute inferno of arcane energy passively radiating off of Kess's being, even while unconscious. In fact, there is no discernable separation this fire energy and Kess's soul; the character concludes that any attempt to sever Kess's connection with the Spark of the Savanna will unavoidably kill her.
>
> - **Attunement: Ragen**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Path: Academy Dropout**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Path: Cindaric Initiate**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Character** **knows the** [[Rune: Life]] **Rune** : The character automatically succeeds on this check.

### Summoning the Ripple

The party may attempt to summon the Ripple of the Rivers into Kess, balancing the energy of the Spark of the Savanna. Because Kess already performed the other half of the ritual, this takes no special equipment — only the knowledge of how to perform this extraordinarily difficult task.

If successful, this summoning ritual restores balance to the Caryx Savanna's seasons, grants Kess the ability to exhibit newfound control over those seasons, and closes the arcane vulnerability through which Fiyara was able to influence Kess. If failed, the improperly performed ritual kills Kess. For this reason, only one character may attempt the ritual. Narration for both outcomes is provided below the associated checks.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Conveying the Risk
>
> Incorrectly performing this ritual immediately kills Kess — these stakes can and likely should be conveyed to the players before they attempt the check, by reminding them of the immense power they have witnessed just from the Spark alone, and how summoning forth another such entity is likely to have dire consequences if done incorrectly.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Fire Meets Flood
>
> Unless the party [[Tidebound Temple]] from Fesira during [[Tidebound Temple]], the ritual must be improvised — a task of monumental difficulty. In such case, the character must make a successful **Arcana (DC 22)** check to summon the Ripple of the Rivers into Kess, balancing the Spark of the Savanna. On a failure, Kess is killed.
>
> - **Attunement: Mayis**: The character gains **+1 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character derived the techniques for balancing the arcane energies of fire and water from the bas reliefs in the** [[Iconostasis]] **in the** [[Temple of the Tides]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> If the party [[Tidebound Temple]] from Fesira, the character must make a successful **Arcana (DC 16)** check to summon the Ripple of the Rivers into Kess, balancing the Spark of the Savanna. On a failure, Kess is killed.
>
> - **Attunement: Mayis**: The character gains **+1 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character derived the techniques for balancing the arcane energies of fire and water from the bas reliefs in the** [[Iconostasis]] **in the** [[Temple of the Tides]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character** **knows the** [[Rune: Storm]] **Rune** : The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Unless the party** [[Tidebound Temple]]**:** The character suffers **-2 Banes** on this check.

#### Mayis Attunement: Summoned the Ripple of the Rivers

The character who successfully summons the Ripple of the Rivers into Kess advances their **Attunement: Mayis (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A shimmering tide of water surrounds wraps itself around Kess's limp and battered body, lifting her gently into the air. Everywhere it curls, the violet flames that once covered her sizzle, steam, and die. The halls of the citadel begin to fade, losing the purple tinge that infected their reds and oranges when viewed out of the corner of the eye. Though the lava of Mount Sitara still blazes, the engravings on the citadel walls look duller than before, as if a terrible power has departed this place.
>
> Finally, as the water wraps around Kess's head, you see the glow of two cool blue eyes. In a flash, Kess stands before you, her wounds healed and her scowl gone — as if the waters of life itself infused her.
>
> > Hiya! I, uh— sorry about that.
>
> She flushes beneath her fur, then starts again.
>
> > What I mean to say is … it's me. The real me. Whatever Fiyara was doing to me … it's gone. Thank you **so** much. Being stuck like that was … awful. And the things I did …
>
> Her gaze lifts up and past you, her expression growing more worried by the second.
>
> > …I've got a lot to make up for, don't I?

Kess is eager to receive comfort and reassurance from the party, but also eager to know what she can do to help offset the harm she caused — willingly or not. At a natural lull in conversation, or if party references the Ripple of the Rivers, she adds:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > I can … feel it. The water. The same way I've always been able to feel fire! Except … not just here. Everywhere! At least … the whole Savanna! It's … hard to describe! But …
> >
> > I think I can use it to help. For real this time! I can feel the balance of the Savanna … or, well, the imbalance. But I think I can fix that now! I just … I want to be careful about what I try, this time around. Maybe go back to Tajar, ask the Wise-Women for some guidance … maybe I'll see you there?

Barring any further questions from the party, Kess parts with them to make her own way back (cleaning up her mess as best she can along the way) and concluding this Event.

### The Death of Kess

If the party opts not to attempt to the summoning, they are left with two options: kill Kess, or refuse the choice and (presumably) allow the chaos to continue consuming the Savanna — though in the latter case, Kess is killed instead by Fiyara.

Party Kills KessIf the party decides to kill Kess, mark the following Event Outcome, note the relevant Attunement, then read or paraphrase the block that follows:

`[[/outcome banished]]`

#### Akon Attunement: Slayed Kess

Any character who advocates for or concurs with the killing of Kess advances their **Attunement: Akon (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As Kess's soul departs, so does the Spark, and all that slipped through along with it. The halls of the citadel begin to fade, losing the violet tinge that infected their reds and oranges when viewed out of the corner of the eye. Though the lava of Mount Sitara still blazes, the runes and engravings on the citadel walls look duller than before, as if a terrible power has departed this place.
>
> All that is left is an eerie and uncomfortable silence. A great suffering has ended — and a great joy has been extinguished.

Barring any final investigation of the citadel from the party, this concludes this Event.

#### Ragen Attunement: Let it Burn

Any character who agrees to let Kess live while corrupted by Fiyara (and allow the Savanna to keep burning) advances their **Attunement: Ragen (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As Kess lays before you, beaten and broken, a terrible grating voice echoes through the halls of the citadel — whispering at first, then growing into a cacophonous boom.
>
> > Weak … weeeeak … **WEEEEEEAK!**
>
> Kess's body floats into the air, head lolling and limbs dangling limply. The voice seems to come from everywhere.
>
> > I **thought** you would be my champion! But you! Are! **WEAK!**
>
> All at once, Kess's body pulls taut, limbs spread and head lifted to the sky. For a moment, her eyelids open, and there is nothing within them but violet flame. And then …
>
> … she is gone, in a single explosive conflagration that consumes her body, leaving nothing but dust. The voice departs with a final whisper:
>
> > You have not bested me, pathetic little mortals … when the time is right … I will return …
>
> With that, the halls of the citadel begin to fade, losing the violet tinge that infected their reds and oranges when viewed out of the corner of the eye. Though the lava of Mount Sitara still blazes, the runes and engravings on the citadel walls look duller than before, as if a great power has departed this place.

`[[/outcome banished]]`

Barring any final investigation of the citadel from the party, this concludes this Event.
