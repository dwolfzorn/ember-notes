# Dousing the Flame

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration and Social Event is guaranteed to occur when the party visits the tile of Mount Sitara southeast of the [[Citadel of Fire]], after completing [[Tidebound Temple]]. By working together with Sorak, the characters can:
>
> - Open a small passageway in the dome of flame around the Citadel of Fire.
> - Make their way into the citadel proper.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Citadel Flamewall]] Level of the [[Vista: Caryx Savanna]] Vista.

### Reuniting with Sorak

When the party arrives, Sorak is — angrily and unsuccessfully — beseeching his god Katu for aid with bringing down the dome of fire. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > **KATU!** FOR YEARS I HAVE CARRIED YOUR ANGER WITHIN ME! BORNE YOUR TEMPER AND HEFTED YOUR BURDENS! AND NOW, WHEN I NEED **YOU** MORE THAN EVER, YOU SHOW NOT YOUR FACE?! WILL YOU LET THIS PLACE BE CONSUMED IN FLAMES?! HAVE YOU NO **HONOR**?! **NO SELF-RESPECT**?! **NO**—
>
> Sorak pauses as he finally notices your presence. The icy blue glow inhabiting his eyes fades, and the winds around him relent, remaining just strong enough to hold back the smog. When Sorak resumes speaking, his voice is as cool as snow.
>
> > Well, adventurers. I do not see Fesira in your midst. Have you failed me too?

If the party attests that they [[Tidebound Temple]] (needing only show the note in [[Fesira's Notebook]] as proof) Sorak is much friendlier, and remains so throughout this Event. He provides the party with the promised  **50** and the [[Talisman of the Storms]]. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > It seems I may have underestimated you. Take your payment, as promised.
> >
> > And I must admit … I am relieved you have proven yourselves worth trusting. For all my cries, Katu still does not answer, that **faulted** and **fickle**…
>
> Sorak eyes you critically, then gives an approving nod.
>
> > …but we are not without a path. You see, as his Stormheart, I carry not just a portion of Katu's wrath, but also a fragment of his power. With it, I might open the smallest gap in this great wall of flame. But … I am not a shard god. I must remain in this place, and in every moment, put all my strength into holding that door open while you adventurers pass through it. Are you ready to face whatever you may find inside?

If [[Tidebound Temple]], Sorak is furious with the party, and remains so throughout this Event. If told Fesira's last words, he dismisses them as *"foolish dreams for a child to die for."*

Afterwards, however, he reveals that he is forced by circumstance to permit the party entry to the citadel regardless. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > What a waste. And as for you all … damn Katu for what I am forced now to entrust you with, despite your **obvious** failure.
>
> Sorak eyes you critically, then gives a disgusted shake of his head.
>
> > Without Katu here to bring his strength to bear, I cannot proceed into the citadel myself. However, as his Stormheart, I carry not just a portion of Katu's wrath, but also a fragment of his power. With it, I might open the smallest gap in this great wall of flame. But … I am not a shard god. I must remain in this place, and in every moment, put all my strength into holding that door open while you all pass through it. If I entrust you yet again, do you **swear** not to fail me twice?

### Aiding Sorak's Ritual

After the players have committed to braving the citadel, Sorak invites them to assist his ritual to open a gap in the dome of fire. Read or paraphrase the following, optionally modifying it to reflect his opinion of the party:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Loathe as I am to admit it, wielding Katu's power to establish a foothold in this blasted wall will be easier with some assistance. I am about to summon a storm of monumental proportions. I am will be the conduit — quite literally the lightning rod — and the scree and stones that litter this mountainside will sail through the air on hurricane winds.
> >
> > Those of you proficient in magic may yet redirect that lightning away from me, and the brave and strong among you might stand before me and bear the bruises of those stones in my stead. That way, my focus may remain on opening a safe passageway through the flames. Are you able? Are you willing?

Characters may participate in one or both efforts to shelter Sorak. The danger the party faces while traversing the dome of fire is reduced if at least one person shelters Sorak with their body, and further reduced if at least one spellcaster succeeds in redirecting the lightning.

Each additional character who physically shelters Sorak spreads the damage more widely, while each spellcaster who attempts to redirect the lightning provides an additional chance of success.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As Sorak begins his ritual, the skies blacken. The rain thickens until the dome of fire becomes visible only as orange smudge behind the monsoon waters. Your ears ring with the ceaseless howling of gale-force winds, punctuated only by deafening thunderclaps that shake the plateau entire. Admidst it all stands Sorak … and you.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Weathering the Storm
>
> Characters who stand tightly around Sorak (rather than tucking themselves into a safe crevasse or overhang) shelter Sorak from wind-tossed stones, taking TODO damage, evenly divided between all characters who encircle Sorak (rounding down).
>
> Alternatively, the party may devise clever alternate solutions to protect Sorak from blunt force harm; however, the storm ritual itself requires he be standing in open air.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Lightning Rods
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 16)** check can safely redirect the lightning away from Sorak, allowing Sorak to focus on the ritual.
>
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character** **knows the** [[Rune: Storm]] **Rune** : The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character takes damage from wind-tossed stones:** The character suffers **-2 Banes** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character successfully redirects the lightning away from Sorak *and* all other characters else who would otherwise redirect the lightning onto themselves.
> - **Critical Failure**: The character redirects the lightning onto themselves (after which it arcs to Sorak) and they take TODO damage.

### Crossing the Gap

After the party finishes assisting Sorak in establishing a foothold, it is time for the party to venture through the gap. Read or paraphrase the following invitation from Sorak, after which the party must brave however narrow a path the ritual opened:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Sorak roars over storm, the toll the ritual is taking on his body shredding his voice to gravel.
>
> > **THIS IS ALL I CAN DO! GO, NOW! WHILE THE PATH IS STILL OPEN!**
>
> Through the pouring rain, you can see a narrow slit in the dome of fire — your only way forward.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Through the Fire and Flame
>
> If the party successfully redirected the lightning and body-blocked or otherwise intercepted the stones, they take no damage moving through the passage — there is *just* enough room to permit them to pass through the sweltering tunnel without touching the flames.
>
> If the party achieved one but not both, the narrow tunnel judders, belching gouts of fire. Each character must make a `[[/save dex 14]]` saving throw or else take `[[/damage 2d6 fire]]` damage during passage. On a success, they instead take `[[/damage 1d6 fire]]` damage.
>
> If the party achieved neither, the tunnel is barely large enough to crawl through on hands and knees, and each character takes `[[/damage 2d6 fire]]` damage as the flames lick their skin throughout the crawl.

While traversing the fire, the party hears Kess's disembodied voice, unresponsive to any replies:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Within the flames, you hear Kess's voice, echoing, ethereal, and full of hatred:
>
> > The mountain! The Savanna! The Plateau! Ember, and all its moons and stars! They'll all learn! They'll all **BURN!**

### Concluding the Event

> [!warning] Gamemaster
