# Summoning the Spark

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event occurs when the party visits the [[Tomb of the Unifiers]] after completing [[Blasted Books]]. By delving into the tomb, the characters can:
>
> - Retrieve magical artifacts crafted by the ancient [[Sedyri]].
> - Battle powerful tomb guardians and overcome dangerous traps left behind by the Sedyri.
> - Help Kess complete the summoning ritual for the Spark of the Savanna — and witness the terrible consequences.
>
> Note that if the party retreats and does not complete the Tomb of the Unifiers (and thus Kess does not complete her ritual) be sure to cancel this Event rather than marking it as complete. The party must return and obtain the Vessel, let Kess perform her ritual, and complete the Event in order to progress this Quest.
>
> #### Area Walkthrough
>
> Exploration of this Event takes place on [[Tomb of the Unifiers]] Area Map. A complete description of its contents can be found in the Area Walkthrough, [[Tomb of the Unifiers]].
>
> The party begins outside the tomb, which is narrated in [[Summoning the Spark]] below. Afterwards, they may begin the Area Walkthrough at the [[Exterior]].

### Outside the Tomb

The party is greeted by [[Kess the Firemage]] at the tomb's [[Exterior]], who briefs them on the progress of her research and the goals of their expedition into the Tomb of the Unifiers. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Having flagged down your attention, Kess shouts excitedly:
>
> > Hiya!! It's all ready!! Everything we need to summon the Spark of the Savanna!! Well, everything except for the Vessel of the Spark … but that should be just inside the Tomb!!
>
> Her eyes flit to the massive door of volcanic rock set flush into the stone edifice.
>
> > I almost got impatient and went in myself, but … I got too scared to go in on my own. The histories don't give any details, they just say it's super-dangerous, but … we **have** to go in!! The Unifiers who rest here once protected the **whole** Savanna from the fires that burn it year after year. If we can grab the Vessel, I know I can do that too!!
>
> As she glances back to the door, her excitement wanes for a moment.
>
> > I'm just … not so good in a fight, or with traps, or anything like that. Do you … want me to come with?

However the party answers, Kess will follow their lead. If she accompanies the party, she will try her best to fight alongside them. If the party advises Kess to remain in the safety of her encampment, they can earn Attunement.

#### Heart Attunement: Kess Remains at Camp

If the party instructs Kess to wait within the safety of her camp, each character advances their **Attunement: Heart of Ember (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Plot Armor
>
> If Kess accompanies the party on their delve into the Tomb of the Unifiers, it is recommended you limit the consequences of Kess going down in combat to — at most — temporary unconsciousness. Were Kess to die, the narrative of [[The Flood and the Flame]] would not be able to continue, and this Quest would necessarily end inside the tomb.

Either way, before they enter the tomb, Kess advises them on how to identify the Vessel of the Spark:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Okay, you're looking for a small bowl made of Cinderspar — a special, **super** durable kind of stone that only the Sedyri knew how to make. The bowl should be full of water, with a tiny flame burning **IN** the water!

Finally, if the party [[Blasted Books]] during [[Blasted Books]], read or paraphrase the following. A party member may then take possession of a [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]].

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > By the way, thanks **so** much for saving my books from the fire last time we met! One of them was a dictionary of the dialect of Scor spoken by the Sedyri who lived on the Arctus Plateau, and whaddya know … I found another copy in the Flamewarden Archives! So if any of you want the copy that **you** saved … it's all yours!

From here, the party is welcome to continue into the Tomb of the Unifiers, beginning at the [[Exterior]].

### Summoning Ritual

After the party retrieves the Vessel of the Spark from the tomb, they regroup with Kess outside the tomb. Regardless of whether Kess joined them inside (and anything that happened within) the firemage is overjoyed to have the vessel in hand.

She provides the party with the  **5** payment she promised, then immediately goes to begin the ritual:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> With uncontainable anticipation, Kess seizes the strange, ever-burning bowl and stands before a table messily covered in books and research notes.
>
> > Okay, summoning time! The ritual calls for one mage — the one who will shape the seasons — to "unite" with the Vessel of the Spark during the summoning, so I'll keep hold of the bowl.
> >
> > But if any of you wanna pitch in, I could use a couple talented spellcasters to help direct and contain any excess energy. Anyone you feel up for it?

If any party members agree, Kess explains the basic magical mechanics necessary to direct the magical energy.

#### Ragen Attunement: Channeling the Spark

Any character who agrees to provide magical assistance to the summoning ritual advances their **Attunement: Ragen (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

Kess then begins the ritual, with any party members (who can perform magic and who elected to help) on standby in case of magical overflow.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The firemage launches into her incantations with exuberant confidence, as the flame within the Vessel begins to judder and spark, before leaping upwards into a spindly column of fire that towers over her head. Her eyes go wide, and she glances back at you as she struggles to shout over the noise of the roaring fire.
>
> > This is! A b-bit more than I expected! Please, t-try to contain it while I channel the Spark into the Vessel!

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Fighting Fire with Fire Magic
>
> Any character who knows at list one Rune and makes a successful **Arcana (DC 15)** check can contain the initial flash, but immediately determines that magical energy is flowing in from a unusual external source at an ever-increasing rate, and it seems nigh-impossible to contain long term. They intuitively understand they are only delaying an inevitable fireball.
>
> - **Character** **attempts to use the** **Talent: Counterspell** **Talent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check, and may roll with **+2 Boons** for the purposes of achieving additional results — in which case, there are no consequences on a failed check.
> - **Character** **knows the** [[Rune: Flame]] **Rune** : The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character determines the excess magical energy is flowing from a divine source.
>
> If any character succeeded, the pillar of fire continues to press at their containment, pushing towards Kess's books and research notes. Meanwhile, a twist of far stranger *purple* flame begins spiraling up and around Kess herself.
>
> If no character succeeded, Kess's books and research notes begin to burn, and Kess herself is being rapidly swallowed by the twist of strange violet flame that is rapidly encircling her.

With both Kess and her research either burning or endangered, the party now has a multiple ways to provide aid. Earlier success on magical containment makes these interventions easier by providing the party with a longer window of opportunity.

In the end, Kess will always be swallowed by the purple fire and disappear. The party's interventions simply allow them to save Kess's *research*, and *delay* Kess's departure. Succeeding on either effort provides the party with information that hints at how the ritual went wrong, and offers clues towards a larger mystery.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Into the Fire
>
> In this fast-paced and escalating disaster, each party member only has time to attempt a **single** check below. They must choose between helping Kess or her research, and between multiple ways to do so. Once everyone has selected an intervention (if any) roll all checks.
>
> - **Any character succeeded on an earlier check to magically contain the fire:** All checks in this block are made with **+2 Boons**.
>
> #### Saving Kess's Research
>
> Party members may attempt to save Kess's research via any of the following methods. Multiple successes to save her research yield no additional rewards, but do provide multiple chances for *someone* to succeed:
>
> - **Athletics (DC 14)** to dart in a hand and pull out Kess's research notes before they are consumed by flame. On a failure, the character takes `[[/damage 2d6 fire]]` damage.
> - **Wilderness (DC 16)** to skillfully smother the flames without destroying the research notes.
> - **Arcana (DC 16)** to magically suppress the arcane flames threatening the research notes (which are palpably weaker than the purple fire that surrounds Kess). Characters who either know the [[Rune: Flame]] Rune or use the **Talent: Counterspell** gain **+2 Boons** on this check. Characters who use the Counterspell Talent and choose the Flame Rune for it instead gain a total of **+4 Boons**.
>
> If any character succeeds, the notes are preserved, and remain available later in this Event.
>
> #### Helping Kess
>
> Party members may attempt to aid Kess via any of the following methods:
>
> - **Athletics (DC 14)** to dart a hand through the encircling flames and get a grip on Kess. On a failure, the character's hand plunges into the flames and they take `[[/damage 2d6 fire]]` damage and as they grope blindly in the fire and fail to establish a grip.
> - **Arcana (DC 18)** to magically slow the advance of the violet fire up and around Kess's body. Characters who either know the [[Rune: Flame]] Rune or use the **Talent: Counterspell** gain **+2 Boons** on this check. Characters who use the Counterspell Talent and choose the Flame Rune for it instead gain a total of **+4 Boons**.
>
> Kess's disappearance within the purple flame proceeds in a number of stages, depending on whether the party members succeeded on any of the above checks. Read or paraphrase the narration in each applicable stage.
>
> **Initial Stage**
>
> In all cases, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > The Kiska shouts to you in panic from within the roaring flames of otherworldly violet fire that spiral around her body. She begins to float off the ground, the flames rising up to meet her.
> >
> > "I can feel something … **calling** me! It's—"
>
> Unless a character succeeded either in establishing a grip on Kess or in magically slowing the fire, skip to [[Summoning the Spark]] below.
>
> **Denial**
>
> > "It wants me to join it. To become **one**.But I won't! I won't. It's— she's—"
>
> As the flames rise farther up Kess's body, characters who choose to maintain their grip take `[[/damage 4d6 fire]]` damage as the flames rise ever higher.
>
> If no characters both established and still maintain a grip on Kess, skip to [[Summoning the Spark]] below.
>
> **Anger**
>
> > "**NO!** I want **none** of your power! It's **WRONG**, I can **FEEL** it**! LET ME GO! LET ME—** "
>
> As the flames cover Kess up to her eyes, characters who choose to maintain their grip take `[[/damage 5d6 fire]]` damage and feel a strange hollowness within themselves. They must make a successful Crucible text TODO or else let go as they feel their empathy for Kess drain out of them, severing all motivation to maintain their grip until they have fully broken contact with the bizarre purple flames.
>
> If no characters still maintain a grip on Kess, skip to [[Summoning the Spark]] below.
>
> **Acceptance**
>
> > Suddenly, the light drains from Kess's eyes, and her pupils fade into pools of inky violet resembling nothing less than the fire that surrounds her. She speaks in a low monotone as she floats just above you, the flames rising over her head.
> >
> > "I understand now. We will punish this world for what it's taken from us. Your citadel awaits."
>
> Proceed with [[Summoning the Spark]] below, in which any characters who still maintained their grip lose it when Kess rockets skyward with the force of a deity's invisible hand.

### Kess's Disappearance

After the party saves and delays whatever they can manage, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> In an explosion of uncontainable arcane energy, Kess rockets skyward, buffeted by a column of dark violet flame that overtakes her even as lifts her. Within the fire, you see two hollow pits — eyes, staring at you with hatred. Then, in a flash, they disappear, taking both Kess and the pillar along with them. All that remains is the scorched earth upon which Kess once stood, and the burning wreckage of a table that once held her research.
>
> Then, another flash, this time on the far horizon, barely visible through the narrow gorge that took you here. Far to the south on the distant peak of Mount Sitara, a flare of incredible energy erupts skyward, creating a blazing column of flame. After several seconds, it fades slightly, but a dark smudge of structure remains in its wake: a towering spike of volcanic rock thrusting from the rocky peak, belching thick smoke and fiery emanations.

### Investigating the Disaster

The party can employ a variety of methods to investigate what went wrong with Kess's ritual.

After a moment to collect their thoughts, they may recall certain information pertinent to the ritual's corruption:

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Spark of Memory
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 18)** or **Science (DC 18)** check recalls — after a quiet moment to think it through — a esoteric account of strange violet flames known as Voidfire, which were employed by [[Fiyara]], a Wild God now known as the Elemental Mistress of Hatred, who once served as the Elemental Sovereign ruling over the moon of Ragen, before her corruption and fall.
>
> - **Knowledge: Gods**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character earlier recognized the magical energy as flowing from a divine source:** The character gains **+1 Boons** on this check.
> - **Party witnessed the Denial, Anger, and/or Acceptance stages of Kess's struggle:** The character gains **+1 Boons** on this check per each of those stages witnessed by connecting hints from Kess's dialogue.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also recalls that Voidfire burns away kindness and empathy, leaving behind only jealousy, hatred, and spite.

If the party saved Kess's research, and spend a few hours analyzing the (extremely scattered and disorganized) notes, they have additional avenues for piecing together what caused the ritual to escalate into such a disaster:

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Research, Reconstructed
>
> Any character who spends a few hours digging carefully through Kess's research and makes a successful **Arcana (DC 14)** check finds key variables unaccounted for in Kess's magical formulae, leading to an imbalance of magical energies — Kess appears to have been approaching the ritual mostly by instinct, and her mistakes opened the ritual to outside influence by potent sources of fire magic.
>
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character has a** [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they consult it — they find Sedyri writing while reviewing Kess's notes.
> - **Character** **uses the** **Talent: Versatile Translator** **Talent** : The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: Careful analysis of the original Sedyri magical formulae that Kess dug up (and partially translated in her notes) suggest a missing water magic component to the ritual, which Kess appears to have missed.
>
> Any character who spends a few hours digging carefully through Kess's research and makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** check determines Kess had little understanding of Sedyri culture. The Sedyri were known to worship opposites and contradictions; Kess's fixation on fire magic shows a clear research gap around water magic and the Unifiers' control of the Savanna's flood season. This may have caused issues for the ritual.
>
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character has a** [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they consult it — they find Sedyri writing while reviewing Kess's notes.
> - **Character** **uses the** **Talent: Versatile Translator** **Talent** : The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: Careful analysis of the original Sedyri text that Kess dug up (and partially translated in her notes) suggest that the Unifiers would summon not only the "Spark of the Savanna," but an accompanying magical entity that allowed for control over water.
>
> Any character who has a [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]] and spends a few hours digging carefully through Kess's research can attempt to translate all of the original Sedyri in Kess's research notes via a **Society (DC 16)** check. Success reveals a translation error by Kess: "Vessel of the Spark" refers not to the artifact recovered in the Tomb of the Unifiers, but to the *person who* *holds the artifact during the ritual.* It appears the such individual *is* the "Vessel" — the Spark of the Savanna is summoned into *them*, not the artifact they hold during the ritual.
>
> - **Character** **uses the** **Talent: Versatile Translator** **Talent** : The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
