# Rising Tides

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This repeatable Combat Event can occur anywhere in the [[Caryx Savanna]] that does not have a notable Location. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Battle enraged water elementals disturbed by the recent environmental upheaval.
> - Investigate the aftermath of the battle for clues.
>
> This Event can repeat at any time between the [[Eye of the Storm]] and the [[Burning Hearts]], and is significantly more likely in Water hexes. It also becomes significantly more likely the closer [[Mayis]] is to Full.
>
> This Event is depicted using the "Flooded" Level of the[[Caryx Savanna]] Area Map.

### Raging Elementals

The water elementals begin the encounter concealed.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Hidden in the Water
>
> As the characters enter the Scene, only the 3 [[Droplet]] are visible, hovering over a pool of water.
>
> The 3 [[Tidegazer]] and 4 [[Riptongue]] are **Hidden** throughout pools of water in the Scene. To better see these Hidden tokens, hold down the Alt key with Token Controls active.
>
> Characters with a `[[/skill perception 18 passive format=long]]` or who make a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check when entering the Scene can detect their camouflaged forms in the pools of water.
>
> As combat begins, **Reveal** the **Hidden** Tidegazers (if they have not been revealed already). This may cause the characters to become &reference[Surprise]{Surprised}.
>
> At the beginning of the second round of combat, **Reveal** the **Hidden** Riptongues (if they have not been revealed already).

As soon as the Droplets detect the characters, combat begins.

> #### Surprise
>
> Characters with a `[[/skill perception 18 passive format=long]]` or who make a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check when entering the Scene can detect the elementals' camouflaged forms. Otherwise, the characters are &reference[surprise]{Surprised} when combat begins by the revealed Tidegazers.
>
> #### Droplet Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Droplet]] will target the nearest creature with its [[Smother]] action.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Droplet will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Droplet will attempt to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} enemies using its [[Smother]] action in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
>
> The Droplet attempts to flee the Scene once &reference[Bloodied]; it succeeds if its movement would carry it past the Scene bounds.
>
> #### Tidegazer Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Tidegazer]] will use its [[Captivating Gaze]] action to &reference[Charmed]{Charm} a creature, prioritizing Small creatures if able.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Tidegazer will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - From range, the Tidegazer will use its [[Water Blast]] action.
> - When threatened, the Tidegazer can use its [[Aqueous Transmission]] Bonus Action to reposition itself.
> - The Tidegazer will attempt to lure in Small or smaller enemies using its [[Captivating Gaze]] action in order to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} them in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
>
> The Tidegazer attempts to flee the Scene once &reference[Bloodied]; it succeeds if its movement would carry it past the Scene bounds.
>
> #### Riptongue Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Riptongue]] will attempt to Grapple a creature using its [[Tongue Lash]] action, prioritizing Medium creatures if able.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Riptongue will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, the Riptongue will use its [[Multiattack]] action.
> - The Riptongue will attempt to reel in Medium or smaller enemies using its [[Tongue Lash]] action in order to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} them in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
>
> The Riptongue attempts to flee the Scene once &reference[Bloodied]; it succeeds if its movement would carry it past the Scene bounds.

### Investigating the Aftermath

After the elementals have been destroyed, the characters may wish to investigate their remains.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Clear Waters
>
> Any character with **Arcana (DC 14, Passive)** who inspects the elementals' remains can confirm that the water elementals were untouched by Abyssal energy, suggesting that Fiyara's corruption does not extend to the forces of water.
>
> - **Attunement: Mayis**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Elementals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> Any character made aware of this fact and who makes a successful **Society (DC 16)** check recalls that the water elementals of the [[Caryx Savanna]] usually rise in response to fire elemental activity; their enraged state is proportionate to the ferocity of their fire elemental counterparts.
>
> - **Culture: Ashka**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Elementals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
