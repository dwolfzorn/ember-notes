# To Judge and Be Judged

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event occurs when the party returns to the [[Earthen Henge]] after defeating [[Slaith]], and choosing whether to imprison or destroy it. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Speak with [[Terrane]] and face the Korvathi sage's approval or disapproval.
> - Debrief with [[Eamon Mariflor]], [[Rala Ushna]], and [[Krafton Lilifeld]] to reflect on the events which have transpired.
> - Consider what their choice means for the future of the Arctus Plateau.
> - Be rewarded for their crucial intervention.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Earthen Henge (active)]] composition of the [[Vista: Redrak Fields]] Vista.

### Preparing for the Ritual

Depending on how the party dealt with Slaith during [[Braving the Elements]], they are received differently by the waiting parties.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Greeting[[Braving the Elements]]
>
> **A:**
>
> Eamon shoots you a stony glare.
>
> > It is time to call Terrane back to Ember. I suppose she'll be pleased that Slaith is back in its cage. I can't say I share that sentiment. Our way of life may be spared … but what of our souls?
>
> He shakes his head in dismissive disapproval, then turns away from you as he speaks.
>
> > I'll let you all speak with her, lest my reservations jeopardize the compact. Simply trace the spiral on the floor plate with this Orial mud, and she will hear your call.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Greeting[[Braving the Elements]] and Terrane convinced to take Slaith's place
>
> **A:**
>
> Eamon greets you warmly, shaking your each of your hands.
>
> > You all did something incredibly brave back at the lake. Not just by confronting Slaith, but by banishing it from Ember's surface, and thus holding a being as powerful as Terrane to the responsibility of fulfilling our compact, **without** making our lives dependent of the permanent imprisonment of another living being. She may be … upset … that your actions have returned Slaith to Cora. I'm here to support you, however I can. You made the right decision. When you're ready, trace the spiral on the floor plate with this Orial mud, and we'll face her together.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Greeting[[Braving the Elements]] and Terrane not convinced to take Slaith's place
>
> **A:**
>
> Eamon greets you warmly, shaking your each of your hands.
>
> > You all did something incredibly brave back at the lake. Not just by confronting Slaith, but also by jumping headfirst an uncertain future, where those of us on Ember's surface will be responsible for discovering just how to make our way of life sustainable. I know we're up to the task, and I'm glad that you feel the same. Terrane, however, may be … upset … that your actions have returned Slaith to Cora. I'm here to support you, however I can. When you're ready, trace the spiral on the floor plate with this Orial mud, and we'll face her together.
>
> Rala steps forward and clears his throat.
>
> > Before that … I've been thinking. Terrane said that the magical properties imbued into the Lake Orial mud by siphoning Slaith were unique to Coran earth elementals. That … would include Terrane herself, would it not? Could she, perhaps, accomplish with her own body, what she coerced from Slaith's? Unlike Slaith, she voluntarily entered into this compact, and promised the result to us. Is it not … her responsibility to see it fulfilled?

If the party is unsure whether to ask Terrane to take Slaith's place, or asks Eamon's opinion, add:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Eamon nods his head, first in consideration, then approval.
>
> > I believe the young apprentice makes an excellent point. If Terrane can be convinced to do so, that may be our simplest route to a sustainable Redrak Fields, no longer premised on an involuntary imprisonment. I can't imagine, however, that she'll be easily convinced. I hesitate to have any agrimage ask it of her, lest we jeopardize the compact itself. Would you all make this case to her?

### Terrane's Gratitude

Once the party traces the spiral on the henge's floor plate with Orial mud, if the party [[Braving the Elements]], read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Nearly as soon as you exit the twisted stone circle, the Korvathi sage appears in a burst of green light. Her voice is just as booming as you remember.
>
> > Greetings, warriors of Ember. I felt the bindings snap into place as you put that beast Slaith back where it belonged. I am highly impressed by the skill and bravery of mere mortals such as yourselves. You have performed a great service to both Cora and Ember. Your names will be known among the Korvathi, and I wish to provide you a token of our gratitude.
>
> With a grinding noise, she slides a faintly glowing stone ring off her equally stony finger, and hands it to you.
>
> > This ring, provided only my people's greatest soldiers, is crafted of a rare and supremely stable Coran stone. The minerals within have a neutralizing effect on the vile acids and poisons of our archenemy. May it protect you as it has protected me for countless millennia.

Terrane hands over a [[Ring of Neutralization]] in gratitude for the party's service.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Unless any lingering issues or questions remain, I will return to Cora and continue the fight, and expect to see whoever leads your Agrimage Circle at the appointed time next century. I hope the compact allows your people to flourish, just as Slaith's imprisonment has protected my own.

### Terrane's Judgement

Once the party traces the spiral on the henge's floor plate with Orial mud, if the party [[Braving the Elements]] and did not already convince Terrane to take Slaith's place, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Nearly as soon as you exit the twisted stone circle, the Korvathi sage appears in a burst of green light. Her booming voice sounds like a landslide bearing directly down on you.
>
> > Imbeciles! What did I tell you about the dangers of destroying Slaith outright?! The battle against evil is not for the brittle of spirit! Even now, my brethren battle in on Cora's surface, among overwhelming horde's of Slinerak's minions! We were barely holding before you sent this foul beast back to our battlefield! How **dare** you?! I will remember what you have done here — and we Korvathi live **very** long lives!

> [!info] Social
> #### Duty Above All
>
> Despite her anger, it is still possible — with great difficulty — to convince Terrane to uphold her end of the compact. To have even the chance of success, they must invoke one of the following arguments:
>
> - Terrane's duty and responsibility to fulfill the terms of the compact.
> - That if Terrane does not uphold her end of the compact, the Korvathi will lose the arcane knowledge and rare plant-based magical components provided by the Agrimage Circle.
> - That if the forces for good on the Arctus Plateau were to collapse, the battle against evil on the surface of Ember would suffer, and thus the battle against evil *everywhere*.
> - Chaos and suffering on the Arctus Plateau would make people more vulnerable to recruiting by cults of Slinerak.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 16)** check is able to discern Terrane's priorities from conversation with her: the principle of duty, supplies for the war effort, the intertwined nature of the interplanetary battle against evil, and the threat of Slinerak cults on Ember's surface.
>
> Any character who makes a successful makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 20)** check can convince Terrane that she must uphold the compact, and provide herself what she once took from Slaith against its will. In such case, she agrees to submerge herself beneath Lake Orial, and continue to enrich its soil from her own body, while continuing to serve in a strategic capacity in the war on Cora via remote magical communication.
>
> - **The party invokes at least three persuasion criteria**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **The party already tried and failed to convince Terrane to take Slaith's place:** The character suffers **-2 Banes** on this check.
>
> Otherwise, Terrane refuses to provide the party and the Plateau with her assistance, and returns to Cora in a furious flash of light — holding an ongoing grudge against the party.

#### Akon Attunement: Terrane Taking Slaith's Place

If the party tries (for the first time) to convince Terrane to take Slaith's place under Lake Orial, any character who advocated for it advances their **Attunement: Akon (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### Terrane's Service

Once the party traces the spiral on the henge's floor plate with Orial mud, if the party [[Braving the Elements]] and already convinced Terrane to take Slaith's place, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Nearly as soon as you exit the twisted stone circle, the Korvathi sage appears in a burst of green light. When she speaks, her is solemn and as hard as the stone of which she is made.
>
> > You have bested the beast Slinerak, and saved the Plateau from the threat which burdens my home. As promised, I will travel to Lake Orial, and submerge myself beneath it, such that I may provide for you all that which I previously seized from Slaith by force. By the terms of our compact, it is my duty, and my responsibility — just as important as the one I have to my brethren. I hope that my service to your Plateau will serve the fight against evil everywhere. In such hope, I have only two asks:
> >
> > First, that you mortal warriors who defeated Slaith continue the battle against evil everywhere, in my stead. Second, that you agrimages continue to come to this place at the agreed upon hour, and provide the arcane knowledge and magical components to my fellow sages. The fight goes on, even without my presence.
> >
> > If you have nothing left to ask of me, I will go and take up my post.

### The Agrimages' Gratitude

Once Terrane has departed, if the party [[Braving the Elements]], read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Eamon takes a sharp breath, and turns to face to you.
>
> > Thank you immensely for facing Terrane with me — not to mention for defeating Slaith, and ridding the Plateau of an uncomfortable bargain that I do not believe truly served the interests of the land and its people.
> >
> > Now, this brings us to my compact with **you**! First, your gold, as promised, and better yet …
>
> The sprightly agrimage digs in his pack, and retrieves two strange artifacts.
>
> > In truth, I begun my research into alternative sources of agrimagical abundance as soon as Terrane revealed the truth of the compact. I believe my discovers may also be of use to adventurers like you.
>
> He hands you gnarled wooden wand, split into spindly roots at its bottom end.
>
> > First, an ancient artifact that predates compact that founded the Circle — one of the earliest experiments with agrimagic. Or, well … a replica thereof. I believe that by applying agrimagical developments of the last millennia, we can replicate at scale what our ancestors thought possible only by archwizards. In this case … a wand capable of massively accelerating plant growth! Use it to ensnare evil, or bring abundance with you wherever you go. Be aware: this prototype must spend a week planted in soil before regaining its charge.
> >
> > And secondly …
>
> He lifts a small terrarium, resembling the one he carries with him, but cover in a glass dome and bound to sturdy metal chain.
>
> > This, I believe, is my finest work. Inside is the bulb of the rare Aenarus plant, which converts sunlight into concentrated life energy . The terrarium is made of durable Coran glass, and the base is my own design: a blend of mechanical and agrimagical technology that stores this life energy and transmutes it to something your body can use. As long as the device's wielder is attuned to it, whenever the plant is in direct sunlight, all attempts to heal the wielder's body will prove incredibly effective. The base can store a single charge of this energy, which is restored when the plant next spends an hour in the sunlight.

Eamon hands over:

- A  **100**
- A [[Wand of Plant Growth]]
- The [[Terrarium of Life Energy]]

He is happy to answer questions about the devices, and to discuss the future of the Redrak Fields.

Before he departs with Rala, unless the party [[To Judge and Be Judged]], also read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Now, I believe it is just about time for me to gather the Circle and put our many capable minds together on finding a way to feed the Plateau. But before I do, I have an additional trinket to bestow, this time upon one such capable mind …
>
> Eamon pulls an pin from his pocket bearing the mark of the Agrimage Circle — identical to the one he wears on his jacket — and holds it out to Rala.
>
> > In recognition of your bravery, wits, wisdom, and service to the people and ecosystem of the Arctus Plateau by warning us of this forthcoming danger and assisting these talented heroes in investigating and resolving it … I hereby name you a full agrimage of the Circle!
>
> As Rala stares on stunned, Eamon approaches and pins it to the young agrimage's tabard.
>
> > I encourage you to apply those same wits and wisdom to tackling the problem we now face, and have no doubt that wherever you wander, your work will be instrumental in solving it.
>
> Rala nods with sheepish enthusiasm, then turns to look at you all, clear gratitude in his eyes for accompanying him on his journey and helping bring him to this moment.

### The Agrimages' Judgement

Once Terrane has departed, if the party [[Braving the Elements]], read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As Eamon turns towards you, the set of his jaw makes it clear Terrane's feelings are not shared. When he speaks, his tone is businesslike.
>
> > By the terms of our compact, I owe you payment for services rendered, and an artifact from the Circle's reliquary. Or, in this case, a replica thereof.
>
> He hands you gnarled wooden wand, split into spindly roots at its bottom end.
>
> > Though I suppose it is now irrelevant, I began my research into alternatives to such an uncomfortable bargain the moment Terrane shared the truth of the compact. In so doing, I developed a prototype replica of one of the first agrimagical artifacts ever discovered — a wand which causes explosive plant growth. Proof positive, in my opinion, that we would have been more than capable of applying the last millennia of agrimagical innovation to solving our problem without siphoning a creature against its will. I suppose its now irrelevant. The prototype is yours, if you wish.
> >
> > Be aware that it contains no inherent well of magical energy — it stores one charge, and it must spend a week planted in soil to restore it.

Eamon hands over a  **100** and a [[Wand of Plant Growth]]. He will answer questions about the wand, and discuss his reservations with the compact — though his attitude remains frosty, and his low opinion of the party lasts well into future.

Rala, meanwhile, approaches the party for a quiet conversation.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The young agrimage apprentice pulls you aside, and speaks in hushed tones.
>
> > Please, don't take Eamon's demeanor too harshly. He only wishes the best for all living beings, and it is a heavy burden to carry, especially as the head of the Circle. While I … share some of his discomfort with what we have done to Slaith, I must admit I am grateful that your actions mean my family can continue a way of life that has brought them great purpose and satisfaction — not to mention guaranteed that the Plateau will not face a historic famine. Those are no small accomplishments. I may not have much to my name and therefore much to offer, but were we ever to cross paths again, you can consider me a friend.

### House Lilifeld's Gratitude

Once the agrimages have departed, [[Krafton Lilifeld]] approaches the party jovially if either:

- The party [[Braving the Elements]].
- The party [[To Judge and Be Judged]].

In such case, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Krafton Lilifeld approaches your party from behind, clapping hands on the backs of the two of you closest to him.
>
> > **Excellent** work heroes, you really showed that vile creature what-for! Not to mention handled that little compact quite deftly, I must say. Not easy to steer such strong personalities as Eamon and that strange sage towards a solution all are happy with … or, well, will tolerate. Let's just say I wouldn't want to be across the negotiating table from you! As for the **dinner** table, however …
>
> Krafton reaches into a pocket, and hands each of you a small paper ticket, filigreed in gold.
>
> > In recognition of all you've done to keep the food flowing, please take these little tokens of my appreciation. Simply show them in any House Lilifeld restaurant across Ordain, the Plateau, and beyond, and you eat for free. Or, well, it will be charged to my estate — I would never dare to short my house's fine proprietors — but, for you: all the same! Enjoy the very best your bravery has allowed to grow, at your leisure … and know that you have an ally in my house!

Krafton hands each of them a [[Lilifeld Meal Ticket]] before taking his leave.

### House Lilifeld's Judgement

Once the agrimages have departed, [[Krafton Lilifeld]] approaches the party in a huff if they neither [[Braving the Elements]] nor [[To Judge and Be Judged]]. In such case, read or paraphrase:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Krafton Lilifeld approaches you, arms crossed over his chest.
>
> > Well! I suppose that's that! We better hope that these agrimages are as capable as Mr. Mariflor believes they are, or else we'll be looking at a lot of empty tables. If it were me, I wouldn't have chosen some foul beast over the prosperity of the Arctus Plateau, but I suppose that's why you're the adventurers and I'm the head of an Ordani trading house.
>
> As makes to leave, he speaks as if to himself, but conspicuously loudly … and conspicuously lacking an invitation to stay in touch.
>
> > Best make haste back to Ordain … we must begin stockpiling, reallocating distribution, potentially even rationing … my, what a pickle we're now in …
