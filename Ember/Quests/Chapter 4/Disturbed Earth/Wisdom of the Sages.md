# Wisdom of the Sages

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event occurs when the party visits the [[Earthen Henge]] after completing the [[Dredging for Answers]] Outcome of [[Dredging for Answers]]. In this Event, the party calls forth [[Terrane]], the Korvathi sage of [[Cora]] responsible for the supernatural fertility of the [[Redrak Fields]]. By calling forth Terrane and conversing with her and the agrimages, the characters can:
>
> - Work with [[Eamon Mariflor]] to prepare and perform the ritual.
> - Become embroiled in negotiation and discussion with the otherworldly Terrane.
> - Learn the true history of the compact between Terrane and the [[Agrimage Circle]] and its dependence on siphoning [[Slaith]], an imprisoned servant of [[Slinerak]].
> - Learn that the bonds Slaith's prison have become weakened and that Terrane can no longer sustain the compact without help to re-bind Slaith.
> - Consider the ethical ramifications of Slaith's imprisonment and the significance of the compact for the agricultural production of the Redrak Fields.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Earthen Henge]] Level of the [[Vista: Redrak Fields]] Vista.

### Preparing the Henge

As Eamon and Rala prepare the ritual to call Terrane, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Eamon begins unpacking various tools and magical components from his pack, but as his eyes return to the center of the henge, he grimaces.
>
> > I am somewhat embarrassed to say that this honored place has … not been well-kept. Besides the travelers who construct cairns in the hopes of receiving Cora's blessing, few have come here to tend it. We'll need to clear rubble from the center of the henge before we can activate the signaling glyph underneath it.
> >
> > If you could move those stones aside while Rala and I ready the ritual, I'd much appreciate it. Be careful as you do so … gravity behaves quite unpredictably here.

Before the ritual can be performed, the central depression of the henge must be cleared of rubble. Doing so is straightforward and easy — doing so *safely* is significantly more challenging.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### A Rocky Start
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Athletics (DC 16)** check can keep a firm grip on the boulders as localized inversions of gravity cause them to buck, kick, and fly away.
>
> Any character who fails this check must withstand the **Boulderfall (Hazard 12, Reflex, Bludgeoning)** as a boulder collides with them at high speed, before it bounces and rolls across the cliffside.
>
> Fully clearing the rubble merely requires attempting three such checks — regardless of success. These checks may be spread across any number of characters.

### Calling Forth Terrane

Calling Terrane requires a difficult magical rite, most easily performed by a large group of agrimages sworn to secrecy every three centuries. However, in the interest of time and given that the other agrimage leaders are dealing with disasters elsewhere, Eamon and Rala are the only agrimages available. As such, they invite the party to assist in the ritual.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### The Rite Steps
>
> Performing the most effective ritual requires two steps. If either step is performed incorrectly, it causes negative side effects during the calling of Terrane.
>
> **Repairing the Symbol**
>
> The Coran symbol on the floor plate is damaged by centuries of wear. It must be carved to precise specifications, with smooth, even curves that match the existing segments, before these grooves are filled with mud from Lake Orial. Eamon can supply a set of [[Scrivener's Toolkit]], but lacks the skills to use them himself.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Science (DC 14)** check can repair the gaps in the symbol to the required precision. This check may be re-attempted once, after which too much stone has been chipped away for success to be possible.
>
> - **Knowledge: Crafts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> **Performing the Ritual**
>
> The ritual is best performed with three magic users, who serve as conduits for the Coran energy the ritual brings forth. After painting symbols upon their arms with the mud of Lake Orial, these three conduits must deliver a spoken incantation alongside precisely synchronized magical gestures.
>
> Any one character who joins Eamon and Rala in the ritual must make a successful **Arcana (DC 16)** to execute their part of the ritual correctly.
>
> - **Attunement: Cora**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character performs with such precision that it negates any consequences of any failure to repair the symbol on the floor plate of the henge, and the ritual proceeds as if no steps were failed.
>
> If the conduit character fails, any watching magic user can perform the difficult task of steering the Coran magic back into the conduit character via a successful **Arcana (DC 18)** check.
>
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> If the above steps are performed correctly, the ground thrums with a pleasant harmonic resonance, and bright green light bursts forth from the floor as Terrane is called and the portal opens.
>
> Otherwise, the portal still opens in a blinding burst of green light … but a localized earthquake shakes the rocky cliffside, and gravity flings nearby stones at high speeds in random directions. Each character must try and evade the **Stone Shrapnel (Hazard 6, Piercing, Reflex)** or suffer damage.

#### Cora Attunement: Summoning Terrane

Any party member who successfully contributes to the ritual advances their **Attunement: Cora (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

Terrane waits, her face an impassive carving of stone, as the party shares what they have learned. If while at [[Ushna Dredging Docks]], the party discovered and chooses to share the connection to the earthquake eleven years ago, or the association between the strength of the Slinerak's influence and proximity of the mud to the floor of Lake Orial, Terrane is verbally impressed, and her disposition towards the party improves.

Afterward, either way, she continues:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The woman snarls and clenches her fist, the crunch and scrape of it echoing off the rocky plateau.
>
> > There is only one explanation. You were right to call me. The corrupt monstrosity Slaith has broken the chains that once bound it.
>
> Eamon's eyes narrow in worry … and confusion. It is obvious from his expression that he knows not of what Terrane speaks. The great sage continues.
>
> > Listen well. It is difficult for mortals to comprehend the scale of the war on Cora. We have been fighting for millennia after millennia. Battles that last as long as your mortal lifetimes. To dedicate my own power to tending your lands when such overwhelming evil threatens **my** home is no minor request. However, when your predecessors called me a millennia ago, seeking my aid, I saw an opportunity.
> >
> > My kin had just crushed a powerful and corrupt elemental servant of Slinerak: the stalagmaw, Slaith. As long as Slinerak lives, killing its servant Slaith would only banish that foul beast temporarily — its essence inevitably returning to Cora and reforming its body. But underneath the corruption of Slinerak, Slaith still bore the rich earth of which it was originally formed.
> >
> > To fulfill the promise made to the first agrimages, I bound Slaith beneath Lake Orial. The magic that binds the creature also siphons nutrients — organic and arcane — from its body, and infuses them into the soil of the lake.
>
> At this, a haunted look grows over Eamon's face. His knowledge of the compact clearly did not include this detail.
>
> > So too does that magic filter Slinerak's corruption … or, it seems … it used to. It appears those bindings have weakened, and are on the verge of collapse. This cannot be ignored. Free on the surface of Ember, the scale of Slaith's destruction would be unimaginable.
> >
> > Go to Orial. Break the bindings, before Slaith breaks them itself. Bring the creature down, then use the silt of the lake to mark the beast with these symbols.
>
> Terrane open her hand, and with an earsplitting crack, fissures open in the rock beneath her, forming intricate arcane glyphs.
>
> > These markings will allow my power to reach across worlds and re-bind that monster. You must do this. You must save Ember, **and** Cora.

> [!info] Social
> #### An Earthshaking Task
>
> Having given the party their task, Terrane has limited time to discuss it with them before she must return to the battle on Cora. During this time, she can discuss:
>
> - Slaith is a gargantuan elemental, capable of draining life and summoning lesser acidic elementals. Its weakest point is the crystals on its head.
> - If Slaith is not re-imprisoned, it will aid Slinerak and threaten both Ember and Cora — and its body would cease to enable the Arctus Plateau's magical agriculture.
> - Matching the current enrichment of the Lake Orial mud requires a renewable source of the *exact* organic and magical nutrients unique to Coran elementals.
> - That she cannot fight in the battle against Slaith, because it is equally important she returns to the battle on Cora, but she can gift an [[Antigravity Stone]] to aid in the fight.
>
> Specific dialogue options for Terrane on the above topics are available below this block.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 14)** check determines that Terrane is speaking truthfully … but that her stubbornness means she is unlikely to have even *considered* the possibility of alternatives to her plan.
>
> #### A Fundamental Responsibility
>
> Anyone who continually presses Terrane on alternatives to siphoning Slaith and makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 14)** check can push Terrane to admit that her own body could provide the same nutrients to the Lake Orial soil. However, this would require she to abandon the fight on Cora and functionally imprison herself in Slaith's place — a concept she is extremely resistant to.
>
> To have any chance of convincing Terrane to take Slaith's place, at least one of the following persuasion criteria must be met:
>
> - The party impressed Terrane via the summoning ritual or investigation of the corruption.
> - The party directly invokes Terrane's duty to fulfill the terms of the compact.
> - The party invokes the utility of the Plateau's people in the overall fight against evil.
>
> From there, a character must make a successful **Diplomacy (DC 18)** check to convince Terrane to take Slaith's place, at which point Terrane admits she is duty-bound to uphold the compact, and swears to find a worthy subordinate to take her position on Cora, then submerge herself under the lake after Slaith is defeated.
>
> - **At least two persuasion criteria are met:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About Slaith?
>
> **A:**
>
> > A stalagmaw: a gargantuan elemental of earth and decay. The size of a building, capable of crushing lesser creatures and draining their very life force — not to mention summoning legions of vile acidic minions to its aid. The first time we defeated it, we found the best strategy was to mount the foul beast, and strike directly at the crystals on its head.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About alternatives to imprisoning Slaith?
>
> **A:**
>
> > As long as Slinerak lives, Slaith itself cannot die. Slay the beast, and its essence will only return to Cora, making the war against Slinerak that much more difficult. And were Slinerak to conquer Cora, the consequences for Ember go far beyond anything Slaith could do on its own. Take it from someone who has witnessed Slinerak's awful power: this binding is a necessity.
> >
> > And on top of that … is life on your precious Plateau not built on that which Slaith's imprisonment enables? What exactly would you suggest in its place?

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About alternatives to siphoning Slaith?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Magic on the scale you seek is no small feat. Even if it was not vitally necessary to keep Slaith contained … the organic and arcane nutrients Slaith's body supplies are unique to Coran elementals. Without such an eternal, renewable source, your little continent could not possibly sustain itself.

Note that a combination of checks in (and immediately before) [[Wisdom of the Sages]] above allow the party to notice what Terrane is *not* considering about alternatives, and press her for additional information.

However, if you wish to beseech the aid of Cora itself, simply gather a few small stones and build a cairn upon the ledge of this henge. Additionally, I offer you this.

>
> > Once per dawn, grasp this and call upon its power, and Cora's influence shall lift you far into the air. Be warned: this gravitational disturbance only sends you one way, so have somewhere to land. Like, for example … the head of the great beast Slaith.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> She hands you a shard of night-black obsidian, tapering to a tip so sharp it glints in the light.
>
> > Go now. Drive this into the bed of the lake, and it will tear Slaith's bindings free completely.
> >
> > We do not know how long it will take Slaith to break those bindings on its own, but we cannot afford for it to happen without anyone there to take the foul creature down. Defeat it, and bind it.

#### Akon Attunement: Terrane Taking Slaith's Place

If the party tries to convince Terrane to take Slaith's place under Lake Orial, any character who advocated for it advances their **Attunement: Akon (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### Debriefing with the Agrimages

Eamon and Rala are openly troubled by this turn of events. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As Terrane steps through and the magic of the portal dims, Eamon and Rala stare on in shocked silence, still struggling to take in what they have learned. Rala speaks first.
>
> > We need to head for the lake immediately. I couldn't live with myself if that monster were to break free and attack my family, and I wasn't there to stop it.
>
> He pauses for a moment, hesitant to speak his next words.
>
> > Still … I can't help but wonder if binding it once again is not … wrong. How can we build our lives on this creature's suffering, and claim to live in harmony with the natural world? There must be another way.
>
> Eamon's reply echoes the young apprentice's concerns.
>
> > I had no idea this was how Terrane chose to fulfill the compact. Surely our predecessors didn't either.
> >
> > I worry that replicating magic on the same scale as a great Korvathi sage would be impossible … but is it not our duty as agrimages to do so?
> >
> > Regardless, that seems a problem for tomorrow. Right now, we must reach the lake. Will you all join us? The Circle would thank you with every resource we can muster; I can't think of a more worthy cause.

### Blessing of Cora

Before departing, any number of party members may beseech Cora for its blessing by constructing a cairn like those initially found on the ledge of the Earthen Henge.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Coran Cairns
>
> Any party member who stacks a few small stones on the ledge of the Earthen Henge receives the [[Blessing of Cora]]. This act requires no particular skill — in fact, it feels as if gravity acts strangely, making such cairns particularly easy to construct, and difficult to knock over.
