# Dredging for Answers

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration Event occurs when the party visits [[Ushna Dredging]] at the request of [[Eamon Mariflor]] following the events of [[Circling the Cause]]. In this Event, the party investigates the dredging operation responsible for harvesting mystical [[Lake Orial]] mud, the magical and nutrient-rich silt essential to the agricultural productivity of the [[Redrak Fields]]. By exploring the docks, the party can:
>
> - Speak with [[Rala Ushna]] and other Ushna family members to learn about the docks' history.
> - Council Rala on a family disagreement between him and his kin.
> - Gather physical and historical evidence relating to environmental disturbance across the Redrak Fields.
> - Fulfill Eamon's request by using the dredging equipment to collect a [[Barrel of Lake Orial Mud]].
>
> #### Area Walkthrough
>
> Exploration of this Event takes place on [[Ushna Dredging Docks]] Area Map. A complete description of its contents can be found in the Area Walkthrough, [[Ushna Dredging Docks]].
>
> The party begins their exploration of the area outside of the docks, on the [[Dock Road]].

### Ushna Family Reunion

Bayar Ushna (Lawful Neutral, Bejak Kivahr, he/him) is the father of Rala Ushna. He has a caring but stern demeanor. When he sets his sights upon Rala — for the first time in years — read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> When the tall, muscular Kivahr man notices you all, his eyes settle quickly upon Rala, and a pained smile crosses his face. He invites the young agrimage in for a hug, and Rala sheepishly moves into the embrace. The man speaks:
>
> > Rala my boy, it is good to see you back in the family home. It has been far too long. You've been well-missed, not just by myself, but your siblings, and the family at large. Even the visiting caravanners ask after that bright little boy who used to bother them for stories.
>
> As the embrace ends, Rala sighs.
>
> > It's good to see you too, father. I have missed everyone as well. But my work has kept me quite busy, and —
>
> The Kivahr cuts him off.
>
> > Too busy to visit your old man?
>
> Rala inhales sharply, then whispers urgently to his father. The two begin whispering back and forth to each other, the sounds growing sharper and more rapid in clear, harried frustration. Finally, Bayar huffs, and strides out of the office, face set in a stony mask. Rala turns to you all, looking exhausted.
>
> > I apologize. My family life is … complicated. In fact … could I ask you for some advice?

> [!info] Social
> #### Between Two Worlds
>
> Rala is at a loss for how to navigate both his passions and commitments, and as a person used to solitary wandering, has few people to ask for advice. He wishes guidance from the party on whether to remain at the Ushna home, or continue his itinerant journeys as an agrimage. To that end, he will discuss:
>
> - Rala's father Bayar thinks the threat to the Redrak Fields and its connection to Lake Orial is more reason than ever for Rala to return home, and use his agrimagic to aid the Ushnas.
> - Rala believes he is of more use traveling the Redrak Fields (and beyond) to search for evidence, repair and safeguard the land, and collaborate with other agrimages.
> - Rala also believes that a traveling lifestyle connects him to his roots among Bejak nomads, but Bayar believes the best way to connect to his roots is to live among Bejak and speak with the Bejak caravans that regularly stop at the Ushna home.
>
> Left to his own volition, Rala will continue his life as an itinerant agrimage. However, he is young, impressionable, and earnest in seeking advice, and:
>
> - If the party councils him to continue traveling, he will do so.
> - If the party councils him to resettle here and practice agrimagic in his family home, he decides to settle at Ushna Dredging permanently after [[Terrane]] is dealt with.
> - If different party members offer him opposing advice, he listens to the party member who makes the highest **Diplomacy (DC undefined)** check.

#### Aura Attunement: Rala the Nomad

Any character who urges Rala to pursue his individual inclinations and continue traveling advances their **Attunement: Aura (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

#### Akon Attunement: Rala the Family Man

Any character who urges Rala to meet his family's expectations and resettle at Ushna Dredging advances their **Attunement: Akon (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### Analyzing the Mud

Once the party has acquired a [[Barrel of Lake Orial Mud]], mark the following Event Outcome:

`[[/outcome collected]]`

The party can also now begin their own investigation into the strange phenomena believed to potentially originate from the mud. Uncovering the origin of the issue is quite difficult, but a number of strategies can return a partial picture.

Taken together, the following clues all suggest that there is an acidic contaminant in the mud of Lake Orial, that quickly breaks down upon leaving the lake, but the contaminant is *reactivated* by the application of any magic to the soil — something likely occur when the agrimages use their magic to transform the silt into topsoil.

While this is likely not enough to derive the full truth — that [[Slaith]] is imprisoned under Lake Orial and causing the crop failures — it does suggest the issue is deeply connected to specific geography of Lake Orial, and that the contamination is as magical as it is natural.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### As Clear as Mud
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Science (DC 14)** check determines there is a strange dearth of the minerals that are purported to make this silt naturally fertile for plant life, but they find no acid or otherwise harmful natural substances.
>
> - **Knowledge: Alchemy**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - C**haracter has** [[Alchemist's Toolkit]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also discovers unusual salts that indicate the silt was *once* contaminated with an acid, before that acid rapidly broke down. In fact, the farther the analyzed sample has traveled from the lake, the more of the acid has broken down to such byproducts.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 14)** check identifies latent magical energies emanating from the mud — but the mud is known for its magical properties, and as such this is not inherently suspicious.
>
> - **Critical Success**: The character also realizes those latent energies are highly reactive to the presence or application of additional magic.
>
> A character who applies any magic to the soil opens up new lines of inquiry. No changes occur that are visible to the naked eye, but the following methods can reveal information:
>
> - **Arcana (DC 16)** immediately upon the application of the magic, to notice the silt responding with an unusual burst of magical energy … and if within eyeshot of Lake Orial, a barely-detectable emanation of similar energy from the lakebed.
> - **Science (DC 16)** or **Knowledge: Alchemy** (and close investigation of the silt) to find renewed acid contamination not present before the application of magic.
