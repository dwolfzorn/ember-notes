# Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Requirements
>
> This Side Quest occurs during Chapter 4, and begins while the party is traveling through the [[Redrak Fields]] biome. There are no specific prerequisites for this Side Quest to occur.
>
> #### Difficulty
>
> "Disturbed Earth" is designed for a party of Level 6 characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
>
> This is a Side Quest that awards 1 [[Milestone Progression]] upon successful completion of [[Braving the Elements]].

Unknown to all but the head of the Agrimage Circle, the secret to their magical topsoil that produces such abundant harvests in the Redrak Fields is a compact established a millenia ago between the agrimages and a powerful Korvathi of [[Cora]], the great sage [[Terrane]]. By the terms of this covenant, Terrane has continually enriched the mud of [[Lake Orial]] with unique minerals and magical properties, which the Agrimage Circle manipulates with agrimagic to produce unparalleled topsoil. The result is an abundant crop of wheat, bellstripes, olives, plumesprouts, and grapes. This elemental infusion also supports unique possibilities for viticulture, producing some of Aterica’s most noteworthy vintages of wine.

Recently, the consistency of the Redrak Fields' agriculture has become threatened and destabilized by sudden crop failures, and even more concerningly, the emergence of fast-growing and invasive obsidian vines that are sprouting forth throughout the region. The source and nature of these problems remains a mystery, and House Lillifeld has grown deeply concerned, sending its leader [[Krafton Lilifeld]] to Rortwark to confer with the Agrimage Circle to establish a plan.

The true cause of this crisis is the spreading corruption of an imprisoned stalagmaw named [[Slaith]], a caustic elemental of [[Slinerak]]. Unbenownskt to the agrimages, the sage Terrane — at war with Slinerak on her home moon of Cora — imprisoned Slaith beneath Lake Orial, and has been siphoning his body to provide the Lake Orial mud with the magical properties that she promised in her compact with the Agrimage Circle. Recent earthquakes — secretly resulting from Zerranyss’ excavations in the Shard Caverns beneath Ordain — have broken ancient runic seals of Slaith’s prison, allowing Slaith to extrude and spread contamination via the mud dredged from Lake Orial that the Redrak Fields' farmers rely upon for nourishment of their crops.

During their travel through Redrak Fields, the party will be pulled into this quest through a chance encounter with the traveling caravan of Krafton Lillifeld, escorted by his house's retinue and a Bejak Agrimage named [[Rala Ushna]]. They intend to resolve the growing crisis and are bound for Rortwark to confer with [[Eamon Mariflor]], Steward of the Agrimage Circle. Eamon and Rala require the party’s help to reestablish connection with Terrane.

Accomplishing this ancient Korvathi rite yields a desperate plan to save Redrak from Slinerak’s befouling grasp. In order to preserve and restore health to the Redrak Fields’ essential ecosystem, the party must release the remaining seals to Slaith’s prison, defeat the stalagmaw, and make a critical decision about what to do with the conquered beast — with far-reaching consequences for the Redrak Fields and beyond.
