# Muddy Waters

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat Event occurs in the [[Redrak Fields]] Biome, in any hex without a notable Location. It is substantially more likely to occur after the party has completed Chapter 3, and can repeat up to once every 24 hours until [[Braving the Elements]] is resolved. By battling hostile earth elementals that rise from tar pits dotting the landscape, the characters can:
>
> - Personally witness the mounting elemental menace afflicting the Redrak Fields.
> - Discover clues that point towards the moon of [[Cora]] and the nearby [[Lake Orial]] as sources of these troubles.
> - Confer with [[Rala Ushna]], if traveling together, about the evolving situation.
> - Protect the caravan of [[Krafton Lilifeld]], if traveling together, and be rewarded for it.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Tar Pits]] Level of the [[Redrak Fields]] Area Map.

### Krafton's Caravan Halted

If the party is currently traveling with the caravan of [[Krafton Lilifeld]] — first encountered in [[Through the Grapevine]] — read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The yarnac-pulled carts of Krafton's caravan struggle to navigate around the increasingly frequent tar pits. When the wheel of one such cart becomes stuck in the pitch, Krafton insists you move on ahead.
>
> > No reason to slow you down such reputable adventurers as yourselves!

If the players express a desire to help Krafton unstick his caravan, he informs them in a hushed whisper that those in House Lilifeld are a proud people — he insists they must be allowed to take care of the issue themselves, and the party should continue on ahead. Once the party has put even a small amount of distance between themselves and the caravan …

### Elemental Attack

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Operating the Tar Pits
>
> To begin and throughout this combat, enemies spawn from pits of living tar. These pits are represented by Interactive Objects on the Area Map. Use the **​** Interact With Objects tool and left-click on a living tar pit to bring up a menu with the following buttons:
>
> - **Open:** This button plays an animation which changes the look of the tar pit to show that it is active and able to produce elementals. Once opened, the two following buttons become available.
> - **Seal:** This button plays an animation which changes the look of the tar pit to show that it is no longer active and able to produce elementals.
> - **Spawn:** After dragging the [[Clodmarl]] and/or [[Mud Globlin]] Actor(s) from the sidebar into the **Spawn Actors** list on the object's menu, press this button to spawn the associated new Actors on top of the tar pit.
>
> #### Beginning the Encounter
>
> As you describe the beginning of the elemental attack:
>
> 1. Use the **Open** buttons to activate the tar pits, playing the associated animation for each.
> 2. Use the **Spawn** buttons on the newly active tar pits to spawn a [[Mud Globlin]] in each of two pits of your choosing, and a [[Clodmarl]] in the third pit.

Three pits of tar come to life around the party, assembling earth elementals that emerge to attack.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Two tar pits begin to boil, and waist-high black shapes take form within them before rising slowly out of the pitch. Even as the tar sloughs off, the bodies revealed underneath continue to roil, their figures made of dark-brown mud that churns even as they shamble towards you.
>
> A third figure forms and rises from the remaining lake of bubbling pitch, unfurling to nearly a story tall. The tar drips away to reveal plates of stone and of fists of clay, as the earthen behemoth lumbers toward you as sizzling acid drips off its body.

> [!abstract] Mud Globlin
> **[[Mud Globlin]]**
>
> Level 3 · Globlin Prankster
>
> ![Mud Globlin](../../../assets/image107.webp)
>
> This stout, slumping creature is made from dark gray mud that flows through its vaguely humanoid form in slow, heavy folds. Its surface bubbles and pops as if stirred by some inner heat or foul reaction, sending up wet burps thick with the stench of rotting plants. Where it drags its stubby limbs across the ground, the mud leaves a clinging stain that blackens anything it touches.

> [!abstract] Clodmarl
> **[[Clodmarl]]**
>
> Level 5 · Stone Elemental Juggernaut
>
> ![Clodmarl](../../../assets/image108.webp)
>
> This elemental has the rough shape and bearing of a person, though up close there is no mistaking it for flesh and blood. Its body is made from dense clay and packed earth, with plates of stone fitted across its shoulders, chest, arms, and brow like armor. Its hands are heavy, blunt masses of hardened soil reinforced with stone, and look more like a mauls than hands. Each step it takes lands with the dull weight of a walking statue.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Recognizing the Elementals
>
> Any character who who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 16)** or **Science (DC 16)** check recognizes the creatures as [[Mud Globlin]] and a [[Clodmarl]] — elementals of Cora. The Mud Globlins are behaving in a characteristically nefarious manner, but Clodmarls are typically peaceful wanderers, known by legend to even tend to the earth of the Redrak Fields.
>
> - **Knowledge: Elementals**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also recalls that Mud Globlins are corrupted by [[Slinerak]], the Coran Wild God of rust and acid, who wishes to conquer all of Ember.

### Krafton's Retreat

If the party is traveling with Krafton's caravan and it is the first time they have encountered this Event or [[The Root Issue]] while doing so, Captain Tyra Saulter calls out to the party across whatever distance the party has moved ahead of the caravan. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> In the distance, an armored woman who you recognize from your time with the caravan as Captain Tyra Saulter, leader of Krafton Lilifeld's guard, begins waving at Krafton and his retinue to fall back the way they came, and leave the mud-mired carts behind.
>
> She gestures to Krafton's retreating form as she calls out to you from across the distance:
>
> > We have to get him out of here! Take care of those creatures, and House Lilifeld will see you paid handsomely!

If the party is traveling with Krafton but they have already encountered this Event or [[The Root Issue]] while traveling with Krafton, instead read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Captain Saulter calls out to you from across the distance:
>
> > You know the drill! Take it down while we move the caravan to safety!

### Battling the Elementals

Unbeknownst to the party, the corrupt influence of [[Slaith]] is causing localized disturbances resembling strange phenomena that occur regularly on Cora.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Bottomless Pits
>
> Three pits of living tar assemble corrupted earth elementals and spit them out in waves, until the party manages to seal or otherwise disable the tar pits. To begin combat:
>
> - Two [[Mud Globlin]] burst forth from two of the tar pits.
> - A [[Clodmarl]] lumbers out of the other tar pit.
>
> At the beginning of each combat round after the first:
>
> - If there is no living Clodmarl, a new one emerges from an active tar pit.
> - Each active tar pit that did not produce a Clodmarl spits out a new Mud Globlin.
> - Consult the Muddy Waters Moments table below to inject a unique environmental hazard into the battle.
>
> #### Sealing the Tar Pits
>
> To win, the party must find a way to prevent elementals from forming inside of or climbing out of each pit, or otherwise rendering the tar pit inactive. This can be accomplished the following ways:
>
> - Freeze the pit by covering a substantial amount of its area with an area spell using [[Rune: Frost]].
> - Fill the pit with earth (or another inert absorbent material) approximately equivalent in volume to a Large creature (including the slain body of a Clodmarl).
> - Any sufficiently clever original solution.
>
> #### Mud Globlin Tactics
>
> The Mud Globlins rush the party to attack them and provoke them with taunts in guttural **Language: Lunix**. They act as [[Pack Hunter]] and will coordinate to flank enemies, using **Tumble** to maneuver in close quarters.
>
> - The Globlins can burrow through the earth, retreating with **Submerging Withdrawal** and erupting with **Explosive Emergence**.
> - They possess minor **Cast Spell** capabilities and will use **Earthen Fan** to spray multiple nearby foes with acid.
> - While they have Focus remaining, they will use a combination of **Distract**, **Scathing Mockery**, and **Dirty Tricks** to debilitate and harass foes.
> - If faced with a single opponent, they will use **Feinting Strike** in melee or **Strike** from distance with their [[Acrid Glob]].
>
> These corrupt and chaotic elementals will gleefully fight to the death and do not flee if **Broken**.
>
> #### Clodmarl Tactics
>
> The Clodmarls are determined brawlers who use a mixture of spells and physical brawn to subdue their opponents.
>
> - At range, they will position themselves to **Cast Spell** a **Earthen Ray** at a group of enemies.
> - Each turn they will advance to close distance towards their targets, using **Overrun** as soon as they get within range to do so.
> - In melee they will use **Grapple** to seize their opponents, **Strike** them with their caustic fists, **Headbutt** to leave them **Staggered**, and **Throw** them to assert battlefield control.
>
> If a Clodmarl becomes **Broken** or if it is close to **Dead**, it will instinctually try to use **Submerging Withdrawal** to retreat to a position where it can recover strength.
>
> #### Rala Ushna Tactics
>
> If the party is accompanied by [[Rala Ushna]], he fights with them. During combat, Rala:
>
> - **Cast Spell** like **Ray of Life** and **Vital Surge**, typically taking his time by using the Compose inflection to conserve upon Focus and using **Refocus** when necessary.
> - Will create an **Ancestral Grove** and remain within it to strengthen his allies.
> - Uses the Earth rune offensively, but will stop once he realizes the elementals he faces are highly resistant or immune to Acid damage, at which point he will **Strike** with his talisman to deal Poison damage instead.
> - Once per combat round, offers the party ideas for sealing the tar pits, such as *"Perhaps we could freeze them?"* or *"If only we had something to soak up all the tar …"*
>
> If **Weakened** or **Broken**, Rala attempts to move as far from hostiles as possible while still within range to support the party with spellcasting. However, Rala will never leave the party to fight alone.

At the beginning of each combat round after the first, roll on the following table:

| Range | #### Muddy Waters Moments |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | #### Tar Bubbles  Each tar pit belches a massive bubble of black slime that pops and explodes onto the surrounding area. Creatures within 10 feet of an edge of an active tar pit must make a **Athletics (DC 14)** or else suffer the **Burning Tar (Hazard 6, Fortitude, Health, Acid)**. |
| 2 | #### Hand of Tar  Living tar seeps upward through the soil in a 15-foot-radius circle, grasping at the legs of all characters within it. Affected characters must succeed on on a **Athletics (DC 16)** check, or else take **Searing Tar (Hazard 4, Fortitude, Health, Acid)** damage. Characters that fail the Athletics check are yanked @Condition[Prone] as the tar gripping their legs dives back into the earth. |
| 3 | #### New Tar Pit  The earth boils as a new active tar pit opens up, from which new elementals can emerge on this and future rounds. |
| 4 | #### Earthquake  The earth shakes beneath the party's feet. All characters must make a **Athletics (DC 12)** check or be knocked @Condition[Prone]. |
| 5 | #### Gravquake  Bizarrely, gravity seems to invert itself for a moment in a 15-foot-radius circle around the largest concentration of party members and their allies, lifting them 5 feet into the air before dropping them. Those affected must succeed on a **Athletics (DC 14)** check to land on their feet, or else suffer an **Unexpected Fall (Hazard 3, Fortitude, Health, Bludgeoning)** damage and be knocked @Condition[Prone]. Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 14)** check or who has **Knowledge: Cosmology** recognizes this as similar to the gravitational disturbances that occur on the moon of [[Cora]]. |
| 6 | #### Acid Rain  A sudden drizzle of acidic liquid rains down from the sky in a 5-foot-radius circle around the largest concentration of party members and their allies. Affected characters must make a **Athletics (DC 14)** check to duck out of the way, or else suffer **Burning Tar (Hazard 6, Fortitude, Health, Acid)**. Characters who succeed must move out of the radius to gain the benefits of a success — doing so requires no movement action, but does provoke Reactive Strikes as normal. Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 16)** check or who has **Knowledge: Cosmology** recognizes this as similar to foul and corrupt substances that permeate the moon of [[Cora]] due to the influence of the Wild God [[Slinerak]]. |

### Rala's Concerns

If Rala is accompanying the party, and they are encountering this Event for the first time, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Rala surveys the field before him with a look of earnest horror.
>
> > Just look at what's happened to the ecosystem here … imagine if this were to happen across the Redrak Fields. And that larger elemental …
> >
> > It was almost certainly a Clodmarl — a peaceful Coran wanderer. I've read accounts of Clodmarls arriving in the wake of disasters to **heal** the earth. What could possibly have driven it to such destruction?

### Investigating the Elementals

Regardless of whether the party is investigating this strange phenomenon on their own, or attempting to answer Rala's question, they can seek information via a number of strategies:

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Sifting for Answers
>
> The party can analyze what they witnessed via any of the following methods, if they have not already uncovered similar information during this or other Events in "Disturbed Earth":
>
> - **Wilderness (DC 12)** or **Knowledge: Plants** to reason that if such tar pits were to become a widespread phenomenon, the entire ecosystem of the Redrak Fields would likely collapse, and that this could lead to famine across the Arctus Plateau.
> - **Society (DC 12)** or **Knowledge: Alchemy** to draw a potential connection between the strange little mud creatures and the magically enriched mud that locals draw out of the nearby [[Lake Orial]] in order to fertilize the crops in Redrak Fields.
> - **Science (DC 15)** or **Knowledge: Elementals** to notice the uncharacteristic volume and potency of the acid trails left in the wake of the Clodmarl(s).
> - **Arcana (DC 15)** or **Knowledge: Gods** to identify — via the living tar and the acidic nature of the elementals — the influence of [[Slinerak]], the corrupt Wild God of acid and rust who rules over the moon of Cora. The character may also speculate that Slinerak's influence perhaps compelled the elementals to behave so aggressively.

If the party is accompanied by Rala and they have not yet completed [[Circling the Cause]], he concludes the conversation by urging the party to push on to [[Rortwark]] and bring their findings to the [[Agrimage Circle]], who Rala believes will know what to do once provided these observations.

### A Reward from House Lilifeld

If the party is traveling with Krafton's caravan, Krafton and his retinue catch up. Captain Saulter approaches the party:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The eyes of Captain Saulter scan tar pits as she approaches, watching for any signs that more strange creatures might still emerge.
>
> > Thank you for fighting off those wretched creatures. I hope you understand the need to escort my charge to safety, and even still, I apologize for leaving you to fight it on your own. As promised, we will amply reward you for the risk you took upon yourselves.

> [!info] Social
> #### On the House
>
> Captain Saulter offers a small chest bearing the crest of House Lilifeld, inside of which is:
>
> - Payment of **`[[/award 25 gp]]`** .
> - One [[Potion of Healing]] per time the party has encountered this Event or [[The Root Issue]] alongside Krafton's caravan.
> - A sealed bottle of [[Lilifeld Vineyard Wine]].
