# Visions of Doom

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration Event occurs as the party traverse the inner reaches of the [[Bloodwoods]] near the Doomsayer stronghold at [[Kadra Zann]]. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Stumble upon a hazardous arcane trap that has been left by [[Kyrban]] and the Doomsayers to thwart would-be interlopers from finding their stronghold.
> - Survive the arcane trap's pernicious (and potentially deadly) confusion effects.
> - Examine the remains of other, less-fortunate travelers who came this way.
> - Study the various components of the trap, including its curious amber wind chimes and a primordial altar dedicated to [[The Silent Pack]], discovering clues that point to Kadra Zann along the way.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Primordian Monument]] Level of the [[Vista: Bloodwoods]] Vista.

### A Curious Trap

The strange noises the characters hear actually hail from a system of magical amber wind chimes that have been left here by the Doomsayers, in an effort to both warn interlopers away from nearby Doomsayer holdings and ensnare potential resources for scavenging. If the party is careful, they can avoid becoming ensorcelled by the trap's magic, or at least become aware of it as they approach. Should the characters approach the source of these mysterious sounds or otherwise survey the area, read the following aloud:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> You find yourself immersed within a small clearing, where a weathered and primeval monument stands stark against the wilderness … Several paces away, sitting oblong on a tiny hill that's been tossed askew by the effortless march of time, a statue or shrine of dark stone is fashioned into the impressionistic likeness of an assembly of black cats. Meanwhile, a scattered array of amber ornaments decorates the trees nearby, no doubt left here by some unknown custodians of the black stone.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### A Careful Approach
>
> If the party is careful about their approach, they can potentially glean some information about the situation at hand before they find themselves fully immersed in it.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 14)** check is able to recognize that an array of amber chimes hung throughout the area are the actual source of the "ghostly song" that attracted the party to this place. Read the following:
>
> > You begin to recognize that the amber baubles which decorate the area are actually some kind of chimes, and that the ghostly song you heard echoes from the hollows of the intricately carven pipes they form. The longer you look at these tawny-colored amber chimes, the more their strangely nacreous sheen propels your mind to far-off places, like crystalized daydream.
>
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: Even from here, the character can also notice the presence of humanoid and bestial bones scattered across the floor of the clearing, covered in a blanket fallen leaves and undergrowth.
>
> In order to avoid the effects of the amber chimes altogether, characters must immediately prohibit the sound from reaching their ears. The characters have exactly one round to make a decision about how to handle this predicament.

If the characters linger here for any amount of time and do not expressly avoid listening to the amber chimes, they are subject to the effects of the Beguiling Chimes Trap as described below.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Beguiling Chimes Trap
>
> Any character who hears the ghostly song of the amber chimes for more than an instant (one turn) must make a successful `[[/save wisdom 16]]` to avoid the beguiling sorcery of the chimes. On a failed result, the character becomes affected by a random [[Amber Chime Behavior]] for a duration of 30 minutes. A creature must repeat this Wisdom save for every 30 minutes they spend here in the location while listening to the amber chimes.
>
> An affected character can't take Bonus Actions or Reactions and must `[[/roll 1d10]]` every 30 minutes to determine their behavior, consulting the table below. At the end of the duration, an affected character repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

|  | #### Amber Chime Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The creature doesn't take an action, and it uses all its movement to move into an adjacent hex. Roll `[[/roll 1d6]]` for the direction: 1, northeast; 2, east; 3, southeast; 4, southwest; 5, west; 6, northwest. |
| 2—6 | The creature doesn't move or take actions. |
| 7—8 | The creature doesn't move, and it takes the Attack action to make one attack, including either a melee attack against a random creature within reach or a ranged attack against a random creature within range. The effected creature uses its most powerful attack. If none are within reach or range, the creature takes no action. |
| 9—10 | The creature chooses its behavior. |

### Collecting Evidence

If one or more characters can successfully avoid the pernicious effects of the amber chimes, they can linger here to investigate the area for clues.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Surveying the Amber Chimes
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 14)** check is able to recognize telltale signs of Enchantment magic upon the various amber chimes.
>
> A character who casts the [[Detect Magic]] Spell is able to detect the presence of Enchantment magic upon each of the amber chimes. Meanwhile, the Shadowcat Shrine radiates a mixed aura of Enchantment, Evocation, and Illusion magics. Prolonged study of the chimes using Detect Magic will reveal them to have been the focus of a special, powerful version of the [[Confusion]] Spell.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 17)** check is able to correlate the amber chimes with the other details of the area to determine that this whole setup is an elaborate trap to either ward people away or confiscate their belongings when they perish here.
>
> - **Knowledge: Forensics**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> #### Exploring the Copse
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check while searching the copse is able to locate the skeletal and rotted remains of various travelers who came this way that were ensnared by the trap. Each character who succeeds on this check can roll on the [[Corpse Loot]] table to determine what they find.
>
> #### The Shadowcat Shrine
>
> Characters who wish to interact with the altar may do so. Read the following if a character comes within 10 feet of the altar:
>
> > This half-forgotten monument appears to be crafted from a single slab of black rock, shaped into the likeness of a clowder of tenebrous cats. Where you can actually make out the distinct form of a feline creature amidst the morass, you notice their eyes are made from glittering yellow jewels. The closer you get to this primeval slab, the more you notice it radiates a faint aura of preternatural cold.
>
> Any character with **Attunement: Primordis** feels a strong kinship to the altar, as if it calls to them for some kind of interaction. The character is instinctively drawn to the stone, and is compelled (but not forced) to place their hand upon the rock.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 13)** check can identify this stone monument as some kind of altar or magical artifact, but is unsure of its precise origin or purpose.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 15)** check is able to recognize this statue as a symbol of The Silent Pack, and understands that it serves as an ersatz altar for the enigmatic group of Casia.
>
> - **Knowledge: Fey**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Gods**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: Additional benefits that occur as a result of critical success. Set [dc] to the base DC of the check.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 13)** check can recognize that the base of the stone altar is fashioned from a slab of rock that bears overt Shent markings, as if it was repurposed from another monument or site.
>
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character is aware of a Shent monument in the mountains of the Bloodwoods, known as [[Kadra Zann]].
>
> #### Communing with The Silent Pack
>
> Characters who interact with the altar can communicate with The Silent Pack themselves, albeit through the esoteric methods of the [[Commune]] Spell. The Shadowcat Shrine has the following properties:
>
> - Any character with **Attunement: Primordis** can communicate with the altar this way, and can only use the altar once per week this way per point of Primordis Attunement. No matter how many points of Primordis Attunement a character has, they can only use the altar once per day.
> - A character with Primordis Attunement is magically aware of their ability to commune via the statue.
> - A character under the effects of [[Ruby]] can also commune with the altar.
> - Alternately, the Shadowcat Shrine can be used as a [[Spellcasting Focus]].
> - The voices of the Silent Pack provide answers that are somewhat dubious but truthful, and will provide the characters with the general location of [[Kadra Zann]].
>
> Additionally, any character who communicates with the Silent Pack using the Shadowcat Shrine must make a successful `[[/save wisdom 15]]` each time they interact with the altar to cast the [[Commune]] Spell. On a failed result, the character immediately takes `[[/damage 1d6 psychic]]` damage and has **-2 Banes** on Intelligence saving throws for a duration of `[[/roll 1d3]]` hours.

### Maevren's Remarks

If the party has established a rapport with their guide [[Maevren]], she may have some valuable remarks to offer during a conversation about the encounter here.

> [!info] Social
> #### Conversation with Maevren
>
> Despite having heard rumors of similar warning signs and missing citizens of Talei (or perhaps because of them), Maevren is spooked by the encounter here with the amber chimes and Shadowcat Shrine — details she has yet to uncover during her explorations of the [[Bloodwoods]]. If the characters are eager to discuss the encounter and any discoveries they might have made, Maevren is equally invested in the pursuit of more information or understanding.
>
> Topics that Maevren is willing to discuss at the moment include (but are not limited to) the following:
>
> - Precisely what is going on here.
> - The esoteric and magical nature of the chimes and shrine.
> - The presence of evidence here, including the appearance of repurposed Shent stone from elsewhere in the forest.
> - The party's next steps.
>
> Questions Maevren might have for the characters themselves include:
>
> > Have you seen anything like this before?
>
> > Is this the work of the Doomsayers, or even darker forces of the forest?
>
> > Where should we go from here?
>
> Maevren has no reason to mislead or withhold information from the characters unless they've been duplicitous or antagonistic to her in the past. Any character with a `[[/skill insight 13 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Deception (DC 11)** check can readily tell that the guide is being honest and forthcoming in her analysis of the encounter.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What's going on here?
>
> **A:**
>
> > This clearing might **look** like a nice place to rest, but I have a bad feeling about this. The trees have eyes.
>
> Maevren peers suspiciously around the tranquil glade as she draws her bow and nocks an arrow.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What about the chimes and shrine?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Those amber chimes are intriguing, to say the least. But no artisan of Talei crafted those; none I know, at least. They bear signs of arcane workmanship, and smack of the forest. And that shrine … the gaze of those hateful cats seems to follow me with every step I take. This place is strange, friends, and could be dangerous to outsiders like us.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Evidence?
>
> **A:**
>
> > That shrine feels ancient, yet somehow familiar, as if it were brought here from somewhere else …

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Next steps?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Whatever comes next, let's steer clear of any amber chimes we encounter in the future. I don't want to see us wind up as corpses on the forest floor.

### Concluding the Event

The event concludes when the characters move on, whether or not they have retrieved additional evidence regarding the secret location of Kadra Zann or not.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Next Steps
>
> Once the party is ready to continue, they can proceed in their search for [[Kadra Zann]], where the final confrontation with [[Kyrban]] and the Doomsayers awaits during [[Where Shadows Lie]].
