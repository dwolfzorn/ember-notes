# The Primordial Grove

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat and Social Event occurs when the party enters the [[Amerasp Grove]] after speaking with [[Avwynn Taol]] in the [[Arbore Sanctorus]]. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Witness Kyrban speaking with his followers and the prisoner, Cael, a [[Sanguinary Warden]].
> - Confront the Doomsayers and their thralls in combat.
> - Investigate the vespiary for clues.
>
> This Event is depicted using the "Amerasp Grove" Level of the [[Bloodwoods]] Area Map.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Remaining Hidden
>
> The party can choose to remain hidden and observe the situation by attempting a group challenge, with each party member making a **Stealth (DC 15)** check.
>
> - **Magical Effects:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they benefit from a magical effect (such as [[Unknown]] or [[Unknown]]).
>
> If at least half of the participating characters succeed, they remain hidden and can observe the following:
>
> - The hooded figures restrain a sedated [[Unknown]] and press her into the open seam of a [[Vespinoth]] pod.
> - The prisoner stirs against their bonds and manages a few hoarse words before [[Kyrban]] strikes them across the face with the end of his amber staff.
> - Once the vespinoth pod seals, he seizes the prisoner by the collar, presses his palm to the nearest black aspen, and vanishes with them.
>
> If over half of the participating characters fail, combat begins immediately; continue with [[The Primordial Grove]].

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Token Reveal: Vespinoths
>
> The two [[Vespinoth]] begin the encounter **Hidden**. To better see these Hidden tokens, hold down the Left Alt Key with Token Controls active. When combat begins, **Reveal** the **Hidden** Vespinoths to represent them bursting forth from the black aspens.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Kyrban's Escape
>
> If combat begins while Kyrban is still present, he does not stay to fight. On initiative count 20 of the first round, he turns sharply toward the hooded figures, barks a command, and begins dragging the bound prisoner by the collar toward the nearest black aspen. Kyrban presses his palm against the tree; the bark splits open like a wet wound, revealing a brief glimpse of red leaves and ash-black roots. Kyrban steps through the opening with the prisoner in tow, and the tree seals behind them.
>
> #### Using the Amerasp Swarms
>
> Each vespiary contains a single [[Unknown]]. These creatures begin the encounter as neutral noncombatants, but the characters can mobilize a swarm against the Doomsayers by making a **Wilderness (DC 15)** check as a Utilize action while standing within 5 feet of the creature.
>
> Throughout the encounter, the Doomsayers will attempt to mobilize the swarms against the characters too, to varying degrees of success (see below).
>
> #### Doomsayer Acolyte Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the Doomsayer Acolyte will use its [[Ensnare]] Bonus Action to &reference[Restrained]{Restrain} the character with the highest Strength score.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Doomsayer Acolyte will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, the Doomsayer Acolyte will cast [[Sorcerous Burst]].
> - When adjacent to an amerasp vespiary, the Doomsayer Acolyte can command an [[Unknown]] to attack the characters. Roll `[[/gmroll 1d6]]`: on a result of 2 or less, the [[Unknown]] attacks the Doomsayers instead.
> - If the characters are reliant on a single damage type, the Doomsayer Acolyte will cast [[Resistance]] to mitigate that damage.
> - If the Doomsayer Acolyte is &reference[Bloodied], it will either cast [[Healing Word]] to restore its Hit Points, or [[Fog Cloud]] to attempt an escape.
>
> #### Doomsayer Shadewright Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the Doomsayer Shadewright will cast [[Barkskin]] on itself as a Bonus Action, then cast [[Haste]] on itself.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Doomsayer Shadewright will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, the Doomsayer Shadewright will use its [[Multiattack]], alternating between its [[Quarterstaff]] and its [[Tenebrous Wisp]] as appropriate.
> - When outnumbered, the Doomsayer Shadewright will cast [[Conjure Animals]] between itself and its enemies.
> - When surrounded by enemies, the Doomsayer Shadewright will use its [[Vespine Form]] to transform into an [[Unknown]], relying on the creature's [[Unknown]] feature and Fly speed to reposition itself.
> - If the Doomsayer Shadewright is &reference[Bloodied], it will either cast [[Healing Word]] to restore its Hit Points, or cast [[Darkness]] to attempt an escape.
>
> #### Amerasp Swarm Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Amerasp Swarm]] will use its [[Unknown]] and [[Unknown]] features to engage the most vulnerable creature in combat.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Amerasp Swarm will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Amerasp Swarm will use its [[Unknown]] attack.
> - When struck, the Amerasp Swarm will use its [[Unknown]] reaction.
>
> #### Vespinoth Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the Vespinoth will position itself within 20 feet of another Vespinoth to benefit from its [[Hive Mind]] feature.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Vespinoth will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Vespinoth will use its [[Gnarled Claw]].
> - From range, the Vespinoth will use its [[Amber Sputum]].
> - If two or more enemies are within 40 feet, the Vespinoth will use its [[Primordial Buzzing]] Bonus Action.
>
> #### Interrogation
>
> If the characters leave a Doomsayer Acolyte or Shadewright alive, they may interrogate them: continue with the [[The Primordial Grove]] section below.
>
> If all Doomsayers have been slain, skip to the [[The Primordial Grove]] section.

> [!info] Social
> #### Interrogating the Doomsayer
>
> If the characters capture a Doomsayer alive, the prisoner refuses to explain Kyrban's plan and dies before they can be properly interrogated, biting down on a chemical sac hidden in their lower jaw. Before dying, at the Gamemaster's discretion, the Doomsayer may confirm that the jellied amerasp queens are taken east.
>
> Once you feel the conversation has been exhausted, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > The prisoner's jaw tightens, and you hear something click between their teeth. Thin black fluid seeps over their lips; their body goes limp.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Vespiary
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - **Jellied Queens:** If the characters inspect one of the jellied queens inside a slain vespinoth, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > Inside the slain vespinoth is an amerasp queen suspended in a thick, amber-red amniotic fluid. Her body remains intact, but changed: her limbs are folded tight against her abdomen, and her chitin has softened to a waxy translucence. The creature is spongy to the touch, like something gelatinous. Whatever the process is, it has prepared the queen for some later use.
>
> - **Unfilled Vespinoth Pods:** Several immature vespinoth pods lie rooted among the black aspens. Each pod is large enough to hold an amerasp queen.
> - **Sedation Tools:** A worktable holds glass syringes containing trace amounts of a sedative, and linen-wrapped hooks used to handle queens without killing them.
> - **Failed Implantation:** One pod has split open, revealing the partially calcified remains of an amerasp queen that managed to break free.
> - [[Doomsayer Trail Map (1)]]:A map marking marble oaks throughout the Bloodwoods (see below).
>
> #### Deciphering the Trail Map
>
> The trail map is depicted using 4 separate items. Each item must be interpreted to find the next stop on the trail. When the characters decipher a trail stop and travel to the designated hex, remove the old trail map from the owner's inventory and award them the next trail map in sequence.
>
> To decipher a trail map, the characters must attempt a group challenge, with each party member making a **Wilderness (DC 15)** check.
>
> - **Guide Present:** The character automatically succeeds on this check if Maevren or another survival guide is accompanying the party. Only one character may benefit from the survival guide. What constitutes a survival guide in this context is up to the Gamemaster's discretion, but ideally it is an NPC with extensive experience navigating the Bloodwoods.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> If at least half of the characters succeed, the party deciphers the map clue; tell them which hex to enter, then award them the next map item once they enter the designated hex.
>
> If over half of the characters fail, however, they do not solve the map clue, and must wait at least 24 hours before re-attempting the group challenge.
>
> 1. ⬢ s.3201.2898 (Starting Hex) - [[Doomsayer Trail Map (1)]]
> 2. ⬢ s.3194.2906 - [[Doomsayer Trail Map (2)]]
> 3. ⬢ s.3195.2909 - [[Doomsayer Trail Map (3)]]
> 4. ⬢ s.3194.2911 - [[Doomsayer Trail Map (Complete)]]
>
> #### Expediting the Process
>
> If you or your players do not have the appetite for a lengthy trail, or if the players are struggling with deciphering the map, you may have the map lead them directly to ⬢ s.3194.2911 after the first successful solve.
>
> #### Trail's End
>
> Once the characters enter ⬢ s.3194.2911, mark the Outcome below as complete. This will reveal Kadra Zann on the Region Map and deactivate the [[Slipping Into Darkness]] Standalone Event, allowing the party to reach Kyrban's stronghold.

### Maevren's Remarks

If the party has established a rapport with their guide [[Maevren]], she may have some valuable remarks to offer during a conversation about the encounter here.

> [!info] Social
> #### Conversation with Maevren
>
> If the characters attempt to share a word with Maevren about their visit here, she is keen to oblige them. Topics that Maevren is willing to discuss at the moment include (but are not limited to) the following:
>
> - Kyrban's abilities as a primal spellcaster and zealous leader.
> - The various personnel of the Doomsayers and their associated tactics.
> - The darker side of the [[The Ruby Trade]] on display here.
> - The party's next steps.
>
> Questions Maevren might have for the characters themselves include:
>
> > How could Kyrban have escaped so quickly?
>
> > What do you know about ruby production?
>
> > Where should we go from here?
>
> Maevren has no reason to mislead or withhold information from the characters unless they've been duplicitous or antagonistic to her in the past. Any character with a `[[/skill insight 13 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Deception (DC 11)** check can readily tell that the guide is being honest and forthcoming in her analysis of the encounter.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What about Kyrban?
>
> **A:**
>
> Maevren looks to the east, as if clinging to some desperate hope of spotting the mad druid along the horizon.
>
> > I assumed, with a reputation like his, this Kyrban fellow would be a real pain in the ass. He did not disappoint. We best be careful with a spellcaster like that. Zeal and magic are a dangerous match.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What about the Doomsayers?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Fey creatures teaming up with primal spellcasters … it's all a bit much, don't you think? I'm used to hunting a few beasts, the odd monster here or there, but these Doomsayers are something else. Tough opponents, if only for the range of skills employed by the assorted villains in their motley crew.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** The purpose of this place?
>
> **A:**
>
> Maevren peers around the grove with a look of disdain. She grits her teeth as she surveys the remnants of the Doomsayer operation.
>
> > The origins of "queen's ruby" are coming to light. The sheer number of vespiaries out here would suggest the Doomsayers have some pretty big plans for expanding their reach. And this might not be their only grove.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Next steps?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I think our mission is clear, even if the path remains obscured … Kyrban's head on a platter, as soon as we find his stronghold.
