# Corrupted Nest

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event occurs when the party discovers a naturally occurring amerasp nest while traveling through the [[Bloodwoods]]. The nest has grown large enough to enter and explore after its true queen was taken by [[Kyrban]] Doomsayers and replaced with a [[Grayling]]. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Explore an overgrown nest full of [[Amerasp Swarms]].
> - Investigate the colony's strange behavior.
> - Confront the colony's false [[Amerasp Queen]].
> - Recover evidence that someone is stealing amerasp queens.
>
> This Event is depicted using the "Corrupted Nest" Level of the [[Bloodwoods]] Area Map.

The wasps do not immediately attack unless the characters damage the hive, harm a wasp, disturb the resin figure, or enter the false queen's chamber. Otherwise, they behave erratically, but with occasional purpose, as if obeying instructions they only partly understand.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Observing the Wasps' Behavior
>
> Any character who observes the wasps and makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** check can deduce that their behavior is likely the result of a faulty or dying queen, and that their erratic behavior poses a threat to nearby wildlife.
>
> - **Knowledge: Beasts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Path: Primalist Disciple**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> #### Examining the Resin Figure
>
> If the characters examine the resin figure, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > The faceless figure is under constant — and often conflicting — work. One swarm may build out a new limb, only for another to dismantle it; materials ferried to its surface are discarded at random. The only thing that goes unchanged is the figure's face, which remains perfectly smooth.
>
> #### Exploring the Nest
>
> By entering the nest, the characters can glean the following details:
>
> - **Temperature:** The air is warm and thick with pheromonic musk.
> - **Larvae:** Some combs are filled with untended larvae, some still twitching, many more dead.
> - **Ill-Colored Resin:** The nest's resin is an unhealthy, mottled clay color.
> - **Swollen Galls:** The galls inside the nest are larger and darker than ordinary amerasp galls. Any character who examines a swollen gall and makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check can confirm that they are overdeveloped and malformed, not magically created.
>
> #### Confronting the Queen
>
> The "queen" of this nest is a sleeping [[Grayling]] in disguise; the original queen was removed by Kyrban. This Grayling is tyrranical in its new role, and as a result, the nest has grown out of control. As the Grayling dreams, it unknowingly commands the swarm to construct its likeness on the outer wall. It is actively preventing the colony from creating a new queen.
>
> The Grayling is &reference[Unconscious] as the characters arrive. Any character can sneak around the chamber and inspect it with a successful **Stealth (DC 18)** check; they glean all details featured in the [[Corrupted Nest]] section.
>
> If woken, the Grayling commands the [[Amerasp Swarms]] to attack. It reveals itself only when directly threatened.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Amerasp Swarm Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Amerasp Swarm]] will use its [[Unknown]] and [[Unknown]] features to engage the most vulnerable creature in combat.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Amerasp Swarm will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Amerasp Swarm will use its [[Unknown]] attack.
> - When struck, the Amerasp Swarm will use its [[Unknown]] reaction.
>
> #### Grayling Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Grayling]] will cast [[Darkness]] centered on the party, relying on its &reference[Blindsight] to navigate the magical darkness.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Grayling will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, the Grayling will use its [[Multiattack]] action.
> - If the Grayling exhausts its supply of [[Amber Dart]], it will use its [[Extract Dart]] action to replenish them.
> - If surrounded, the Grayling will attempt to disengage by casting [[Phantasmal Force]] and then [[Misty Step]] as a Bonus Action.
>
> Utterly loyal to Kyrban's cause, the Grayling fights to the death. However, if the characters can subdue the creature, they may interrogate it: continue with the [[Corrupted Nest]] section below.
>
> If the Grayling is slain and cannot be interrogated, skip to the [[Corrupted Nest]] section.

> [!info] Social
> #### Interrogating the Grayling
>
> The Grayling has no interest in explaining itself. As a Fey creature, it is immune to the &reference[Charmed] condition, and because it is willing to die, it cannot be persuaded or intimidated into plainly explaining Kyrban's plan. However, it may answer questions in cryptic phrases that contain kernels of truth. Its talking points are summarized here, and answers to likely questions are explicated below.
>
> - **The Grayling's Nature:** The Grayling was once a mortal like the characters, but considers its new Fey form to be an improvement.
> - **Impersonating the Queen:** The Grayling was left in the nest to keep the colony alive after the original queen was removed. Normally, the colony would produce a new queen to take its place, but this particular Grayling is unwilling to let that happen.
> - **Who it Serves:** The Grayling describes its master as a liberator. It will not give Kyrban's name.
>
> Once you feel the conversation has been exhausted, the Grayling bites down on a chemical sac in its lower jaw with an audible click. Read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > The creature's jaw tightens, and something clicks between its teeth. Its smile stiffens into a rictus as thin black fluid seeps over its lips. The creature spasms once, then collapses into itself, drawing its limbs to its chest like a dead insect.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What are you?
>
> **A:**
>
> A twitch ripples across the creature's face.
>
> > I was once like you, bound to this … slab of rock. Bound to a dying heart. But no more. I have been freed. Can you say the same?

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Why impersonate an amerasp queen?
>
> **A:**
>
> > My master required the true queen for his divine mission. I have been left to dream my master's dream in this little house. I dream of the cyclopean forest, the place of predation, within which we all — even you — would be boundless.
>
> The creature's glassy eyes darken briefly, as if turned inward.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Who is your master?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I shall not speak his name. For liberating me, he has my loyalty. And my life. You cannot take what has already been given.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Battle's Aftermath
>
> After the battle — or if the characters are stealthy enough — the characters can search the false queen's chamber for evidence of what happened to the nest. Any character who examines the chamber can glean the following details:
>
> - **Removed Queen:** The deepest wall of the chamber contains a queen-sized hollow, filled with ragged frills of hardened resin. Any character who inspects the hollow and makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** or **Science (DC 14)** check can deduce that an amerasp queen was forcibly torn from the hollow.
> - **Scuffed Floor:** The nest floor is covered in scuff marks. Any character who scrutinizes the scuff marks and makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** or **Science (DC 14)** check can deduce that a group of humanoids were here recently.
>
> If the characters discover one or both of the details above, mark the Outcome below as complete.

### Maevren's Remarks

If the party has established a rapport with their guide [[Maevren]], she may have some valuable remarks to offer during a post-mortem conversation about the encounter here.

> [!info] Social
> #### Conversation with Maevren
>
> Maevren is somewhat confounded by the encounter with the grayling, as she's never seen this type of fey creature during all her explorations of the [[Bloodwoods]]. If the characters are eager to discuss the altercation and any discoveries they might have made, Maevren is equally invested in the pursuit of more information or understanding.
>
> Topics that Maevren is willing to discuss at the moment include (but are not limited to) the following:
>
> - Precisely what is going on here.
> - The nature and identity of the grayling, including speculation about the creature's origin and motivations.
> - The presence of other evidence here, including signs of corruption from an exterior source.
> - The party's next steps.
>
> Questions Maevren might have for the characters themselves include:
>
> > Do you have any experience with fey creatures like this gray-skinned intruder?
>
> > Time to pilfer the nest. Any volunteers?
>
> > Where should we go from here?
>
> Maevren has no reason to mislead or withhold information from the characters unless they've been duplicitous or antagonistic to her in the past. Any character with a `[[/skill insight 13 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Deception (DC 11)** check can readily tell that the guide is being honest and forthcoming in her analysis of the encounter.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What's going on here?
>
> **A:**
>
> > In all my years of prowling the Bloodwoods, I've never seen anything quite like this before. There is an element of chicanery here, have no doubt.
> >
> > If I didn't know any better, I'd say someone — or some **thing** — has been tampering with these amerasps, and this counterfeit queen is at the middle of it all. When we arrived, the amerasps here were suspiciously docile, considering they had an intruder in their midsts.

If the party has not yet experienced [[The Primordial Grove]]:

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What is this new creature we encountered?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Fey creatures are known to roam the Bloodwoods thanks to the otherworldly influence of Primordis here. But, until now, I've never laid eyes on a specimen such as this. This intruder was quite capable of armed combat, and I shudder to think what it was truly capable of.

If the party alread encountered a grayling during [[The Primordial Grove]]:

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Another one of these strange fey creatures?
>
> **A:**
>
> > It seems the fey warrior we encountered at the Doomsayer grove wasn't the only one of these creatures to turn up in the Bloodwoods. While a bit less aggressive than his counterpart, this imposter was obviously sent here on a mission to confuse and mislead these poor amerasps. Precisely why remains to be seen.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Signs of corruption?
>
> **A:**
>
> > A missing amerasp queen … scant tracks from humanoid outsiders … I'd say something rather suspicious is going on here, as if someone — a Doomsayer, perhaps — has corrupted this hive for their own mysterious aims. Inward and onward …
