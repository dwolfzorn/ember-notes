# Capricious Concerns

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event occurs when the party returns to the [[Arbore Sanctorus]] after confronting [[Kyrban]] at [[Kadra Zann]]. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Return Cael, the captured [[Sanguinary Warden]] — or deliver news of his death.
> - Report what happened at Kadra Zann to [[Avwynn Taol]] and [[Nimaelle]].
> - Receive Nimaelle's Blessing if they opposed [[The Silent Pack]], or face Nimaelle's judgement if they accepted the Pack's influence.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Vista: Arbore Sanctorus]] Vista.

### Returning Cael

Avwynn's reaction depends on whether Cael survived his ordeal with the Doomsayers. However she receives the news, Avwynn remains controlled in front of her Wardens. She allows herself grief or relief as appropriate, but does not get overly emotional.

> [!info] Social
> #### Cael Alive
>
> If Cael is returned alive, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> For a moment, Avwynn's composure breaks. She crosses the room and embraces Cael; the Wardens behind her lower their eyes, giving them a measure of privacy. Then Avwynn releases him, looks him over once, and her voice returns to its usual severity.
>
> > Do not get caught again.
>
> She nods to the Sanguinaries behind her. At her command, they lead Cael deeper into the Arbore Sanctorus. Avwynn then turns to you.
>
> > Thank the Heart. Know that you have returned more than a Warden to me. In Cael, I see a path to redeeming my own failure. I will not forget it.

> [!info] Social
> #### Cael Dead
>
> If the characters report that Cael is dead, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Avwynn receives the news without looking away. One of the Wardens behind her lets out a small cry before covering their face. Avwynn's voice remains steady.
>
> > At least we know what happened to him. I thank you for that. We will record his deeds in our annals, so that his name will not be lost to time.
>
> She pauses, mulling over a question she may not want the answer to.
>
> > How did Cael pass? Was it a good death? Did he suffer?

### Kyrban's Fate

The party must update Avwynn and Nimaelle on what happened at Kadra Zann.

> [!info] Social
> #### Report
>
> Avwynn submits the characters to a rigorous line of questioning. She wants to know:
>
> - If Kyrban is alive or dead.
> - How the characters discovered Kadra Zann in the first place (she will pay  **100** for the [[Doomsayer Trail Map (Complete)]], since it is the only known way to reach Kadra Zann).
> - What they saw at Kadra Zann.
> - Whether or not a threat remains in the ruins.
>
> Depending on the characters' answers, she may immediately dispatch several [[Sanguinary Warden]] to secure the ruins.

### Nimaelle's Judgement

Nimaelle waits for the characters to finish speaking with Avwynn before passing her judgement. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As Avwynn finishes speaking, she steps back. Nimaelle rises, stretching her forelegs, and walks unhurriedly between you and the gathered Sanguinaries. Her shadow passes over the floor like an eclipse, in the shape of something larger than her body. She breathes in, and her whiskers tremble.

> [!info] Social
> #### Rejected the Silent Pack
>
> If the characters rejected the Silent Pack's influence, Nimaelle gives them her blessing (see the Work in Progress note below). Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A gentle purring stirs in Nimaelle's chest.
>
> > The Silent Pack offered you a place at their side, and you recognized it as an invitation to despair. I commend your resolve in the face of temptation. Would that Kyrban had possessed the same strength … but alas. I bestow upon you my blessing, which is the blessing of Primordis. May the shadows of the First Forest hide your steps, and when we meet again, may it be in friendship.

Mark the "Friend to the Sanguinaries" Outcome below as complete.

`[[/outcome sanguinaryAlly]]`

#### Nimaelle's Blessing

In a future update, Nimaelle's Blessing will be an ongoing effect applied to the characters for a certain amount of time. For now, give each character the [[Nimaelle's Blessing]] item, and make a note to yourself to take it away after 14 days of in-game time have passed.

> [!info] Social
> #### Accepted the Silent Pack
>
> If the characters accepted the Silent Pack's influence, Nimaelle admonishes the characters for giving in to temptations of power. Once they leave the Arbore Sanctorus, the characters will no longer be welcome there, an ultimatum that will be put to the test in a future chapter. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Nimaelle bares her teeth and lets out a low growl.
>
> > I smell the shadow of the Silent Pack upon you. You have willingly joined your appetite to theirs. I do not know what they promised you, but know this … in due time, you will unravel in their shadow, be unmade by inches, and there will not be enough left for even the Heart to recognize in death. Begone from this place. And never return.

Mark the "Banished from the Sanguinaries" Outcome below as complete.

`[[/outcome sanguinaryAdversary]]`

### Further Questions

If the characters are not immediately banished from the Arbore Sanctorus, they may wish to speak with Avwynn and Nimaelle further. Answers to some likely questions are explicated below.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What happens to the Sanguinaries now?
>
> **A:**
>
> Avwynn folds her hands inside the sleeves of her cloak.
>
> > I will account for my failure before the grieving families of Talei and before my own Wardens. Kyrban was my student. I bear the responsibility for his crimes.
>
> Her voice falters for a moment, then steadies again.
>
> > But we will not dissolve in shame. Now that the shadow of the Silent Pack no longer haunts our every step, we will resume our mission. The order must heal.
>
> She looks to you.
>
> > You have seen more of this matter than most. When I come before the people of Talei, what would you have me say?

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What will become of Kadra Zann?
>
> **A:**
>
> Nimaelle's tail flicks across the floor.
>
> > It is an old place, one long hidden from the eyes of mortals. If anything good has come from this incident, it is that Kadra Zann has been found again. We will study it, discern its original purpose, and see if it can be bent to our mission. Until then, it will remain secret.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Will the Sanguinaries contact Cindarin Temple?
>
> **A:**
>
> Avwynn looks toward the arched windows and the red canopy beyond them.
>
> > Eventually, yes. The Cindarics deserve to know the origin of the substance poisoning their city. They deserve to know that Kyrban was trained here, that he broke from us, and that I failed to stop him before his heresy took root.
>
> She turns back to you.
>
> > I will take responsibility, but I will not apologize — not to them. They have made us devils in the public eye, and I will not allow this incident to vindicate their rhetoric. If reconciliation comes, it must begin with their understanding, not our humiliation. Tell me … if it were up to you, would you open the door to them?
