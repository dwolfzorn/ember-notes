# The Ruby Trade

The following contains information regarding the cultivation, production, distribution, and consumption of the drug-like substances known as [[Ruby]] and [[Queen's Ruby]]. While the manufacturing and distribution of standard ruby has become a relatively commonplace part of regional life throughout the last century, the appearance of queen's ruby is a novel occurrence and is one of the prime movers in the narrative of this Quest.

### Standard Ruby

The euphoria-inducing narcotic known as [[Ruby]] is relatively commonplace throughout the [[Arctus Plateau]], and its production is rooted in the [[Bloodwoods]] and conjoined with the hardworking people of [[Talei]]. Ruby production and distribution involves the following steps:

1. A female [[Amerasp]] wasp lays her eggs in the plant tissue of a black aspen of the Bloodwoods.
2. A Small-sized gall forms around each larvae, which becomes full of a resinous substance derived from black aspen sap and the otherworldly biology of the amerasp.
3. As the amerasp larvae develops into an adult, the gall "ripens" and leaves behind a raw, untreated form of ruby after the Tiny-sized amerasp hatches.
4. Ruby harvesters of Talei must gather the raw ruby from the gall within three days to avoid rot — if not before other Bloodwoods predators or poachers get access to the material themselves (such as the Doomsayers, see below).
5. Harvesters take their raw ruby to Talei, where the resinous jelly is processed into one of several easily-distributable forms like syrups, candies, and confections.
6. Ruby products are exported from Talei to Ordain and beyond via local merchants and business owners of Talei.

### Queen's Ruby

The pernicious substance known as [[Queen's Ruby]] is a more potent version of its namesake, and is concocted by [[Kyrban]] and the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] of [[Kadra Zann]] in a covert effort to lure people into their insidious schemes. Queen's ruby production and distribution involves the following steps:

1. An [[Amerasp Queen]] is stolen from a nest or hive, and a [[Grayling]] takes its place until a new queen is naturally installed by the colony itself.
2. The stolen amerasp queen is planted in a vespinoth pod to create a monstrous [[Vespinoth]]. During the three-week lifespan of the vespinoth, the amerasp queen jellifies within the monster's amniotic torso.
3. A jellied queen is taken to [[Kyrban’s Chancery]] in Kadra Zann, where it is exposed to the primordial sap of the [[Shadebranch]].
4. Using instructions from [[The Silent Pack]], Kyrban alchemically creates a batch of queen's ruby using distillates from the jellied amerasp queen and the sap of the shadebranch.
5. [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] and their [[Arcturian]] agents distribute queen's ruby in Talei and beyond via shady connections with local black markets (often disguising their novel substance as normal ruby, much to the chagrin of their customers).

Ongoing activities include the sabotage and confiscation of standard ruby from traditional harvesters of Talei, which not only provides additional assets for production, but is conveniently responsible for the chaotic obfuscation of Doomsayer motives.
