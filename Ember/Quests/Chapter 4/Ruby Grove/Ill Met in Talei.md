# Ill Met in Talei

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event occurs when the party journeys to [[Talei]] for the first time during Chapter 4. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Meet with a local leader named [[Proctus Caylas]], who serves as the Reeve of Talei, and learn about the frontier town's history and trade — including their production of the regional narcotic known as [[Ruby]].
> - Learn about the unpredictable hazards of the [[Bloodwoods]] beyond the city, a forested area known for its ever-changing growth and dangerous wildlife.
> - Witness and respond to the timely explosion of a ruby production plant in town known as the Riverside Ruby Works.
> - Identify the potential causes for this so-called accident before setting out on a proper investigation with [[Maevren]], one of Talei's most talented trackers.
>
> This Event is depicted using the [[Vista: Talei]] Vista.

### Meetings & Greetings

As the party arrives and settles in, they'll have a few moments to learn more about Talei while meeting the local leadership, a man named Proctus Caylas who serves as the Reeve.

> [!abstract] Proctus Caylas
> **[[Proctus Caylas]]**
>
> Level 1 · Unknown Unknown
>
> ![Proctus Caylas](../../../assets/image140.webp)

> [!info] Social
> #### Conversation with Proctus
>
> Proctus wears a kind demeanor, but is constantly wary of outsiders. A seasoned judge of character, he'll attempt to discern whether or not the party is a threat as he makes small talk with them (in an effort to learn their reason for visiting Talei, if nothing else). If the party is forthright in their conversation here, the aged and avuncular Reeve has no reason to suspect them of ill intent.
>
> Proctus is eager to provide a casual lay of the land to all friendly visitors of Talei, including the following topics of information:
>
> - The local amenities of [[Talei]], including options for lodging and provisions.
> - Aspects of day-to-day life in Talei, an [[Arcturian]] settlement like no other.
> - The natural and supernatural dangers of the [[Bloodwoods]].
>
> If the party seems especially allied with Talei's interests, Proctus provides the characters with the additional pieces of information:
>
> - The basics of Talei's [[The Ruby Trade]].
> - A brief description of Talei's tenuous alliance with [[Avwynn Taol]] and the [[Sanguinaries]] of [[Arbore Sanctorus]].
> - The recent arrival of shady black market ruby vendors, who have disrupted the town's trade and the safety of its people.
>
> If Proctus has any reason to be suspicious of the party because of their behavior, any character who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 15)** check is able to convince the Reeve that the party's reason for being here is trustworthy enough to share these finder details. Each character can also make this check to simply gain Proctus' favor in direct search of more intimate details, whether the party acts suspicious or not.
>
> - **Path: Cindaric Initiate**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Politics**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Trade**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: Proctus reveals in hushed confidence that one or more citizens of Talei have also gone missing in recent weeks, including a young trapper named Hodge.
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill insight passive 14 format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Deception (DC 12)** check can stoutly trust the harmlessly selective truth of the Reeve's words, and can readily tell that Proctus respects the duties of his station. All pleasantries aside, the Reeve seems to be concerned first and foremost for the town and its people, and can be seen casting protective glances at the townsfolk as they pass by.
>
> - **Culture: Arcturian**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character can reasonably assume that some of the Reeve's protective nature is a result of a criminal element of some kind, which he seems to be scanning the horizon for at all times. Part of his initial scan of the party, in fact, was an attempt to notice any concealed contraband. This is clearly a concerning issue (whether Proctus has mentioned it directly or not).
>
> Specific dialogue options for Proctus are featured below.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Amenities of Talei?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Despite our presence upon the threshold of the Bloodwoods, Talei is a town of comforts.
>
> The kind-eyed Reeve gestures about town as he speaks, starting with the petrified monument nearby.
>
> > The waters of the Founding Tree are cool and clean year-round, and folks from here to the Savannah are known to envy a drink from its wellspring. If there's a provision you need, for whatever the reason, Porden's Sundries has you covered. Ro's Cantina is a fine place for refreshment, and Confectioner's Lane has all the treats and candies a sweet tooth with a taste for ruby could ask for. If you need something else in particular, perhaps I can steer you in the right direction.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Life in Talei?
>
> **A:**
>
> > A life lived in Talei is not always an easy one. Nor is it always a struggle. Community binds this place together, along with a stout pride in our work and more a bit of hubris for the quality of produce we offer. Talei has earned its place on the map, and we intend to keep it, even if outsiders grow envious of our relative prosperity here.
> >
> > As the Reeve, I keep administration our local government on an even keel, and the warriors of The Scout's Lodge keep us safe from Bloodwoods incursion — whether natural, or otherwise.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** The Bloodwoods?
>
> **A:**
>
> Proctus peers off towards the northern horizon, past town and into the heart of the Bloodwoods themselves.
>
> > The myths and legends surrounding that dark expanse are many. But the realities of how the Bloodwoods came to be are far simpler than one might think. As the story goes, an epic battle was fought there long ago, and a mighty scar was left upon the land. They say the first Sanguinary planted a primordial seed on the battlefield that sprouted into the Bloodwoods, and the black aspens have dominated the northern swathe of the plateau ever since.
> >
> > Make no mistake, the Bloodwoods are as dangerous as their name suggests. Outsiders like yourselves would be downright foolish to journey into that hazardous, shad0w-haunted expanse without a local guide to keep you company. Simply put, you might never make it back.

If the party manages to gain the Reeve's confidence, the following dialogue options are also readily available to the characters:

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** The ruby trade?
>
> **A:**
>
> > The cultivation of ruby has made this town what it is today. And while there are those throughout society that would condemn the drug for its nefarious properties, most people understand that — like any substance — ruby is best taken in moderation, when its medicinal and recreational qualities can thrive without the dangers of toxicity and addiction. We don't advocate for substance abuse here. But we are proud of what we make, and the lives we help shape as a result of our efforts.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** The Sanguinaries?
>
> **A:**
>
> > We don't know much about that mysterious lot. Cindaric pilgrims are known to visit Talei from time to time, but they rarely venture into the heart of the Bloodwoods when they do. Every one of these sages or scholars has a story to tell, however, about the mysteries of the Arbore Sanctorus and the strange spellcasters who dwell within. But whether or not these tales are merely the stuff of legend remains to be seen.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Black market ruby dealers?
>
> **A:**
>
> Proctus instinctively casts a concerned glance about town as he speaks, automatically lowering his volume in the process.
>
> > Scoundrels and rogues, the lot of them. As far as we can tell, the first of their number started arriving about three or four weeks ago. And while they seem unallied, informants tell me there is a small (if informal) network among them. This so-called "queen's ruby" they sell is dangerous, and goes against everything we stand for. With each day that passes, I grow increasingly concerned about these black market vendors and their curious alliance.

### Riverside Explosion!

The party's meeting with Proctus is interrupted by the untimely explosion of the Riverside Ruby Works barge on the southern end of town, which they must quickly investigate if they wish to gather all worthwhile clues here in Talei. Read the following aloud as you activate the [[Barge Explosion]] Vista Level:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Before you can speak another word, a sudden and alarming commotion cracks the southern sky as some massive explosion rocks the Riverside Ruby Works to its core. Timber shatters and is hurled out into the river as an enormous plume of red-black smoke billows into the air. A few frantic townsfolk can be seen running into and out of the wreckage. Proctus shouts:
>
> > By the gods … Please, do whatever you can to help!
>
> As you rush forward to join a small cadre of locals who've assembled on the dock to provide first aid, you're joined by a hardy Arcturian woman with a muscular physique. After tossing you a brief nod of respect, she towards past the flames in search of survivors.

The Arcturian warrior is Maevren, one of the famed trackers of [[Talei]] and a local hero of high-standing repute.

> [!abstract] Maevren
> **[[Maevren]]**
>
> Level 1 · Unknown Unknown
>
> ![Maevren](../../../assets/image141.webp)

The characters now have a limited amount of time to react to the matter at hand. The sooner the party leaps into action, the more details they'll be able to uncover about the clandestine culprits of this nefarious sabotage. If they miss these extra details, they might be able to sleuth for them later; otherwise, only exploration of the Bloodwoods will do.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### First Response: Clues from the Burning Wreckage
>
> If the party decides to assist with the immediate rescue effort, they may be able to earn a couple of clues that will aid in their investigation; but the characters must survive the fiery hazards of the burning wreckage as they participate. Each character who participates must choose one of the following activities and contend with the associated skill checks and saving throws.
>
> **Aid the Survivors**
>
> Any character that chooses to aid the survivors must make a successful `[[/save dexterity 16]]` save.
>
> - **Use of appropriate Spells**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Failure**: The character gains one level of &Reference[Exhaustion].
>
> Failure: The character takes `[[/damage 2d8 bludgeoning]]` damage and `[[/damage 2d8 fire]]` damage from the raging flames that engulf the crumbling wreckage.
>
> Success: The character overhears some idle chatter from the survivors, including an anecdote about a curious encounter with the [[Corrupted Nest]].
>
> **Extinguish the Flames**
>
> Any character that chooses to extinguish the flames must make a successful `[[/save strength 16]]` save.
>
> - **Use of appropriate Spells**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Seafaring**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Failure**: The character gains one level of &Reference[Exhaustion].
>
> Failure: The character takes `[[/damage 4d8 fire]]` damage from a shower of hot cinders cast upon them by the raging flames.
>
> Success: The character spots some suspicious onlookers while battling the flames, and gains **+2 Boons** on attempts made to locate a black market ruby dealer in town (see [[Ill Met in Talei]] below).
>
> **Secure the Inventory**
>
> Any character that chooses to secure the inventory must make a successful `[[/save wisdom 16]]` save.
>
> - **Use of appropriate Spells**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Alchemy**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Failure**: The character gains one level of &Reference[Exhaustion].
>
> Failure: The character takes `[[/damage 2d8 fire]]` damage and `[[/damage 2d8 poison]]` damage from the smoke inhalation, and consumes the equivalent of one dose of [[Ruby]].
>
> Success: `[[/roll 1d2]]` extra doses of [[Ruby]] as compensation for their efforts.
>
> No matter what activity the characters choose, if the party offers their assistance at this time, the owners of the Riverside Ruby Works offer each character `[[/roll 1d2]]` doses of [[Ruby]] as compensation for their efforts (whether they succeed or not).

> [!info] Social
> #### Conversation with Maevren
>
> If the party was helpful at all during the aftermath of the explosion, Maevren is eager to share her thanks, and is willing to hastily discuss the following:
>
> - Her role as a tracker of The Scout's Lodge here in Talei.
> - The unseen dangers of the Bloodwoods.
> - Potential causes of the explosion here, and whether or not it was an accident.
> - Her suspicion of the black market ruby dealers who have taken up in town.
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill insight passive 15 format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Deception (DC 13)** check can readily trust the veracity of Maevren's accounts.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 15)** check is able to recount a tale or two of Maevren's accolades, and can confirm her status as a well-known and trustworthy hero of Talei.
>
> - **Culture: Arcturian**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character is also acutely aware that the skilled trackers and scouts of Talei like Maevren have the training and knowledge necessary to navigate the Bloodwoods with relative ease.
>
> Specific dialogue options for Maevren are featured below.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Who are you?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Well met, traveler. My name is Maevren. I serve the people of Talei as one of the dedicated trackers of The Scout's Lodge. We keep the town safe from incursion, and its people safe from risky wanderlust. If you aim to travel the Bloodwoods, you best accompany an experienced tracker.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Dangers of the Bloodwoods?
>
> **A:**
>
> She instinctively adjusts the scabbard at her side, which cradles a long blade of western design.
>
> > If the trees don't swallow you whole, the wildlife certainly will. The Bloodwoods are a dangerous place for outsiders. Most neophytes will either turn straight back to town or venture out to never be heard from again. You look like sensible folks; I'd hate to see you become another cautionary tale.
> >
> > Know this: the Bloodwoods are alive, and the forest's heart beats with the primeval blood of Primordis itself. Mischevious fey magic lurks behind every shadow. You'd be wise to heed my warnings.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** The explosion?
>
> **A:**
>
> Maevren takes a brief moment to survey the site of the explosion with a notable look of frustration, bordering on rage.
>
> > Something like this never would have happened before the advent of the black market in Talei. Something about these shady vendors doesn't sit well with me, and I'd be hard-pressed to believe these rogues don't have anything to do with the explosion.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Black market ruby?
>
> **A:**
>
> > It can't mean anything good for the people of Talei. If regulations exist here, they exist to keep the people safe, and to keep prices fair. This "queen's ruby" that has been going around has a bad vibe to it, if you ask me. I don't know why just yet, but I don't trust it — or the scoundrels who sell it.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Ruin on the Riverside
>
> Once the fire has completely subsided, the party can safely examine the smoldering wreckage of the Riverside Ruby Works, but the answers they can find at this time are somewhat limited.
>
> Any character who makes either a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** or **Awareness (DC 13)** check while searching in and around the smoldering Ruby Works barge is able to locate a couple of interesting clues. First, the explosion seems to have been sparked by [[Alchemist's Fire]]. Second, a threat has been hastily carved into the hull of the barge: "The woods are watching."
>
> - **Search for 1+ hour**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Forensics**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Crime**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also notices that a black market ruby vendor can be seen lurking suspiciously nearby, surveying the aftermath with a spyglass; but this shady character eludes any potential questioning and disappears before the party can catch up to him.

Eventually, the party is approached once more by Proctus, who has an offer for them in the wake of this so-called accident, as well as a suggestion or two for their next course of action.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As the wreckage of the Ruby Works smolders in the riverside breeze, the smell of sandalwood draws nearby, along with the steady clacking of Proctus' cane. The Reeve stands shoulder-t0-shouder with you and surveys the damage with consternation.
>
> > That smell of burnt ruby will linger for weeks. Gods know how long it will take for Kivvo and the team to rebuild the section of the barge that's missing … but I'm sure we'll bounce back from this ordeal in swift fashion, moons willing.
>
> He takes a deep breath as he scans the perimeter of the riverbank, along with the various dwellings and businesses near the shore.
>
> > It would be rather nice to know who was responsible for this mess. And you lot look rather capable when it comes to investigations. What say you? Can you spare some time for Talei? I can pay you, of course. We have five hundred gold pieces set aside for emergencies, and I'd say the sabotage of the town's largest ruby factory is cause for alarm.
>
> The tracker Maevren lingers at the perimeter of your conversation, tending to a few minor wounds suffered while extracting townsfolk from the fire. There isn't a speck of envy or suspicion in her eyes, but you do notice a trace of concern, like the apprehensions of an older sister. She offers you a nod of approval when you catch her eye. Proctus continues:
>
> > So, do we have an accord? What questions might you have before I equip you with some information of my own?

> [!info] Social
> #### An Offer from Proctus
>
> Proctus offers the party a 500 gp reward for their investigation, provided upon receipt of a reasonable field report. He offers the party the following pieces of information to help them start their journey:
>
> - The black market ruby dealers traffic in a drug called [[Queen's Ruby]], which is more potent and more dangerous than standard [[Ruby]]. Their operation and alliances are very suspicious, and they are among the top suspects at this time.
> - Talei has a tenuous alliance with the [[Sanguinaries]] of [[Arbore Sanctorus]] on the northern edge of the Bloodwoods. Perhaps the characters can seek out this legendary place in search of answers.
> - It is very dangerous to travel the Bloodwoods, particularly for the inexperienced. Proctus recommends that the party entreat the services of Maevren, lest they succumb to the biome's mercurial perils.
> - Proctus reveals in confidence that a skilled young trapper named Hodge has gone missing, and was last seen a week ago. His family are close friends to Proctus, and the young man's disappearance is a personal concern as much as a professional one. News of his fate would be appreciated.
>
> #### Maevren's Favor
>
> Maevren is willing to act the party's guide through the Bloodwoods if the characters agree to contribute 100 gp of their payment from Proctus to her and The Scout's Lodge.

### Picking up the Pieces

As long as the party lingers in Talei, they can attempt to gather clues and information from the locals about various topics of information.

> [!info] Social
> #### Local Information
>
> Any character who spends at least one hour to canvas town and makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** or **Awareness (DC 13)** check is able to locate one of the suspicious black market ruby dealers that Proctus and others have been so concerned about.
>
> - **Knowledge: Crime**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Intrigue**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Trade**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: Thanks to some inside information they've gathered, the character also gains Advantage on any social interactions with the black market dealer named Verno Kreed (see below).
>
> #### Interrogating a Black Market Dealer
>
> The party can coerce, extort, or interrogate one of Talei's black market ruby dealers to reveal more information that may help with the investigation. As luck would have it, the dealer they manage to track down is a shady [[Arcturian]] peddler named [[Verno Kreed]], who they find in the midst of his daily rounds.
>
> Verno Kreed always leads his conversations with a sales pitch (whether the customer is a returning one or not). Without provocation, Verno Kreed will extoll the virtues of Queen's Ruby as a more natural alternative to the over-produced, over-commoditized standard; he'll try to sell the characters at least one dose each.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 15)** or **Diplomacy (DC 15)** check is able to convince Verno to share some basic information with them.
>
> - **Knowledge: Trade**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Alchemy**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Crime**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: Verno also explains that the one time he caught a glimpse of a supplier during a dead drop, he noticed the cloaked figure was wearing a mask carved into the face of some animal or creature.
>
> This basic info includes the following:
>
> - Queen's ruby is culled from a "royal jelly" produced by an amerasp queen, and is more potent (and more delicious) than its standard counterpart.
> - Queen's ruby is processed by a supplier who lives outside of Talei, somewhere in the tangled depths of the Bloodwoods. Naturally, the identity of this supplier is kept secret for their safety and the continued operation of the trade.
> - According to Verno and his associates, the influx of "alternative" ruby vendors supports ideas of free trade and open commerce for the people of the plateau; attempts to regulate the sale of ruby are little more than authoritarian initiatives to control the people and tax their product.
> - Each batch of queen's ruby is personally tested by Verno or one of his associates when it comes in. The product has never been deemed unsafe for consumption.
> - If someone can't handle the potent effects of queen's ruby, they simply shouldn't take it. Verno doesn't sell to minors, and keeps his nose as clean as he can. But a free market means consumers can exercise free choice (something that Verno is all-too-happy to exploit).
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Intimidation (DC 18)** check is able to convince Verno to share more secrets of his suppliers, including this detail: the one time Verno caught a glimpse of a supplier during a dead drop, he noticed the cloaked figure was wearing a mask carved into the face of some animal or creature.
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 15 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check notices that Verno always keeps one eye over his shoulder, as if he's worried about suspicion himself — or as if he has someone else to appease or avoid.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 13)** check can tell that Verno is relatively honest with his forthcomings, but that some dark truth lurks at the heart of it all — a truth that Verno would rather remain ignorant of.
>
> Specific dialogue options for Verno Kreed are featured below.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Queen's ruby?
>
> **A:**
>
> > They don't call it queen's ruby for nothing, I assure you that, my friend. I offer a premium distillate of the Bloodwoods' most sought-after commodity. Harvested from the amerasp queen herself, this ruby is so potent, you'll never want to trifle with the normal stuff again. And the taste? You've never tasted a syrup so sweet. Fortunately, this moment of bliss can be yours for the paltry sum of five gold pieces.
> >
> > So tell me … how many vials would you like?

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Your supplier?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Tsk, tsk … there are some trade secrets that need to be kept safe, I'm sure you understand. You can trust that queen's ruby is manufactured by some intrepid natives of the Bloodwoods, using tried and true Arcturian techniques passed down for generations. Our suppliers maintain a certain amount of anonymity, and we are glad to indulge them. After all, it's hard to run a grass-roots business with … bureaucratic oversight. And there are, or course, the safety concerns associated with competitive markets in the drug trade … I'm sure you understand.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** The ruby trade?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Ruby isn't a mere commodity here in Talei. It's a way of life. And people deserve the power of consumer choice when it comes to the precise quality of that life. Thanks to the dedication of our suppliers and our customers alike, business is booming as far as queen's ruby is concerned.
> >
> > As for my clientele, folks from all walks of life have come looking for a taste of queen's ruby. After all, who doesn't want a nice distraction from the world's follies every once in a while? Sometimes, the woods themselves come a-calling, and the only good answer is a dram of ruby and a jaunt through the brush.

### Concluding the Event

The Event is concluded when the party leaves Talei, allowing them ample time to gather information and provisions from the locals before setting out.
