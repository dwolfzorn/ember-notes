# Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Requirements
>
> This Side Quest occurs during Chapter 4 as the party arrives in [[Talei]], a frontier town on the edge of the hazardous and ever-shifting [[Bloodwoods]] north of [[Ordain]]. There are no prerequisites for this Quest and it can be undertaken at any time.
>
> #### Difficulty
>
> The "Ruby Grove" Quest is designed for a party of Level 6 characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
>
> This is a Side Quest that awards 1 [[Milestone Progression]] upon completion of [[Where Shadows Lie]].

The prosperity of [[Talei]] depends on Ruby, a jam-like substance harvested from the galls of [[Amerasps]]. While ruby has become fashionable in [[Ordain]] as a recreational narcotic, its true importance lies in Talei, where controlled consumption helps locals endure the [[Bloodwoods]] [[Primordis]] influence. This balance has recently been disrupted by the arrival of black market dealers selling an altered form of ruby produced through experiments on stolen [[Amerasp Queens]].

Unknown to Talei, the crisis originates with [[Kyrban]], a [[Sanguinaries]] apostate who has gathered a splinter sect of Doomsayers in service to dark agents of Primordis called [[The Silent Pack]]. Kyrban's followers have infiltrated the ruby trade, using it to conceal a campaign of abduction and ecological corruption. Amerasp queens are stolen from their hives, locals vanish into the Bloodwoods, and the kidnapped are transformed into [[Grayling]]. Kyrban's work is meant to prepare the Bloodwoods for a permanent incursion of Primordis influence, centered on the ancient [[Shent]] ruin of [[Kadra Zann]].

During this Side Quest, the party first encounters ruby through The Most August Society of Aythorn, who invite them to visit Talei. Shortly after the party arrives, the Riverside Ruby Works barge explodes, drawing the characters into an investigation aided by [[Maevren]], one of Talei's most capable Bloodwoods trackers. The trail eventually carries them into the Bloodwoods and to the ancient [[Arbore Sanctorus]], where the Sanguinaries help reveal the nature of Kyrban's betrayal. From there, the party can pursue the Doomsayers to Kadra Zann, confront Kyrban, and decide whether to reject the Silent Pack or take his place as their agents.

After Kyrban's defeat, the party may return to Talei to report what happened and determine how the town responds to the corruption of its ruby trade. They may also return to the Arbore Sanctorus to deliver news of Kyrban's fate, return or mourn the captured Sanguinary Cael, and receive [[Nimaelle]] judgment. Depending on their choices, the characters may leave as friends of the Sanguinaries, or as willing agents of the Silent Pack whose presence will no longer be tolerated within the Arbore Sanctorus.
