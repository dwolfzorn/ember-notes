# Where Shadows Lie

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event occurs when the party journeys to [[Kadra Zann]] to confront [[Kyrban]] and the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]]. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Scout the [[Courtyard]] of Kadra Zann for an alternate method of entry, and make their choice about how best to proceed: the front gate, a side stair to the [[Vespiary]], or a tunnel in the ground that leads to the [[Amerasp Caves]].
> - Explore Kadra Zann's three-story interior for clues about Kyrban's plot to bring [[Primordis]] to Ember via the magic of Bloodwoods.
> - Confront Kyrban and his minions in their inner sanctum, where a nefarious [[Shadebranch]] is being used to create a new [[Grayling]] in a dark ritual.
> - Decide whether or not to ally themselves with Kyrban's tenebrous and furtive patrons: [[The Silent Pack]].
>
> #### Area Walkthrough
>
> Exploration of this Event takes place on [[Kadra Zann]] Area Map. A complete description of its contents can be found in the [[Kadra Zann]] Area Walkthrough.

### Exploring the Shent Ruins

Once the party reaches Kyrban's stronghold, they must find a way inside and explore its shadow-haunted reaches for signs of the mad druid and his master plan. A full description of the [[Kadra Zann]] Location can be found in the [[Kadra Zann]] Area Walkthrough.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Scouting the Perimeter
>
> Three primary methods of entry are available to the characters once they reach the [[Courtyard]] area:
>
> - The front gates of Kadra Zann immediately south of the Courtyard area.
> - A rickety stairway on the eastern exterior of the temple that leads to the [[Vespiary]] on the Upper Level.
> - A concealed tunnel entrance connected to the [[Amerasp Caves]] in the lower reaches.

The party's precise method of entry determines the results of the following Attunement progression moments.

#### Ragen Attunement: The Direct Approach

If the party infiltrates Kadra Zann via a direct frontal assault, each character advances their **Attunement: Ragen (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

#### Primordis Attunement: The Clandestine Approach

If the party infiltrates Kadra Zann unseen via the secret hive entrance or some other covert method, each character advances their **Attunement: Primordis (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

### The Pack's Offer

During the party's exploration of the [[Sanctuary]] chamber, the characters can make contact with [[The Silent Pack]] — the powerful group of [[Casia]] on [[Primordis]] who guide Kyrban and the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] from afar — and must decide whether or not to accept their enticing offer for mercurial otherworldly power.

#### Primordis Attunement: One with the Pack

If the party accepts The Silent Pack's offer, each character advances their **Attunement: Primordis (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

`[[/outcome allied]]`

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Warlock Patron: The Silent Pack
>
> If the party allies themselves with [[The Silent Pack]], they will have the opportunity to forge a relationship with these mysterious [[Casia]] of [[Primordis]]. Each character can pursue one or more Levels of the [[Warlock]] class using the Silent Pack as their patrons, at the Gamemaster's discretion.

### The Captive Warden

During their exploration of Kadra Zann's [[Dungeon]] area, the party has the opportunity to rescue the imprisoned [[Sanguinary Warden]] named Cael. Should they manage to save the prisoner, the characters will earn the cosmic favor of the [[Heart of Ember]] as well as additional rewards from [[Nimaelle]] & Co. during [[Capricious Concerns]].

#### Heart Attunement: Captive Spared

If the party manages to rescue the Sanguinary Warden named Cael from his imprisonment, each character advances their **Attunement: Heart of Ember (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event.

`[[/outcome spared]]`

### Maevren's Remarks

If the party has established a rapport with their guide [[Maevren]], she may have some valuable remarks to offer during a conversation about the encounter here.

> [!info] Social
> #### Conversation with Maevren
>
> If the characters attempt to share a word with Maevren about their visit here, she is keen to oblige them. Topics that Maevren is willing to discuss at the moment include (but are not limited to) the following:
>
> - Kyrban's defeat.
> - Kadra Zann's future.
> - The party's next steps.
>
> Questions Maevren might have for the characters themselves include:
>
> > How did Kyrban create so much calamity?
>
> > Will you also return to Arbore Sanctorus and the Sanguinaries?
>
> > What's next for your group?
>
> Maevren has no reason to mislead or withhold information from the characters unless they've been duplicitous or antagonistic to her in the past. Any character with a `[[/skill insight 13 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Deception (DC 11)** check can readily tell that the guide is being honest and forthcoming in her analysis of the encounter.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What about Kyrban?
>
> **A:**
>
> Maevren sheathes her blade after wiping away the stains of battle.
>
> > With any luck, the Doomsayers have died alongside Kyrban. And queen's ruby will no longer plague the good people of Talei. By the moons, we've slain a monster or two here today, my friends.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What about Kadra Zann?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Who knows? I'd imagine the Anachraenum will be interested to learn more about this place. And the Sanguinaries have probably earned their own stewardship of Kadra Zann. Time will tell.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Next steps?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I should return to Talei with news of what happened here. And you should join with me. Proctus no doubt has a reward waiting for you, and the town deserves to know more about the people who spared it from Kyrban's poisonous grip.

### Concluding the Event

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Milestone Point
>
> Completing this Event earns the party 1 [[Milestone Progression]], potentially advancing them in Level.
>
> #### Next Steps
>
> Once Kyrban has been defeated, the party can journey back to [[Talei]] for the aptly-named [[Return to Talei]], so they might report their success to the troubled town. If they managed to obtain [[Hodge's Heirloom]], the characters can claim a reward from [[Proctus Caylas]] for their efforts.
>
> Additionally, the party can take another trip to the [[Arbore Sanctorus]] to share tale of their exploits with [[Nimaelle]] and [[Avwynn Taol]], who may present the characters with a reward of their own.
