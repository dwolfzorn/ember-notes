# Just a Taste

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event occurs when the party encounters a group of revelers in Ordain. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Meet "The Most August Society of Aythorn."
> - Learn more about [[Talei]], the [[Ruby]] substance, and [[Aythorn]].
> - Participate in the festivities by dancing, navigating the revelers' impromptu customs, and making an offering to Aythorn.
>
> This Event takes place on the Region Map.

**BarkaChaotic Good, Arcturian Thornling, she/her**

Barka is a spritely worshiper of the [[Shard Gods]] [[Aythorn]], and a member of The Most August Society of Aythorn. She is a gentle but excitable girl who genuinely enjoys meeting new people and trying new things — even when she shouldn't.

**ValianChaotic Good, Arcturian Keth, he/him**

Valian is a silver-haired reveler of The Most August Society of Aythorn. He is charming, distractable, and tends to help himself to other people's drink and personal space. He has made fast friends with Brond, and together they watch over Barka with brotherly affection.

**BrondChaotic Good, Arcturian Kivahr, he/him**

Brond is a broad and red-faced Kivahr of The Most August Society of Aythorn, whose unmistakable belly laugh keeps everyone around him in good cheer. He is a quick-witted storyteller often surrounded by a small group of eager listeners.

> [!info] Social
> #### Speaking with Barka
>
> Barka greets the characters heartily and throws a silk sash around the neck of the grumpiest character. She welcomes the characters to The Most August Society of Aythorn, and invites them to join in the reveling. As she speaks, any character with a **Awareness (DC 14, Passive)** notices that her tongue is stained red. Before she is willing to answer questions, the party must spend 1 hour to attempt a group challenge to participate in the revelry, with each party member rolling one of the following checks:
>
> - **Performance (DC 16)** to dance or otherwise perform.
> - **Society (DC 16)** to navigate the society's impromptu (and often made up) "customs."
> - **Science (DC 16)** to make an appropriate offering to Aythorn.
>
> When making any of the above checks:
>
> - **Path: Shard God Devotee**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they are a worshiper of Aythorn.
>
> If the group challenge is successful, Barka collapses in exhaustion, giggling to herself; impressed with the characters' propensity for revelry, she agrees to take a breather and talk about the following topics:
>
> - The Most August Society of Aythorn.
> - Aythorn.
> - Her personal origin.
> - The group's destination.
>
> If the group challenge is failed, each character who participated suffers one level of &reference[exhaustion]. Barka chides them for "killing the mood," but agrees to answer any questions they may have nonetheless.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What is The Most August Society of Aythorn?
>
> **A:**
>
> > We are worshipers of Aythorn.
>
> Barka looks at the motley crew of revelers and pauses.
>
> > Well, most of us are. Some of us just like to drink. Like Valian and Brond over there.
>
> She points at the silver-haired Keth and the rotund Kivahr, who have thrown their arms around one another and begun singing two different melodies in the same deep tenor.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Who is Aythorn?
>
> **A:**
>
> Barka's eyes widen.
>
> > You've never heard of Aythorn? Well, I guess they're pretty new here on the Plateau. They were once a Thornling — like me! — who tricked a leviathan into giving them one of its teeth, which turned him into a Shard God! Crazy, right?

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Where did you come from?
>
> **A:**
>
> > We just came from Talei. But before that, Brevin — that's when I joined the Society! And before Brevin … well, I don't know.
>
> Barka gives you a placid smile.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Where are you going?
>
> **A:**
>
> Barka shrugs.
>
> > The Society has no fixed destination. You know that moment during a party when everyone suddenly understands it's time to leave? It's exactly like that. Could be an hour from now, a day, a week … we'll get going eventually.

Before the characters depart, Barka offers them a cup of fruit and its accompanying parcel.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> You hear Barka's voice cut through the din of revelers.
>
> > Wait, hold on!
>
> She snatches a handful of fruit cups and twine-bound packets from the table and holds them out for you.
>
> > Have a taste of Talei — then join us for the night!

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Examining the Packet
>
> The twine-bound paper packet contains a russet-colored, jam-like substance. Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** or **Medicine (DC 14)** check recognizes the substance as a single dose of [[Ruby]], an analgesic harvested and prepared in Talei. The Cindarics brew ruby into a medicinal tea, and in recent years, the raw substance has gained popularity among the upper crust of Ordain.
>
> - **Encountered Ruby:** The character automatically succeeds on this check if they identified and/or consumed ruby in a previous situation.
> - **Path: Cindaric Initiate**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Culture: Ordani**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Path: Caravanner**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Path: Cosmopolitan Fashionista**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.

## Spending the Night

Barka invites the characters to spend the night with The Most August Society of Aythorn. If the characters refuse, Barka bids them farewell, then rejoins the revelers. If the characters accept, perform the following actions:

- Advance time to 12:00pm the following day.
- Determine where each character wakes up by moving them `[[/gmroll 1d2]]` miles away in any direction (do not move the party token; track where each character is manually).
- Roll once on the table below for each character to determine what situation they find themselves in upon waking; if two or more characters share a location, you may roll once for that group instead.

|  | #### A Night of Revelry |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | #### A Shallow Grave  The character wakes up in a shallow grave, cradling a shovel and covered in flowers.  The character can examine the flowers and make a successful **Awareness (DC 12)** check to find a small packet of [[Ruby]] tied to a flower stem. |
| 2 | #### A New Pet  The character wakes up to find a small [[Casir Cat]] licking their face. The cat has a flower garland around its neck and a paper tag that reads "Mayor."  The character can make a **Wilderness (DC 14)** check to make Mayor their permanent pet. |
| 3 | #### Lost Clothes  The character is rudely awoken by a [[Burnished Hand Protector]], who is preparing to fine the character 15 gp for public indecency. The character is stark naked; their clothes and equipment are scattered nearby, folded into several neat piles.  The character can talk the guard out of the fine by making a successful **Diplomacy (DC 12)** check. |
| 4 | #### Carousing  The character wakes up in a tavern, surrounded by dozens of other revelers. The tavern is heavily damaged, and the character's coin purse is `[[/gmroll 4d6]]` gp lighter.  As the [[Ordani]] enters the building, the character can make a successful **Stealth (DC 12)** check to avoid them and leave unnoticed. Otherwise, they and the other revelers must spend an hour cleaning up the tavern. |
| 5 | #### Stranded  The character awakens on top of a precariously high structure, with no clear way to descend.  The character can climb down by making a successful **Athletics (DC 12)** check. On a failure, they fall 20 feet, suffering **A Terrible Fall (Hazard 7)** and landing **Prone**.  Alternatively, the character can wait an hour for help, attracting a sizable crowd and suffering **-2 Banes** on their next **Diplomacy (DC undefined)** check made in Ordain before they finish a **Rest**. |
| 6 | #### Floating  The character wakes up to find themselves floating in a canal or small pool of water, clutching a plank of wood with one hand and holding an improvised fishing rod in the other. Hooked on the line is a large fish, which begins to struggle again as the character awakens.  The character can reel the fish ashore by making a successful **Athletics (DC 12)** check. The fish can be cleaned and cooked into `[[/gmroll 1d4]]` rations. |
