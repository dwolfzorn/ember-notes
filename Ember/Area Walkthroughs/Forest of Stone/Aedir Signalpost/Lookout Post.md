# Lookout Post

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As you exit the elevator, your breath catches for a moment as you realize that you can see for what seems like miles from here, across the canopy of the Forest of Stone and into the orange-yellow of the Golden Flats in the center of the Arctus Plateau.
>
> Whoever once lived here took advantage of those views — telescopes line the outer edge of the tower's rooftop, and tracks in the center of the tower seem to indicate some kind of signaling system that may have used mirrors and glass.

While searching the area, characters may inspect the circular track and system of lenses installed atop the lookout post.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Area
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 15)** check faintly hears the distant flapping of wings from some creature far overhead.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 15)** check recalls that the ancient Aedir were rumored to be able to communicate instantaneously across vast distances by encoding messages within beams of light using a sophisticated set of directed mirrors and lenses.
>
> - **Knowledge: Aedir**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> #### Observation Lens
>
> One telescopic lens in the northwest edge of the lookout post remains functional. Its lens is scratched and blurry from centuries of neglect, but a character who peers through it can still survey a considerable stretch of the Forest of Stone from this lofty perch.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Vantage Point
>
> The **Observation Lens** described above is the first Vantage Point that can be discovered in the game. Interacting with this telescope clears the **Fog of War** from a large section of the Forest of Stone on the **Region Map**.

From this location, characters can see the [[Suarrok Nest]] which is perched atop a giant stone pillar, 40 feet above the floor of the Lookout.
