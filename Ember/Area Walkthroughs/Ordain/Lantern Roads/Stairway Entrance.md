# Stairway Entrance

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The dark stone of the spiral staircase exits into a narrow hallway whose smooth, glowing floors and hum of activity promise something completely different from the elegant manor that sits above.

This stairway connects Marlstone Manor and Wandren HQ. Once used by characters, it remains unlocked until Hephiss appears in the facility in [[Not All Who Wandren]], at which point it is locked and cannot be used as an exit.
