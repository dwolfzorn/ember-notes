# Funar Cevher's Cell

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This bottom-row cell is marked with a yellow band and a glyph beside its door. Inside, an Ordani man sits against the wall with one wrist chained to a metal pipe. The pipe is dented near his hand, where he has been striking it with the chain hard enough to send the thumping sound through the surrounding walls.
>
> He stops when he sees you. His clothes are torn, one side of his face is bruised, and he looks exhausted.

If Lyla Cevher is with the party, she and Funar embrace and exchange rapid, relieved greetings.

> [!info] Social
> #### A Conversation with Funar
>
> Funar Cevher is relieved to see help, but wary of strangers. If Lyla is with the party, he trusts them immediately. If Lyla is not with the party, any character who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 15)** check can convince Funar that they are working with her.
>
> - **Personal Detail:** The character automatically succeeds on this check if they share a detail about Lyla only she or Funar would know.
>
> Once Funar trusts the party, he can share the following:
>
> - **His capture:** Funar was on his way to see his uncle Darius when he was ambushed. He remembers choking on a foul-smelling dust, then waking up in this cell.
> - **Darius' death:** A guard brought Funar a copy of *Overheard in Ordain* so he would know that Darius had been killed and that Funar was being blamed for it.
> - **His condition:** Funar was injured during the ambush. His wounds are not life-threatening, but he needs medical attention once the party is somewhere safe.
> - **Other prisoners:** Funar saw a few prisoners, including elder members of House Wandren, but the most curious was a being made entirely out of light. This creature was subdued and dragged out of the holding cells after trying to escape.
> - **Getting out:** Funar doesn't know how to release the holding cell lock, but knows that the guards never brought a key with them. He posits that the release mechanism is controlled remotely.
>
> Funar wants to rescue the being of light before leaving, but once freed, he focuses on keeping up with the party and delays longer conversation until after they escape.
>
> A few specific dialogue options for Funar are provided below.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Are you okay?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Thank you for asking. No, not especially. But I am not dying, and that is more than I expected today. I would welcome a physician once we are far away from this place. And a cold drink.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** How were you imprisoned?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I was on my way to see my uncle Darius — may his soul return unburdened — about a business matter. It all happened so fast … I remember choking on air … and hands grabbing my arms. Then I woke up here.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What do you know about Darius' death?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Enough to know I am being framed. One of the guards brought me a copy of Overheard in Ordain and made sure I saw the story. What a horrible gossip rag! Not only was my uncle murdered, but so was my reputation!

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Were there other prisoners?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Occasionally, yes. Some were even members of House Wandren — the elders. The guards kept them separated from me, but I heard enough. I heard arguments, then screaming, then nothing. If any of these cells have remains in them, I suspect them to have once worn the Wandren name. Someone decided to clean house.
> >
> > There was another, too, a creature made entirely of light. The guards spoke to it in a different language. After a few days, I heard a loud crash. The guards came back and dragged the creature away. I do hope it is still alive. I do not want to leave without it.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** How do we open your cell?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Not with a key, I can tell you that. Whenever the guards would show up, the cell door opened without them doing anything. I can only assume that the controls are somewhere else in the facility.
