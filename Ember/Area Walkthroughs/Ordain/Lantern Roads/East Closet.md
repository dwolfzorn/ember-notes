# East Closet

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This storage room is packed with heavy mechanical parts, most too large to move by hand. A few containers hold pale mineral chunks and dull crystals.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the East Closet
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - Amidst the dull crystals lies a small amethyst shard worth  **50**.
> - Enough components to assemble a set of [[Unknown]] over the course of 10 minutes.
> - An [[Unlit Kynryth Torch]].
