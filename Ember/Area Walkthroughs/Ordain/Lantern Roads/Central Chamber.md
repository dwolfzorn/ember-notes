# Central Chamber

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Beneath the glassy floor at the edge of the chamber, metal gears turn in a steady rhythm. The sound is punctuated by a faint, repeated thumping.
>
> In the center of the chamber, a large metal dome rises from the floor. It glows faintly from within, producing a golden light that seems to vibrate against your skin.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Chamber
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - Only some of the gears beneath the glassy floor are moving.
> - The central dome is not a single structure. Through the north-facing opening in the outer ring, the characters can see another curved metal wall behind it, set into its own circular groove in the floor.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 15)** check while examining the unmoving gears realize that they must be activated elsewhere in the facility.
>
> - **Knowledge: Machines**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 15 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check can tell that the irregular thumping is echoing through the wall from the west.
>
> #### Aligning the Dome Rings
>
> The central dome is made from three nested rings: an outer ring, a middle ring, and an inner ring. Each ring has an opening, but the openings must be aligned before the party can enter the [[Inner Chamber]].
>
> The rings are controlled by three wheels located elsewhere in Wandren HQ:
>
> - **Outer ring:** [[Blessing Chamber]].
> - **Middle ring:** [[Assemblyworks]].
> - **Inner ring:** [[Stasis Chamber]].
>
> Each opening can be rotated to one of eight positions: 12:00, 1:30, 3:00, 4:30, 6:00, 7:30, 9:00, or 10:30.
>
> The only solution is to align all three openings at 12:00, facing north. Other matching positions do not open the way into the [[Inner Chamber]].
>
> When the party first enters the chamber, the rings are positioned as follows:
>
> - **Outer ring:** 12:00, facing north.
> - **Middle ring:** 10:30, facing northwest.
> - **Inner ring:** 3:00, facing east.
>
> Each time a wheel is turned, its corresponding ring rotates to the next available position. A character can continue turning a wheel until the ring reaches the desired position, unless that position is locked out by another ring.
>
> #### Examining the Dome Rings
>
> Any character who examines the **outer ring** can see that it has been coated with a thin layer of some kind of substance. Characters with **Knowledge: Alchemy** recognize the substance as [[Thieves' Lament]].
>
> Any character who examines the **middle ring** and makes a successful **Arcana (DC 15)** check recognizes that it is charged with magical electricity.
>
> Any character who examines the **inner ring** and makes a successful **Arcana (DC 15)** check recognizes that it is charged with radiant energy, and that its magic does not originate on Ember.
>
> When making any of the above checks:
>
> - [[Identify]]: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> [[Dispel Magic]] or a similar effect targeting the middle or inner ring suppresses its effects for 10 minutes.
>
> Spells cannot pass through one dome ring to affect the rings beyond it.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Ring Lockouts
>
> The dome's mechanisms prevent the rings from being aligned in the wrong order:
>
> - If the outer ring is already at 12:00, the middle ring cannot be turned to 12:00.
> - If the inner ring is already at 12:00, the outer ring cannot be turned to 12:00.
>
> When a ring is locked out, the wheel refuses to turn farther. The gears beneath the floor grind loudly, the corresponding ring shudders in place, and then the mechanism settles back into its previous position.
>
> #### Solution
>
> To solve the puzzle, the party must set the rings in the following order:
>
> - Move the outer ring away from 12:00.
> - Set the middle ring to 12:00.
> - Set the outer ring back to 12:00.
> - Set the inner ring to 12:00.
>
> Once all three rings are aligned at 12:00, the openings form a north-facing passage into the [[Inner Chamber]].

#### Work in Progress

In a future update, interacting with the wheels within Wandren HQ will visually rotate the nested domes on the Area Map. For now, you must rely on theater of the mind.

Additionally, the dome walls themselves are dangerous to touch.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Touching the Dome Rings
>
> Each ring has been treated to harm anything that touches it; the characters can [[Central Chamber]] to determine what they might do.
>
> - **Outer ring:** The outer ring has been treated with [[Thieves' Lament]].
> - **Middle ring:** The middle ring is charged with magical lightning. Any character who touches it or passes through its opening before the rings are fully aligned must make a `[[/save constitution 16]]` saving throw, taking `[[/damage 1d6 Lightning]]` damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
> - **Inner ring:** The inner ring radiates celestial energy. Any character who comes within 5 feet of its surface before the rings are fully aligned must make a `[[/save constitution 16]]` saving throw, taking `[[/damage 1d8 Radiant]]` damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
