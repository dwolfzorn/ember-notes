# Terrace Gardens: Nature's Glory

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This rooftop garden is arranged in low, orderly beds of dark soil and red-brown herbs. Pale flowers grow from the central planters, flanked by a bed of small golden pods and a stand of black mushrooms.
>
> A sign beside the garden reads:
>
> > Not all beauty is worn on the outside.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching Nature's Glory
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - The central bed is filled with pale, star-shaped flowers and clusters of a wispy, red-brown plant; they have a sharp medicinal smell.
> - The west bed is filled with golden pods that hang from the ends of bowing, thin stalks; they emit a sugary scent.
> - The east bed is filled with black mushrooms that appear waxy; the earth from which they grow is similarly blackened and smells of fertilizer.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 13)** check can identify the plants' properties:
>
> - The pale, star-shaped flowers can be crushed into a topical anesthetic or boiled into a sour numbing tea.
> - The golden pods are filled with a sweet, pollen-filled nectar. When the pods eventually burst, the pollen becomes aerosolized.
> - The black mushrooms enrich the soil in which they are planted. It's likely that the soil throughout the gardens was derived from this single bed.
>
> - **Knowledge: Plants**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also recognizes the star-shaped flowers as a likely ingredient in the paralytic poison used by the Beacon Brigade.
