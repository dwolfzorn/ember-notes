# Local Treasures

### Outside of The Shop

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Beneath a sign that reads "Local Treasures," a large shadowbox window displays the Ordani skyline in silhouette. The city's dark rooftops, bridges, and towers seem to move in small, rhythmic motions, as if something behind the display is causing the pattern to shift.

The shadowbox display conceals the shop interior, but it does not fully block sound or movement from within.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Observing the Exterior
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 17 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 15)** check hears coins clinking and heavy objects being placed on the floor inside the shop.
>
> - **Critical Success**: The character also hears faint murmuring between two people and makes out the following:
>
> > When we're done counting this up, I'm getting a nice, cold drink back at the barracks.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 17)** check while studying the shadowbox's movement realizes that two people inside the shop are making repetitive motions behind the display.

Characters can reveal the shop interior by shining light on the shadowbox window.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Seeing Behind the Shadowbox
>
> If a light source is brought within 10 feet of the shadowbox window, the display becomes transparent enough to see through, revealing the Beacon Brigade members inside. This also reveals the party's presence to them; see [[Local Treasures]] below.
>
> If the party looks through the lit shadowbox, read the following:
>
> > The Ordani skyline fades from the glass. Behind it, two locals count coins into small bags, then place the bags inside a nearly full chest on the floor.

### Inside the Shop

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The shop's shelves and countertops are crowded with books, curios, and small trinkets arranged for display.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Beacon Brigade Counters
>
> The two locals counting money are [[Wandren Patroller]]. They are guarding the Brigade's money and will not allow the party to enter the shop unchallenged.
>
> If the Patrollers detect the party outside (by illuminating the shadowbox, for example), they will investigate the disturbance.
>
> If the party enters the shop without alerting the Patrollers to their presence, the Patrollers are &reference[surprise]{Surprised}.

> [!abstract] Wandren Patroller
> **[[Wandren Patroller]]**
>
> Level 1 · Unknown Unknown
>
> ![Wandren Patroller](../../../assets/image050.webp)

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Beacon Brigade Patroller Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Wandren Patroller]] will move to strike an enemy with its [[Hollowed Dagger]], applying either [[Paralyzing Poison]] or [[Slowing Serum]], per the Gamemaster's discretion.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Patroller will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Patroller will use their [[Multiattack]] feature to apply [[Paralyzing Poison]] or [[Slowing Serum]] to as many enemies as possible.
> - Whenever able, the Patroller will position themselves amongst allies to take advantage of their [[Pack Tactics]] feature.
>
> Once reduced below half their Hit Point maximum, the Patroller will attempt to flee toward the nearest ally.

Once the shop is empty, the party can search it.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching Local Treasures
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - Books, curios, and small trinkets arranged to suit the shop's shadowbox display.
> - An iron-bound chest on the floor.
>
> The chest on the floor contains 8 [[Bag of Coins]].
