# Assemblyworks

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Heavy cables run across the walls and floor of this chamber, feeding into alcoves that look built to hold bodies rather than cargo. At the east end, exposed gears turn around a fixed silver wheel, their motion slow and deliberate.
>
> Beside the machinery, a metallic figure rests half-assembled in its frame. Its limbs are in place, but cables still hang from its body, and several plates along its torso have not been sealed.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Assemblyworks
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - A silver wheel fixed to the east wall.
> - A dormant [[Kynryth Husk]] connected to the surrounding machinery.

The characters can interact with the wheel to manipulate the position of the middle ring in the [[Central Chamber]].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Using the Wheel
>
> The wheel can be rotated to one of eight positions: 12:00, 1:30, 3:00, 4:30, 6:00, 7:30, 9:00, or 10:30.
>
> When the party first enters, the wheel is positioned at 10:30.
>
> If the outer ring is already at 12:00, the wheel cannot be turned to 12:00. See the puzzle [[Central Chamber]] for more information.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Kynryth Husk Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Kynryth Husk]] will use its [[Multiattack]] Action to make one [[Tentacle Swipe]] attack and use [[Tentacle Grab]].
>
> Over the course of combat, the Kynryth Husk will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Kynryth Husk will use its [[Multiattack]] Action.
> - Whenever able, the Kynryth Husk will [[Unknown]] to transform into a fully powered [[Kynryth]].
