# Weapons Cache

### Outside of the building

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This plain wooden cabin sits among the other buildings in the northern side of Lantern Roads. Its shutters are closed, and nothing about the exterior marks it as more than a small storage building.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Locked Door
>
> The front door is reinforced and locked, but can be unlocked with a [[Brigade Key]] or a successful `[[/skill sleightofhand 20 tool=thief]]`; alternatively, it can be bashed open with a successful **Athletics (DC 30)** check. Any character who attempts to bash the door open alerts any Beacon Brigade members within 30 feet.

### Inside of the building

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The inside of the cabin is packed with weapons, shields, and small glass vials. Most of the metalwork is crude, with uneven edges and cheap fittings, but there is enough equipment here to arm a small crew.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Weapons Cache
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - 8 [[Low-Quality Daggers]].
> - 6 [[Low-Quality Shields]].
> - 12 [[Empty Vials]].
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 15)** check while searching the room discovers a single [[Withering Dagger]] wrapped in cloth and tucked behind a stack of shields. The weapon is nearly identical to the one found at the scene of Darius Cevher's murder, except someone has carved "BB" into the handle.
>
> The weapon is clear evidence of the Beacon Brigade's involvement in Darius Cevher's death and can help clear Funar's name. Mark the appropriate Outcome in [[Revealed by Lantern]].
