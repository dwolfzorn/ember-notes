# West Closet

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Shelves and open storage frames line the walls of this closet. Fine clothing has been folded, bundled, or thrown wherever there was space. Several garments are stained dark in places, and the room smells of sweat and dried blood.

A [[Wandren Patroller]] loiters in this area, rummaging through the clothes. They attack intruders on sight.

> [!abstract] Wandren Patroller
> **[[Wandren Patroller]]**
>
> Level 1 · Unknown Unknown
>
> ![Wandren Patroller](../../../assets/image050.webp)

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Wandren Patroller Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Wandren Patroller]] will move to strike an enemy with its [[Hollowed Dagger]], applying either [[Paralyzing Poison]] or [[Slowing Serum]], per the Gamemaster's discretion.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Patroller will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Patroller will use their [[Multiattack]] feature to apply [[Paralyzing Poison]] or [[Slowing Serum]] to as many enemies as possible.
> - Whenever able, the Patroller will position themselves amongst allies to take advantage of their [[Pack Tactics]] feature.
>
> Once reduced below half their Hit Point maximum, the Patroller will attempt to flee toward the nearest ally.

Once the area is clear of enemies, the characters can search the room.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the West Closet
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - Fine, Ordani-style clothing; soiled and torn.
> - Bloodstained cuffs and collars.
> - A few personal effects left in pockets, such as handkerchiefs, cufflinks, and broken jewelry — including an earring.
>
> Any character who examines the clothing and makes a successful **Society (DC 15)** check recognizes some of the clothing and personal effects as belonging to members of House Wandren. The earring belongs to Funar Cevher and is keyed to the [[Locator Rod]].
>
> - **Lyla Cevher:** The character automatically succeeds on this check if Lyla Cevher is accompanying the party.
> - **Knowledge: Politics**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Path: Cosmopolitan Fashionista**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> If Lyla Cevher is present, she is visibly unsettled by the sight of the earring, but remains adamant that Funar is still alive (if the party has not found him already).
