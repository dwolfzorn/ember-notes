# Vitt's Balcony

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A small table and several chairs sit on this rooftop. Two armed people stand at opposite corners, looking out over Lantern Roads.

The entrances to [[Vitt's Front Room]] are closed and locked.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Locked Doors & Window
>
> The doors and reinforced window to [[Vitt's Front Room]] are locked, but can be unlocked using a [[Brigade Key]] or with a successful `[[/skill sleightofhand 15 tool=thief]]`. Alternatively, the doors can be bashed open with a successful **Athletics (DC 22)** check, and the reinforced window can be broken with a successful **Athletics (DC 15)** check. Any character who attempts to bash a door or window open alerts all Beacon Brigade members within 30 feet, including [[Vitt Wandren]]: see [[Vitt's Bedroom]].

Two [[Wandren Watcher]] are stationed on the balcony. Both carry [[Brigade Key]]. Their attention is directed outward, but they challenge anyone who reaches the balcony without an obvious reason to be there.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Beacon Brigade Watchers
>
> The party can deal with these Watchers using the general options described in [[Area Overview]], with the following changes:
>
> - The party cannot convince the Watchers that they reached the balcony by accident.
> - If a character carries a [[Courier Bag]], the Watchers do not attack immediately. Instead, they demand that the character turn over their letter and leave.
>
> A character carrying a [[Courier Bag]] can convince the Watchers that they were instructed to hand their letter directly to [[Vitt Wandren]] by making a **`[[/skill Deception 20]]`** check. On a success, the Watchers unlock the south door to [[Vitt's Front Room]], but will only allow the character carrying the letter to enter.

> [!abstract] Wandren Watcher
> **[[Wandren Watcher]]**
>
> Level 1 · Unknown Unknown
>

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Beacon Brigade Watcher Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Wandren Watcher]] will use their [[Spotlight Shot]] to illuminate an enemy and alert any Beacon Brigade members within 60 feet to come to their aid.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Watcher will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Watcher will use their [[Multiattack]] Action to make two attacks with their [[Shortsword]].
> - From range, the Watcher will use their [[Multiattack]] Action to make two attacks with their [[Longbow]].
> - If a character is hidden or trying to remain unseen, the Watcher will use their [[Spotlight Shot]] to make hiding more difficult and potentially inflict the &Reference[blinded] condition on nearby enemies.
>
> Once reduced below half their Hit Point maximum, the Beacon Brigade Watcher will attempt to flee toward the nearest Beacon Brigade member.

A narrow gap separates Vitt's Balcony and the [[Terracini Balcony]].

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Jumping the Gap - Terracini Balcony
>
> The gap between the ledges is approximately 5 feet. Any character may attempt to [[Jump]] the gap. A character who fails to clear the full distance falls 20 feet into the plaza below, taking `[[/damage 2d6 Bludgeoning]]` damage and landing &reference[prone].

A rocky outcropping rises near the edge of the rooftop, close enough to reach [[Lookout Point]] with a difficult climb or a dangerous jump.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Climbing the Gap - Lookout Point
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Athletics (DC 15)** check can climb across the rocky outcropping to reach [[Lookout Point]]. A character who fails falls 20 feet into the plaza below, taking `[[/damage 2d6 Bludgeoning]]` damage and landing &reference[prone].
>
> - **Proper Equipment:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they use proper equipment (pitons, for example).
>
> #### Jumping the Gap - Lookout Point
>
> The gap between the ledges is approximately 15 feet. Any character may attempt to [[Jump]] the gap. A character who fails to clear the full distance falls 20 feet into the plaza below, taking `[[/damage 2d6 Bludgeoning]]` damage and landing &reference[prone].
