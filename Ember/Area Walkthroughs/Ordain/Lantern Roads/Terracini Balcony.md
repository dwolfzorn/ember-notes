# Terracini Balcony

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Two nervous-looking customers sit at a small table, speaking in low voices over untouched drinks. A lantern sits on the table between them. Every so often, they glance toward the armed Watchers on a nearby roof. The Watchers stare out over Lantern Roads and pay no obvious attention to the couple, the cafe, or you.

The Watchers on [[Vitt's Balcony]] do not react to the party unless the party attacks them, threatens the Terracini customers, or otherwise creates an obvious distraction.

> [!info] Social
> #### Talking with the Recruits
>
> The two customers are prospective Beacon Brigade recruits waiting to be called across to the northern side of Lantern Roads. They are nervous, but try to hide it by acting as if they already understand what is happening.
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 17 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 15)** check when they come within 10 feet of the customers can overhear a snippet of hushed conversation:
>
> > … you need to relax! We already took the money. We go over, we hear what they want, and then we decide.
>
> If the party approaches them, the recruits assume the party is also here to join the Beacon Brigade. As long as the party does not contradict this assumption, the recruits readily discuss the following:
>
> - **The lantern:** The recruits were told to bring a lantern to the balcony and wait until someone on the far roof signals them. If the party does not produce a lantern or similar light-producing item, the recruits grow suspicious and will not speak further.
> - **Second Light:** The recruits believe the real test starts at [[Second Light]], where recruits are supposed to trade in something that makes light. If the party appears unaware of this detail, the recruits grow suspicious and will not speak further.
> - **The phrase:** The recruits know they are supposed to say a secret phrase at Second Light, but do not volunteer it to the characters. Any character who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 15)** check can convince them to reveal the phrase: "Have a bright day."

A narrow gap separates the Terracini Balcony and [[Vitt's Balcony]].

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Jumping the Gap
>
> The gap between the ledges is approximately 5 feet. Any character may attempt to [[Jump]] the gap. A character who fails to clear the full distance falls 20 feet into the plaza below, taking `[[/damage 2d6 Bludgeoning]]` damage and landing &reference[prone].
