# Inner Chamber

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Inside the aligned metal domes, machinery fills the chamber like something grown inside a shell. Thick golden cords run from the walls into a central console built against the south wall, then branch outward to four smaller consoles arranged around the room.
>
> In the center of the chamber, within a silver metal ring built into the floor, a creature hangs suspended in a beam of light. The four outer consoles pulse in sequence, each one flashing with a different color before fading back into the room's steady golden hum.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Inner Chamber
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - A curved control station built into the southern wall of the chamber.
> - Four outer consoles set apart from the central station.
> - Golden cords running from the console into the surrounding machinery.
> - A silver metal ring built into the center of the floor; inside which a creature lies suspended in a beam of light.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check realizes that several cords run out through the floor and walls toward the west; they can reasonably conclude that the panels and central station control more than this room.
>
> - **Knowledge: Machines**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> #### Reading the Outer Consoles
>
> A glyph is etched on each of the four outer consoles. Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 15)** check recognizes the glyphs as Harmos, the language used by the [[Tyraphem]] who dwell on the Inner Realm of [[Luxarum]].
>
> - **Language: Harmos**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Celestials**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> Any character who can read Harmos understands the glyphs to mean "Holds," "Light," "Darkness," and "Releases."
>
> #### Reading the Central Console
>
> The central console controls the [[Holding Cells]] elsewhere in the facility. Along its lower edge, five glyphs are arranged beneath four vertical colors bands: Orange, Blue, Yellow, and Purple. Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 15)** check recognizes the glyphs as Harmos, the language used by the Tyraphem who dwell on the Inner Realm of Luxarum.
>
> - **Language: Harmos**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Celestials**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> Any character who can read Harmos understands the glyphs to mean numbers ranging from 1-5.
>
> Each holding cell is identified by a color and a number. To open a holding cell, a character must touch the correct colored band on the central console, then touch the correct number glyph.
>
> Refer to [[Holding Cells]] for possible combinations.
>
> #### Beam of Light
>
> Any character who examines the beam of light and makes a successful **Arcana (DC 15)** check can identify it as a field of imprisoning magic generated by the metal ring on the floor. The ring appears to be connected to the four outer consoles.
>
> - **Knowledge: Celestials**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also recognizes that the magic originates from the Inner Realm of Luxarum, where the Tyraphem dwell.

The silver beam is dangerous to touch.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Silver Beam
>
> The edge of the beam repels anyone who touches it. Any character who touches the beam must make a `[[/save constitution 16]]` saving throw.
>
> On a failed save, the character is repelled 10 feet away from the beam, taking `[[/damage 1d6 Bludgeoning]]` damage and landing &reference[prone]. On a successful save, the character is repelled 5 feet away, takes no damage, and is not knocked &reference[prone].

To deactivate the beam, the characters must interact with the four outer consoles in the correct order.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Deactivating the Beam
>
> The four outer consoles flash in a repeating color sequence. Each console flashes one color, then the next console flashes, continuing until the sequence is complete. After a short pause, the sequence begins again.
>
> A character adjacent to an outer console can use its Action to touch the console.
>
> If the party touches the outer consoles in the correct order, all four consoles glow at once, and the silver beam collapses into the ring in the floor, freeing the creature suspended inside. Award the corresponding Attunement in the [[Not All Who Wandren]] Event.
>
> If a character touches the wrong console, the sequence fails: any creature within 5 feet of a console must make a `[[/save Constitution 15]]` saving throw or take `[[/damage 1d6 Lightning]]` damage.
>
> #### Console Sequence
>
> Use the following sequence for the four outer consoles. Each console light also corresponds to a Harmos glyph.
>
> - **First:** Northwest (Orange) "Light"
> - **Second:** Southeast (Blue) "Holds"
> - **Third:** Northeast (Yellow) "Darkness"
> - **Fourth:** Southwest (Purple) "Releases"

### Meeting the Prisoner

#### Work in Progress

In a future update, the celestial prisoner will have an Actor and interactable Token. For now, treat the creature as a noncombatant.

If a character comes within 5 feet of the light prison, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The creature suspended in the beam turns their head toward you with visible effort. Their body hangs loose in the silver light, held upright by the force around them rather than their own strength.
>
> When they speak, their voice is dry and strained. The words are not Common or Arcden.

> [!info] Social
> #### A Conversation with the Prisoner
>
> Any character who knows **Language: Harmos** can translate the prisoner's first words:
>
> > Please … help me. I am being held against my will.
>
> If a character replies in Harmos, the prisoner answers in Harmos, then switches to Common:
>
> > You speak like one from my home … your companions may not. I will use your common tongue. I am called Ylith.
>
> If no one in the party answers in Harmos, Ylith switches to Common on their own:
>
> > I am sorry … I will use the common tongue. I am tired … please … help me out of this light.
>
> Ylith is exhausted, frightened, and in visible physical distress. Any character who makes a successful **Medicine (DC 12)** or **Deception (DC 12)** check can tell that Ylith is close to collapse and is forcing themselves to remain conscious.
>
> #### What Ylith Knows
>
> Ylith answers the party's questions as best they can, though their speech grows slower and more strained as the conversation continues.
>
> - **Who they are:** Ylith is a Tyraphem from Luxarum. Among their people, they are known as an Architect of Light, a builder of sacred structures and luminous machines. They were captured and forced to help build this facility, though they never saw their captor clearly. They know only a voice.
> - **The facility's purpose:** Ylith believes the facility is meant to create celestial creatures on Ember, or to transform mortals into something that could serve in a celestial war. A human prisoner is being held somewhere in the facility's cells, but Ylith does not know the prisoner's name or cell code.
> - **The consoles:** The silver beam is generated by the holding ring in the floor. The four outer consoles control the release sequence for the ring. Each outer console is marked with a Harmos word; read in the correct order, the words form a sentence that gives the release sequence. The central console controls systems throughout the facility, including the holding cells, which are opened by entering the correct color and number combination.
> - **Exit:** Ylith reveals that he built a [[Secret Exit]], and can show the characters where once freed.
>
> After Ylith shares this information, their strength fails. They slump in the beam and must be revived using the radiant orb in the [[Blessing Room]] before continuing conversation (and revealing the [[Secret Exit]]).
>
> A few specific dialogue options for Ylith are provided below.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Who are you?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I am Ylith, of Luxarum. Among my people, I am called an Architect of Light … I was made to shape holy structures, not this prison.
>
> They draw a thin breath, and their body sags inside the beam.
>
> > This place was built with my knowledge and against my will … I have not left this ring since they finished it.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What is Luxarum and what are the Tyraphem?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Luxarum is one of the Three. You would call them Inner Realms. Luxarum is a place of holy light … we Tyraphem fight for the right to be the keepers of its glory.
>
> Ylith gasps with a rattling sound, body slightly trembling.
>
> > But as you can see … I have been reduced … to fight for mere survival.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Who is your captor?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I do not know their name … nor their form … but they are Tyraphem. Of that, I am certain. I heard only their voice … and the creatures that came when I refused it.
>
> Ylith tries to lift their head, but the beam holds them in place.
>
> > They desired a facility with which to create celestial servants here on Ember … or to transform mortals into a facsimile … brutal, crude things fit for a holy war.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** How do we release you?
>
> **A:**
>
> > The four outer consoles release this ring. Each bears a word in Harmos … read them as a sentence … touch the consoles in that order.
>
> Their eyes shift to the south wall.
>
> > The central console controls the holding cells … elsewhere in the facility … each cell has a color and a number. A human prisoner was taken there.
>
> Ylith's voice thins as they finish.
>
> > Free me from this light … and I can tell you about … the exit I built … until then …
>
> Ylith's body goes slack.
