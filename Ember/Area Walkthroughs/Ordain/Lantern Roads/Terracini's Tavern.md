# Terracini's Tavern

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Terracini's Tavern is nearly empty. A few clean tables sit under low lanternlight, and the bar is stocked, but there are no customers and no smell of food being cooked. The shadows in the corners of the room are thick, as if the lanterns cannot quite penetrate them.
>
> Behind the bar, a man in a long, gray cloak looks up as you enter.
>
> > Welcome to Terracini's Tavern. I'm Penni Terracini, and you're welcome to take a table, though you might find the service to be underwhelming.
>
> He gives you a wink.
>
> > If you're expected on the balcony, you know where to go. If you're not, I'd stay in here.

> [!info] Social
> #### Talking to Penni Terracini
>
> **Penni Terracini** (Neutral, Ordani Human, he/him) runs Terracini's Tavern, though he was born Penne Talarand and is of no relation to the legendary thief. He uses the name because it gives the tavern a reputation, though he has since come to regret it. The Beacon Brigade has taken the story at face value and spared him from the protection extortion imposed on other Lantern Roads businesses. Penni knows he would be in grave danger if the story collapsed.
>
> Penni does not readily explain what he means by being "expected on the balcony." He gives polite, generic answers unless the party convinces him they are not here to cause trouble.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 16)** check realizes that Penni is choosing his words carefully to neither condone nor condemn the Beacon Brigade's activities.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 15)** check knows that the legendary Terracini is not believed to have any surviving relatives in Ordain.
>
> - **Culture: Ordani**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also recalls that "Penni Terracini" is not among the known aliases, descendants, or close associates attached to the legend.
>
> Penni is not inclined to speak with the characters at length. However, any character who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 15)** check can convince him to speak on the topics below:
>
> - **The Beacon Brigade:** Penni says members and associates of the Beacon Brigade sometimes use the balcony as a gathering spot. He does not like it, but refusing them would invite trouble.
> - **Getting across Lantern Roads:** Penni knows the rooftops can be used to reach the other side of the neighborhood. He has also heard there may be a path through Shadowbox Alley, beyond the abandoned theater.
> - **The Tavern:** Terracini's Tavern often looks empty, but that is part of the point: the tavern uses shadowbox magic to hide certain corners from casual view upon request, allowing customers a discreet place to discuss their business. The tavern also used to host larger shadowbox performances, but has not hosted one since the Beacon Brigade took over the neighborhood.
>
> - **Discovered Identity:** The character automatically succeeds on this check if they discovered that Penni is not actually related to the famous Terracini and threatens to reveal his secret.
>
> If the party threatens to expose Penni's false identity, he shares the above information but becomes frightened and resentful. He will not help the party further.
