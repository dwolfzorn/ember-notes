# Locked Gate

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A large metal gate cuts through the center of Lantern Roads. To the south, pedestrians move between shops and stalls under the neighborhood's many lanterns. To the north, the streets are quieter. The people there move with purpose, and most appear armed.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Climbing the Gate
>
> The gate is 20-feet high. Any character who makes a successful **Athletics (DC 15)** check can scale the gate.
>
> - **Proper Equipment:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they use proper equipment (a grappling hook, for example).
>
> The top of the gate is coated in a paralytic poison. Any character who touches the top of the gate must make a `[[/save constitution 17]]` saving throw or become &reference[poisoned] for `[[/gmroll 1d4]]` hours. While &reference[poisoned] in this way, the character is also &reference[paralyzed]. A character that becomes &reference[paralyzed] from touching the top of the gate falls, taking `[[/damage 2d6 Bludgeoning]]` damage and landing &reference[prone].
>
> #### Forcing the Gate
>
> The gate is locked, but can be unlocked with a successful `[[/skill sleightofhand 20 tool=thief]]`; alternatively, it can be bashed open with a successful **Athletics (DC 30)** check.
>
> #### Brigade Response
>
> The gate is watched closely by the Beacon Brigade. If the party attempts to scale or break through the gate in plain view, all Brigade members with line of sight to the gate converge on the area.
>
> If the party continues forcing the gate or refuses to retreat, the Brigade attacks.
