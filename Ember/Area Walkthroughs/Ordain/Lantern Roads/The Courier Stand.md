# The Courier Stand

### Outside of the Shop

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The shadowbox window at the front of this stand shows piles of letters and packages in silhouette. Above it, a sign reads "Courier Stand." A piece of paper has been nailed beneath the sign:
>
> > Back in 20.

No one is currently inside the Courier Stand. The door is locked.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Locked Door
>
> The door can be unlocked with a successful `[[/skill sleightofhand 15 tool=thief]]`; alternatively, it can be bashed open with a successful **Athletics (DC 22)** check.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Observing the Exterior
>
> The Courier Stand appears quiet from the outside, with no visible movement behind the shadowbox window.
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 17 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 15)** check notices the complete lack of sound from inside the stand.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 15)** check while investigating the door notices that its hinges have been greased, and that no dust has gathered around the doorframe.

Characters can reveal the stand's interior by shining light on the shadowbox.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Seeing Behind the Shadowbox
>
> If a light source is brought within 10 feet of the shadowbox window, the display becomes transparent enough to see through.
>
> If the party looks through the lit shadowbox, read the following:
>
> > Letters, packages, and stamps litter a simple courier counter inside the stand. Beside an open courier bag, a pile of letters sits half opened and half still sealed, as if whoever was sorting them expects to return shortly.

### Inside The Shop

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The Courier Stand is cramped and still. Letters, packages, and stamps cover the counter, with more mail tucked into narrow shelves along the walls. Beside an open courier bag lies a pile of half-sorted letters.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Courier Stand
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - A [[Courier Bag]].
> - A letter addressed to Lyla Cevher.
>
> The letter is laced with [[Choking Fog Dust]], which affects any character who opens it.

After 20 minutes, a [[Wandren Patroller]] returns to the stand.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Token Reveal
>
> The [[Wandren Patroller]] Token outside the Courier Stand is initially Hidden; reveal their Token now.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### The Courier Returns
>
> If the party is still inside, the courier immediately notices the intrusion and demands to know what they are doing there.
>
> The party can deal with this courier using the general options described in [[Area Overview]].
>
> If the party fails to explain themselves, refuses to leave, or attacks, the courier calls for help and alerts the Beacon Brigade.

> [!abstract] Wandren Patroller
> **[[Wandren Patroller]]**
>
> Level 1 · Unknown Unknown
>
> ![Wandren Patroller](../../../assets/image050.webp)

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Beacon Brigade Courier Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Wandren Patroller]] will move to strike an enemy with its [[Hollowed Dagger]], applying either [[Paralyzing Poison]] or [[Slowing Serum]], per the Gamemaster's discretion.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Courier will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Courier will use their [[Multiattack]] feature to apply [[Paralyzing Poison]] or [[Slowing Serum]] to as many enemies as possible.
> - Whenever able, the Courier will position themselves amongst allies to take advantage of their [[Pack Tactics]] feature.
>
> Once reduced below half their Hit Point maximum, the Courier will attempt to flee toward the nearest ally.
