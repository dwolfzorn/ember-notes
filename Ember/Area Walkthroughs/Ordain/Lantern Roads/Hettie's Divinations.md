# Hettie's Divinations

### Outside Of The Shop

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Shapes move constantly in the shop window, forming one constellation after another before arranging themselves into words: "Hettie's Divinations."

A narrow alley runs between Hettie's Divinations and Ordani Imports.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Trapped Alleyway
>
> The alleyway between Hettie's Divinations and Ordani Imports contains a spike trap hidden beneath the wooden floorboards. The trap is coated with a paralytic poison and rigged to an alarm bell used by the Beacon Brigade.
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 19 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 17)** check notices the trap beneath the floorboards before stepping on it.
>
> Any character who makes a successful `[[/skill sleightofhand 18 tool=thief]]` can disarm the trap.
>
> Any character who triggers the trap causes spikes to shoot up from the floor. The character must make a `[[/save dexterity 17]]` saving throw or take `[[/damage 1d10 Piercing]]` damage and become &reference[poisoned] for `[[/gmroll 1d4]]` hours. While &reference[poisoned] in this way, the character is also &reference[paralyzed].
>
> When the trap is triggered, an alarm bell rings, alerting any members of the Beacon Brigade within 60 feet.

### Inside Of The shop

When the party enters Hettie's Divination for the first time, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The smell of incense hangs in the shop. Candles burn on small shelves and side tables, and a glowing crystal ball sits in the center of a ceremonial table surrounded by empty chairs.
>
> As you step inside, a voice calls from around the corner:
>
> > Vitt, I'm a diviner, not a miracle worker. You'll have your money next week, same as —
>
> The voice cuts off as a Kiska woman appears.
>
> > Oh, I'm sorry. You're not — forget all of that. Please. Welcome to Hettie's Divinations. How may I help you look into your future today?

When the party returns to the shop after meeting Hettie, read the following instead:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Hettie is nowhere to be seen, though faint humming carries from behind the closed door in the middle of the shop.

**Hettie** (Lawful Neutral, Ordani Kiska, she/her) is willing to speak with the party, but she expects at least one character to sit for a reading first.

> [!info] Social
> #### A Reading from Hettie
>
> Hettie prefers to be paid in interesting items rather than coin. An item counts as interesting if it is worth at least 50 gp or is of Uncommon rarity or rarer.
>
> Hettie accepts 50 gp in coin, but considers such payment dull and inauspicious.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 20)** check can convince her to give the reading for free.
>
> - **Paid in Coin:** The character suffers **-2 Banes** on this check if they paid Hettie in coin.
>
> When Hettie performs the reading, roll on the following table; if the character paid in coin, roll twice and take the lower result:
>
> |  | #### Hettie's Divinations |
> | --- | --- |
> | 1 | #### "Take care. Bad luck waits around the corner."  The character suffers **-2 Banes** on their next check. |
> | 2 | #### "You will seize a triumph from failure."  The next time the character fails a check, they gain **+1 Boons** on the next check they make before the end of their next turn. |
> | 3 | #### "You understand more than you think you do."  The character gains **+2 Boons** on their next Wisdom (Insight) check. |
> | 4 | #### "A dark cloud obscures two paths."  The next time the character fails a check, they can reroll, but must use the new result. |
> | 5 | #### "Your passing is cloaked in shadow."  The character gains **+2 Boons** on their next Dexterity (Stealth) check. |
> | 6 | #### "Stolen wealth, wreathed in blue glass."  Hetties sees blue-framed windows and coin changing hands. She identifies this as one of the Beacon Brigade's supply rooms and tells the character that the room likely holds the Brigade's money. |

After the reading concludes, Hettie is willing to answer a few questions:

> [!info] Social
> #### Speaking with Hettie
>
> Hettie is frightened of the Beacon Brigade, but she is angry enough to talk. She speaks carefully at first, then more plainly if the party shows that they already know about Vitt Wandren or the Brigade's hold over Lantern Roads. Noteworthy topics include:
>
> - Vitt Wandren's connection to the Beacon Brigade as its leader.
> - The Beacon Brigade's extortion of local shopkeepers.
> - Vitt Wandren's recent rise in influence after making a "new friend," Hephiss Wandren.
> - The passage toward Brigade territory near Hettie's shop.
>
> If asked about Marlstone Manor, Hettie explains that the entrance near Vitt's Candy Shop can only be opened from inside Marlstone Manor. To reach Marlstone Manor, the party will need to pass through Brigade territory first.
>
> Hettie is uncomfortable letting the party use the route near her shop, since the Beacon Brigade will retaliate it they learn she helped. Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check can convince her to look the other way while the party passes through.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 13)** check realizes that Hettie wants the Brigade gone, but does not believe anyone in Lantern Roads can protect her if the party fails.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Sneaking Past Hettie
>
> If the characters cannot convince Hettie to let them pass, they may still attempt to pass through her shop undetected by using the normal rules for [[Cover]], [[Hiding]], noise, and line of sight. As a default, compare the characters' Dexterity (Stealth) checks against Hettie's passive Perception. If the party is moving together, the Gamemaster may call for a group check.
>
> On a success, the characters move past Hettie without being detected. On a failure, Hettie alerts the Beacon Brigade and calls for the nearest **Patroller**. Any **Patroller** within 60 feet of Hettie immediately begins moving toward her to intercept the intruders.
