# Stasis Chamber

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Two large metallic creatures hang in recessed stations along the walls, their limbs folded and their heavy bodies held upright by cables and metal braces. A soft glow pulses from the cores set into their chests.
>
> Beneath them, the floor panels vibrate with the motion of unseen machinery. As you step farther into the room, the gears beneath the floor begin to turn faster.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Stasis Chamber
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - A silver wheel fixed to the east wall.
> - Two dormant [[Kynryth Husk]].

The characters can interact with the wheel to manipulate the position of the inner ring in the [[Central Chamber]].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Using the Wheel
>
> The wheel can be rotated to one of eight positions: 12:00, 1:30, 3:00, 4:30, 6:00, 7:30, 9:00, or 10:30.
>
> When the party first enters, the wheel is positioned at 3:00.
>
> See the puzzle [[Central Chamber]] for more information.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Kynryth Husk Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Kynryth Husk]] will use its [[Multiattack]] Action to make one [[Tentacle Swipe]] attack and use [[Tentacle Grab]].
>
> Over the course of combat, the Kynryth Husk will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Kynryth Husk will use its [[Multiattack]] Action.
> - Whenever able, the Kynryth Husk will [[Unknown]] to transform into a fully powered [[Kynryth]].
