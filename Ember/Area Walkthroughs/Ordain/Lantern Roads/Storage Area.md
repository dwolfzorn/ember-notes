# Storage Area

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The alley terminates in a dead end. A storage area has been cut into the stone: crates, barrels, rope, and bundled supplies sit in uneven stacks along the walls, leaving a narrow path through the middle. Most of the supplies look recently handled.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Storage Area
>
> A simple search reveals the following:
>
> - Crates of lantern oil, rope, cloth, and other basic supplies.
> - A large metal wall on the north end.
>
> Characters adjacent to the wall can hear a low, mechanical hum, and characters who touch the wall feel steady vibrations.
>
> #### The Metal Wall
>
> Any character with **Knowledge: Crafts** who examines the metal wall can tell that it is part of a much larger structure and would be extraordinarily difficult to break through from this side.
>
> The wall has AC 21, 27 Hit Points, immunity to Poison and Psychic damage, and a damage threshold of 20.
