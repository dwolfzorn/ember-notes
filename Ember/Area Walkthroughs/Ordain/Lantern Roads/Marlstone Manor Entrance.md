# Marlstone Manor Entrance

This stairway leads from Lantern Roads to the [[Guardhouse]] at the back of [[Marlstone Manor - First Floor]].

### Ascending to Marlstone Manor

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The stairs climb away from Lantern Roads in a long, winding rise. With each turn, Marlstone Manor grows larger above you, until its rear wall fills the view ahead. A squat guardhouse stands between the top of the stairs and the manor's back entrance.

### Descending from Marlstone Manor

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Marlstone Manor recedes behind you as the stairs wind back down toward Lantern Roads. Below, the neighborhood's lanterns burn in steady rows of warm light.
