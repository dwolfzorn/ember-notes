# Area Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Area Map Context
>
> The [[Marlstone Manor - First Floor]] Area Map depicts the entirety of Marlstone Manor. It is featured in the [[Casing the Joint]] and [[The Marlstone Gala]] Events of the [[Disgraced House]] Main Quest.
>
> - The Area Map depicts two Scene Levels: the First Floor and the Second Floor.
> - The characters arrive in the [[Entrance Plaza]].

## Gameplay Details

The areas of Marlstone Manor have the following features unless specified otherwise in the text.

### Levels & Elevation

The Marlstone Manor Scene features a total of two vertical Levels of elevation, including:

- **First Floor:** The first floor of Marlstone Manor is set to an elevation of 0 and has 20-foot-high ceilings.
- **Second Floor:** The second floor of Marlstone Manor is set to an elevation of +20 and has 20-foot-high ceilings.

### Sound

During the [[The Marlstone Gala]] Event, the combined conversation of guests makes it difficult to hear: all creatures inside the manor have **-2 Banes** on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sound.

### Illumination

Outdoor areas are subject to exterior lighting and weather conditions. Interior areas are well lit by candle light.

### Terrain

Treat all terrain as normal terrain.

### Inhabitants

### Secret Door

Marlstone Manor contains a hidden passage that connects [[Hephiss' Downstairs Closet]] to the underground facility beneath the manor. The door is magically concealed and cannot be opened until it is revealed.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Revealing the Secret Door
>
> To reveal the secret door, the party must first learn the correct passphrase and speak it aloud near the concealed doorway.
>
> A character who searches the back wall and makes a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check notices that the stonework does not quite match the surrounding wall.
>
> The correct passphrase is **Indigo**, a reference to Indigo Ray, Hephiss Wandren's favorite artist.
>
> The party can learn or infer this passphrase in the following ways:
>
> - By examining Hephiss Wandren's artwork in the [[Gallery]].
> - By using the catalogue in the [[Library]] to learn more about Hephiss Wandren's interest in Indigo Ray.
> - By discovering that the same passphrase opens the wall safe in [[Hephiss' Study]].
>
> When the passphrase is spoken aloud near the concealed doorway, the stonework shifts just enough to reveal a narrow seam. The wall can then be pulled open, revealing the secret door behind it.

The secret door is reinforced, locked, and coated in [[Thieves' Lament]].

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Locked Door
>
> The secret door is reinforced and locked, but can be unlocked with a successful `[[/skill sleightofhand 30 tool=thief]]`; alternatively, it can be bashed open with a successful **Athletics (DC 30)** check. Any character who attempts to bash the door down alerts all House Guards within 60 feet.
>
> Finally, the door can be unlocked using [[Hephiss' Keys]].
>
> #### Thieves' Lament
>
> Any character who touches the door is exposed to [[Thieves' Lament]].

### Manor Access

As the party moves through Marlstone Manor, each room has one of three access levels: **Open**, **Restricted**, or **Forbidden**.

**Open Rooms** can be entered and occupied without attracting attention unless the characters do something disruptive, such as breaking into a locked container, threatening someone, or otherwise causing a disturbance.

**Restricted Rooms** are rooms the characters may or may not have reason to enter depending on their role during the [[Casing the Joint]] Event. They do not have permission to enter Restricted Rooms during the [[The Marlstone Gala]] Event.

**Forbidden Rooms** are rooms no guest or ordinary staff member is permitted to enter.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Guard Rotation
>
> Restricted and Forbidden Rooms are periodically checked by House Guards. When the party enters one of these rooms without clear permission, roll `[[/gmroll 1d4+1]]`. The result is the number of minutes before the nearest House Guard moves to inspect the area for intruders.
>
> Every time a character searches the room or performs another time-consuming action inside the room (such as reading notes, picking a lock, etc.), 1 minute passes.
>
> If the party is discovered occupying a Restricted or Forbidden Room by a House Guard, they must attempt a group challenge to avoid raising their suspicion, with each party member rolling one of the following checks:
>
> - **Deception (DC 16)** to convince the House Guard that the party had permission to enter the room.
> - **Performance (DC 16)** to convince the House Guard that they made an honest mistake.
>
> When making any of the above checks:
>
> - **Path: Cosmopolitan Fashionista**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Forbidden Room:** The character suffers **-2 Banes** on this check if the area is a Forbidden Room.

### Using the Locator Rod

A character who uses the [[Searching for Funar]] inside the manor will activate its **Proximity Mode**, indicating Funar's earring is somewhere in the **Vicinity**.

The Locator Rod will emit a **Double Note** sound if activated on the west side of the [[Veranda]], indicating that Funar's earring is **Adjacent** (below) the area. The Locator Rod will emit a **Single Note** sound if activated in areas adjacent to the west side of the Veranda, indicating that Funar's earring is **Nearby**.
