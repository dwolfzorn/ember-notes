# East Wraparound Balcony

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Access Level
>
> This area is **Restricted**.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Though the light that pours into the windows from the eastern balcony is not quite as bright as that from the west, it creates a subtle glow that can be enjoyed by those strolling around the rooms on the east side of the manor.

The East wraparound balcony connects several rooms on the east side of the manor, including the [[Contemplation Room]], [[Guest Bathroom]], and [[Art Studio]]. It ends in the [[Elevated Terrace]], which has been set up as an outdoor seating area.
