# Garden Fountains

### [[Casing the Joint]]

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Access Level
>
> This area is **Open** to **Gardeners** and **Decorators**. This area is **Restricted** to **Kitchen Staff**.

The fountains are currently not in operation.

### [[The Marlstone Gala]]

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Access Level
>
> This area is **Open**.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Like the fountains at the front of the manor, these fountains make a gentle burbling sound that fades into the background while creating a sense of calm. You suddenly feel as if you couldn't get angry or sad while in the presence of the water even if you wanted to, as if an invisible hand was trying to lift your spirits.

The waters of the garden fountains run on a separate system than the front fountains, and use [[Calming Water]].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Collecting the Water
>
> The water from the Garden Fountains can be collected in a normal jug of water, but if any Guest or Staff member is within 30 feet, the character must succeed on a `[[/check stealth 17]]` or `[[/check sleightofhand]]` check to collect it without calling attention to themself, or their suspicion level raises.
>
> Once collected, the water has the same effect as standard water.
