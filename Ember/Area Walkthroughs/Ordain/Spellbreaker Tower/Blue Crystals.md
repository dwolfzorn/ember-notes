# Blue Crystals

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A triangular blue crystal glows within a larger triangular housing made from black basalt, its polished facets gleaming brightly.
>
> Thin rays of arcane energy spill from its edges in straight, shimmering lines, then curl and braid as they reach outward, entwining with distant strands drawn from other matching stones set around the tower.
>
> Where the currents meet, the air flickers with a faint, steady shimmer. Periodically these swirling eddies of energy spiral down to the large cluster of crystals at the center of the area.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Magical Intake
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 16)** check can adequately deduce that these azure crystals are feeding energy into the central [[Crystal Cluster]], and might be powered from elsewhere in the tower.
>
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Machines**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: Given the extensive anti-magic properties of the tower, the character can reasonably assume that these gems are part of the mechanism siphoning magic into the central cluster.
