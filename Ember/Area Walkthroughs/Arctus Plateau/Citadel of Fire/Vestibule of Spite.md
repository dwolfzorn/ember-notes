# Vestibule of Spite

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Purple-black coals smolder in dense clusters across the floor, casting a sickly violet light through the chamber. At the center rests a shallow Cinderspar basin filled with dark flame that gives off no warmth. A jagged violet crystal sits within the fire, pulsing faintly as the flames curl around it; faint images dance between its vertices.

The smoldering Voidfire in this chamber radiates a low, persistent psychic pressure.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Hazard: Voidfire Corruption
>
> The first time a creature enters this chamber, it must a `[[/save wisdom 15]]` saving throw or take `[[/damage 3d6 Psychic]]` damage as a sudden surge of anger and resentment overwhelms it. It must immediately make one attack against the nearest creature it can see. If multiple creatures are equally near, determine the target randomly.
>
> A creature that succeeds on this saving throw takes half damage and does not attack the nearest creature.
>
> After making this saving throw, regardless of the result, the creature becomes immune to this hazard (both here and in the [[Crystalline Conjunction]] area) until it finishes a &reference[Long Rest].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Vestibule of Spite
>
> A simple search of the chamber reveals the following:
>
> - The central basin is carved from dark Cinderspar and contains a heatless violet flame: pure Voidfire. A jagged crystal, a piece of [[Crystallized Spite]], rests within the flame.
> - Fine **Language: Scor** inscriptions surround the basin. Characters who can understand **Language: Scor** or consult a [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]] identify the inscriptions as scrying magic used to communicate with a single entity: Fiyara.
>
> #### Examining the Scrying Bowl
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check understands that the flame within the basin acts as a conduit to Kess, with the [[Crystallized Spite]]serving as its focus.
>
> - **Knowledge: Cosmology**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Attunement: The Abyss** and [[Unknown]] **or Higher:** The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> A character who places their hands upon the rim and concentrates while the [[Crystallized Spite]]remains within immediately exposes the creature to Kess' affliction. Read or paraphrase the following (you can also mix in results from the [[Kess the Corrupted Whispers]] table below):
>
> > The violet flame collapses inward, and suddenly the chamber is gone. A vast pressure of heat and hatred overwhelms your thoughts — but somewhere beyond it, a familiar voice breaks through in fragments, strained, frightened, and unmistakably Kess.
> >
> > … Help me … PLEASE … I can't …
> >
> > The voice is swallowed by a surge of fury that chokes your throat. For one terrible instant, something much larger seems to turn its attention toward you. You feel its baleful gaze bore a hole into your heart, and you hear a single word as hissing flame: OUT.
> >
> > Then the vision snaps apart, and you find yourself back in the Citadel.
>
> Each character can commune with Kess only once. Repeated communion allows limited communication with Kess, but to no tangible benefit: she cannot break Fiyara's influence. Roll on or choose from the table below:

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Claiming the Crystallized Spite
>
> The [[Crystallized Spite]]can be removed in the following ways:
>
> - A character with **Attunement: The Abyss** and [[Unknown]] or higher can take the coal without harm.
> - A creature with Resistance or Immunity to Psychic damage can handle the coal safely.
> - A character can use metal tongs or another suitable implement to remove it without touching it directly.
>
> A character who attempts to seize the crystal barehanded without such protection takes `[[/damage 4d6 Psychic]]` damage.
>
> Once the [[Crystallized Spite]]is removed from the basin, the flame within gutters out, and the scrying bowl can no longer be used.
