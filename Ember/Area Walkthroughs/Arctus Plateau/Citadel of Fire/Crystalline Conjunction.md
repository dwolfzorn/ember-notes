# Crystalline Conjunction

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The volcanic rock gives way to veins of exposed crystal, violet formations erupting from the floor and walls in dense, jagged clusters. Voidfire smolders between their crevices. Some are fractured or blackened by the heat, while others remain clear enough to catch the Voidfire's light and split it into shifting bands of color.

The smoldering Voidfire in this chamber radiates a low, persistent psychic pressure.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Hazard: Voidfire Corruption
>
> The first time a creature enters this chamber, it must a `[[/save wisdom 15]]` saving throw or take `[[/damage 3d6 Psychic]]` damage as a sudden surge of anger and resentment overwhelms it. It must immediately make one attack against the nearest creature it can see. If multiple creatures are equally near, determine the target randomly.
>
> A creature that succeeds on this saving throw takes half damage and does not attack the nearest creature.
>
> After making this saving throw, regardless of the result, the creature becomes immune to this hazard (both here and in the [[Vestibule of Spite]] area) until it finishes a &reference[Long Rest].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Crystalline Conjunction
>
> A simple search of the chamber reveals the following:
>
> - Scorched fragments of ancient [[Sedyri]] stonework remain embedded among the crystals. The stonework is engraved with markings too damaged to read.
> - The crystal growth appears to predate the [[Citadel of Fire]]: portions vanish beneath the more ancient masonry, suggesting they were exposed when the citadel's emergence disturbed the surrounding structure.
> - One such crystal growth appears abnormally geometric and clear: the [[Conjunction Prism]].
>
> #### Examining the Crystals
>
> Any character who examines the crystals and makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check recognizes that the crystals formed under prolonged exposure to tremendous elemental heat, and that their structure naturally absorbs and polarizes opposing elemental forces.
>
> - **Knowledge: Subterranea**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character can reasonably conclude that the crystals formed during the long life of the original Sedyri fire temple, and were later exposed when the Citadel of Fire displaced or shattered portions of the older complex.
>
> #### Claiming the Conjunction Prism
>
> The [[Conjunction Prism]]can be broken free from the surrounding formation with a successful **`[[/skill strength 14]]`** check using a suitable tool. On a failure, the [[Conjunction Prism]]is still recovered, but the character takes `[[/damage 2d6 Force]]` damage as the surrounding crystals discharge accumulated energies.
>
> - **Proficiency in** [[Unknown]]**:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
