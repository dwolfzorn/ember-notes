# Area Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Area Map Context
>
>  The [[Citadel of Fire]] Area Map depicts the entirety of the [[Citadel of Fire]], and is featured in the [[Burning Hearts]] Event of [[The Flood and the Flame]] Side Quest.
>
>  - The Area Map depicts four Scene Levels: Caldera, Lower Peak, Upper Peak, and the Crown.
> - This fortress of volcanic rock and black steel sits atop the ruins of an ancient [[Sedyri]] temple once dedicated to the force of fire.
> - The party arrives outside the [[Entrance]].

 ## Gameplay Details

 The areas of the Citadel of Fire have the following features unless specified otherwise in the text.

 ### Levels & Elevation

 The Citadel of Fire Scene features a total of four vertical Levels of elevation, including:

 - **Caldera:** The ground Level is set to an elevation of 0 and has 40-foot-high ceilings.
- **Lower Peak:** The second Level is set to an elevation of +40 and has 40-foot-high ceilings.
- **Upper Peak:** The third Level is set to an elevation of +80 and has 40-foot-high ceilings.
- **Crown:** The topmost Level is set to an elevation of +300 and has 40-foot-high ceilings.

 Make note of the various **Transition Regions** in the Scene, which will automatically transport characters to a different part of the map once entered.

 ### Illumination

 Natural features and fixtures may provide &reference[Dim Light] or &reference[Bright Light].

 ### Terrain

 The oppressive heat of the citadel counts as [[Extreme Heat]].

 ### Inhabitants

 An imprisoned, non-hostile [[Ignarch]] called Sathyr is imprisoned within the [[Caldera]].

 ### Enemies

 During the [[Burning Hearts]] Event, the following enemies are encountered here:

 - 2 [[Vexan]]
- 3 [[Fiyaran Cultist]]
- 3 [[Pyreling]]
- 1 [[Sirohawk]]
- 3 [[Voidfire Cinder]]
- [[Kess the Firemage]]
