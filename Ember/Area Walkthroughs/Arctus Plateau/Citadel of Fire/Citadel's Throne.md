# Citadel's Throne

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Before you lies a hall carved from volcanic rock and bracketed with black steel. A square platform of smoke-stained glass is set into the center; broad cauldrons of bubbling fire burn to either side. At the far end of the room, a throne of iron rises from a raised dais — upon it, Kess writhes and clutches at herself, scratching and clawing as if fighting against something inside of her skin. Small lidless eyes stare at her from the surrounding rock, their surfaces reflecting a scorched plane that does not belong to Ember.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Elevator Platform
>
> Characters can use this platform to descend to the [[Procession of Flame]].

Before combat begins, the characters may make one final plea to [[Kess the Firemage]] to resist the influence of [[Fiyara]] and stand down. It will inevitably prove unsuccessful, but may help contextualize Kess' behavior.

> [!info] Social
> #### A Final Plea
>
> With Fiyara whispering in her ear and Fiyara's Voidfire burning away Kess' empathy, the Kiska the party encounters here bears little resemblance to the kind young mage they once knew. However, attempts to communicate with Kess may provoke her into sharing the following:
>
> - **Kess's Motivations:** Kess tried to become a Flamewarden, but they found her too reckless. She believes they were jealous, and wants revenge for their rejection.
> - **Fiyara:** Kess relates to Fiyara because they were both ejected from their homes, and both feel betrayed.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **`[[/skill diplomacy 12]]`** check can tell that Kess absolutely cannot be reached, as Fiyara's influence (and the effect of Fiyara's Voidfire) is simply too great; in this state, Kess appears fundamentally incapable of trusting the party's motivations, and will always assume the worst.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About Kess's motivations?
>
> **A:**
>
> > No one understands me. They never have. I just wanted to protect people! After my family home burned down, I tried to join the Flamewardens, but **nooooooooo**, I didn't follow **'tradition,'** I took too many **'risks.'**
> >
> > They were just jealous of my magic! No one has **EVER** appreciated what I can do! Not until Fiyara … my Mistress of Hate … she wants me to **EMBRACE** my power — to actually **use** what I can do!
> >
> > I'm gonna show the Flamewardens, and Tajar, and everyone **exactly** how much I'm capable of. In the last moments before my Mistress's flames consume them, they'll finally understand that they should have **LISTENED!**
>
> The Kiska wraps her arms around her shoulders, rocking back and forth as she begins whispering to herself.
>
> > It's the only way … it's the only way … the only way!!

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About Fiyara?
>
> **A:**
>
> > We're not so different, Fiyara and I. Both our homes went up in flames!!
> >
> > I'll never forget during the Great Burning, when my moms and I stood outside the home that our family had lived in for generations … where were the Flamewardens then?! What were **they** doing?! They're just like Lorgi and Ravela, forcing my Mistress out of her home just because she discovered a truth that the people around her couldn't understand.
> >
> > They **betrayed** us! Abandoned us, when we needed them most! And now they're **all** going to **PAY!!**

### Battling Kess

After Kess becomes too frustrated — or the party ceases trying to reach her — the battle begins.

> [!abstract] Kess the Firemage
> **[[Kess the Firemage]]**
>
> Level 1 · Unknown Unknown
>
> ![Kess the Firemage](../../../assets/image117.webp)

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Kess the Corrupted Tactics: Phase 1
>
> At the start of combat, Kess the Corrupted will use her [[Unknown]] Action and [[Unknown]] Bonus Action to hurt and debilitate as many enemies as possible.
>
> Over the course of combat, Kess the Corrupted will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, Kess will use her [[Unknown]] Action to make two [[Unknown]] attacks.
> - If Kess fails a saving throw, she can expend a use of [[Unknown]] to succeed instead.
> - If Kess is threatened in melee, she will use her [[Unknown]] Legendary Action; otherwise, she will use her [[Unknown]] Legendary Action.
> - Kess will use her [[Unknown]] Action and [[Unknown]] Bonus Action whenever able.
>
> When Kess becomes &reference[Bloodied] for the first time, her [[Unknown]] feature immediately triggers, shunting everyone onto the [[Procession of Flame]] below. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Suddenly, Kess lets loose a bloodcurdling scream, as if torn from her throat by something else. The caldera below surges with Voidfire. Violet flames erupt around her in a spinning vortex; the glass platform in the center of the chamber shatters, and you are pulled down, buffeted by the winds of Kess' rage before landing with a crash on the platform below.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Kess the Corrupted Tactics: Phase 2
>
> Kess uses the same tactics as [[Citadel's Throne]], with the following changes:
>
> - She gains a Fly Speed and a hover feature: you must manually activate her [[Unknown]] feature to grant her these benefits.
> - She gains access to her [[Unknown]] Action, which she can use as part of her [[Unknown]] Action or [[Unknown]] Legendary Action.
> - Her [[Unknown]] Legendary Action allows her to move up to her full Speed without provoking &reference[Opportunity Attacks].
> - If Sathyr remains bound within the caldera below, Kess can use her [[Unknown]] Legendary Action. If the characters freed Sathyr without her knowledge, Kess should attempt to use this ability once — expending Legendary Actions as normal — to no effect; roleplay her surprise and anger at this discovery!
>
> #### Falling Off the Platform
>
> A character knocked off the platform by [[Unknown]] can make a `[[/save Dexterity 12]]` saving throw to grasp the platform's edge and avoid falling into the caldera below. They can expend 10 feet of movement to climb back onto the platform.
>
> A creature that falls into the caldera lands &reference[prone], taking `[[/damage average 8d6 Bludgeoning]]` damage (from the fall) and `[[/damage average 10d10 Fire]]` damage (from the lava). A creature that starts its turn in lava takes `[[/damage average 10d10 Fire]]` damage.
>
> When Kess is reduced to 0 Hit Points, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Kess collapses as the last of the Voidfire gutters out around her. She lies unconscious and barely breathing, her body scarred and trembling to the faint pulse of the Spark's magic.

The magic of the Spark is keeping Kess alive, but the young firemage remains unconscious. Continue with the [[Burning Hearts]] section of the [[Burning Hearts]] Event.
