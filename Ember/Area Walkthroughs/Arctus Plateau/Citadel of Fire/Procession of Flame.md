# Procession of Flame

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A vast platform of thick, glowing glass stretches across this chamber, suspended above the churning caldera below. Intricate lines of molten light twist beneath its surface in looping patterns. A narrow causeway leads north to a platform of blackened steel.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Elevator Platform
>
> Characters can use this platform to ascend to [[Citadel's Throne]].
