# Vestibule of Fire

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Volcanic heat radiates from every surface of this chamber, fed by channels of molten stone that glow along its walls and floor. At the center rests a shallow stone basin filled with living flame, its surface rippling like liquid glass. A single black-red coal burns within the fire without being consumed; faint images seem to stir beneath the blaze.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Vestibule of Fire
>
> A simple search of the chamber reveals the following:
>
> - The central basin is carved from dark Cinderspar and contains a persistent, liquid-like flame. Suspended within it is a single black-red [[Everburning Coal]]: a piece of [[Ragen]] itself brought to Ember.
> - Fine **Language: Scor** inscriptions surround the basin. Characters who can understand **Language: Scor** or consult a [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]] identify the inscriptions as scrying magic used to communicate with elemental forces on Ragen.
>
> #### Examining the Scrying Bowl
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check understands that the flame within the basin acts as a conduit to Ragen, with the [[Everburning Coal]]serving as its focus.
>
> - **Knowledge: Cosmology**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Attunement: Ragen** and [[Unknown]] **or Higher:** The character automatically succeeds on this check.
>
> A character who places their hands upon the rim and concentrates while the [[Everburning Coal]]remains within can briefly perceive Ragen through the flame. Read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > The fire flattens into a glassy red surface. For an instant, the chamber falls away, replaced by a scorched horizon beneath a smoldering sky. Vast shapes move through distant gouts of flame and smoke; they turn languorously toward you, as if awaiting your word.
>
> The character can ask a single question concerning the elemental forces within the citadel; the Gamemaster should answer with a brief image, sensation, or symbolic vision rather than direct speech. Once answered, the character is forced away from Ragen. Each character can commune with Ragen only once.
>
> #### Claiming the Everburning Coal
>
> The [[Everburning Coal]]can be removed in the following ways:
>
> - A character with **Attunement: Ragen** and [[Unknown]] or higher can take the coal without harm.
> - A creature with Resistance or Immunity to Fire damage can handle the coal safely.
> - A character can use metal tongs or another suitable heat-resistant implement to remove it without touching it directly.
>
> A character who attempts to seize the coal barehanded without such protection takes `[[/damage 4d6 Fire]]` damage.
>
> Once the [[Everburning Coal]]is removed from the basin, the flame within gutters out, and the scrying bowl can no longer be used.
