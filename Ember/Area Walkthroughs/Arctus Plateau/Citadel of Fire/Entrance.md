# Entrance

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The entrance chamber is composed of dark volcanic rock and runed steel that glows a fiery orange. Waves of heat pulsate from beyond the northern doors, carrying the roar of bubbling fire and the steady cadence of distant chanting.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Entrance
>
> A simple search of the chamber reveals the following:
>
> - Lines of glowing **Language: Scor** runes have been etched into the steel, their light brightening and fading with the nearby flow of heat.
> - The sound of several voices chanting in unison carries through the northern doors, accompanied by an occasional deep-bellowing of pain from some large creature.
>
> #### Reading the Runes
>
> Any character who understands **Language: Scor** recognizes the inscriptions as a devotional mantra celebrating fire as a force of transformation through which weakness is burned away. A character who can read the mantra translates it as:
>
> > What cannot survive the flame is not meant to remain.
>
> Characters who consult the [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]] achieve a paraphrased understanding of the text.
>
> #### Listening at the Doors
>
> Any character who listens at the doors and makes a successful **Awareness (DC 14)** check picks out the [[Fiyaran Cultist]] chanting in **Language: Scor**. A character who can understand **Language: Scor** translates the words as powerful binding magic.
