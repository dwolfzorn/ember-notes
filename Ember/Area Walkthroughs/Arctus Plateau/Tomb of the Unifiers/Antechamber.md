# Antechamber

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Ankle-deep water covers the floor of this long stone chamber. Four pillars of knotted volcanic rock rise from the water, their surfaces carved with crisp pictographs seemingly untouched by age. More reliefs line the walls, showing robed figures carrying bowls of flame in a solemn procession.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Antechamber
>
> A simple search of the antechamber reveals the following:
>
> - Pictographic reliefs have been carved into the Cinderspar walls, as vivid today as when they were first inscribed.
> - Four Cinderspar pillars, similarly inscribed with pictographic reliefs, rise from the water, two on each side of the room.
>
> #### Examining the Pictographs
>
> Any character who examines the pictographic reliefs can identify a scene depicting four pillars surrounding a chamber filled with steaming water. Robed acolytes set bowls of fire at the base of each pillar, suggesting that all four must be heated.
>
> A character who makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check recognizes the scene as part of a larger triptych: the first scene is shown wreathed in steam, the second sealed beneath ice, and the final scene divided between the two.
>
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Language: Scor**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character has a** [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they consult it.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also notices that two serpentine guardians appear throughout the sequence. In the final scene, both stand beside a raised sarcophagus in opposing states: one surrounded by fire, the other by frost.
>
> #### Examining the Pillars
>
> Any character who examines a Control Pillar notices a small hollow just below the waterline. The hollow bears signs of repeated exposure to extreme heat and cold.
>
> #### Changing the Water
>
> The standing water in the Antechamber begins in a **Neutral** state.
>
> - When all four Control Pillars are heated, the water becomes **Superheated**.
> - When all four Control Pillars are cooled, the water becomes **Frozen**.
> - Any other combination causes the water to revert to **Neutral**.
>
> Changing the standing water's temperature state can also revive an &reference[incapacitated] Hydrotherm through its [[Elemental Reconstitution]] feature.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Hydrotherms Attack
>
> After the characters interact with a Control Pillar, two [[Antechamber]] and attack. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Suddenly, a hiss of steam bursts from the two snake-head statues, and their jaws unhinge wide — impossibly wide. Two large, serpentine creatures uncoil from the statues' mouths with liquid grace, their glassy eyes fixed on you.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Token Reveal: Hydrotherms
>
> The two [[Hydrotherm]] begin the encounter **Hidden** in the Scene. To better see these Hidden tokens, hold down the Left Alt Key with Token Controls active. When you are ready for combat to begin, **Reveal** the **Hidden** Hydrotherms to represent them materializing.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Hydrotherm Tactics
>
> The right [[Hydrotherm]] begins combat in its [[Frozen Form]]. The left [[Hydrotherm]] begins combat in its [[Superheated Form]].
>
> If the water surrounding a Hydrotherm becomes **Frozen** while it is in its Superheated Form, it will transition to its Frozen Form; if the water surrounding a Hydrotherm becomes **Superheated** while it is in its Frozen Form, it will transition to its Superheated Form. Use the Effects tab on the Actor's sheet to toggle its forms off and on as needed.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Hydrotherms will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - The Hydrotherms will use their [[Multiattack]] to make two [[Scalding Slam]] or [[Pressurized Jet]] attacks, depending on their current form.
> - A Hydrotherm reduced to 0 Hit Points becomes &reference[Incapacitated] until revived by its [[Elemental Reconstitution]] feature.
> - A Hydrotherm can quickly reposition to a different part of the Scene using its [[Snake Passage]] Bonus Action.
>
> The Hydrotherms fight until &reference[Incapacitated].
>
> #### Kess' Assistance
>
> If Kess is accompanying the party, she can help fend off the Hydrotherm guardians. Over the course of combat, Kess will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, Kess will cast her most powerful fire-based spells.
> - If struck in combat, Kess may cast [[Unknown]] to protect herself.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Opening the Secret Door
>
> The secret door opens whenever the standing water in this chamber is **Superheated** and closes if the chamber enters any other temperature state. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Steam coils across the surface of the water as the final hollow catches alight; the reliefs seem to shiver in the heat. Along the southeast wall, the volcanic rock begins to grind and shift, and a seam appears where there was only stone before. Then the recessed slab turns, revealing a chamber beyond.

> [!info] Social
> #### Kess' Assistance
>
> If the party is unable to solve the puzzle on their own and Kess is accompanying them, Kess can offer a hint or arrive at the correct conclusion after observing the pictographic reliefs.
>
> > One of these reliefs shows acolytes taking fire below the surface of the water to set the pillars alight … why would they do that? And these two … **creatures** keep recurring throughout as a motif. Maybe they're not just monsters, but … something else?
