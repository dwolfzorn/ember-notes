# Area Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Area Map Context
>
> The [[Tomb of the Unifiers]] Area Map depicts a long-abandoned tomb for ancient Sedyri mage-priests. It is featured in the [[Summoning the Spark]] Event of the [[The Flood and the Flame]] Side Quest.
>
> - The Area Map depicts 1 Scene Level that encompasses the tomb and the area immediately outside of it.
> - The party arrives in the narrow gorge outside the tomb's [[Antechamber]].

## Gameplay Details

The areas of the Tomb of the Unifiers have the following features unless specified otherwise in the text.

### Levels & Elevation

The Tomb of the Unifiers Scene features a single Level, and its areas are organized into a single section.

- **Tomb of the Unifiers:** The Tomb of the Unifiers is set to an elevation of 0 and has 20-foot-high ceilings.

### Illumination

Aside from occasional wall-mounted gems that shed bright blue light, the Tomb of the Unifiers is completely dark.

The unmarked gorge area is subject to exterior lighting and weather conditions.

### Terrain

Certain areas have ankle-deep water, but it does not count as &reference[Difficult Terrain]. Areas with ankle-deep water also have one or more dry areas.

### Enemies

The following enemies are encountered here:

- 2 [[Hydrotherm]]

### Opening the Sarcophagus

In order to unlock the north sarcophagus in the [[Unifier Plenary]] and recover the [[Vessel of the Spark]], the characters must balance the forces of fire and water throughout the tomb by manipulating the standing water temperature of its chambers. As the water becomes **Superheated** or **Frozen**, the Cinderspar expands or contracts, changing the composition of the tomb and activating hidden mechanisms buried in its structure.

The standing water temperature of a chamber is determined by **Control Pillars**, which can be heated or cooled using fire or cold magic, respectively.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Operating the Control Pillars
>
> Each Control Pillar is an ​ **Interactable Object**. To see all Interactable Objects on a Scene Level, you may hold down the Left Alt Key.
>
> As the Gamemaster, you can Left Click on the Interactable Object to bring up a dialogue window. In this window, you'll see three fields:
>
> - **Heated:** When all Control Pillars in a section are heated, the water becomes **Superheated**.
> - **Neutral:** A mixed combination causes the water to revert to **Neutral**.
> - **Cooled:** When all Control Pillars in a section are cooled, the water becomes **Frozen**.

To unlock the north sarcophagus in the [[Unifier Plenary]], the following conditions must be met:

- The [[Antechamber]] is **Superheated**.
- The [[Chamber of Memory]] is **Frozen**.
- The [[Unifier Plenary]] is balanced: one half of the room must be **Frozen**, and the other half must be **Superheated**.
- Both Hydrotherms are active, with one in Superheated Form and the other in Frozen Form.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Managing the Hydrotherms
>
> The two Hydrotherms are intended to remain active participants in the tomb's puzzle rather than conventional enemies to be permanently defeated. Defeating them can make exploration temporarily easier, but both must eventually be restored via their [[Elemental Reconstitution]] feature in opposing forms to unlock the north sarcophagus in the Unifier Plenary.
>
> The characters must carefully manage the Hydrotherm's aggression, forms, and the temperature state of each room in order to solve the puzzle.

Once these conditions are met, the Hydrotherms cease attacking. Each enters the Unifier Plenary (if not already present) and takes up a position on opposite sides of the northern sarcophagus.
