# Chamber of Memory

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This chamber lies submerged beneath steaming, ankle-deep water. Four Cinderspar pillars stand at each corner of the room, burning bright.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Chamber of Memory
>
> A simple search of the chamber reveals the following:
>
> - Pictographic reliefs have been carved into the Cinderspar pillars.
>
> #### Examining the Pictographs
>
> Any character who examines the pictographic reliefs can identify a scene depicting frost spreading from congregated acolytes toward the center of the chamber.
>
> A character who makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check notices that serpentine guardians appear throughout the reliefs alongside the acolytes. In the final scene, two such guardians stand beside a raised sarcophagus in opposing states: one surrounded by fire, the other by frost.
>
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Language: Scor**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character has a** [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they consult it.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also notices a scene depicting a fallen guardian lying inert beneath the water. As the water around it changes to a new state, the guardian rises again in a different form.
>
> #### Examining the Pillars
>
> Any character who examines a pillar notices a small hollow just below the waterline. The hollow bears signs of repeated exposure to extreme heat and cold.
>
> #### Changing the Water
>
> The standing water in the Chamber of Memory begins in a **Superheated** state.
>
> - When all four Control Pillars are heated, the water becomes **Superheated**.
> - When all four Control Pillars are cooled, the water becomes **Frozen**.
> - Any other combination causes the water to revert to **Neutral**.
>
> Changing the standing water's temperature state can also revive an &reference[incapacitated] Hydrotherm through its [[Elemental Reconstitution]] feature.
>
> #### Secret Door Failsafe
>
> If the secret door closes while the characters are inside the tomb, the Chamber of Memory provides a failsafe. Making the standing water in this chamber **Frozen** causes the secret door to open, regardless of the Antechamber's current temperature state.
