# Exterior

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A massive door of dark volcanic rock stands inset into the canyon wall, its surface nearly flush with the surrounding stone. Weathered carvings are etched into its face.

> [!info] Social
> #### Speaking with Kess
>
> For Kess' conversation points, refer to the [[Summoning the Spark]] section of the [[Summoning the Spark]] Event.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Examining the Door
>
> A cursory examination of the door reveals the following:
>
> - The slab is made from Cinderspar and fits tightly into grooves cut into the surrounding rock. Despite its age and immense weight, the door shows little erosion or structural damage. It can be pulled outward by hand.
> - Weathered pictographs and lines of ancient **Language: Scor** have been carved across its face. A character who can read the inscription translates it as:
>
> > What lies divided must be made whole. The Flood and the Flame are one.
>
> Characters who consult the [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]] achieve a paraphrased understanding of the text.
