# Unifier Plenary

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The tomb opens into a broad ceremonial chamber, its floor covered by a thin sheet of glassy ice. Four pillars of dark Cinderspar rise around a central strip of volcanic rock, while sarcophagi rest in rows along the walls. Three lie to either side of the chamber, with a seventh raised above them on a platform to the north.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Plenary
>
> A simple search of the plenary reveals the following:
>
> - Seven sealed sarcophagi line the walls: three on each side, and one on a raised platform on the north. Each is covered by plates of volcanic rock carved with pictographic reliefs.
> - Four pillars rise from the frozen water, two on each side of the room.
> - The standing water in this chamber is **Frozen** when the characters first arrive.
> - The dark stone running through the center of the room is slightly raised above the surrounding water, suggesting the water is divided into two sections.
>
> #### Examining the Sarcophagi Exteriors
>
> The pictographic reliefs carved into the sarcophagi depict the lives and funerals of the Unifiers interred within. Though the individual scenes differ, the final relief on each sarcophagus follows a consistent pattern: attendants approach from opposite sides bearing symbols of fire and water, which meet around the sealed dead.
>
> A character who examines these reliefs and makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check recognizes that neither force is depicted as overcoming the other. Instead, the rites emphasize holding fire and water in equal opposition.
>
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character has a** [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they consult it.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also notices two serpentine guardians depicted beside the northern sarcophagus. One radiates heat and steam, while the other is surrounded by frost, mirroring the opposing forces represented elsewhere in the reliefs.
>
> #### Examining the Pillars
>
> Any character who examines a pillar notices a small hollow just below the waterline. The hollow bears signs of repeated exposure to extreme heat and cold.
>
> #### Changing the Water
>
> The standing water in the Unifier Plenary is divided into **two halves**. Both halves begin in a **Frozen** state.
>
> Each half's temperature state is controlled by its two pillars.
>
> - When both Control Pillars are heated, the water in that half becomes **Superheated**.
> - When both Control Pillars are cooled, the water in that half becomes **Frozen**.
> - Any other combination causes the water in that half to revert to **Neutral**.
>
> Changing the standing water's temperature state can also revive an &reference[incapacitated] Hydrotherm through its [[Elemental Reconstitution]] feature.
>
> #### Unlocking the North Sarcophagus
>
> To unlock the north sarcophagus, the following conditions must be met:
>
> - The Antechamber is **Superheated**.
> - The Chamber of Memory is **Frozen**.
> - The Unifier Plenary is balanced: one half of the room must be **Frozen**, and the other half must be **Superheated**.
> - Both Hydrotherms are active, with one in Superheated Form and the other in Frozen Form.
>
> Once these conditions are met, the Hydrotherms cease attacking. Each enters the Unifier Plenary (if not already present) and takes up a position on opposite sides of the northern sarcophagus.
>
> Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Steam curls from one side of the sarcophagus as frost spreads across the other. The two snake-like creatures coil beside the raised platform, their Cinderspar bodies glowing in opposing colors. A deep vibration passes through the chamber, followed by a series of heavy clicks from within the stone. Slowly, the plates sealing the sarcophagi shift loose.

> [!info] Social
> #### Kess' Assistance
>
> If the party has reached the Plenary but is unable to determine the final configuration, Kess can compare the sarcophagi reliefs to the instructions discovered earlier.
>
> > These must be the Unifiers … what's left of them, anyway. Let's see … it seems like water and fire must meet here, somehow, and those two guardians are shown beside the sarcophagus, one hot and one cold.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Investigating the Sarcophagi Interiors
>
> Each sarcophagus contains the remains of an ancient Sedyri Unifier. Only the north sarcophagus is sealed.
>
> - Their bodies are remarkably well preserved, wrapped in shrouds and desiccated by age; their shrouds were once brightly colored, but are now faded and brittle.
> - Their webbed hands are folded across their chests around personal objects, including small Cinderspar funerary statuettes depicting the Unifier in life.
> - Each Unifier is adorned with  **6d20** worth of ancient Sedyri jewelry.
>
> The Unifier in the north sarcophagus clutches the [[Vessel of the Spark]].
>
> The Unifier in the bottom right sarcophagus wears a [[Ring of the Unifier]].
