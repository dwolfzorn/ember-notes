# Hall of Memory

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The air in this narrow hall is dry, hot, and smells faintly of sulfur. Ahead, the passage bends into shadow, its far end hidden from view.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Steam Traps
>
> Several sections of the Hall of Memory are protected by ancient steam traps built into the Cinderspar walls. Each trap consists of a concealed pressure plate connected to rows of narrow bronze tubes hidden throughout the walls.
>
> When a creature steps onto a pressure plate, the tubes immediately vent a blast of superheated steam across a 15-foot-long section of hallway. Each creature in the affected area must make a `[[/save Dexterity 15]]` saving throw, taking `[[/damage 4d6 Fire]]` damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> Once triggered, a steam trap requires 1 minute to build sufficient pressure before it can activate again.
>
> #### Detecting a Steam Trap
>
> A character searching a section of hallway can detect a steam trap with a successful **Awareness (DC 15)** or **Awareness (DC 15)** check. On a success, the character notices the following signs:
>
> - Tiny bronze openings concealed within the Cinderspar walls.
> - Small deposits of pale mineral residue collecting in grooves adjacent to the bronze openings; the residue smells of sulfur.
>
> A character who detects a steam trap can approximate the area affected by the trap, as well as the location of its pressure plate.
>
> #### Disabling a Steam Trap
>
> A character adjacent to a discovered pressure plate can disable it with a successful `[[/skill sleightofhand 15 tool=thief]]` or jam it with a successful **`[[/skill strength 20]]`** check using a suitable implement. On a success, the character wedges the plate in place or disconnects the pressure mechanism, preventing the trap from triggering. On a failure, the trap is activated.
>
> Alternatively, the characters can disable or circumvent a steam trap in any of the following ways:
>
> - **Jam the Vents.** The characters can block enough of the bronze tubes to prevent the trap from venting safely.
> - **Bypass the Pressure Plate.** A creature can jump over the pressure plate to avoid activating it.
> - **Trigger it Deliberately.** The pressure plate can be pressed deliberately from a safe distance using a pole or thrown object; the trap will remain inactive for 1 minute afterward.
