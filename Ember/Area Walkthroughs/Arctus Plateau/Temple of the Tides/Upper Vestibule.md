# Upper Vestibule

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Semi-translucent yellow fronds push through cracks along the walls, gathered beneath the blank stare of a snake-head statue. At the center of the room, a large serpentine form lies collapsed and motionless across the stone floor — beyond the eastern doorway, intermittent flashes of colored light pulse against the walls.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Fallen Hydrotherm
>
> A [[Hydrotherm]] lies &reference[incapacitated] in its Superheated Form in the center of this chamber. It can be revived by cooling the water around it with cold magic, triggering its [[Elemental Reconstitution]] feature.
>
> If revived, this Hydrotherm is hostile to other elemental creatures within the temple and the party unless one of the characters is wearing the [[Ring of the Unifier]].
