# West Maze Chamber

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This square chamber is spare and undecorated, its cracked Cinderspar floor scattered with silt and loose stone. Two glowing fixtures wash the area in a pale blue light, while a heavy, ornate hand wheel is set into the masonry beneath.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Maze Wheel #2
>
> The second Hand Wheel required to navigate the temple's maze is located here. The Hand Wheel is an ​ **Interactable Object**. To see all Interactable Objects on a Scene Level, you may hold down the Left Alt Key. Refer to [[Area Overview]] for a full walkthrough.
