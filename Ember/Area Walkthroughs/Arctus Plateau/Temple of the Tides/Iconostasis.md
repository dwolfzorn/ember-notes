# Iconostasis

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A spacious hall opens before you, its walls carved with intricate bas reliefs depicting figures in various acts of ritual and ceremony. In the center of the chamber is a recessed floor where two large water elemental creatures float before a raised pedestal on the northern wall; smaller motes of animated water orbit their bodies in slow, restless currents. The larger elementals emit light from the center of their bodies — pressure surges from one of the snake-head statues, and a grinding mass of Cinderspar plates twists free from its recess and uncoils into the chamber.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Elemental Guardians
>
> As the characters enter the room, the [[Benthweaver]] are in the process of awakening a [[Hydrotherm]]. The Hydrotherm emerges as soon as combat begins.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Hydrotherm Tactics
>
> The [[Hydrotherm]] begins combat in its [[Superheated Form]].
>
> If a character freezes the water surrounding the Hydrotherm via cold magic while it is in its Superheated Form, it will transition to its Frozen Form; if a character heats the water surrounding the Hydrotherm via fire magic while it is in its Frozen Form, it will transition to its Superheated Form. Use the Effects tab on the Actor's sheet to toggle its forms off and on as needed.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Hydrotherm will prioritize the following Actions and abilities:
>
> - If the Hydrotherm is hostile to the party, it will split its attacks evenly between the characters and the water elementals.
> - The Hydrotherm will use its [[Multiattack]] to make two [[Scalding Slam]] or [[Pressurized Jet]] attacks, depending on its current form.
> - A Hydrotherm reduced to 0 Hit Points becomes &reference[Incapacitated] until revived by its [[Elemental Reconstitution]] feature.
> - A Hydrotherm can quickly reposition to a different part of the Scene using its [[Snake Passage]] Bonus Action.
>
> The Hydrotherm fights until &reference[Incapacitated].
>
> #### Benthweaver Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Benthweaver]] will use its [[Icy Whorl]] Action on as many enemies as possible.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Benthweaver will prioritize the following Actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, the Benthweaver will use its [[Multiattack]] Action.
> - The Benthweaver can use its [[Waterstep]] Bonus Action defensively to move away from enemies, or offensively to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} enemies in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
> - The Benthweaver will use its [[Icy Whorl]] Action whenever it is available.
> - [[Icy Whorl]] can revive a Hydrotherm through its [[Elemental Reconstitution]] feature and cause it to shift forms through its [[Elemental Polarity]] feature.
>
> The Benthweaver fights until destroyed.
>
> #### Droplet Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Droplet]] will target the nearest creature with its [[Smother]] Action.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Droplet will prioritize the following Actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Droplet will attempt to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} enemies using its [[Smother]] Action in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
>
> The Droplet fights until destroyed.

### Exploring the Iconostasis

After the elementals are destroyed, the characters can investigate the area.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Maze Wheel #1
>
> The first Hand Wheel required to navigate the temple's maze is located here. The Hand Wheel is an ​ **Interactable Object**. To see all Interactable Objects on a Scene Level, you may hold down the Left Alt Key. Refer to [[Area Overview]] for a full walkthrough.
>
> #### The Spark and the Ripple
>
> Any character with**Awareness (DC 14, Passive)** notices a conspicuous but aesthetically unusual symmetry to the arrangement of the bas reliefs, suggesting that their position and arrangement convey information, rather than serving decorative purpose.
>
> - **Knowledge: Arts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> Any character who inspects the bas reliefs and makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** check recognizes the symbology as belonging to the [[Sedyri]] Unifiers, indicating this temple once served a ritual purpose for their order, along with a specific thematic arrangement: water on the outside, fire within it, and the unity of both at the center.
>
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character is aware of the conspicuous symmetries:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> Any character who inspects the bas reliefs and makes a successful **Arcana (DC 16)** check realizes that intricate geometries of the reliefs are physical depictions of underlying arcane formulae — a series of techniques for balancing the arcane energies of fire and water.
>
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **The character is aware of both the symmetries and thematic arrangement:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **The character is not aware of either the symmetries or thematic arrangement:** The character suffers **-2 Banes** on this check.
>
> A character who derives these techniques will be better equipped to derive the correct ritual for summoning the Ripple of the Rivers during the [[Tidebound Temple]] section of [[Tidebound Temple]] or the [[Burning Hearts]] section of [[Burning Hearts]].
>
