# Diaconicon

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Broken pottery litters the edges of this dark chamber and lies scattered among cracked tiles and pockets of silt. Most of the vessels have collapsed into jagged fragments, though a few larger jars remain intact along the walls, their surfaces dulled by age and discolored by prolonged submersion.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Searching the Jars
>
> Three intact jars of the Gamemaster's choice contain small pieces of treasure: roll on the [[Temple of the Tides Treasure]] table below to determine what they contain.
>
> |  | #### Temple of the Tides Treasure |
> | --- | --- |
> | 1 | #### Ritual Herbs  Waterlogged bundles of ritual herbs: dark, fibrous clumps that cloud the water when disturbed. |
> | 2 | #### Mineral Salts  Thumb-sized Cinderspar vials sealed with a translucent, hardened resin; contains coarse mineral salts, still dry. Worth  **1d6** to cooking connoisseurs. |
> | 3 | #### Sedyri Artisan Tools  A set of ancient Sedyri artisan's tools (Gamemaster's choice), covered in green corrosion; worth  **30**. |
> | 4 | #### Ancient Coins  Ancient copper coins fused together beneath thick layers of verdigris; worth  **3d10** once carefully separated. |
> | 5 | #### Votive Candles  `[[/gmroll 1d4]]` small votive [[Sedyri Candle]] of pale wax set in Cinderspar cups. The wax and wicks are surprisingly intact; each cup is engraved with paired flame-and-water motifs, but several have had the flame symbols deliberately chipped away. The cups are worth  **1d6** each.  These candles can be lit underwater while within the Temple of the Tides. |
> | 6 | #### Funerary Effigies  `[[/gmroll 1d4]]` tiny funerary effigies carved from Cinderspar, depicting hooded Ashka. Flecks of sapphire are inset into the eye sockets. Each effigy is worth  **2d6**. |
