# Area Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Area Map Context
>
> The [[Temple of the Tides]] Area Map depicts the entirety of the [[Sedyri]] water temple, and is featured in the [[Tidebound Temple]] of [[The Flood and the Flame]] Side Quest.
>
> - The Area Map depicts three Scene Levels: Temple Grounds, Upper Temple, and Lower Temple.
> - This ancient Sedyri temple was once dedicated to the concept of Unification, but was later rededicated solely to the force of water amid the twilight of the Sedyri culture.
> - The party arrives on the far side of the [[Chasm]]. When the party spawns, change each character's primary movement speed to their Swim speed.

## Gameplay Details

The areas of the Temple of the Tides have the following features unless specified otherwise in the text.

### Levels & Elevation

The Temple of the Tides Scene features a total of three vertical Levels of elevation, including:

- **Temple Grounds:** The ground level is set to an elevation of 0 and has 40-foot-high ceilings.
- **Upper Temple:** The top level is set to an elevation of +40 and has 40-foot-high ceilings.
- **Lower Temple:** The lower level is set to an elevation of -40 and has 40-foot-high ceilings.

Make note of the various **Transition Regions** in the Scene, which will automatically transport characters to a different part of the map once entered.

### Sound

Since the entire temple lies underwater, **`[[/skill Perception]]`** checks that rely on sound and smell are made with **-2 Banes**.

### Illumination

Rare fixtures or natural features may provide &reference[Dim Light]{Dim Light}, but aside from those, treat the temple as having no light.

### Terrain

During [[Tidebound Temple]], the [[Unknown]] grants the characters a Swim Speed and the ability to breathe underwater. If the characters return without these boons, refer to the [[Unknown]] and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocation} rules.

Various [[Narthex]] are scattered throughout the temple. These plants have pockets of oxygen trapped in their stems; breaking them open allows a creature to draw a single breath.

### Enemies

The following enemies are encountered here:

- 4 [[Benthweaver]]
- 4 [[Riptongue]]
- 5 [[Tidegazer]]
- 8 [[Droplet]]
- 3 [[Hydrotherm]]
- 1 [[Spawn of Bwagaw]]

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Managing Hydrotherms
>
> The Hydrotherms in the temple are hostile to all other water elementals; they are trying to quell these angry creatures in a futile effort to rebalance the temple.
>
> If a character retrieved the [[Ring of the Unifier]] from the [[Tomb of the Unifiers]] and wears it, they may be able to use these Hydrotherms as temporary allies. Even with the ring, however, the Hydrotherms will not leave the temple grounds.
>
> If no character wears the [[Ring of the Unifier]], the Hydrotherms will treat the party as enemies.

### Water Maze

The ancient Sedyri would test the resolve of new initiates by forcing them to blindly traverse an underwater maze. In order for the characters to reach the [[Undercroft]] and rescue [[Fesira Inari]], they must undergo a similar trial.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Navigating the Maze
>
> Pressurized currents flow through the maze, preventing the party from moving in certain directions. The party can change the direction of these pressurized currents by finding and manipulating **Hand Wheels** throughout the temple. Each Hand Wheel is an ​ **Interactable Object**. To see all Interactable Objects on a Scene Level, you may hold down the Left Alt Key.
>
> To reach the [[Undercroft]], the party must do the following:
>
> 1. Activate the Hand Wheel located in the [[Iconostasis]].
> 2. Activate the Hand Wheel located in the [[West Maze Chamber]].
> 3. Retrieve the top half of the 3rd Hand Wheel from the [[Sedyri Ossuary]].
> 4. Activate the Hand Wheel located on the lower right platform of the [[Concourse]].
> 5. Activate the Hand Wheel located in the [[East Sea Cavern]].
