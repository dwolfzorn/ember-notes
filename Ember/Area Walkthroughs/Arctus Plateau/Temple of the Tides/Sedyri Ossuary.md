# Sedyri Ossuary

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Strange Cinderspar sarcophagi are arrayed around this chamber, their heavy lids carved with dense, flowing patterns; a larger tomb rests on a raised platform at the northern end, flanked by two narrower caskets below. Silt and scattered debris have gathered in the spaces between them, but the stonework itself remains remarkably intact. Motes of animated water flit about the chamber, as if agitated by some unseen force.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Ossuary Guardians
>
> As the characters enter the room, only the 3 [[Droplet]] are visible in the chamber.
>
> The 3 [[Tidegazer]] and 3 [[Riptongue]] are **Hidden** in the Scene. To better see these Hidden tokens, hold down the Left Alt Key with Token Controls active. These Actors are revealed in stages: refer to the instructions in the [[Sedyri Ossuary]] section.
>
> Characters with a `[[/skill perception 18 passive format=long]]` or who make a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check when entering the room can detect their camouflaged forms. If the Tidegazers are still hidden when combat begins, the characters are &reference[surprise]{Surprised}.

>
> #### Droplet Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Droplet]] will target the nearest creature with its [[Smother]] Action.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Droplet will prioritize the following Actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Droplet will attempt to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} enemies using its [[Smother]] Action in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
>
> The Droplet fights until destroyed.
>
> #### Tidegazer Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Tidegazer]] will use its [[Captivating Gaze]] Action to &reference[Charmed]{Charm} a creature, prioritizing Small creatures if able.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Tidegazer will prioritize the following Actions and abilities:
>
> - From range, the Tidegazer will use its [[Water Blast]] Action.
> - When threatened, the Tidegazer can use its [[Aqueous Transmission]] Bonus Action to reposition itself.
> - The Tidegazer will attempt to lure in Small or smaller enemies using its [[Captivating Gaze]] Action in order to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} them in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
>
> The Tidegazer fights until destroyed.
>
> #### Riptongue Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Riptongue]] will attempt to Grapple a creature using its [[Tongue Lash]] Action, prioritizing Medium creatures if able.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Riptongue will prioritize the following Actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, the Riptongue will use its [[Multiattack]] Action.
> - The Riptongue will attempt to reel in Medium or smaller enemies using its [[Tongue Lash]] Action in order to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} them in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
>
> The Riptongue fights until destroyed.

### Exploring the Ossuary

After the elementals are destroyed, the characters can investigate the area.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Sedyri Ossuary
>
> A simple search of the ossuary reveals the following:
>
> - The top half of Hand Wheel #3.
> - Sealed Cinderspar caskets engraved with water motifs.
>
> #### Hand Wheel #3 Top Half
>
> The top half of the third Hand Wheel must be returned and installed to the Hand Wheel base located in the [[Concourse]]. Refer to [[Area Overview]] for a full walkthrough.
>
> #### Investigating the Sealed Sarcophagi
>
> A character can open a sealed Cinderspar sarcophagus with a successful **`[[/skill Strength 16]]`** check using a proper tool. Inside each lies the remains of an ancient Sedyri water priest.
>
> - Their bodies are wrapped in faded shrouds, which immediately begin to disintegrate upon contact with the surrounding water. The remains themselves deteriorate rapidly once removed from the protective sarcophagus.
> - Each water priest is adorned with  **3d20** worth of ancient Sedyri jewelry.
