# East Sea Cavern

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This cavern narrows around a stretch of natural rock. Carved Cinderspar stairs rise on its north edge to the temple above. Nearby, a heavy mechanism rests against the cavern floor, half-submerged beneath silt and loose gravel.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Scene Transition
>
> Characters can automatically transition to the Temple Grounds by moving into the [[Transition: Swim]] Scene Region.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Slumbering Elementals
>
> Elemental guardians slumber in this chamber. Characters with a `[[/skill perception 18 passive format=long]]` or who make a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check when entering the area can detect their camouflaged forms.
>
> A character who approaches the Hand Wheel [[East Sea Cavern]], causing them to attack. If the creatures were still hidden, the characters are &Reference[Surprise]{Surprised}.

### Awakening the Elementals

Water elementals slumber on the bed of this sea cavern.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Token Reveal: Elementals
>
> The [[Benthweaver]] and 3 [[Droplet]] begin the encounter **Hidden** in the Scene. To better see these Hidden tokens, hold down the Left Alt Key with Token Controls active. When you are ready for combat to begin, **Reveal** the **Hidden** Elementals to represent them awakening.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Surprise
>
> Characters with a `[[/skill perception 18 passive format=long]]` or who make a successful **Awareness (DC 16)** check when entering the cavern can detect the elementals' camouflaged forms. Otherwise, the characters are &reference[surprise]{Surprised} when combat begins.
>
> #### Benthweaver Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Benthweaver]] will use its [[Icy Whorl]] action on as many enemies as possible.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Benthweaver will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, the Benthweaver will use its [[Multiattack]] action.
> - The Benthweaver can use its [[Waterstep]] Bonus Action defensively to move away from enemies, or offensively to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} enemies in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
> - The Benthweaver will use its [[Icy Whorl]] action whenever it is available.
>
> The Benthweaver fights until destroyed.
>
> #### Droplet Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the [[Droplet]] will target the nearest creature with its [[Smother]] action.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Droplet will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee, the Droplet will attempt to &reference[blinded]{Blind} and &reference[suffocating]{Suffocate} enemies using its [[Smother]] action in conjunction with its [[Water Form]] feature.
>
> The Droplet fights until destroyed.

### Exploring the Sea Cavern

After the elementals are destroyed, the characters can investigate the area.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Maze Wheel #4
>
> The fourth and final Hand Wheel required to navigate the temple's maze is located here. The Hand Wheel is an ​ **Interactable Object**. To see all Interactable Objects on a Scene Level, you may hold down the Left Alt Key. Refer to [[Area Overview]] for a full walkthrough.
