# Narthex

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The central floor of this broad Cinderspar narthex is laid with patterned tiles. Silt and pale aquatic growth have spread across the mosaic; the hardy yellow plants sprout from fissures in the stone. At the north end, a wide stair climbs toward the temple's upper level, vanishing into the flooded halls above.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Scene Transition: Upper Temple
>
> Characters can automatically transition to the Upper Temple by moving into the [[Transition: Stairs Center]] Scene Region.
>
> #### Scene Transition: Lower Temple
>
> Characters can automatically transition to the Lower Temple by moving into the [[Transition: Swim]] or [[Transition: Stairs East]] Scene Regions.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Narthex
>
> A simple search of the chamber reveals the following:
>
> - The central mosaic has been altered since its original construction.
> - The yellow plants growing through the floor are adapted to the Savanna's flood cycle: their thick, translucent stems contain pockets of trapped air, allowing them to survive long periods underwater. A creature can break one open to draw a single breath before the pockets collapse.
>
> #### Investigating the Mosaic
>
> A successful **Awareness (DC 14)** or **Society (DC 14)** check reveals traces of orange stone beneath several of the tiles, suggesting an older design incorporating both fire and water motifs was later covered over.
>
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The alterations were made well after the original floor was laid. Combined with the older fire and water design beneath, the character can reasonably conclude that the temple was originally built according to traditional Sedyri Unifier beliefs, then later rededicated solely to water.
>
> If the characters fail to glean information here, they can examine a similar mosaic in the [[Sedyri Tribune]] area.
>
> #### Maze Entrances
>
> The east and west rooms attached to the Narthex hold entrances to the maze. When the characters first arrive, neither entrance is accessible: pressurized water currents prevent entry. Refer to [[Area Overview]] for a full walkthrough.
