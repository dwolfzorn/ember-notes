# Prothesis

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Sheer cliffs rise close around this exposed chamber; parts of its walls have collapsed inward, exposing naked earth. A raised stone platform at the southern edge overlooks its crumbling masonry. Its surface is carved with detailed bas-reliefs, and a small circular depression.

This chamber once stored what Kess named the "Vessel of the Spark" — an important component of the magical ritual used to summon both the Spark of the Savanna and the Ripple of the Rivers — before that ritual bowl was laid to rest with the last of the Unifiers in the [[Tomb of the Unifiers]].

Inspection of the sacred object's ancient altar can point the party toward the importance of the artistic bas-reliefs found in the [[Unknown]], and suggest that the magical formulae contained within those reliefs are connected to summoning the Spark and the Ripple.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Where Once a Vessel
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 14)** check notices that the small depression in the center of the raised stone altar is the exact size and shape of the bowl Kess named the "Vessel of the Spark" that she used in her ritual.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** check recognizes the bas-reliefs around the depression as incomplete, and likely part of a larger series of artistic works presumably located elsewhere in the temple; such works are likely, therefore, related to the ritual object this altar once held.
>
> - **Knowledge: Arts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
