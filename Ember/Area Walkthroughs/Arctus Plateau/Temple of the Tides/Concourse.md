# Concourse

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The upper level opens onto a broad concourse of carved volcanic rock overlooking a recessed central court. Faded mosaics cover the lower floor, broken by patches of translucent yellow plants erupting from cracks in the stone. Weathered Sedyri statues stand along the surrounding ledges. On a small platform to the southeast, the stone base of a hand wheel remains fixed in place, though the wheel itself is missing.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Scene Transition
>
> Characters can automatically transition to the Temple Grounds by moving into the [[Transition: Stairs Center]] Scene Region.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Concourse
>
> A simple search of the area reveals the following:
>
> - Weathered Sedyri statues stand along the outer ledges. Several have been damaged or altered, but none appear recently disturbed.
> - Yellow plants have erupted through seams in the masonry: their thick, translucent stems contain pockets of trapped air, allowing them to survive long periods underwater. A creature can break one open to draw a single breath before the pockets collapse.
> - On the southeastern platform stands the stone base of a Hand Wheel mechanism, but the wheel itself is missing. The exposed fitting remains intact, suggesting the mechanism could still function if the missing wheel were replaced.
>
> #### Examining the Statue Heads
>
> The statues are stylized Ashka heads carved from Cinderspar, their hoods and facial features exaggerated into broad, geometric shapes. A successful **Arcana (DC 14)** or **Society (DC 14)** check reveals that each was originally accompanied by a paired inscription invoking both fire and water. The references to fire have been removed, but the remaining text was carefully recarved so that each passage now attributes Sedyri prosperity to water alone.
>
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character** **uses the** **Talent: Versatile Translator** **Talent** : The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Character has a** [[Dictionary of Late Arcturian Scor]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they consult it.
> - **Critical Success**: The character can reasonably conclude that whoever rededicated the temple did not believe that they were abandoning Sedyri tradition; they were attempting to reframe Unification, not destroy the concept.
>
> Characters who examine the largest snake-head statue up close notice something black glinting in its eye sockets: refer to the [[Sedyri Tribune]] section of the [[Sedyri Tribune]] area.
>
> #### Maze Wheel #3 (Missing Top Half)
>
> The third Hand Wheel required to navigate the temple's maze is located here, on the bottom right platform. However, it is missing its top half. The top half can be recovered from the [[Sedyri Ossuary]]. The Hand Wheel is an ​ **Interactable Object**. To see all Interactable Objects on a Scene Level, you may hold down the Left Alt Key. Refer to [[Area Overview]] for a full walkthrough.
