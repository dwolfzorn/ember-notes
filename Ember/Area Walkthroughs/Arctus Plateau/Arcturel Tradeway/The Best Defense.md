# The Best Defense

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Stepping through the doors, you are greeted by immediate warmth and the mingled smells of leather, oiled steel, dried fruit, and parchment. The shop is packed at every turn. Shelves bow beneath books, boots, sacks of goods, helmets, and more. Racks and cases bristle with weapons of all kinds, and the walls are decorated with hanging items, all with prices.
>
> On the wall is a map of the world of Ember, and a portrait of a zeph. The placard on the painted claims this is Briez Flywell, renowned treasure hunter and entrepreneur. The map is marked with numerous places, all locations that Flywell has visited and procured items for sale.

Open to the public from 8am to 8pm every day of the week, this small general store provides all manner of goods for the people of Arcturel, with a particular focus on equipment for adventurers and explorers of the [[Sinkhole Depths]]. Pitons & More is owned and operated by a discerning [[Arcturian]] merchant named Dreva Vineborn.

**Dreva Vineborn (Chaotic Good, Arcturian Human, she/her)**

Purchased the store from a retired shopkeeper two decades ago.

> #### Assorted Inventory
>
> Flywell's provides a little bit of everything, and most every kind of mundane equipment can be procured here without too much issue. In most cases the items are Arcturian in origin, but there are a high number of foreign-made versions of things, and a wide selection of books in different languages.
>
> #### Secret Stash
>
> A trapdoor in the northeast corner of the room leads to a basement beneath the store, where Darvin Nolde stores his surplus inventory and his rarest items. These rare items include:
>
> - A Longsword +1
> - A Cloak of Guile
>
> #### After Hours
>
> The front door to the store remains locked outside of normal business hours. Any character who makes a successful **Stealth (DC 18)** check requiring [[Lockpicks]] is able to pick the lock. Meanwhile, a successful **Stealth (DC 18)** check is required to avoid being detected by onlookers during the act.
>
> Alternately, any character who makes a successful **Athletics (DC 20)** is able to bash the door in with brute force. This cannot be done stealthily without the aid of magic, and will most likely alert any townsfolk located nearby.
>
> If any characters are caught red-handed while attempting to break into or steal from the store, they will be met with a retaliatory force of local townsfolk and adventurers — including friendly assistance from folks at [[Rallyhome]], and opportunistic support from the [[Silver Beam Headquarters]].
