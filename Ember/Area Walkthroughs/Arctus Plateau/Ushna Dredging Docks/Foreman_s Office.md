# Foreman's Office

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The foreman’s office is warmer and more orderly than the work spaces outside, the muck and mire of the work never quite making it to the rustic wooden floors underfoot. At its center stands a desk covered in papers, ledgers, and sealed notes. Beneath the desk lies a fine cream-and-gold carpet, too elegant for the mud-stained business of the docks but carefully protected from the worst of it.
>
> A bookcase stands against one wall, holding ledgers, manuals, maps, and a few well-kept volumes. Opposite it, against the far wall, a cabinet contains a teapot, cups, and other small comforts for long days of work.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### "Disturbed Earth" Quest Progression: Ushna Family Reunion
>
> If the party visits this room during [[Dredging for Answers]] of [[Disturbed Earth]] and is accompanied by [[Rala Ushna]] then:
>
> - Rala's father Bayar Ushna is currently in the office.
> - Immediately consult the [[Dredging for Answers]] section of that Event for the ensuing conversation with Bayar and Rala.

On the central desk is the [[Ushna Dredging Docks Ledger]] — a thick, leather-bound ledger of incoming and outgoing deliveries.

> [!info] Social
> #### Watching Hosts
>
> If the party is accompanied by Yaphta Ushna or the office is currently occupied by Bayar Ushna, they will regard inspection of the ledger with some suspicion, but are open to the party digging through it if provided a reasonable explanation — such as investigation into the Lake Orial mud and disruption to agriculture during [[Disturbed Earth]].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Reading Between the Lines
>
> Any character who reads closely through the ledger and makes a successful **Science (DC 13)** check notices a steady rate of outgoing deliveries of Lake Orial mud, but less and less incoming shipments of food and other goods for the Ushnas. The differences are not stark until this season, but careful calculation reveals the reduction in goods-received started a eleven years ago and worsened slowly but consistently — just slowly enough to be written off as natural fluctuations.
>
> - **Knowledge: Trade**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also notices a small, unexplained reduction in outgoing mud shipments during a single month of the season before the slow degradation began.

> [!info] Social
> #### Oral History
>
> If Yaphta or Bayar Ushna are asked about the events of eleven years ago, they recall:
>
> - Shortly after a major earthquake eleven years ago, a strange oil spill in the lake — from an unknown source — made the silt temporarily unsuitable for transformation into topsoil.
> - Management of this issue was overseen by the previous dockmaster, the late Ochir Ushna.
> - To Yaphta and/or Bayar's recollection, the lake water cleared and returned to normal naturally over the course of a week, and no such incidents have recurred since.
