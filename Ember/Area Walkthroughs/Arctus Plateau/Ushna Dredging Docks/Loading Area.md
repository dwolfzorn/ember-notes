# Loading Area

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The loading area is a broad stone promenade built on a heavy foundation extending into the lake. Large tiles cover the ground, their seams packed with layers of old mud that have been trampled, scraped, dried, and wetted again until they seem part of the stone itself. From the marks on the ground, wagons pull in here regularly, likely to to receive barrels of mud, or deliver materials for the warehouse.
>
> A canopied table stands to one side, serving as an administrative station, where outgoing deliveries of silt and incoming deliveries of staple goods for the Ushna family are logged and routed to the proper destination.

Just outside the canopy of the administrative station stands the chief of dock operations, Yaphta Ushna (Lawful Neutral, Bejak Kivahr, she/her). Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The older Kivahr woman who stands at the administrative station waves you over with a weathered hand, its leathery skin carved with craggy lines reflecting a lifetime of manual labor.
>
> > Greetings travelers. Welcome to Ushna Dredging. You've caught us just as the rest of the family has gone on break. What brings you to our docks?

If the party is accompanied by [[Rala Ushna]], add the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As her eyes settle on Rala, a hint of disapproval creeps into her expression.
>
> > Welcome home Rala. You have been missed greatly. I'm sure your father would like to see you — he's in the foreman's office right now.
>
> She gestures to the nearby office, as Rala suppresses a worried grimace.

> [!info] Social
> #### The Daily Dredge
>
> While Yaphta's stern countenance can make her seem cold or unwelcoming, it is clear she is proud of the work done at her family's docks, and she is enthused—in her own muted way—to tour the party around the facility, teach them about how the dredging operation works, and discuss the following topics:
>
> - She knows little of the mud's magic properties, as the Ushna docks only dredge and dry it, and the agrimages handle the rest.
> - In the centuries since settling here, the Ushnas have lived harmoniously with the ecosystem, never taking too much.
> - She trusts labor councils of the Redrak Fields to provide for the Ushna family's needs.
> - She will happily demonstrate how to use the dredging equipment on the [[Unknown]].
> - See the [[Ushna Dredging Docks]] section below for additional discussion topics if visiting during [[Dredging for Answers]].
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 12)** check notices that Yaphta is hiding unease beneath her stoic manner, and if pressed, she reveals anxiety about smaller size of recent deliveries from the labor councils — even if she trusts them to provide all they can.
>
> Specific dialogue options for Yaphta on the above topics are presented below this block.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About the Lake Orial mud?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I know little about its supposed magical properties—we leave that to the agrimages. What I **do** know is everything about dredging it, drying it, packing it, shipping it, and most importantly, how to ensure our actions never take more than Lake Orial can give.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About the lake ecosystem?
>
> **A:**
>
> > In the centuries the Ushnas have lived here, we have always maintained a balance. We are careful to minimize the silt we kick up into the lake water, and we monitor the erosion of the lakebed carefully. Any time the area around the crane seems unstable, we switch over to our mobile wheelboats until the bed resettles and solidifies.
> >
> > But now that I'm thinking of it … some of the lake-bottom plants have looked less healthy this season. We've typically never had to worry about the flora — it's always grown profusely on its own. Strange.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About trade with the labor councils and Agrimage Circle?
>
> **A:**
>
> > A mutual trust and obligation, cemented by centuries of cooperation. We ship the silt to the agrimages, they use their agrimagic to turn it to topsoil, and the labor councils use it to grow a bountiful harvest. Those councils, in turn, bring us a portion of that harvest, and other goods they obtain from trading their excess with the Ordani trading houses.
> >
> > It's not a life of luxury, but our family has never wanted such things. Hard work and to live in harmony with the land and each other, knowing our needs will always be met, are the greatest gifts we can ask for.
>
> One side of his mouth turns up, the closest Yaphta has come to a smile … though it seems as if something more complicated might lie behind it.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** How does the dredging equipment work / can we dredge some mud?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I believe a demonstration is in order. It's an impressive system, if you'll permit me this one vanity.
> >
> > When you're ready to see it in action, I'll accompany you to the dock proper, and we'll harvest Orial's bounty together.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About his anxieties?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Ah, did I make it that obvious? Well … it's just that the incoming deliveries from the labor councils have been ... smaller than usual. Nothing we can't handle, but in the last few shipments, even the foodstuffs were barely enough to feed our family's many mouths after days full of hard labor. I trust the councils to give us a fair portion, but that is the case, then this season's harvest must be quite small. It is an ill omen.

Once the party is done conversing with Yaphta, she will readily accompany them to the [[Unknown]] to demonstrate proper usage of the dredging equipment, or point out the [[Unknown]] and [[Unknown]] for them to explore — the Ushnas are quite trusting of guests.

### [[Disturbed Earth]]

If the party is currently investigating the elemental threat and environmental disruption of [[Disturbed Earth]], Yaphta will also speak about these additional topics.

> [!info] Social
> #### The Coming Wave
>
> Yaphta will also discuss the following:
>
> - She has not witnessed any elemental attacks, but her ancestors spoke of visits by friendly elementals, and Yaphta will alert the local guard to be ready for hostile ones.
> - If prompted specifically about acid or rust, she indicates they have seen strange corrosion on the clamshell at the end of the crane, but not in any of the rest of the equipment.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About the elemental attacks?
>
> **A:**
>
> > My late grandmother told tales of friendly elementals visiting to tend to the lake and the wilderness around it, but I myself have never seen them. Certainly none have attacked us, but I will let Redsai's Roadman know to be on guard. The warning is appreciated.

> [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Signs of strange acid or rust?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Oh, I wouldn't have thought to mention it, but our clamshell scoop on the end of our crane has been rusting strangely fast this season. It's odd, because the rest of the equipment has been fine … it's **only** the crane scoop. Do you know the cause?

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Eaten Away
>
> Any character who inspects the clamshell scoop and makes a successful **Science (DC 12)** check notes that not only has the scoop rusted, but it has also been eaten away at its edges, as if submerged in a powerful acid.
>
> - **Knowledge: Alchemy**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also notices the chain that connects the scoop to the crane shows no sign of acid damage, indicating the source of the damage is unlikely to be the lake water, which the entire apparatus would be regularly submerged in.
