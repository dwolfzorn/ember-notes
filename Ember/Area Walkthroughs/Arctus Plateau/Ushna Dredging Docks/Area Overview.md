# Area Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Area Map Context
>
> The [[Ushna Dredging Docks]] Area Map depicts one single dredging dock with warehouse and storage tanks that are part of the larger Dredger Shores area, and is featured in [[Dredging for Answers]] during the [[Disturbed Earth]] Side Quest.
>
> - The Area Map depicts 1 Scene Level that encompasses the full dock area.
> - The party arrives outside on the dirt road approach.

## Gameplay Details

The areas of the Ushna Dredging Dock have the following features unless specified otherwise in the text.

### Levels & Elevation

The Ushna Dredger Docks Scene features a single Level, and its areas are organized into a few locations on the lake shore.

- **Lake Shore:** The majority of the dredger dock area is set to an elevation range of 0, though the water of the lake reaches a depth of -6 at its furthest point from the shore here.

### Sound

The sounds of yarnac working, the large wooden crane moving overhead, and lake waves lapping against the docs create a steady din of noise, but nothing loud enough to impact hearing.

### Illumination

The dredger operations are lit by natural light and lanterns.

### Terrain

Only the lake itself presents a notable area of difficult terrain, with the deeper sections of the water requiring swimming, and the rest just slowing down anyone moving through it.

### Inhabitants

The docks are operated and occupied by a team of workers at all hours which can be represented by the [[Arcturian]] actor.

### Enemies

During the events of [[Disturbed Earth]] the following enemies are encountered here:

- [[Mud Globlin]]
- Corrupted Crystallath
- [[Slaith]]
