# Lake Orial

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Lake Orial stretches outward in a vast sheet of placid purple-gray water, its surface calm enough to hold the sky in dull, wavering reflections. The far shore is lost to distance, the lake reaching all the way to the horizon, giving the dock site the feeling of standing at the edge of a vast mirror.
>
> Out on the water floats a dredger wheel, its broad wooden frame standing out against the lake’s muted surface. Rowboats lashed to it help keep it afloat, and though the wheel is still now, the stains on it prove it has seen recent and heavy use.

If the party thinks to perform the strange (and practically difficult) task of analyzing the silt on the lake bed without removing it from the water, they discover its unusually strong acid content.

If the silt sample is removed from the lake, the acid quickly begins breaking down — instead follow the analysis guidelines in the [[Dredging for Answers]] section of [[Dredging for Answers]].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Underwater Investigation
>
> Any character who analyzes the silt of the lake bed while it is still submerged in the lake and makes a successful **Science (DC 18)** check determines not just a strange dearth of the minerals that are purported to make this silt naturally fertile for plant life, but also an incredibly strong acid content (which decays rapidly upon removal from the lake, against all scientific reasoning).
>
> - **Knowledge: Alchemy**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - C**haracter has** [[Alchemist's Toolkit]]: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
