# Dock Road

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A sandy road winds through the red grass toward the dock site from the west, little more than a footpath really, its surface broken by footprints and hoofprints weaving between patches of hardy red grass growing in wild clumps on either side of it.
>
> A wooden sign hangs over the main dock entrance, and the sandy path leading up to it widens into broader packed-dirt lane that continues east toward the main road. This stretch is firmer and darker from constant use, with deep ruts hardened into place and scattered flecks of dried lake mud marking the passage of loaded wagons.

As the party approaches, there are minor signs of disruption to the Lake Orial ecosystem.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Withered Warning
>
> Any character with **Awareness (DC 13, Passive)** notices withered red plants submerged beneath the water of the lakeshore, in stark contrast to the healthier plants further inland.
>
> Once observed, any character who succeeds on a further **`[[/skill wilderness 16]]`** check notices some of the pristine inland leaves glistening with splashed lake water, and concludes the issue must therefore stem from the soil of the lakebed, not the water.
>
> - **Knowledge: Plants**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
