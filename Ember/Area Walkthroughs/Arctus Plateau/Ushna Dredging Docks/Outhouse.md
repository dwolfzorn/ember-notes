# Outhouse

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This outhouse is a small, plain wooden shack set apart from the busier work areas. It has two stalls inside, each built over a deep pit with rustic plank seating worn smooth from use. The structure is roomier than it looks from outside, but it is windowless, close, and dim.
>
> The smell inside is immediate and unforgiving, held in the still air by the lack of ventilation.

The inside of the outhouse is as unpleasant as its nature would suggest — unsurprising for a dock that sees all-day labor, all days of the week.
