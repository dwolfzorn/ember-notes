# Stone Dock

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The stone docks run long and broad out into Lake Orial, built to bear serious weight and constant work. The lake laps quietly below, purple-gray and still, though the water nearest the docks carries faint cloudy traces of disturbed silt.
>
> Large metal storage tanks stand along the dock edges, each, filled with lake silt and connected by valves, fittings, and heavy pipes. Beside them are larger square reservoirs where purple silt swirls slowly in thick suspension, left for the sun to dry out over time.
>
> At the end of the dock rises a massive wooden crane, its frame braced with metal fittings stained to a dark patina from exposure to the elements. Pipes run along its arm to a large tank positioned on its top. Beyond the crane the whole lake opens up, vast and quiet, stretching toward the horizon.

As the party makes their way down the dock, if they are not already accompanied by Yaphta Ushna, she glances up from the canopied table at the [[Unknown]] and makes her way over to them.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Yaphta gestures at the interconnected machinery.
>
> > While at my age I now handle mostly logistics, not loading vats, I've got more experience with this equipment than anyone.
>
> She rests her hand on the wheel of nearby valve, and looks on proudly.
>
> > If you'd like, I can give you a hands-on lesson in the equipment's operation, and you can take home a full barrel, assuming you have a use for it. After all, as a Bejak of Redrak, I know both that there's virtue in a visitor getting local dirt on their hands, and that all who labor should be welcome to its fruit.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Operating the Equipment
>
> If the party agrees to let Yaphta demonstrate the equipment, use this as an opportunity to tutorialize the usage of the various Interactive Objects present on the Area Map. The party will likely wish to make use of these Interactive Objects during the battle with Slaith in [[Braving the Elements]].
>
> Yaphta guides the party through cleaning a drying vat, filling it with fresh lake silt, then — after they contribute their labor — she allows them to fill a barrel from a full drying vat.
>
> #### Purging the Tanks
>
> Yaphta first demonstrates how to clean a drying vat, which requires opening a valve to allow large quantity of clean water (stored in tanks below the docks) to flow through the drying vat, then back out into the lake.
>
> Have a player move to one of the two drying vats nearest the shore, click on the "Dredging Valve" Interactable Object attached to it, then click the "Purge" button.
>
> After purging, under Yaphta's guidance, have the player click the valve again, then click the "Fill" button to refill the tank under the docks.
>
> These Interactable Objects will be relevant again during [[Braving the Elements]], when the players can purge vats to clear sections of befouled water during the fight with [[Slaith]].
>
> #### Using the Crane
>
> Next, Yaphta demonstrates how to use the crane to retrieve silt and drop it into a vat.
>
> Have a player move to the crane platform at the end of the docks, then use the Interactable Object represented by smaller, bottom-left control wheel in order to rotate the crane.
>
> Next, under Yaphta's guidance, have the player use the larger, top-left control wheel to raise and lower the [[Dredging Claw]] at the end of the crane.
>
> These two interactions visualize the process of retrieving lake silt and rotating the crane to drop the silt into above a drying vat — Yaphta asks the party member to pour it into the vat they just cleaned.
>
> These Interactable Objects will be relevant again during [[Braving the Elements]], when the players can drop the Dredging Claw on the head of [[Slaith]] to break its crystalline crown.
>
> #### Retrieving the Mud
>
> Finally, after the party has made their contribution to the daily work of the docks, Yaphta allows them to fill an empty barrel with dried lake mud from one of the other drying vats. Simply place a [[Barrel of Lake Orial Mud]] into the relevant character's inventory — though its incredible weight means they will likely wish to load it onto a yarnac-pulled cart.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### "Disturbed Earth" Quest Progression: Analyzing the Mud
>
> If the party obtains a [[Barrel of Lake Orial Mud]] during [[Dredging for Answers]] in [[Disturbed Earth]], consult the [[Dredging for Answers]] section of that Event for checks related to analysis of that mud.'
>
> Mark the "Orial Mud Collected" Event Outcome in that section (regardless of whether the party chooses to investigate the mud themselves immediately).
