# Warehouse

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The warehouse is a spacious timber building with high rafters, beams, and mud-stained plank floors. Skylights overhead let pale daylight spill down across stacks of barrels, coils of rope, neatly swept floors forever stained with mud ground into the wood grain by countless feet.
>
> An overhead winch system runs along heavy beams above, its chains and hooks positioned to lift packed casks of mud and swing them over to the wagon bay where waiting transports can receive them.
>
> One corner of the spare is given over to cooperage work, with barrel staves, iron hoops, mallets, shaving curls, and half-finished casks arranged around a sturdy bench, while the opposite corners of the warehouse sit mostly empty.

Inside, the empty spaces of the warehouse tell a tale to those observant enough to notice it.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Unboxed
>
> Any character with **Awareness (DC 14, Passive)** spots grooves and stains marring the emptier sides of the warehouse, whose rectangular shapes and variety of colors look far different than those left by the circular mud barrels used by the Ushna family.
>
> - **Knowledge: Forensics**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> Once observed, a character who succeeds on a further **`[[/skill society 14]]`** check reasons that these empty corners must be where incoming shipments are typically stored, and while the outgoing barrels seem as prolific as ever, such incoming deliveries seem far smaller than the floor's wear and tear suggests they once were.
>
> - **Knowledge: Trade**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.

Upon leaving the warehouse (or carefully inspecting it) the party easily spots [[Yaphta's Warehouse Note]] tacked to the inside of the door frame.
