# Pathways Elevator

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A great stone circle thirty feet wide rests in the center of the space, with multiple concentric rings of ornately decorated stone making up its internal area. Numerous magical runes can be seen scattered through the cracked and worn stone, most prominently along the outer lip of the circle.

The passage down into the Pathways is actually an ancient elevator built nearly thirty thousand years ago using magic that predates even the oldest known civilizations.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### The Sealed Elevator
>
> The elevator platform sits dark and inert. Its runes are dormant, and nothing the party does to the platform itself causes it to move.
>
> A character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 13)** check traces the dormant runes along the outer lip of the platform. Seven channels of runework run outward from the circle, each pointing toward one of the seven gems suspended around the [[Mezzanine Balcony]]. The elevator draws its power from those gems, and will remain sealed until they are awakened.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character recognizes that the runes do not respond to simply lighting the gems. They describe a sequence, meaning the gems must be awakened in a particular order.
>
> A character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 12)** check notices a worn band of carvings encircling the platform's lip: seven robed giants, each bearing an orb. Some of the figures hold their orbs aloft, others at their necks, others hidden in clasped hands. The figures match the seven sages carved into the walls of the [[Hall of Sages]], where the elevator from the upper halls arrives.
>
> - **Knowledge: Cosmology**: The character automatically succeeds on this check, and recognizes the orbs as Ember's six moons and Lantyr.
>
> Characters who can read **Language: Pathward** can decipher an inscription running beneath the carved sages:
>
> > As the sages watched, so must you. Wake the lights of the heavens, each as it ascends to its height.
>
> #### Elevator Control Panel
>
> A character who succeeds on a **Awareness (DC 13)** check notices a runic inscription upon a section of the elevator's surface which encircles a giant-sized handprint in the stone floor. Characters that speak **Language: Pathward** can translate the inscription as a single concept, "descend".

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Elevator Unlocking Puzzle
>
> The Pathways Elevator can only be unsealed and activated for use by correctly solving a puzzle that requires activating each of the seven gems caged along the [[Mezzanine Balcony]]. Each gem represents one of Ember's elemental moons or Lantyr, the sun. Touching one of the gems causes it to temporarily awaken, shining brightly. Waking all seven gems in the correct order unseals the ancient lift for use.
>
> The puzzle solution requires activating each gem in the order that each celestial body will next reach its zenith. For a moon, this means when that moon will next be full. For the sun, that means when next it will be midday when the sun is highest overhead. There is not a single fixed solution to this puzzle, as the solution always changes as time passes.
>
> #### Solving the Puzzle
>
> As the gamemaster, you may view the currently correct solution by using the  interaction controls and clicking on the Pathways Elevator. The dialog that appears will tell you the current puzzle solution, or alternatively allow you to bypass the puzzle by automatically solving it.
>
> Players can wake each gem themselves to interactively solve the puzzle using the  interaction controls with each of the seven gemstones around the [[Mezzanine Balcony]]. Whether a solution is correct or incorrect is not realized until all seven gems have been illuminated in some order.
>
> - If the order is wrong, all seven gems go dark and require the solution to be attempted again.
> - If the order is right, a resonant chord sounds and stone mechanisms grind deep below Mythspire. The gems remain illuminated and the [[Guardian of Myth]] outcome is recorded as complete.
> - Touching a gem which is already lit causes it to deactivate, resetting the entire puzzle and allowing the players to restart.
>
> #### Clues for Players
>
> The party can learn several clues from the surrounding environment about how to solve this puzzle, but if you find them needing some hints, key portions of the answer can be found using the calendar UI at the top of the screen.
>
> - Hovering on a moon in the calendar displays its current phase: Waxing, Waning, or Full.
> - Moons shown on the right of the calendar are Waxing and are close to becoming Full.
> - Moons on the left of the calendar are Waning and have recently passed their zenith. They will be the last to return to full prominence.
> - Moons at the bottom center are currently Full and count as being at their zenith now.
> - Moons not depicted on the calendar are somewhere in the middle of the order, and the players must reason these out or guess their sequence.
> - Lantyr reaches its zenith every day at noon (12:00), so it comes first in the sequence unless a moon is currently Full or will become Full before the next noon.
>
> #### Elevator Controls
>
> Once the puzzle has been solved and the elevator unlocked, as the Gamemaster you can interact with the elevator again to choose one of the following:
>
> - **Descend to the Pathways** lowers the platform along with all tokens standing upon it, then moves the party to the Pathways section of the region map, one hour later. If the Guardian of Myth event is still running, it is completed.
> - **Descend** lowers the platform and its tokens, but the party remains in this scene.
> - **Lock Elevator** puts out the gems and seals the elevator again. The puzzle must be solved anew, and its solution may have changed in the meantime.
>
> Once the platform is down, interacting with it again offers **Ascend** to return it to the chamber.

Once the party solves the puzzle, re-enabling the ancient Pathways Elevator, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The ancient masonry groans and shifts, unseen mechanisms clicking into place with rapidity. Then, there is a moment of stillness, followed by a deep shuddering in the structure. A moment later a brightly glowing rune appears in the air over the center of the lift above the handprint etched in its stone.

If the party activates the lift to descend downwards into the Pathways, read the following aloud:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> With a deep, resonant hum, the stone platform begins its descent, sinking slowly at first and then picking up speed as ancient magic guides it deeper into the earth. Runes along the lift's edge glow steadily, passing by in a predictable rhythm, their light dancing off the walls as they do.
>
> Minutes pass, yet the lift carries on, moving steadily downward. The air grows warmer, and the color and texture of the stone changes over time, as you descend through more and more layers of Ember's crust.
>
> Finally, the lift slows its descent, and then comes to a smooth halt with a final, magical hum that reverberates through your core. Before you, massive, meandering passages wind into the distance and darkness.
