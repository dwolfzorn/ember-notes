# Area Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Area Map Context
>
> The [[Mythspire Observatory]] Area Map depicts the legendary [[Shent]] stronghold in the [[Wedgelands]] east of [[Skybrush]], which serves as a potential conduit to the [[Pathways]]. It is featured in the [[Guardian of Myth]] Event of the [[Ancient Paths]] Main Quest.
>
> - This map depicts two Scene Levels: [[Upper Halls]] and [[Lower Chamber]].
> - The observatory provides passage between the surface of [[World of Ember]] and the [[Mycelian Expanse]] of the subterranean Pathways.
> - Mythspire Observatory is guarded to this day by an immortal sentinel known as the [[Mythspire Guardian]].
> - The party first enters the area at the [[Mythspire Approach]] area of the map.

## Gameplay Details

The areas of Mythspire Observatory have the following features unless otherwise specified.

## Levels & Elevation

The ruins of Mythspire Observatory stand roughly 100 feet in height above the mountain on which it is constructed, however the upper-most reaches of the ruin are inaccessible precarious patches of stone — near collapse — that suggest a more grand structure that once towered above the surrounding landscape.

The explorable scene features two Levels of gameplay including:

- [[Upper Halls]] - The upper halls define the reference elevation 0 for the Scene. They have grand 40-foot-high ceilings. A small balcony section of the Upper Halls is 30 feet below this elevation.
- [[Lower Chamber]] - The bottom chamber of the observatory is 200 feet below the upper level where the party enters. It is connected by an elevator which transports passengers between the Upper Halls and a mezzanine balcony that wraps around the Lower Chamber floor. There is no specific ceiling of the Lower Chamber as the entire interior of Mythspire Observatory is a hollow cylinder.

Due to damage and decay in the structure, only these two Levels are explorable. Any intervening areas are too choked with debris to be accessed.

## Illumination

The exterior of Mythspire Observatory is exposed to natural light during the day, and is pitch black at night.

The interior of Mythspire Observatory is illuminated with dim light due to a network of faintly flickering crystalline light fixtures that fill the interior with a low blueish glow. Each crystal light is mounted on the wall about 30 feet above the nearest floor and creates bright light in a radius of 20 feet which does not quite reach the floor below them.

## Terrain

Though once an enclosed structure with a massive dome of stone and glass, some catastrophic failure in the past has left these ruins open to the sky. The mountain winds howl through the gaps in ancient stone, making the ruins gloomy and cold at all times.

The high altitudes and dry air make the environment feel harsher than it is. Nothing lives here — no plants, no animals, the top of the mountain is barren and devoid of life.

The terrain is ancient ruins. Crumbling stone, uneven footing, and scattered debris make travel difficult in many places. The scale alone of this place makes it clear that this place was not built by or for conventional humanoids, but giants.

## Additional Mechanics

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Falling Hazard
>
> In the event that a character gets too close to any of the open edges of the scene the stone crumbles under foot. A character can make a successful **Athletics (DC 12, Passive)** check to jump back before being swept away in the collapse.
>
> If a character fails this check, a nearby ally can lunge to rescue them, requiring a successful **Athletics (DC 14)** to haul their ally back to safety.
>
> Failure results in them falling, causing them to land on a stone catwalk some 30 feet down, exposing them to a **Minor Fall (Hazard 30)**.
>
> Characters can climb back up from there with a successful **Athletics (DC 14)** check. If they have help of a rope or other climbing tools no check is needed.
