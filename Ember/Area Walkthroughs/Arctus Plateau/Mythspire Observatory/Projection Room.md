# Projection Room

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> You enter a broad chamber where the floor is dominated by a pair of long metal rails, fixed firmly into the ancient stone and stretching in parallel from the room’s center to a nearby balcony. At either end of the rails is a circular recess etched with runes.
>
> Set into the walls are two crystalline fixtures mounted about 10 feet above the floor upon rune-covered plinths of stone. One holds an array of blue and red gemstones set into apertures in its face. The other appears to have been badly burned and possibly plundered.
>
> Opposite these fixtures, the room curves into a long, smooth wall, utterly unbroken save for a border of runes at its outermost edges.

The party may examine the pair of fixtures for clues about their purpose.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Ancient Projector
>
> The party may investigate the unburnt crystalline machine on the west wall.
>
> A successful **Arcana (DC 15)** check while examining the runes around the unburnt crystal, the recess at the end of the rails, and the curved wall determines that they worked in concert.
>
> - **Language: Pathward**: Characters who can decipher the Shent language can read the controls and identify that this mechanism was used to project light through crystal orbs onto the large curved wall.
> - **Knowledge: Machines**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check and additionally suspects that the machinery may have been used for testing, charging, or etching large orbs like the one hanging overhead in the central well of the observatory.
> - **Critical Success**: The presence of specific illusion and divination runes etched on the crystals likely provided some visual and predictive elements to the projection. The gems would project magical light, which was meant to hit something sitting in the cradle, and project light onto the wall beyond.
>
> #### Destroyed Mechanism
>
> The party may also investigate the partially destroyed apparatus on the northwest wall of this room.
>
> A successful **Science (DC 14)** check recognizes that — at some point — a portion of the machinery on the wall suffered a catastrophic failure that left the stone badly burned, destroying several of the runes etched into it. The remaining gems here have suffered extensive cracks, rendering them inert.
>
> - **Language: Pathward**: The character can read surviving etchings which suggest this mechanism once controlled all of the power for the central dome's projection system.
> - **Critical Success**: The character intuits that this machine was meant to control the flow of magical energy through the room. Extensive evocation and abjuration runes were used to dampen and control the energy; if they failed, it could have resulted in the damage you see here.
