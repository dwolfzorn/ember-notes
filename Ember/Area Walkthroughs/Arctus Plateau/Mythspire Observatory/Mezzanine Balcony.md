# Mezzanine Balcony

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A wide balcony extends to encircle the open space here. Along the curve of its mezzanine are seven evenly spaced alcoves which each feature a prominent hexagonal pedestal. Upon each pedestal is suspended a large and colorful gemstone that hovers by some unseen magic. The gems are anchored in place with heavy bronze chains that encircle and secure them.
>
> Encircling the outer perimeter of the rotunda are faded pictographic reliefs carved and etched into the massive stone walls. These reliefs appear to depict historical or legendary scenes from ancient and forgotten history.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Cosmic Gems
>
> A character who succeeds on a **Science (DC 14, Passive)** check recognizes that six of the seven gems coincide with the traditional colors used to depict the elemental moons of Ember, while the seventh gem is a golden hue typically associated with Lantyr, or possibly Luxarum.
>
> - **Knowledge: Cosmology**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character recognizes that the number of gemstones here, seven, corresponds identically to the number of statues depicted in the [[Hall of Sages]].
>
> Clockwise from the east side of the encircling balcony, the gems are ordered as:
>
> - **The Gem of Lantyr** is as golden and warm as the sun itself.
> - **The Gem of Akon** is nearly black, and featureless with a faint hint of green.
> - **The Gem of Orbis** is a chaotic shifting mess of pinks, blues, and purples.
> - **The Gem of Ragen** is a deep red and radiates warmth even when dim.
> - **The Gem of Mayis** is deep blue, and beneath its facets are dim currents of light.
> - **The Gem of Cora** is a rich green and it exudes a palpable sensation of heaviness.
> - **The Gem of Aura** is an ethereal pink gem with facets that swirl with clouds.
>
> #### Theft Resistant
>
> The gems are all magically fixed in their fittings and the chains are reinforced many times over with magic. It should be functionally impossible for the fittings or their chains to be damaged or broken, making theft of these ancient gems beyond the party. All attempts to do so automatically fail.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Interactable Gems
>
> Each gem features an interactable element, which can be engaged with by toggling  interaction controls. Players are allowed to interact with these gems themselves, and this interaction plays an important role during both the combat encounter and puzzle sequences set in the Lower Chamber.
>
> - [[Central Rotunda]]: During combat with the Mythspire Guardian, timely interaction with these gems provides a means to negate its powerful magic.
> - [[Pathways Elevator]]: As part of the puzzle to unlock the Pathways Elevator, these gems must be activated in a specific unlocking sequence.
>
> Complete details are presented on those respective pages.

The party can glean some clues about what they may be expected to do by inspecting stone reliefs that encircle the outer circumference of the chamber. If the party spends 10 minutes examining the carvings that surround the rotunda, they are able to piece together the general concept of their recorded story.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The carved reliefs that surround the rotunda depict a people who burrowed out of the body of Ember and tunneled upward through the mountains. They emerged to behold the sky overhead, and were awed.
>
> They built a great structure at their emergence point, perhaps this very building, and within it rested a feline guardian, whom the people respected.
>
> The reliefs depict many more of their kind ascending to the surface, each experiencing the same reverence for the passing moons and stars overhead. These people spread across the mountains and constructed many dwellings both high and low.
>
> Behind each of the seven alcoves, the relief depicts groups of robed giants gazing upwards towards the heavens, towards a stylized depiction of the celestial body whose gem hovers above the alcove's pedestal. The figures appear to be holding scrolls or hand-held devices, as if to record measurements.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Interpreting the Reliefs
>
> A character who succeeds on a **Society (DC 14)** check is able to connect some dots, noting that the reverence to sky and moons feels like a very classical marker of the Shent people.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Ancients**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character discerns that the history retold here likely confirms the existence of even more ancient precursor giants who may have preceded the Shent.
>
> A character who succeeds on a **Arcana (DC 14)** check understands that the depiction of the feline guardian is almost certainly the very Mythspire Guardian that occupies this rotunda today, dating it as tens of thousands of years old!
>
> - **Knowledge: Fey**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The guardian is likely a type of Casia, or closely related to them, who are known to be feline beings of tremendous power from Primordis. This lore suggests that Casia might have served as guardians or guides to ancient giants, a revelation that would constitute a remarkable and novel scholarly discovery.
