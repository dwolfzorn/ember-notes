# Crumbling Walkway

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> You find yourself on a broad, curved walkway, partially buried beneath piles of broken stone and ancient debris. The outer edge is protected by a chest-high stone barrier, cracked in several places, which keeps you from a long, fatal fall to the lower levels.
>
> The balcony leads only in one direction; the rest is blocked by collapsed sections. At the northern end, a set of broad and unusually tall steps rises up to the level above.

On the south side, the walkway ends in ruins, but characters could attempt to climb back upwards to the [[Service Room]].

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Walkway Ascent
>
> This walkway is 30 feet below the areas above. A character could attempt to climb back upwards as described in the "Falling Hazard" rules on the [[Area Overview]] page.

On the north end, the walkway reaches an alcove from which ascends a spiral staircase towards the [[Projection Room]].

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Mutagist Tripwire
>
> The Mutagists left a trap at the north end of the walkway. A thin tripwire is strung from wall to wall across the approach to the alcove, rigged to a bundle of alchemical flasks wedged into a crumbled nook in the stone wall above.
>
> A character with **Awareness (DC 14, Passive)** notices the tripwire before anyone walks into it. A character who knows the wire is there can simply step over it or cut it safely.
>
> If anyone trips the wire, the flasks tumble loose and shatter on the floor, releasing a cloud of poisonous gas. Each creature in the area suffers a **Poison Gas (Hazard 8)**. The gas then thins out harmlessly within a round or so.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Springing the Trap
>
> This trap is automated. A token crossing the wire stops, the game pauses, and every visible creature in the gas is exposed to the hazard. To disarm the trap, disable the [[Trap Trigger]] behavior of the [[Trap: Tripwire]] region; re-enable it to re-arm.
