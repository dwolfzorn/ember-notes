# Mythspire Foyer

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Crossing the threshold of Mythspire Observatory, you are greeted by an almost oppressive silence, punctuated only by the whispering wind as it sneaks through gaps in the ancient stonework. The air within feels heavy and colder than outside, as if the earth itself greedily absorbs all the heat it can.
>
> The entire structure is caught in a slow spiral toward oblivion. Rubble lies strewn across the floor, and exterior light pierces the great shattered dome above — casting stark, uneven beams upon an otherwise dark interior.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Inspecting the Ruins
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 15)** check is able to identify the hallmarks of ancient [[Shent]] construction throughout the interior of this place.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Legends**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Science (DC 14)** check while searching the area can identify the presence of refurbished stonework, and can tell that the structure has been repaired (if not rebuilt) more than once.
>
> - **Knowledge: Crafts**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
