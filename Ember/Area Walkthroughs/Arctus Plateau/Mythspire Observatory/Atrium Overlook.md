# Atrium Overlook

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> You step onto a broad, open platform bounded by a chest-high stone railing to the north and west. Beyond the railing, your view of the atrium plunges down hundreds of feet into the vast, central void of the observatory — a breathtaking span of ruined walkways, shattered stairwells, and inaccessible spaces between. Overhead, the massive arch of a cracked dome looms high above, its concave ceiling etched with some ancient pattern or artwork. Beneath the dome, a large metallic sprawl of some damaged, rune-marked contraption can be seen closer to eye level, suspended above the void below.
>
> As you stand among the dark vastness of this forgotten atrium, the perpetual howl of the mountain wind threads is way through the pockmarked ruin, resounding throughout the shadow-haunted edifice like some ghostly chorus.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Overlook
>
> If the party decides to take a closer look at the atrium from the advantageous perspective of the overlook here, they'll notice a few remarkable details:
>
> **Above**
>
> The damaged domed ceiling is etched with a massive celestial map, which appears to detail the various constellations of the night sky along with the moons and Inner Realms.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check while examining the ceiling is able to accurately deduce that some of the damages to the dome appear to have been caused by something ripping the stonework away from outside — a mighty feat for any living creature of Ember.
>
> - **Knowledge: Forensics**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> **Eye Level**
>
> Beneath the dome, the damaged components of some enormous mechanical contraption are precariously suspended above the atrium at various intervals. Among the wreckage, which is comprised of concentric rings of alloyed metal, a single undamaged sphere made of crystal or glass stands in defiance of the deterioration here. The walls nearby and the contraption itself are marked with an array of ancient [[Shent]] runes.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 12)**check while examining the contraption can identify its purpose as an orrery — a model of the [[Cosmos]] that charts and predicts the location of celestial bodies in their respective orbits.
>
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Machines**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also recognizes that the magical runes in the walls and the machinery here are connected to powerful Illusion magic that would have once augmented the orrery with sounds and visual projections.
>
> **Below**
>
> Down below, the atrium falls away into abject darkness, and extends further into the earth for hundreds of feet. The edges of countless catwalks and balconies can be seen lining the observatory walls, but their various destinations and adjoining chambers lurk just out of sight.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 12)** check while examining the atrium depths also notices that the lower levels are badly damaged, with the various walkways and balconies having collapsed on themselves, or fallen away entirely.
>
> - **Knowledge: Subterranea**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also recognizes that there may have once been a half-dozen other levels to explore in this tower, but there is no clear or safe way to reach them. Even if one could levitate or fly, it looks like all of the entrances and corridors are choked with rubble.
