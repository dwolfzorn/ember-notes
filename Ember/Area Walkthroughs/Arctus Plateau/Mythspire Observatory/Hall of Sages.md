# Hall of Sages

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As you descend into the darkness you can see a vast chamber awaiting you, large glimmering rune-stones affixed to the wall serving as the only source of light. The space is square, and adorned with ancient reliefs of towering figures twice your height at least.
>
> The stone lift thuds abruptly at the end of its descent, the impact resounding through the dim chamber beyond. Your arrival disturbs the cool, dry air here, causing the scent of rot to drift in to greet you from somewhere nearby.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Residual Power
>
> A character who succeeds on an **Arcana (DC 13)** check is able to infer that the imbued crystals here are likely responsible for providing power to the lift. If they were removed, returning to the upper halls might become impossible.
>
> - [[Lunar Awakenings]]: Characters who encountered similar artifice before recognize this automatically.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Puzzle Clue
>
> The reliefs of giant figures provide an important clue to the mechanism and puzzle the party will face later when attempting to unlock the [[Unknown]]. The orbs carried by each of the seven figures here depict one of Ember's elemental moons or Lantyr, the sun. The puzzle solution requires demonstrating knowledge of when each body will next be prominent in the sky.
>
> The statues foreshadow that solution by presenting their orb with different postures, some shrouded and held low at their waist, others hanging partially visible at their necks, while others are held high aloft. Full details of the puzzle are presented on the [[Pathways Elevator]] page.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Reliefs of giant-sized figures loom against the northern and eastern walls of this room. There are seven, each holding an orb. Some of the figures raise their orbs overhead, others wear them around their necks, while others conceal them behind clasped hands. Each figure's head is tilted back slightly, gazing skyward.
>
> Integrated into the elaborate relief are other ancient carvings and runic engravings that depict strange creatures nestled about the legs of the giant figures.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Seven Ancients
>
> A character who makes a successful **Science (DC 14)** check is able to interpret that the orbs borne by each figure likely represent Ember's six elemental moons, plus one additional sphere.
>
> - **Knowledge: Cosmology**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character recognizes that the seventh orb is a stylized depiction of Lantyr.
>
> A character who succeeds on a **Society (DC 14)** check surmises that the figures likely represented priests of these ancient people. With limited knowledge of Shent legend, it's possible that these seven figures were seers.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: These oversized figures are depicted in subtle ways that seem wrong for Shent - their pose, garb, and style of carving seem somehow even more ancient, suggesting that they may actually be ancestors of the Shent.
>
> Characters who can translate **Language: Pathward** are able to decipher an engraving at the base of the reliefs:
>
> > The seven sky watchers observe the motions of the cosmos as honored Vanexis stand guard. Blessed are those born beneath the skies of Ember, may they never walk in starless voids again.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Depicted alongside the giant figures in the stone are numerous graceful yet lanky cat-like beings with long feathered tails and similarly feathered wings. They do not look up as the humanoid figures do, but instead keep their eyes level, as though watching over the figures.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Unfamiliar Creatures
>
> These creatures are [[Vanexis]]. Their forms are unfamiliar and do not appear in any lore, history, or religion of the Shent people.
>
> - Characters with **Knowledge: Fey** identify that the likeness of the creatures resembles that of [[Casia]], however with some notable differences.
> - Characters proficient with **Language: Pathward** that read the above inscription can infer that the creatures depicted are "Vanexis."
