# Sanctuary

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Lined with a half-dozen wooden pews and adorned with a large primordial altar to the south, this overgrown chamber is obviously some kind of sanctuary or chapel, but the austere ceremony that once presided here seems to have been replaced by the savage relics and rites of more nefarious occupants … a large runic symbol etched into the floor appears sullied with blood or crimson amber (if not both), and a pile of assorted bones crowds the southern alcove near the altar.

This chamber was originally the main chamber and sanctuary of Kadra Zann, where adherents of Zor-Thal and pilgrims alike were welcome to worship, rest, and commune. Over time, the place was ravaged by the rampant growth of the [[Bloodwoods]] and the encroachment of local wildlife. Now, it stands defiled by the Doomsayers — converted into their own baleful sanctum for the worship of [[Primordis]] and [[The Silent Pack]].

Currently, a lone [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] is stationed here with a mop and bucket performing the daily cleaning chores. If the characters somehow manage to enter the Sanctuary without alerting the solitary Acolyte, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As you peer into the sanctuary, you notice a lone occupant near the far side of the chamber who appears quite busy with the menial task of swabbing the floors and other surfaces with a wet rag — too busy, in fact, to notice your arrival.

If the characters don't avoid detection as they enter, but have managed to avoid detection in the Courtyard, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> As you step into the sanctuary, the grinding of the front gate seems to have alerted a lone occupant near the far side of the chamber. They immediately abandon their menial task of swabbing the floors and other surfaces here to confront you.
>
> > You look a little lost, friends … Care to explain what the hell you're doing here?

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Doomsayer Acolyte Tactics
>
> No matter how the characters prepare for this moment, the [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] is &Reference[surprise]{Surprised} by their untimely appearance.
>
> At the start of combat, the Doomsayer Acolyte will raise the alarm by triggering the Alarm Spell on the [[Courtyard's]] Locked Gate and cast the [[Fog Cloud]] Spell on the party or near the threshold using their [[Spellcasting]] before retreating to the [[Doomsayer Barracks]] to alert the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] located there.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Acolyte will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - Obviously outnumbered, the Acolyte will target themself with the [[Resistance]] Spell in an attempt to curb any overt enemy damage types.
> - Otherwise, the Acolyte will ready a ranged [[Pitchvine Net]] attack from the relative safety of the Sanctuary, targeting as many enemy characters as possible as they enter.
> - If the party has an allied Beast with them, the Acolyte will target the Beast with the [[Animal Friendship]] Spell in an attempt to slow the party's progress.
> - If the Acolyte is dealt substantial damage and has the opportunity to heal, they'll cast the [[Healing Word]] Spell as they retreat.
> - If cornered, the Acolyte will resort to melee attacks with their [[Quarterstaff]] during a desperate sortie.
>
> Unlike other Doomsayers of Kadra Zann, this Acolyte is new to the order and his loyalties are not as severe. If the characters manage to subdue the Acolyte, they may interrogate him with meaningful results: continue with the Interrogating the Acolyte section below. Otherwise, skip to the Exploring the Sanctuary.

> [!info] Social
> #### Interrogating the Acolyte
>
> If the party manages to successfully subdue the Doomsayer Acolyte before he retreats or raises the alarm, the neophyte Doomsayer will be their most reliable source of information about what lurks within Kadra Zann.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 14)**, **Intimidation (DC 17)**, or **Diplomacy (DC 14)** check is able to convince the Acolyte to share some information, although his answers are desperate, cryptic, and accusatory. This information includes:
>
> - The Acolyte's name (Hargos) and origin as a native of Brevin.
> - Kyrban's current location in the [[Atrium Floor]] area, where he is conducting a secret primordial rite.
> - The Doomsayer plot to bring [[Primordis]] to terrestrial Ember.
> - The location of Hodge, the [[Arcturian]] trap-springer from Talei, and a hint of his fate as a victim of Kyrban's ritual.
> - The location of the [[Sanguinary Warden]] named Cael, who is imprisoned in the [[Dungeon]] below.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 13)** check is able to confirm the relative veracity of the Acolyte's claims.
>
> The Acolyte's answers are brief and few. Once the conversation feels appropriately exhausted, with just enough tension remaining, the Acolyte resigns himself to an early fate and says the following:
>
> > I've said all I can say. Kyrban's wrath is more dire than any punishment you could possibly mete out. Go ahead. Do your worst.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Sanctuary
>
> The curious arcane sphere that hovers above the southern altar is the singular-most interesting feature of the sanctuary, and radiates a strange, otherwordly energy.
>
> Characters with **Attunement: Primordis** are inexplicably and metaphysically drawn to the sphere, as if spiritually called to it.
>
> A cursory examination of the sphere reveals the following:
>
> - The outer part of the sphere is crafted from some unknown alloy, and behaves like a plasma globe when touched — magenta currents of harmless arcane lightning arc out from the orb at the center of the sphere to lick the fingertips of whoever touches the outer sphere.
> - The orb at the center of the sphere shines with a radiant, otherworldly light. If one stares at this light for too long, their head swims with images of a dark forest and the sound of far-off whispers.
> - Mundane light sources that draw near to the sphere begin to magically dim the closer they get to the orb, including the nearby candelabra.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 16)** check can identify this curious element as a "Fin’ian Sphere," and understands it as an immobile artifact capable of serving as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for spellcasters of all types.
>
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Cosmology**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also knows that this artifact (and others like it) originates from [[Primordis]], and that it is not a native part of Kadra Zann as the [[Shent]] built it so long ago.
>
> #### Communing with the Sphere
>
> Any character who touches any part of the sphere becomes the target of a cosmic transmission from Primordis, where an unnamed member of [[The Silent Pack]] addresses them from afar. Read the following:
>
> > A sultry, phantasmal voice addresses you from beyond the veil of space and time, borne on the echoes of some ghostly purr:
> >
> > "So, you have come to Kadra Zann in search of Kyrban and his Doomsayers. You deserve congratulations on your inventiveness and dedication; but your quarry still awaits, and time is of the essence, my pet. Before you take your leave, I will answer thee thy questions three … "
>
> The first character who chooses to touch the orb at the center of the Fin’ian Sphere becomes the target of the [[Commune]] Spell, with The Silent Pack serving as the divine being.
>
> > The phantom voice speaks once more:
> >
> > "Kyrban's ambitions will soon exceed our plans for him, and his madness is all-consuming. We have need of new champions, with new blood, and new hungers. Are you fit to run with the Silent Pack? Our covenant can be yours … you need only sever Kyrban's mortal soul by making him drink of the Shadebranch sap. Then, and only then, will he truly witness the Gloaming of the First Forest and be released from his madness. And only then will you earn your place among us, and the power that it holds. The decision is yours."
> >
> > And with that, the voice retreats back into nothingness from whence it came.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Warlock Patron: The Silent Pack
>
> This interaction with the Fin’ian Sphere marks a pivotal moment for the [[Where Shadows Lie]] Event, and presents the party with an opportunity to ally themselves with the mysterious and mercurial Silent Pack.
>
> Any characters who take up the Silent Pack's mission at this time will earn the opportunity to forge a future relationship with these mysterious [[Casia]] of [[Primordis]]. Following the conclusion of the Ruby Grove Side Quest, each participating character can pursue one or more Levels of the [[Warlock]] class using the Silent Pack as their patrons, at the Gamemaster's discretion.
