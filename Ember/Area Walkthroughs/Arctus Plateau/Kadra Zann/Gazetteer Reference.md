# Gazetteer Reference

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A grand stone structure peeks out of the massively overgrown brambles and vines that choke this mountainous section of the Bloodwoods. Crafted from huge slabs of pale stone that have all but fallen into abject ruin, the ancient edifice appears to be crafted among the hollows of the mountain itself, with steps ascending to a main gate from the north. Meanwhile, the surrounding area seems completely obstructed by rampant Bloodwoods growth and the ravages of time. A lone statue of an imposing humanoid figure can be seen lurking at the apex of this monument, like some timeless sentinel left by Kadra Zann's creators.

The ancient Shent ruin known as Kadra Zann was once a temple to a forgotten deity of the Sentina, a pantheon that — much like this ancient edifice — has been all but lost to time. Now, this overgrown place serves as the clandestine headquarters of a covert organization of primordial spellcasters known as the Doomsayers, whose furtive and questionable motives spell danger for the Arctus Plateau. Whatever lies within the ruin of Kadra Zann remains a mystery to the people of the Bloodwoods, who know aught but legends about the enigmatic, overgrown sanctuary.

Kadra Zann is an ancient [[Shent]] ruin nestled in a mountainous pocket of the Bloodwoods north of Talei, and was once a temple to the forgotten [[Sentina]] god known as Zor-Thal, The Builder. Overrun by amerasp nests and pernicious Bloodwoods foliage, this clandestine place now serves as the secret headquarters for the mad druid Kyrban and his Doomsayers — heralds of [[The Silent Pack]] who aim to transplant a portion of [[Primordis]] to Ember itself, and usher in a new age of fey influence.

### Environment

Most of the stonework and landscape surrounding the main edifice of Kadra Zann has been reclaimed by the wilderness, and much of the thorny brambles and vines that choke the nearby wilderness have grown on and within the ruined Shent structure itself. Other than those areas still defined by the ancient flagstones of the Shent architects who built this place, a lot of the terrain here is rather difficult for adventurers who seek to explore its secrets (new and forgotten alike).

### Ecology

For time uncounted, Kadra Zann sat forgotten and abandoned, so the only wildlife that frequented the area was that of the Bloodwoods and nearby [[Wedgelands]] — and even those creatures were hesitant to prowl the ancient ruins of the place out of some untenable, primal fear. Now, thanks to the influence of Kyrban and his Doomsayers, Kadra Zann is a thriving home for various flora and fauna that participate in the hypernaturalistic Doomsayer ecosystem. These include typical Bloodwoods wildlife alongside amerasps and fey graylings. Meanwhile, the Doomsayers have cultivated a few plants of Primordis that aren't normally encountered on Ember, like the gadha flower, grav root, and the malevolent Shadebranch that grows within Kadra Zann's lowest level.

### Lore

Kadra Zann's prominence as a place of worship for devotees of Zor-Thal is precisely what made the long-forgotten temple a source of interest for Kyrban and the Doomsayers, who identified its latent potential for serving as a powerful cosmic anchor for Primordis. After all, what better place to form a trans-planar conduit to the Realm of Capricious Sorcery than a sanctuary devoted to the god of anchors and earth? In the catacombs below Kadra Zann, the Doomsayers have secretly rooted a Primordial Tree above one of Zor-Thal's most powerful artifacts — a cosmic measuring tool known as the Star Compass — which now lays buried in the ground, hidden and subverted by Doomsayer magic. This tree is the source of much potency for the Doomsayers, who use its otherworldly sap to transmogrify humanoids of Ember into fey creatures (among other unsavory deeds).
