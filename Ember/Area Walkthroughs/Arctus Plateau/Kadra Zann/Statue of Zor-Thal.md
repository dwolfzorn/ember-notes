# Statue of Zor-Thal

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> An impressive stone statue of a deific giant stands proud and mighty above Kadra Zann, bearing an ornamented hammer in one hand and his outstretched palm in the other — as if beckoning the entire Cosmos to heed his call.

This statue of Zor-Thal stands here as a testament to the Builder God and his legacy, and is responsible for channeling divine and arcane energies into the recesses of Kadra Zann.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Examining the Statue
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check is able to recognize the statue as an effigy of the [[Sentina]] god known as Zor-Thal, The Builder. Kadra Zann was once a mighty temple to Zor-Thal, erected here of all places thanks to the powerful forces of magic that flow through the region; and the temple itself served as a conduit for the force of arcane magic, a power that's been subverted by the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] for their nefarious means.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check, and achieves the critical success result listed below.
> - **Knowledge: Gods**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also knows that a powerful and esoteric Shent artifact developed by Zor-Thal presides at the [[Atrium Floor]] level, marked by six globe-crowned pillars of stone.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 12)** check can recognize that the exquisite stonework of this statue was designed by masters of magical artifice, and once worked as a divine and arcane magical focus. No doubt, the corruption of Kadra Zann via the Doomsayers has compromised its abilities.
>
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character automatically succeeds on this check, and achieves the critical success result listed below.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character automatically succeeds on this check, and achieves the critical success result listed below.
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character can also deduce that the statue's ability to channel magical energy can penetrate the lowest reaches of Kadra Zann, and that there is likely corresponding equipment of an esoteric nature somewhere below.

### After Kyrban's Defeat

Once the [[Where Shadows Lie]] Event has been completed, the nefarious influence of [[Kyrban]] and the Doomsayers no longer stains the magic of Zor-Thal's statue.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Interacting with the Statue
>
> Any spellcaster who places their hand upon the statue is imbued with the understanding that the statue itself works as a [[Spellcasting Focus]].
>
> Any character who touches the statue while uttering a prayer or secular homage to Zor-Thal gains a temporary +1 bonus to Wisdom Saving Throws for a duration of `[[/roll 2d4]]` hours. A character can only benefit from this effect once per week.
