# Area Overview

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Area Map Context
>
> The [[Kadra Zann]] Area Map depicts an ancient [[Shent]] temple in the [[Bloodwoods]] north of [[Talei]], which serves as the hidden stronghold for [[Kyrban]] and the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]]. It is featured in the [[Where Shadows Lie]] Event of the [[Ruby Grove]] Side Quest.
>
> - This map depicts three Scene Levels: Ground Leve, Upper Level, and Lower Level.
> - Although this edifice once stood as a temple to the [[Sentina]] god known as Zor-Thal, The Builder, it has been repurposed by the Doomsayers as a sanctuary to [[The Silent Pack]] — who aim to transplant a slice of [[Primordis]] to this section of the [[Arctus Plateau]] via the sap of the [[Shadebranch]].
> - The party arrives at either the [[Courtyard]] or the easternmost section of the [[Amerasp Caves]], depending on whether or not they manage to locate the secret entrance during their scout of the location's perimeter.
> - While scouting the Courtyard, the party can discover a third entrance attached to the [[Vespiary]] area of the map.

## Gameplay Details

The areas of Kadra Zann have the following features unless otherwise specified.

### Levels & Elevation

The Kadra Zann Scene features three vertical Levels of elevation, including:

- **Kadra Zann - Ground Level:** The ground level is set to an elevation of 0 and has 60-foot-high ceilings.
- **Kadra Zann - Upper Level:** The upper level is set to an elevation of 60 and has 60-foot-high ceilings.
- **Kadra Zann - Lower Level:** The lower level is set to an elevation of -60 and has 60-foot-high ceilings.

The distance between levels is 60 feet in most places, and stairways serve as &reference[Difficult Terrain] due to the height of steps. Due to damage and decay in the structure, only the upper chambers and lower chambers are available, with the intervening levels too choked with debris to be accessible.

### Illumination

The interiors of Kadra Zann are &reference[dim light]{Dimly Lit} by candelabras, braziers, and lambent amerasp galls.

Outside areas are subject to exterior lighting and associated weather conditions.

### Terrain

The ruinous terrain here resembles the typical landscape of a well-constructed but remarkably aged Shent edifice, with enough clutter, rubble, and detritus to create intermittent spots of &reference[difficult terrain]. The scale of this ancient monument makes it abundantly clear that this place was built by and for [[Giant]] rather than conventional humanoids of the modern era.
