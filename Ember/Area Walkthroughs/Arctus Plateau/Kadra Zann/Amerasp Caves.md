# Amerasp Caves

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The twisting earthen tunnels here are layered with the telltale signs of amerasp nests, novel and remnant alike. A subtle thrumming noise echoes throughout, along with the steady and lambent pulsation of several galls or pods, which appear to line the tunnels at irregular intervals.

The northern half of Kadra Zann's Lower Level is defined by a lattice of cavernous tunnels. Lined with amerasp nests, these earthen corridors can be perilous for those who might disturb the sweltering amerasp galls here, ripe with newborn [[Amerasp Swarms]].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Surveying the Amerasp Caves
>
> Any character moving through this area with a `[[/skill perception 14 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 12)** check is able to more readily notice one or more of the eleven Amerasp Gall Traps that line the tunnels here. See the "Amerasp Gall Traps" section below for a full description of how these traps work.
>
> - **Knowledge: Beasts**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Subterranea**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Amerasp Gall Traps
>
> The amerasp galls located throughout the cave tunnels here are ripe with newborn [[Amerasp Swarms]]. Any character who moves within 20 feet of an amerasp gall must make a successful `[[/save Dexterity 15]]` save or cause the gall to burst and an Amerasp Swarm to appear.
>
> - **Knowledge: Beasts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> Roll initiative for each swarm when it appears. Per your discretion, this combat may alert [[Kyrban]] and his Doomsayer allies on the [[Atrium Floor]].

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Interactable Objects: Amerasp Gall Traps
>
> Each amerasp gall trap is an Interactable Object that will cause the gall to burst and spawn an [[Amerasp Swarm]] when activated.

>
> At the start of combat, the [[Amerasp Swarm]] will use its [[Unknown]] and [[Unknown]] features to engage the most vulnerable creature in combat.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Amerasp Swarm will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
