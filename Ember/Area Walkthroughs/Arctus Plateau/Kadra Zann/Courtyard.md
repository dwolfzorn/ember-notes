# Courtyard

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The small clearing to the north of the main gate is remarkably serene, as if the tiny meadow were somehow sitting in the eye of an otherworldly storm. The threshold itself is an imposing one, and a striking image is etched onto the face of the gate's heavy set of double stone doors: you see an image of Ember's cosmos with the living planet at its center, encircled by the moons and inner realms and flanked by the shadow of an imposing humanoid figure in the background. Armed with a large hammer, this figure seems to be some kind of deific blacksmith, and possesses the telltale features of a Shent giant. As you linger here, a breeze rustles through the Bloodwoods around you, like whispers from the dark unseen.

The courtyard here is relatively unremarkable, save for the front gate to Kadra Zann. There are scant signs of the Doomsayers themselves, as they often take efforts to conceal their timely comings and goings. The front gate itself is locked and trapped, but observant characters can overcome these obstacles; if not, the party can wait long enough for a lone [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] to open the door.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Courtyard
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check is able to notice the following:
>
> - The upper reaches of Kadra Zann's exteriors are overgrown with uncounted decades of [[Bloodwoods]] growth, and the eastern tower appears to be pockmarked with the remnants of scattered clusters of [[Amerasp]] nests.
> - The sound of multiple small waterfalls cascades down the mountainside somewhere nearby.
> - The steps here are coated with residual stains of what could be mop water or some other aqueous substance.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Arcana (DC 13)** check can identify the telltale signs of dangerous arcane glyphs hidden among the etchings on the gate. Surely, it must be trapped with some kind of magical deterrent.
>
> - [[Detect Magic]] **spell**: The character automatically succeeds on this check, and achieves the critical success result listed below.
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character can also determine that the etched image of [[Akon]] conceals an arcane button that can temporarily disable the magic.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 15)** check can determine the following: according to a few sets of tracks, roving bands of humanoids appear to come and go from the front gate with irregularity. If the characters have identified the tracks of the Doomsayers during a previous encounter like [[Corrupted Nest]] or [[The Primordial Grove]], they will recognize the booted and sandaled footprints as those of [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] or [[Doomsayer Acolyte]].
>
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character automatically succeeds on this check, and achieves the critical success result listed below.
> - **Knowledge: Forensics**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character is able to notice that the tracks lead towards the depiction of Akon on the etching — the specific moon needed to bypass the trapped gate (see below).
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 14)** or **Arcana (DC 14)** check is able to recognize the humanoid figure depicted in the etching on the gate as the [[Sentina]] god known as Zor-Thal, The Builder.
>
> - **Knowledge: Shent**: The character automatically succeeds on this check, and achieves the critical success result listed below.
> - **Knowledge: Gods**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character is well-versed enough with Zor-Thal to know that the Shent deity praised the discipline of Akon above all else in the cosmos. This serves as a clue to the trapped gate (see below).

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Locked Gate
>
> The front gate to Kadra Zann is locked with ancient Shent mechanisms. Any character who makes a successful `[[/tool thief 16]]` check with proficiency is able to unlock the gate. Otherwise, it can be unlocked using one of two [[Kadra Zann Skeleton Key]]; one of these keys is in the possession of Kyrban himself (currently located in the [[Atrium Floor]] area), and the other communal key is located on a wall hook in the [[Study]] nearby.
>
> #### Baneful Doorway Trap
>
> The trap here is more elaborate and requires a more direct examination of the gate, accompanied by the following skill check.
>
> Any character with **Awareness (DC 14, Passive)** notices that the doors here are magically trapped, detecting the faint glimmer of dangerous arcane luminance deep within the beveled grooves of the etchings.
>
> - **Critical Success**: The character identifies that the shape of the image of Akon as a centerpiece to the etchings and of some importance to the gate's mechanism — regardless of whether they are aware that the symbol itself relates to Zor-Thal.
>
> Any creature who attempts to open the doors without touching or tracing the etched image of Akon must face **Judgment of Sockets (Hazard 4)** and are **Weakened** until they finish a Rest.
>
> The gate is also the target of a daily [[Alarm]] Spell. If a character opens the gate without touching the etched image of Akon or otherwise dispelling the magic, the gate emits the sound of a handbell for 10 seconds within 60 feet — alerting the [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] in the [[Sanctuary]] and the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] in the [[Doomsayer Barracks]].

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Sounding the Alarm
>
> If the party manages to trigger the Alarm Spell trap that is placed on the Courtyard gate, or if they otherwise alert the nearby Doomsayers and allow them ample time to inform others, the ritual that is currently taking place in the Atrium Floor area will proceed as planned. However, all enemy creatures in Kadra Zann will be alerted to the party's presence, and will prepare for their arrival accordingly.
>
> Please refer to the "Sounding the Alarm" section of each chamber for a description of any important changes to the game state.

### Lying in Wait

If the characters decide to wait for the front door to open thanks to the comings and goings of the Doomsayers, their patience will eventually be rewarded.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Hiding from Doomsayer View
>
> If the party tries to deliberately hide outside the front gate of Kadra Zann, each character must make a successful **Stealth (DC 12)** check to avoid detection from the [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] who will emerge from the nearby [[Sanctuary]] to investigate any casual disturbances.

After an hour has passed, read the following if the characters manage to successfully hide from onlookers:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Your mind wanders as you wait, and just when you begin to think you might have exerted more foolishness than patience, a sudden noise grabs your attention from the south — where the stone gate to Kadra Zann slowly cracks open with a stony grind. The eastern door opens roughly two feet wide before a young and disheveled Arcturian steps outside to toss a bucket of dirty water down the front steps. With his chores apparently done, the rugged steward lights a dustweed cigarette on the stoop and lets his mind wander. If you dare act quickly enough, you may be able to do something before he steps back inside.

Alternately, if the party manages to make enough noise to alert the residents of Kadra Zann of their presence outside, the fledgling Doomsayer Acolyte may open the door to personally investigate what's going on outside. Read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Suddenly and without warning, the stone gate to Kadra Zann cracks open with a stony grind. The eastern door opens roughly five inches wide before you spy a young and disheveled Arcturian looking through the narrow gap. As soon as his face appears, you hear the distinct sound of an alarm bell ringing throughout the yard.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Doomsayer Acolyte Tactics
>
> No matter how the characters prepare for this moment, the [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] is &Reference[surprise]{Surprised} by their untimely appearance.
>
> At the start of combat, the Doomsayer Acolyte will raise the alarm by triggering the Alarm Spell on the Locked Gate (see above) and cast the [[Fog Cloud]] Spell on or near the threshold using their [[Spellcasting]] before retreating to the [[Doomsayer Barracks]] to alert the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] located there.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Acolyte will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - Obviously outnumbered, the Acolyte will target themself with the [[Resistance]] Spell in an attempt to curb any overt enemy damage types.
> - Otherwise, the Acolyte will ready a ranged [[Pitchvine Net]] attack from the relative safety of the Sanctuary, targeting as many enemy characters as possible as they enter.
> - If the party has an allied Beast with them, the Acolyte will target the Beast with the [[Animal Friendship]] Spell in an attempt to slow the party's progress.
> - If the Acolyte is dealt substantial damage and has the opportunity to heal, they'll cast the [[Healing Word]] Spell as they retreat.
> - If cornered, the Acolyte will resort to melee attacks with their [[Quarterstaff]] during a desperate sortie.
>
> Unlike other Doomsayers of Kadra Zann, this Acolyte is new to the order and his loyalties are not as severe. If the characters manage to subdue the Acolyte, they may interrogate him with meaningful results: continue with the Interrogating the Acolyte section below.

> [!info] Social
> #### Interrogating the Acolyte
>
> If the party manages to successfully subdue the Doomsayer Acolyte before he retreats or raises the alarm, the neophyte Doomsayer will be their most reliable source of information about what lurks within Kadra Zann.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 14)**, **Intimidation (DC 17)**, or **Diplomacy (DC 14)** check is able to convince the Acolyte to share some information, although his answers are desperate, cryptic, and accusatory. This information include:
>
> - The Acolyte's name (Hargos) and origin as a native of Brevin.
> - Kyrban's current location in the [[Atrium Floor]] area, where he is conducting a secret primordial rite.
> - The Doomsayer plot to bring [[Primordis]] to terrestrial Ember.
> - The location of Hodge, the [[Arcturian]] trap-springer from Talei, and a hint of his fate as a victim of Kyrban's ritual.
> - The location of the [[Sanguinary Warden]] named Cael, who is imprisoned in the [[Dungeon]] below.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 13)** check is able to confirm the relative veracity of the Acolyte's claims.
>
> The Acolyte's answers are brief and few. Once the conversation feels appropriately exhausted, with just enough tension remaining, the Acolyte resigns himself to an early fate and says the following:
>
> > I've said all I can say. Kyrban's wrath is more dire than any punishment you could possibly mete out. Go ahead. Do your worst.

### The Hiddden Entrance

A concealed entrance to the Lower Level [[Amerasp Caves]] is located on the easternmost edge of the Courtyard.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Spotting the Concealed Cave Entrance
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 22 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 20)** check while scouting the perimeter of Kadra Zann from the Courtyard is able to locate the hole in the ground to the east that leads to the [[Amerasp Caves]].
>
> - **Knowledge: Beasts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Subterranea**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.

If the party manages to discover this alternate entrance, read the following aloud:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Your survey of the area proves fruitful when you eventually spy a lone amerasp hovering above an opening in the stony ground to the east, where a natural tunnel seems to wind its way down to Kadra Zann's lower levels. And the closer you get to this furtive maw, the more you detect the faint sound of wasp wings in the dark distance.

Once the party approaches, the amerasp flies away to relative safety.
