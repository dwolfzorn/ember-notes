# Vespiary

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> An assortment of large wasp nests dot this hideously overgrown chamber, clinging onto the archaic stone like some cancerous infestation, rotted and brown and thrumming with primal antagonism. The smell of incense fills the room, but you spy very little in the way of civilization here — no more than a couple of netted cages and a few tattered rugs. The room has otherwise gone to the wasps, and your ears can't shake the sound of countless amerasp wings lurking within the earthy crimson nests before you.

This chamber serves as a dedicated place for Kyrban to cultivate a novel breed of docile amerasps that allow him to conduct his experiments in secrecy, and with a greater amount of control. Four [[Amerasp Swarms]] and a single [[Amerasp Queen]] currently dwell here.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Vespiary
>
> A few features and elements of the chamber are plain for all to see:
>
> - The room appears to have been stripped of most of the meaningful furniture or decoration from its previous area, as the nests and a few netted cages are all that reside here.
> - A concealed entrance to the chamber is located on the eastern wall, and is connected to a rickety wooden staircase outside that terminates somewhere on the Ground Level nearby.
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 15 passive format=long]]` who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check will notice the following:
>
> - Each of the two netted cages here contains an exquisitely-carved carnelian incense burner worth  **25**, fashioned into the likeness of a howling [[Casia]], along with a dose of [[Ruby]].
>
> Each turn a character attempt to actively search or navigate the chamber, they must succeed on a **Stealth (DC 10)** check to avoid attracting the unwanted attention of the [[Amerasp Swarms]] located here.
>
> On a failed result, combat begins, and it will likely be loud enough to attract the attention of the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] in the [[Doomsayer Barracks]] below, or the [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] in the adjoining [[Sanctuary]].

A total of four Amerasp Swarms attack the party as they enter the room; their queen hides in the safety of her nest at the room's center.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Token Reveal: Amerasp Queen
>
> The [[Amerasp Queen]] is initially **Hidden** at the center of the room. On Initiative Count 20 of the second round of combat, **Reveal** the token and have it join the combat.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Amerasp Swarm Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, each Amerasp Swarm will move to target the closest enemy character with [[Stings]] using their [[Swarm]] feature if possible, and their [[Shimmer]] ability as needed.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Amerasp Swarms will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - One Amerasp Swarm remains dedicated to the protection of the Amerasp Queen inside the central nest.
> - As long as the feature continues to recharge, the Amerasp Swarms will use their [[Shimmer]] ability as a reaction each turn.
> - The three swarms that aren't dedicated to the Amerasp Queen will follow the first enemy they attack using additional [[Stings]] attacks along with their [[Swarm]] feature.
>
> All three Amerasp Swarms will remain here and fight to the death in an attempt to protect the Amerasp Queen. If the Queen retreats, all of the remaining Swarms will retreat alongside her.
>
> #### Amerasp Queen Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the Amerasp Queen will take a defensive position in the nest and activate her [[Shimmer]] ability if targeted.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Amerasp Queen will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - As long as the feature continues to recharge, the Amerasp Queen will use her [[Shimmer]] ability as a reaction each turn.
> - If one or more enemy characters engage the Queen in melee combat, she will utilize her [[Multiattack]] and [[Sting]] actions accordingly.
>
> If the Amerasp Queen loses half or more of her hit points during combat, she will attempt to flee via the closest possible escape route (using the concealed entrance on the eastern wall if no better options are available). Any surviving Amerasp Swarms will attempt to escort the Queen as she escapes.
