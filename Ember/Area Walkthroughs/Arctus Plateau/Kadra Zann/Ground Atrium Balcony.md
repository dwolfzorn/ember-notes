# Ground Atrium Balcony

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This ground-level balcony area is tucked away like some unused alcove, but nevertheless provides a stately view of the atrium below. Like most areas of this half-forgotten temple, the Bloodwoods overgrowth here is rampant.

The balcony here overlooks the central atrium of Kadra Zann, and provides a noteworthy vantage point for characters who might observe the [[Atrium Floor]]. Although it's unlikely that the party will explore this specific area, industrious characters may find an inspired way to navigate the temple's various Levels.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Balcony
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 13)** or **Wilderness (DC 13)** check is able to locate some vines here that are strong enough to enable their descent to the next level below. Treat the vine as a 50-foot [[Rope]]. (Reminder: each Level has 60-foot-high ceilings).
>
> - **Knowledge: Plants**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also gains **+2 Boons** on any skill checks made to climb or descend the vine.
