# Workshop

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This chamber is decorated with an array of alchemical tools and clinical equipment, and the air here is rife with the lingering smell of some curious material experimentation. Based on your experience with ruby, you'd quickly identify that sweet concoction as one of the loitering odors, accompanied by several other ghostly scents you can't readily place. The tables and shelves here are adorned with various substances and components, many of which bear a nacreous crimson luster you've grown accustomed to seeing throughout the Bloodwoods.

This chamber serves as Kyrban's workshop for alchemical and biological experimentation, and is where he derived the specific formula for [[Queen's Ruby]].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Workshop
>
> The room is decorated with an array of materials and equipment for alchemical experimentation. If the characters take a brief moment to search the area, they'll locate the following:
>
> - [[Alchemist's Supplies]]
> - `[[/roll 1d2+1]]` doses of [[Ruby]].
> - `[[/roll 2d2]]` doses of [[Queen's Ruby]].
> - The raw biological components of several dead [[Amerasps]] and [[Amerasp Queens]].
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check while searching the room is also able to find the following:
>
> - `[[/roll 1d2]]` doses of [[Antitoxin]].
> - The components of a [[Healer's Kit]].
> - One dose of [[Basic Poison]].
