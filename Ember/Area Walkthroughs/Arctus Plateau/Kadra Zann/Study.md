# Study

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> An array of desks and bookshelves crowd this chamber, which serves as a makeshift library or study of some kind. Despite their careful organization, many of the moth-eaten scrolls here seem to be fraught with the ravages of weather and the ceaseless march of time.

This converted chamber serves as a nontraditional library and communal worship area, with desks covered in research dedicated to vespiculture and dark druidism.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Study
>
> A quick examination of the area reveals the following:
>
> - Many of the scrolls and books here are rotten to the point of illegibility, but 2 noteworthy [[Books]] lie among the tatters: a non-fiction book called "An Examination of the Flora of the Northern Arctus Plateau," and a fiction book labeled "Pirates of the Yawning Sea."
> - The contents of a [[Scholar's Pack]].
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 15 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check while searching the room is able to locate a [[Kadra Zann Skeleton Key]], which hangs on a hook on the northeastern wall here, just behind the candelabra.
