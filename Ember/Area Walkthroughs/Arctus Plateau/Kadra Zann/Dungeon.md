# Dungeon

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The air of this grimy chamber is laden with the stink of sweat and piss. If the quartet of rusty cages is any indication, this room has been converted into a dungeon. A solitary prisoner bound in manacles resides here in the eastern cell, a Sanguinary by the looks of his bedraggled garb. The despondent caster doesn't even look up when you arrive, seemingly numb to the comings and goings of the Doomsayers who've imprisoned him.

This chamber serves as a novel dungeon for prisoners of Kadra Zann, which tend to include humanoids that [[Kyrban]] has deemed worthy of transmogrification into [[Grayling]] (often after an extended period of cruel experimentation). Many captives have been held here throughout the days that have led up to this moment, from wayward travelers to those who would label themselves enemies of the Doomsayers.

Currently, the only prisoner is a [[Sanguinary Warden]] named Hodge, who is caged and manacled in the easternmost holding cell. He ignores the party until they make it obvious that they're not Doomsayers (whether of their own volition or not). If and when the party makes their presence known, read the following:

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The prisoner in the cage look up at you with a start, and you can recognize a look of unexpected hope in the young man's eyes. Based on Avwynn's description of the missing Sanguinary, you'd wager this is the warden named Cael. Without speaking a word, the long-haired prisoner holds a finger to his lips in warning, and points towards the atrium to the north before pantomiming a monstrous visage. Finally, he clings to the rusty bars of the cage door with newfound optimism, motioning towards the lock with pointed fervor.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Dungeon
>
> Any character that makes a successful **Arcana (DC 13)** or **Awareness (DC 18)** check is able to discern that the cages themselves are emblazoned with strange glyphs and wrapped in curious vines, contributing to some kind of anti-magic field for their interior spaces.
>
> - [[Detect Magic]] **spell**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Artifacts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> Any character that makes a successful **Medicine (DC 12)** check can see that Cael is malnourished. He has been stripped of nearly all of his belongings, save the clothes of a Sanguinary Warden on his back.

> [!info] Social
> #### Conversation with Cael
>
> The prisoner in the easternmost cage is the missing [[Sanguinaries]] named Cael, who the party might have learned of during [[Local Experts]].
>
> **CaelChaotic Good, Arcturian Human, he/him**
>
> This foolhardy young [[Sanguinary Warden]] originally hails from [[Nain]], and has been a proud adherent to the tenets of [[Nimaelle]] ever since a timely encounter with [[Avwynn Taol]] at [[Corpin Sanctuary]].
>
> Despite his bedraggled condition, Cael is invigorated by the party's arrival, and will do whatever he can to help assist their efforts to defeat Kyrban and the Doomsayers. Although he maintains silence as much as possible, bringing his voice only as loud as the softest whisper, Cael is eager to discuss the following:
>
> - His identity as a Sanguinary Warden of [[Arbore Sanctorus]].
> - The curious location of his missing equipment. Unbeknownst to Cael (but known to all Doomsayers), the Sanguinary Warden's equipment is located in the [[Storage Chamber]] nearby to the east.
> - Kyrban and the nefarious Doomsayer plot.
> - The current location of Hodge, the missing trap-springer from Talei, who is currently being subjected to Kyrban's ritual in the [[Atrium Floor]] area nearby.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 13)** check is able to confirm the veracity of Cael's claims.

>
> **A:**
>
> > My name is Cael. I am a warden of these woods, as dangerous and unpredictable as they are. These Doomsayers have imprisoned me here for their nefarious plot, and I fear my life is forfeit if you cannot find a way to release me.

>
> **A:**
>

They seek to supplant the Bloodwoods with a patch of The First Forest, grafted straight from the darkest reaches of Primordis itself. And these "graylings" that conspire with the dark druids were once people of Ember … twisted and metamorphosed into the fey creatures they are now. Perhaps I will meet such a gruesome end myself …

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Unlocking the Cages
>
> The four cages located here are locked, and can be unlocked using one of two [[Kadra Zann Skeleton Key]]. One of these keys is in the possession of Kyrban himself (currently located in the nearby [[Atrium Floor]] area), and the other communal key is located on a wall hook in the [[Study]] upstairs.
>
> Any character who makes a successful `[[/tool thief 16]]` check with proficiency is able to unlock a cage.
>
> The [[Manacles]] that currently bind Cael's wrists can also be unlocked using a [[Kadra Zann Skeleton Key]].
>
> #### Unlocking the Atrium Floor
>
> Before the characters can explore the nearby [[Atrium Floor]] area, they must contend with the locked door located to the north. This door can also be unlocked using a [[Kadra Zann Skeleton Key]].
>
> Any character who makes a successful `[[/tool thief 16]]` check with proficiency is able to unlock the door. Alternately, the door can be bashed in with a successful **`[[/skill strength 30]]`** check.
