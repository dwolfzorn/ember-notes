# Kyrban’s Chancery

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This stately room appears to be the private quarters of some spellcaster or learned sage. A desk to the south is littered with scholastic accoutrements, and the walls are lined with shelves that contain a wide assortments of books, maps, and parchments — many of which pertain to esoteric subjects and forgotten lore of the Bloodwoods (if not far, far beyond). It would take some time to sort through all of this material, but you imagine the task would be worth it, based on the sheer lunacy of some of the inspired organization.

This chamber serves as the private study of Kyrban himself, and contains various insights into his specific madness and favored methods. Most notably, it conceals a tome called [[The Primordial Fragments]]: an account of Kyrban's journey to become the fated head of the Doomsayers and their ultimate plot to bring Primordis to Ember.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Unlocking the Chancery
>
> Before the characters can explore Kyrban's Chancery, they must contend with the two locked inner-doors located to the east and west.
>
> The doors can be unlocked using [[Kyrban's Chancery Key]], which is in the possession of Kyrban himself (currently located in the nearby [[Atrium Floor]] area).
>
> Any character who makes a successful `[[/tool thief 20]]` check with proficiency is able to unlock one of the doors. Alternately, the doors can be bashed in with a successful **`[[/skill strength 30]]`** check.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring Kyrban's Chancery
>
> The desk is covered in notes, most of which are written in an incoherent shorthand scrawl of Kyrban's. Any character who takes a brief moment to search the area will readily spot the following:
>
> - A [[Book]] of absurdist fiction and dubious non-fiction of [[Primordis]] called "Myths and Fables of Far Primordis."
> - The [[Shadebranch Missive]].
> - The contents of a [[Scholar's Pack]].
>
> Any character with a `[[/skill perception 15 passive format=long]]` or who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check is also able to discover the following:
>
> - [[The Primordial Fragments]], Kyrban's personal diary and a treatise on his profane discoveries about the Inner Realm of Primordis.
> - 2 [[Spell Scrolls]] of [[Identify]].
> - A lockbox containing  **225** worth of assorted coins and gemstones.
>
> Alternately, all of the aforementioned can be discovered by a character who spends at least one hour searching the room and studying its contents.
