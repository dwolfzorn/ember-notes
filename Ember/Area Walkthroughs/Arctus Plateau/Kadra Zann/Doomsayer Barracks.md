# Doomsayer Barracks

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This chamber has clearly been retrofitted as the residential area of the temple. Whatever this vestibule's original purpose, it is now outfitted with a half-dozen human-sized beds and other accoutrements — like a central fire pit, private storage containers, and assorted personal belongings.

This room serves as the main bedroom area for the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] and [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] that dwell in Kadra Zann. As it is furnished with only six beds, the Barracks are used in rotation as Doomsayers are procedurally sent to and return from the field. The growth of the Doomsayer ranks is a slow but steady endeavor.

A solitary Doomsayer Shadewright lurks here when the party first explores the area: this Shadewright is recuperating from an injury sustained while working in the amerasp groves, and is abstaining from the ceremony currently taking place in the [[Atrium Floor]].

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Doomsayer Shadewright Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the Doomsayer Shadewright will cast [[Barkskin]] on herself as a Bonus Action, then cast [[Haste]] on itself.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Doomsayer Shadewright will prioritize the following actions and abilities:
>
> - In melee and from range, the Doomsayer Shadewright will use her [[Multiattack]], alternating between her [[Quarterstaff]] and her [[Tenebrous Wisp]] as appropriate.
> - When outnumbered, the Doomsayer Shadewright will cast [[Conjure Animals]] between herself and her enemies.
> - When surrounded by enemies, the Doomsayer Shadewright will use her [[Vespine Form]] to transform into an [[Unknown]], relying on the creature's [[Unknown]] feature and Fly speed to reposition herself.
>
> If the Doomsayer Shadewright is &reference[Bloodied], she will either cast [[Healing Word]] to restore her Hit Points, or cast [[Darkness]] in an attempt to reposition herself.
>
> This Shadewright is utterly loyal to Kyrban's cause and will fight to the death. If the characters manage to subdue the Shadewright, they may attempt to interrogate her: continue with the Interrogating the Shadewright section below.
>
> If the Shadewright is slain and cannot be interrogated, skip to the [[Doomsayer Barracks]] section.

> [!info] Social
> #### Interrogating the Shadewright
>
> If the party manages to interrogate the [[Doomsayer Shadewright]], she will have very little to offer in the way of proper explanation. Rather than answering the characters truthfully, the Shadewright will attempt to stall their investigation via overt riddles and threats.
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 15)**, **Intimidation (DC 18)**, or **Diplomacy (DC 15)** check is able to convince the Shadewright to share some information, although her answers are desperate, cryptic, and accusatory. This information includes:
>
> - The Shadewright's name (Helena) and origin as a former [[Sanguinaries]], and her current injury (an ornery vespinoth attack).
> - Kyrban's current location in the [[Atrium Floor]] area, where he is conducting a secret primordial rite.
> - The Doomsayer plot to bring [[Primordis]] to terrestrial Ember.
> - The location of Hodge, the [[Arcturian]] trap-springer from Talei, and a hint of his fate as a victim of Kyrban's ritual.
> - The location of the [[Sanguinary Warden]] named Cael, who is imprisoned in the [[Dungeon]] below.
>
> Any character who succeeds on a **Deception (DC 15)** check is able to tell that the Doomsayer Shadewright conceals truths behind his overt lies and ridicule.
>
> Some examples of specific dialogue for the Shadewright are listed below.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Barracks
>
> A brief search of the area reveals the following:
>
> - An extra set of [[Doomsayer Acolyte]] gear, including a [[Quarterstaff]], [[Leather Armor]], a [[Pitchvine Net]], a [[White Aspen Wand]], and Doomsayer Acolyte Clothes.
> - An extra set of [[Doomsayer Shadewright]] gear, including a [[Quarterstaff]], [[Studded Leather Armor]], a [[White Aspen Wand]], and Doomsayer Shadewright Clothes (but no Vespine Domino).
> - `[[/roll 2d2]]` doses of [[Queen's Ruby]].
> - A bottle of [[Arcturian Liquor]].
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 14)** check is also able to locate a few items that the Doomsayers who live here tried to hide away:
>
> - A [[Potion of Healing]].
> - `[[/roll 1d3+1]]` doses of [[Dustweed]].
