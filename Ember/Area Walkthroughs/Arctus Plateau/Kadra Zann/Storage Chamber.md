# Storage Chamber

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Judging by the haphazard pile of crates and sacks that line the northern wall, this crowded vestibule appears to have been converted into an ersatz storage room. Some of the containers here are in a relative state of disarray, and you notice various provisions and foodstuffs among the clutter.

This cluttered room serves as a makeshift storage chamber for the Doomsayers, and hosts a sundry of miscellaneous items and consumables like lamp oil and dried food goods — some of which are dedicated to the prisoners of the prisoners of the nearby [[Dungeon]] chamber to the west.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Storage Chamber
>
> Any character who takes a brief moment to search this chamber is able to locate the following noteworthy equipment:
>
> - A [[Barrel]] containing the precise equivalent of 100 bottles of red [[Ordani Wine (Common)]].
> - 60 units of dried [[Rations]].
> - The contents of an [[Explorer's Pack]]
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Awareness (DC 13)** check or spends at least 10 minutes searching the chamber is also able to locate an array of personal gear stashed in the barrel to the southeast, including:
>
> - Cael's confiscated equipment, the assorted gear of a [[Sanguinary Warden]].
> - Hodge's confiscated equipment, the assorted gear of an [[Arcturian]]. (Note: At this time, [[Hodge's Heirloom]] is in Hodge's possession in the [[Atrium Floor]] area, where the poor trap-springer is being turned into a [[Grayling]]).
> - `[[/roll 2d2]]` results from the [[Corpse Loot]] table.
