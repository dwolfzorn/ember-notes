# Upper Atrium Balcony

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> From the dizzying heights of this Shent-made promontory, the view of the atrium below is a majestic one. And even here, the overgrowth of the Bloodwoods is ever-present.
>
> On the far side of the atrium, the impressive statue of some deific giant clad in archaic garments stands mighty and proud, like some timeless sentinel from a bygone era.

The balcony here overlooks the central atrium of Kadra Zann, and provides a noteworthy vantage point for characters who might observe the [[Atrium Floor]].

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Balcony
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** or **Wilderness (DC 14)** check is able to locate some vines here that are strong enough to enable their descent to the next level below. Treat the vine as a 100-foot [[Rope]]. (Reminder: each Level has 60-foot-high ceilings, and the Atrium Floor is located 120 feet beneath this balcony area).
>
> - **Knowledge: Plants**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also gains **+2 Boons** on any skill checks made to climb or descend the vine.
