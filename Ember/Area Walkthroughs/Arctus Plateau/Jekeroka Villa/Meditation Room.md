# Meditation Room

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The polished stone floor of this chamber is decorated with tiny glyphs that illuminate the central space with a soft radiance, and a lavish circular pillow made of blue silk sits atop a low dais in the alcove to the north. Next to the pillow, you spy a note alongside a few empty candle holders. Meanwhile, the southern wall appears to have collapsed inward during some seismic disturbance, and large roots have twisted their way inward from the subterranea beyond.

This chamber once served as Jekeroka's private quarters, where he could meditate and rest during his stays at the villa, as well as contemplate his arcane research and revelations.

If the party can successfully solve the riddle of the note left on the northern dais, they can reveal the alloy toggle switch needed to progress the trial.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Exploring the Meditation Room
>
> Any character who attempts to investigate the note will notice it is written in **Language: Common**, scrawled in a vaguely familiar handwriting that resembles other correspondence from the [[Anachraenum]]. The note reads:
>
> > To find what you are looking for,
> > name the treasure we adore;
> > found in places warm and bright,
> > where shadows dance in softest light.
>
> The answer to this riddle is "**knowledge**," which must be spoken aloud by one of the characters in order to [[Jekeroka Villa]].
>
> Any character who makes a successful **Society (DC 13)** check is able to recognize that the glyphs are [[Aedir]] in nature.
>
> - **Knowledge: Aedir**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> Additionally, any character who can read **Language: Towyr** can understand what the glyphs say:
>
> > "Knowledge is key." - Jekeroka
>
> A successful **Arcana (DC 13)** check reveals that the glyphs are also powered by the elemental energy within the main chamber and have been active since [[The Shattering]], a truly astonishing feat of magic.
>
> - **Knowledge: Rituals**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> #### Locating the Switch
>
> The party must solve the note's riddle if they wish to find the Workshop switch and progress.
>
> Any character who makes a successful `[[/check investigation 12]]` check is able to discern the correct answer, following a brief period of scrutiny. Otherwise, the party must figure out the answer on their own.
>
> The first character who speaks the word "**knowledge**" activates the door to a hidden compartment on the northern dais, which conceals the Workshop switch.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Activating the Workshop Switch
>
> This room contains one of three switches necessary to summon the guardians of the [[Trial Challenge Key]] in the [[Main Chamber]].
>
> Made of some curious unbreakable alloy, the toggle switch in this chamber is located in a hidden compartment on the northern dais, and can be activated as an Action.
>
> Unless the [[Librarium]] and [[Workshop]] switches have both been activated, there is no immediate perceptible effect when activating the Workshop switch. All three of these switches must be activated in order to summon the [[Anachraenum Aetherial]] that guard the Trial Challenge Key in the villa's Main Chamber.
