# Entry Tunnels

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> In the gloom, you can see the roughly dug out walls of this tunnel, streaks of bioluminescent moss growing here and there, casting the narrow passage in an eerie light. A rusting metal bridge crosses over swiftly flowing water. A haze of swirling spores fills the tunnel, pouring from the caps of numerous large mushrooms growing on the walls.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Strange Growths
>
> Examining the mushrooms with a successful **Wilderness (DC 16)** check yields insight into the bright yellow ones: glowing, brightly colored, and likely highly poisonous as a result. Their production of spores is prodigious, and the sole cause of the tunnel being choked with the haze.
>
> A successful **Medicine (DC 16)** check reveals that some sort of face cover will protect you from the spores, though it'll be impossible to avoid spores coating your equipment. The party will want to keep an eye out for growths in their packs after being here.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### No Fun Fungi
>
> Characters passing through the haze of spores suffer **Airborne Contaminants (Hazard 3)** for each turn they spend in the fog.
>
> Any sort of face cover that prevents inhaling the spores allows characters to navigate the area without having to make a saving throw.
