# Compound Lab

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Wooden posts rise to meet the ceiling, remnants of the walls between them still visible in places. This hastily expanded workspace is filled with complexly arranged glassware brimming with strange alchemical mixtures. Four separate arrangements methodically distill, filter, stir, and transform compounds for unknown reasons.
>
> Behind them all, a desk, strewn with an assortment of books and papers that hold the promise of more information. To its immediate right a window looks out upon a churning waterwheel, its motion driving something unseen elsewhere.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Head of Studies
>
> Kalasak is the overseer and director of this lab, and a dangerous individual in his own right. Upon first encounter he attempts to reason with interlopers, drawing them into a sense of safety, or at least reducing their vigilance. He is accompanied by three [[Mutagist Vivisector]] that follow their overseer's lead. If the lab was forewarned, Kalasak skips the pretense and ambushes the party as they enter (see [[Gameplay Details]]).
>
> Kalasak knows that failure to defend this lab and subdue the invaders will mean certain death for him, and as a result he has nothing to lose. He fights ferociously, and without mercy.
>
> Kalasak and each of his Vivisectors carry a **Fatal Contingency**, and bite down on it rather than be taken alive. See [[Evidence]] for how they can be captured, Kalasak's tell, and what they reveal under interrogation.
>
> #### Kalasak Tactics
>
> Before combat begins, Kalasak secretly coats his [[Dagger]] using @Action[Actor.FJFtoeSh014tPlJC poisonApply]. Once he sees an opening, he strikes an unwitting foe with **Lunge**, then falls back to the alchemical workbenches to hurl their concoctions at the party. If he gains **Heroism**, Kalasak will attempt a **Drakonic Touch of Flame** on the weakest nearby target.
>
> #### Mutagist Tactics
>
> Each Vivisector coats its dagger using @Action[Actor.kxOh8R1rpCls67e8 paralyticApply] before attacking with **Lunge**. The Vivisectors place themselves between the party and Kalasak, keeping enemies away from him while he works the tables, and use **Combat Medicine** to keep him in the fight. Like their overseer, they fight to the death.
>
> #### Dangerous Suspensions
>
> There are four tables with different alchemical compounds loaded into them. Kalasak can use these to grab and throw vials of dangerous chemicals. These tables can also be smashed, causing them to spray dangerous compounds and shards of glass in all directions.
>
> GMs can use the following explosives from Kalasak's Backpack, but these are designed to reflect him throwing them from the proximity of each specific work bench.
>
> - @Action[Actor.FJFtoeSh014tPlJC alchemistsFire]
> - @Action[Actor.FJFtoeSh014tPlJC causticPhial]
> - @Action[Actor.FJFtoeSh014tPlJC electroAmpoule]
> - @Action[Actor.FJFtoeSh014tPlJC frostFlask]
>
> Both Kalasak and the Party can choose to destroy the tables with a successful **Athletics (DC 12)** check by flipping the table or hurling an object into it to knock it over. Doing so causes the table to explode in a shattering of glass spraying compounds in a 5 foot blast, those affected suffer **Hazard 2** from glass shards and **Hazard 2** damage. Once a setup has been destroyed, Kalasak can't benefit from it to concoct an alchemical attack.
>
> If any of the workbenches survive the combat, the party can loot up to 2 of the bombs the bench provided, any others aren't in usable condition.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Alchemical Accounting
>
> As long as the tables aren't destroyed, ransacking their collections of vials and bottles yields a collection of [[Rare Chemicals]] per table.
>
> Kalasak's desk holds a personal journal containing [[Kalasak's Observations]] and a stack of [[Experiment Reports]].

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Elevator
>
> An elevator connects this level to the [[Mixing Room]] on the lower level. To operate it, click on the elevator using the  Interact With Objects mode in the Tokens layer of Scene Controls and choose **Descend**. Any tokens standing on the platform ride down with it and arrive on the lower level when it stops.
