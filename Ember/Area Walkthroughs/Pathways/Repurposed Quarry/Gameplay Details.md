# Gameplay Details

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Dangerous Ground
>
> The entire repurposed quarry is in a state of disarray, and there are numerous dangerous situations unfolding across the entire facility. Parties attempting to go in quietly may have a hard time due to the long sight lines and large numbers of enemies in the area.
>
> To their advantage, the relative chaos incited by scuttling the base means that enemies might not immediately realize they have a band of intruders even if they do spot the party.

## Illumination

The quarry is suffused with dim light throughout from a multitude of bioluminescent mushrooms and small crevices in the rock far overhead that let faint rays of light filter down.

## Elevation

The ceiling of the pathways above is far beyond the reach of any player character, likely in the many hundreds of feet. Levels of the quarry are 40 feet apart, posing a serious risk of injury for characters that fall, and the sheer stone walls of most of the quarry lack hand holds, making climbing between levels difficult without a natural climbing speed.

## Terrain

Some sections of the quarry are water, which modifies the movement cost of creatures without a swim speed. The pooled ooze of the [[Alchemical Spill]] in the Middle Ledges underneath the [[Destroyed Lab]] also counts as difficult terrain.

## Enemies

This quarry is filled with multiple members of the Mutagist faction on all levels. The denizens of this lab are actively attempting to destroy evidence in the lab, and are preparing to depart from the site.

- Stopping the Mutagist Bombardier at the [[Chemical Storage]] area prevents the Mutagists from destroying any more of the lab.
- Several Mutated Drakes are squared off in combat against Mutagist Vivisectors in the [[Creature Pens]].
- Two [[Mutagist Scout]] and a [[Mutagist Vivisector]] are in the [[Dormitory]].
- The party can encounter [[Kalasak the Cutter]], overseer of the laboratory, and his three [[Mutagist Vivisector]] in the [[Compound Lab]].
- On the lowest level the party will find a challenging opponent in the [[Mutated Ultra Drake]] that has been kept by the Mutagists.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Resting
>
> It is not possible to rest in the quarry area until the party has scoured the site of hostiles, driving off or defeating any enemies first.
>
> #### Fleeing
>
> Should the party need to flee the area, they may do so by retreating from the quarry interior toward the entrance tunnel they arrived at, or the cave cliff. Mutagists will not pursue them beyond the immediate area the map covers.
>
> Beyond the laboratory, past the edge of the map, an old ore lift runs up a shaft to the abandoned quarry on the surface. The Mutagists brought their supplies in by this lift, and the party can use it to leave the quarry once the lab is cleared.
>
> #### Forewarned
>
> If any scouts escaped during [[Missing Scouts]] to warn [[Kalasak the Cutter]], the lab is on alert when the party arrives and the destruction of the lab's records is further along.
>
> **[[Chemical Storage]]**
>
> The Bombardier has already finished their work: this room was destroyed before the party arrived, and its evidence is lost. The race described in the [[Quarry Ledge]] does not take place.
>
> **[[Mixing Room]]**
>
> The records have already been burned, and this evidence is lost.
>
> **[[Dormitory]]**
>
> The Mutagists are awake and armed at the doorway, and cannot be caught by surprise.
>
> **[[Compound Lab]]**
>
> Kalasak does not attempt to reason with the party. He and his Vivisectors are ready and waiting to ambush them as they enter.
