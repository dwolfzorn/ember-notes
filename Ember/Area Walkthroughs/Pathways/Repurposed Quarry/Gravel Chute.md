# Gravel Chute

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The metal plates creak and groan underfoot, sinking slightly as weight is placed upon them as you enter this space. Heavy rusted metal floors and walls surround you, their surface pockmarked and corroded by time.
>
> Dual pulleys hang ominously over two large metal plates. At first it looks like a lift, but after a moment you realize they seal off a shaft that descends to the middle level. A net and heavy rope sway slightly with a rhythmic creak overhead.
>
> The air here carries a musty odor of rust and pulverized stone.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Rusted Plates
>
> A character with **Awareness (DC 13, Passive)** notices that the central plates sag under their own weight and that the latch holding them shut has rusted nearly through. Characters who keep to the edges of the room can cross it safely.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Falling Hazard
>
> Characters stepping onto the central plates of the chute feel the metal give way as the trapdoor drops open beneath them, plunging them 40 feet into the [[Gravel Pit]] on the middle level of this scene. Each falling character suffers a **Serious Fall (Hazard 40, Severe)**.
>
> #### Springing the Trap
>
> This trap is automated. When a character steps onto the central plates, the trapdoor swings open and the game pauses so that no one else moves. Each character standing on the plates becomes **Falling** and drops to the Gravel Pit below. Resolve each fall, then resume the game.
>
> While the trapdoor is open, nothing supports a character over the shaft, and the trap cannot spring again.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Manual Activation
>
> You can manually activate or reset the trap by clicking on the trapdoor using the  Interact With Objects mode in the Tokens layer of Scene Controls. Resetting the trap closes the trapdoor, which restores the plates and re-arms the trap.
