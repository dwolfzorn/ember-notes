# Destroyed Lab

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Burning Lab
>
> This lab starts intact on the map, but it should already be burning by the time the party reaches it. Set it off during the Lab Explosion on the [[Quarry Ledge]], or before the party arrives if the lab was forewarned.
>
> You can manually set off or reset the lab by clicking on it using the  Interact With Objects mode in the Tokens layer of Scene Controls. Setting it off plays the explosion, leaves the wreckage burning, and activates the hazard region over its fire and fumes. Resetting it restores the intact lab and deactivates the region.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Fire and smoke fill this destroyed lab space, making it hard to examine the contents. Through the heat and haze you can make out a massive vat spilling green ooze out onto the rusted metal floors, and pouring through holes down to the lower levels.
>
> All else in this space are tables and shelves scattered with the remnants of lab distillers and filters, glassware, vessels and more destroyed by the explosion.
>
> The resulting fire and smoke are laced with chemicals that cause your skin to itch, eyes to water, and lungs to burn.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Caustic Clouds
>
> Characters passing through the destroyed lab are **Blinded** and if they breathe, will begin choking, suffering **Noxious Fumes (Hazard 4)** for each round they remain in the gas.
>
> Characters with [[Protective Goggles]] avoid the blinded condition while in the fumes, while characters wearing a [[Protective Mask]] don't suffer the blinded or choking conditions.
>
> #### Alchemical Fire
>
> The flames can also cause **Minor Burns (Hazard 3)**.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Hazardous Salvage
>
> Searching the lab while it burns is difficult, and the searcher remains exposed to the Caustic Clouds and Alchemical Fire throughout. A successful **Awareness (DC 17)** check notices that a dose of [[Drakonbane Poison]] can be claimed here. Otherwise, nothing in this lab survived the explosion, and there is nothing that can be saved.
>
> #### Fire Management
>
> Characters with **Knowledge: Alchemy** or who succeed on an **Alchemy (DC 14, Passive)** check know that this is an alchemical fire, and won't be easily extinguished. In fact, they will likely burn for hours unabated, and water would be completely ineffective.
>
> Instead, to extinguish these flames, soil, sand, clay and dirt must be used. The quarry has ample amounts of this, and scattered tools, but the time necessary to move enough earth would be considerable and cause the characters to be exposed to the fumes the entire time.
