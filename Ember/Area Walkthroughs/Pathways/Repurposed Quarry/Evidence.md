## Captives

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Fatal Contingency
>
> Every [[Mutagist Vivisector]], [[Kalasak the Cutter]] included, carries a Fatal Contingency: an alchemical capsule of corrosive poison lodged behind the teeth. When a Vivisector becomes **Dead** or **Broken**, they bite down on it, and the corrosive destroys their mouth and throat, killing them and leaving nothing to question. The Vivisectors are zealous enough in their cause to use it without hesitation.
>
> A Vivisector can only be captured if they fall while **Stunned**, **Paralyzed**, **Restrained**, or **Incapacitated**, leaving them unable to bite down. If so, Ankarist moves swiftly to pry the capsule from their mouth before they recover, and the party can take them prisoner.
>
> The party will most likely first see a Vivisector die this way in the [[Creature Pens]], or among Kalasak's companions in the [[Compound Lab]]. Having seen it once, they may come to expect it from the others.
>
> #### Kalasak's Tell
>
> Kalasak's death is the expected outcome of his fight, and the party has only one chance to take him alive. A character with **Awareness (DC 15, Passive)** notices Kalasak working his jaw around something during the fight, as though he is ready to spit out or bite down on something.
>
> #### Support Personnel
>
> The [[Mutagist Scout]] and [[Mutagist Bombardier]] carry no contingency. They are support personnel with specific skills, and they follow their orders to destroy the lab and abandon the site without understanding the mission. Interrogating one reveals nothing of value, and capturing one does not count as evidence.
>
> #### Evidence from Captives
>
> Capturing a Vivisector alive adds +1 to the party's evidence score, and capturing Kalasak alive adds +3. These do not stack: if the party captures Kalasak, also capturing a Vivisector adds nothing.

## Interrogation

> [!info] Social
> #### Interrogating a Vivisector
>
> Only the Vivisectors know what the Mutagists are doing here. A captured Vivisector can only confirm what the party has likely already discovered or gleaned:
>
> - The Mutagists are experimenting on drakes captured within the Pathways.
> - They are trying to make the drakes stronger through alchemical mutation.
> - They have tested several series of experiments by releasing them into the wild, including the attack on [[Helkas]].
> - They sent a team to [[Skybrush]] to open an alternative route through the Pathways.
>
> #### Interrogating Kalasak
>
> Of the Vivisectors, only Kalasak has true knowledge of the Mutagists' goals, and even his perspective is limited by the secretive, asymmetric structure of their cabal. Beyond what any Vivisector knows, he can reveal:
>
> - Their greatest success was the creation of an Ultra Drake, although the resulting mutation was unstable.
> - He reports to a superior: a cowled figure with a scaly face, either a Drakon or a Cor'ak.
> - He does not know whether this superior leads the Mutagists, or where to find him. The superior always comes to Kalasak, never the other way around.
> - Kalasak has a single mission, the empowerment of drakes, and is given the autonomy to pursue it with limited oversight.
> - He last met with his superior in the days leading up to the attack on Helkas, when he arranged to transport several drake specimens.
> - The superior took a crate of mutagen with him, from the same batch that produced the Ultra Drake.
> - Kalasak believes there are several other Mutagist cabals working on similar experiments.

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### The Mutagist Conspiracy
>
> Kalasak's superior is [[Serethus]], but Kalasak knows neither his name nor his association with the [[Veiled Chain]].
