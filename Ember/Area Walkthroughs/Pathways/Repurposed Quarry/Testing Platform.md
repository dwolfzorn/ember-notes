# Testing Platform

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The testing platform is a massive block of stone twenty feet by twenty feet, standing starkly above the churning water, and only accessible by a bronze ramp.
>
> At its center, a drakeling lies motionless on an examination table, its body connected by a series of tubes snaking from a tank of bubbling chemicals just next to it. Nearby, a complex glass apparatus orchestrates the flow of blood and chemicals within interconnected tubes and vials for some unknown purpose.

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Surgical Tools
>
> The numerous tools here are well made, and well used, stained with the accumulated blood of numerous creatures. You doubt they were ever cleaned.
>
> #### Blood Distiller
>
> The complex collection of vials, tubes, mixers, and boilers is completely incomprehensible in purpose. A character who succeeds on a **Alchemy (DC 14, Passive)** check can determine that the array is processing partially congealed drake blood into a substance for testing.
>
> - **Knowledge: Alchemy**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The distillate is the base for the green medium in the nearby drum of ooze. The lab is cultivating its own reagent from the blood of its test subjects.
>
> #### Dead Drakeling
>
> A badly afflicted drakeling is strapped to the steel table here, and is long dead.
>
> A character who succeeds on a **Medicine (DC 15)** check determines that much of its blood has been pumped out of its body. Additionally, numerous incisions have been made across its body to remove tumors, explore seeping wounds, and examine organs.
>
> - **Knowledge: Forensics**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The tumors and seeping wounds are side effects of the mutagen, not a natural affliction, and the drakeling died during the procedure rather than from its condition.
>
> #### Drum of Ooze
>
> The vat is set up to dispense samples into vials. A character who succeeds on a **Alchemy (DC 14)** using [[Alchemist's Toolkit]] check recognizes the same volatile chemical that was being utilized in the lab on the upper level, and can safely draw a [[Vial of Mutagenic Medium]].
>
> - **Knowledge: Alchemy**: The character automatically succeeds on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also realizes that the green chemical is a special alchemical medium. It is useless by itself, but if mixed with proper activator compounds it becomes a potent solution.
>
> Characters who read the Examination Notes (below) learn the same thing about the medium.
>
> #### Books and Journals
>
> A lectern holds [[Examination Notes]] relating to this drake. A rack nearby holds the following other documents including a [[Manual of Drake Anatomy]] and a journal that documents the slab's [[Testing Schedule]].
