# Slipping Into Darkness

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This repeatable Exploration Event occurs whenever the party attempts to cross into the mountain hexes of the [[Bloodwoods]] (marked as [[Wedgelands]] in the Terrain Overlay tool. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Cross into an interdimensional pocket of the Inner Realm of [[Primordis]] and witness something strange.
> - Be forcibly relocated to a different Hex of the Bloodwoods.
>
> This Event takes place on the [[The Arctus Plateau]] Region Map.
>
> #### Event Deactivation
>
> This Standalone Event becomes deactivated once the characters have solved the trail map found in the [[The Primordial Grove]] Event of the [[Ruby Grove]] Side Quest, allowing them access to [[Kadra Zann]]. This is tracked by marking the "Kadra Zann Revealed" Outcome of that Event as complete.

During their brief visit to Primordis, the characters witness something strange and alien. Roll on or choose from the table below to determine what they see.

|  | #### Visions of Primordis |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | #### Shadow Cats  The forest ahead fills with the shapes of great cats made only of shadow, each one draped across a different branch. None of them move, but their red eyes open one by one. Then the darkness snaps shut, and the Bloodwoods returns around you. |
| 2 | #### Fey Figures  Thousands of tiny, semi-translucent Fey figures sit, hang, and drape along the branches of this primeval forest, wearing masks of bone; they move as insects, twitching and stuttering toward you. Then the darkness snaps shut, and the Bloodwoods returns around you. |
| 3 | #### Banquet  A path of silvery pale mushrooms appears beneath your feet, leading toward a mile-long banquet table set with raw meat and bowls of sweet milk. Humanoid shapes are hunched over the table, porcine and grotesque, tearing at the meat, lapping at the milk bowls. Then the darkness snaps shut, and the Bloodwoods returns around you. |
| 4 | #### Ripped Shadow  You feel a pulling sensation ripple across your body; your shadow detaches from your feet, coils inward, and bounds between the trees. The iron tang of blood fills your mouth, and for a moment, you feel exhilarated. Then the darkness snaps shut, and the Bloodwoods returns around you. |

> [!tip] Exploration
> #### Relocation
>
> After the party has experienced their strange vision of Primordis, you may conclude the Event, causing the [[DreamTeam]] Token to be relocated to a random hex in the Bloodwoods.

### Concluding the Event

Once the party has been forcibly relocated to a different hex of the Bloodwoods, they are free to continue on their journey.
