# Family Feud

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
>  This Standalone Event takes place while the party travels through the area of the [[Redrak Fields]] surrounding [[Lake Orial]], along traditional [[Bejak]] migration routes to and from [[Ushna Dredging]]. In this Standalone Event, the characters can:
>
>  - Encounter a standoff between two Bejak caravans, blocking the party's progress.
> - Negotiate a solution to a feud between two Bejak families.
>
>  This Event takes place on [[The Arctus Plateau]] Region Map and may occur as long as the party is moving and not taking a rest.

 > [!tip] Exploration
> #### Avoiding the Barricade
>
>  If at any time the party decides to backtrack and search for another route in order to avoid the barricades, they may do so, but they should first be warned it will require them to either:
>
>  - Return to their previous hex and avoid this one entirely along their route.
> - Navigate the rocky and dangerously uneven surface above the depression, requiring 4 hours of travel to traverse the hex, and a successful **Athletics (DC 16)** check from each party member, or else that party member takes `[[/damage 2d6 bludgeoning]]` damage due to repeated falls during the crossing. If you wish to present even greater danger, also threaten that on a **Critical Failure** they tumble all the way down the slope, taking `[[/damage 8d6 bludgeoning]]` damage.

 While traversing the area surrounding Lake Orial — upon which lies the nearby Ushna Dredging, the home of a Bejak family and common travel stop for nomadic Bejak caravans — the party finds two Bejak families blockading a narrow pass, halting their progress. Read or paraphrase the following:

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> An elderly Kivahr with leathery grey skin steps out from the caravan on the opposite side from you, and shouts:
>
>  > Qara, you pathetic weakling, you know a Beast Clash is the only way to settle this! How dare you call yourself a Bejak while disregarding our traditions so readily! Do you care not for the ways of our ancestors, or are you simply a coward unwilling to face a challenge you know you cannot win?
>
>  A Kivahr woman bearing a braid of wintery hair steps forward defiantly from the caravan closest to you.
>
>  > Galar, you reckless old fool, how can you stand to invoke the traditions of our ancestors when yours violated them so gravely? I will not clash with you, or anyone else in the Uldun family. Stand aside, and save yourself the humiliation of dredging up your ancestors' sins.
>
>  After a tense moment, Galar spots you, and Qara turns to follow her gaze. It is Qara who breaks from the argument to approach you.
>
>  > You've chosen a poor time to cross this route, travelers. What is your business here?

 > [!info] Social
> #### Violence and Blood
>
>  Qara Baran (Lawful Good, Bejak Kivahr, she/her) is a proud, stoic woman and the head of the Baran family, which has an ancient feud with the family of Galar Uldun. Qara explains to the party that there will be no passage through the depression unless the standoff between the two families ends. In the process, she can discuss:
>
>  - Both families claim this to be their own ancestral migration path between Ushna Dredging and the rest of the Plateau, and thus that the other family is not welcome to cross it.
> - The Bejak traditionally settle disputes using a Beast Clash, in which each Bejak's bonded beast attempts to maneuver and intimidate the other out of a mutually agreed-upon area.
> - Galar's ancestors challenged one of Qara's ancestors to a Beast Clash, in which Galar's ancestor's beast killed that of Qara's ancestor — an extreme violation of traditional custom. Qara does not trust the Ulduns not to kill her own bonded yarnac, Saffron.
> - By tradition, anyone with a bonded beast may clash in Qara's stead.
> - If the party is accompanied by [[Rala Ushna]], he affirms that some Bejak believe Qara's story, while others do not — provoking Qara's ire.
>
>  Specific dialogue options for Qara on the above topics are presented below this block.
>
>  Anyone who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 11)** check determines that Qara believes she is telling the truth about the history of the Barans and Ulduns, but also that she is clearly dramatizing for the sake of the gathered audience.
>
>  - **Knowledge: Politics**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also determines that Qara's stubborn refusal to break the barricade is less about protecting the migration path, and more that she does not want the family she leads to perceive her as weak for ceding to Galar's demands — she might be appeased by an alternate framing.
>
>  If the party wishes to convince Qara to break the barricade without first speaking to Galar, see [[Ending the Barricade]] below.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About blockading the path?
>
> **A:**
>
> > This is an ancestral migration path of the Baran family, running from Ushna Dredging — where the Ushnas, a family of settled Bejak, host traveling caravans — and leading all across the Plateau. It was established by my family centuries ago, when they traveled to the Plateau with the very first Bejak to make landfall here.
> >
> >  As a nomadic people, we Bejak respect each other's traditional paths, and take alternate routes when other families have claim — or at least, honest Bejak do. Galar and her tyrant kin claim dominion over anywhere they happen to tread. We all know her ancestors had no claim to this route.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About the Beast Clash?
>
> **A:**
>
> > A Beast Clash is the traditional Bejak way of settling disputes. From childhood, each Bejak bonds closely with an animal, and when a conflict must be resolved, we guide our animals in a fight of territorial dominance. The clash is a test of strategy and prowess, and is how our people have avoided intra-Bejak warfare for millennia. It is not a violent endeavor … or well, it's not **supposed** to be. The Uldun family, however, believes otherwise.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About the Ulduns?
>
> **A:**
>
> > A crass and violent family from a long heritage of tyrants. While the Beast Clash we Bejak use to settle conflict is not meant to be a violent affair, centuries ago an Uldun challenged one of my ancestors to a clash, and their beast **killed** that of my family's! The names have been lost to time, but the crime remains — as does the untrustworthiness of an Uldun.
>
>  Her eyes flick to Galar, and she spits on the ground. Afterwards, she turns to the yarnac next to her, and places a steady hand on its back.
>
>  > I will not risk my precious Saffron in a clash with Galar's untamed monstrosity.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About ending the barricade?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Here we'll remain until the Ulduns cease their false claims. I will not break the barricade for even a moment, lest Galar dare to rush our position — I wouldn't put it past her, after all.
> >
> >  And I **certainly** will not risk Saffron in a clash. By tradition, anyone with a bonded beast can stand in my stead, but none in my family would risk their beloved companion clashing with an Uldun.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Qara notices RalaParty is accompanied by [[Rala Ushna]]
>
> **A:**
>
> > Wait, is that Rala Ushna that I spy? My, you were just a little boy the last time I saw you at your home. Tell them, son, of the Ulduns' crimes.
>
>  Rala blushes, his cheeks yellowing, and fidgets uncomfortably as he mumbles a reply.
>
>  > Well, some do say that the Uldun's ancestors did kill a Baran beast during a clash …
>
>  Qara nods in agreement, but halts sharply as Rala continues …
>
>  > … but others say it is nothing but an old rumor.
>
>  Qara scowls furiously, and snaps:
>
>  > You've been away from your home too long, boy. You forget the true stories of our people.
>
>  Rala's pained wince is difficult to watch.

 After the party has finished speaking with Qara, Galar shouts to them.

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> Galar calls out to you from across the Baran barricade.
>
>  > You there! Travelers! What lies is she filling your heads with? Qara, you silver-tongued snake, allow me to speak with them! Or do you fear what the truth will do?
>
>  Qara, snarling, nods her head in the direction of her family's cart barricade, begrudgingly allowing you to approach and speak to Galar across it.

 > [!info] Social
> #### Lies and Slander
>
>  Galar Uldun (Lawful Neutral, Bejak Kivahr, she/her) is a proud, hot-headed woman and the head of the Uldun family. Like Qara, she refuses to end her family's barricade, but offers a different picture of the past by discussing the following topics:
>
>  - She cannot break the barricade, as it would legitimate Qara's claims about ancestral history.
> - The Barans have been spreading disparaging rumors about the Ulduns for centuries, to discredit them and steal their migration paths.
> - Refusing the Beast Clash leaves Galar without a traditional way to resolve the conflict or clear her family's name.
> - If the party is accompanied by Rala Ushna, he affirms that some Bejak believe Galar's story, while others do not — provoking Galar's ire.
>
>  Specific dialogue options for Galar on the above topics are presented below this block.
>
>  Anyone who makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 11)** check determines that Galar believes she is telling the truth about the history between the families.
>
>  - **Knowledge: Politics**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Success**: The character also determines that Galar is clearly desperate for a way to clear the Ulduns' name, and is using this barricade as a wedge issue to force a clash, more than anything about the migration path itself.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About ending the barricade?
>
> **A:**
>
> > I cannot. To cede our ancestral migration path to their false claim would be to accept the word of the Barans as trustworthy. At such point, I might as well come out and say their slanderous lies about my ancestors are true. The Ulduns will not move an **inch** until these lying cowards turn around leave!

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About Qara's accusation?
>
> **A:**
>
> > For centuries, the Barans have wrongfully claimed that one of my ancestors' beasts slayed theirs in a clash. My family's companions are the most disciplined and well-trained on Aterica. It's **impossible.**
> >
> >  The Barans simply wish to discredit us in the eyes of other Bejak, and weaken our claims to migration paths.
> >
> >  I would prove the discipline of my family's beasts in the traditional way, but this coward refuses to face Glory and I in a clash she knows she'll lose.
>
>  She proudly pats the yarnac beside her.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About Qara's refusal of the Beast Clash?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Other than her obvious cowardice, there is an even more sinister motivation. She knows a clash is the only way my family can clear our name. It's how we Bejak settle conflicts, and an opportunity to demonstrate the discipline and restraint the Barans claim we lack. And thus, she refuses us that chance. It's utterly unjust, and unspeakably cruel. Surely you understand the dilemma I face?

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** Galar notices RalaParty is accompanied by [[Rala Ushna]]
>
> **A:**
>
> > Rala, little one! Well, not so little anymore I see! It's been quite awhile since we sat around the campfire at Caravan Circle, you on your parents' lap.
>
>  She claps him firmly on the shoulder.
>
>  > Good to see you've grown up strong — and Buttercup as well! Now, certainly you can tell your friends here that the Barans cannot be trusted?
>
>  Rala's reply is an uncomfortable whisper.
>
>  > It's true that some doubt the Barans' accusation … but others believe they speak the truth.
>
>  Galar's face darkens into a scowl.
>
>  > You straddle an uncomfortable fence, boy. Those without a side have nowhere to reside, as the saying goes.

 ### Ending the Barricade

 To end the barricade and proceed through the sloping depression, the party must break the stalemate between the families.

 > [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Running the Negotiation
>
>  The conflict between the Barans and Ulduns runs deep, and the checks needed to convince them to give up the feud are difficult. To narrativize why the families would be willing to do so at all, draw upon the motivations of the parties involved.
>
>  At your discretion, you may **require** the party to invoke the following motivations in their arguments, either before allowing them to even **attempt** any relevant checks, or in order to gain **+2 Boons** on those checks:
>
>  - Qara believes the Ulduns are violent, unpredictable, and dangerous, and thus may be convinced that the maintaining a barricade in the Ulduns path puts Qara's family at risk.
> - Qara believes the Barans are morally superior to the Ulduns, and thus may be convinced that allowing the Ulduns through would demonstrate the moral high ground.
> - Galar wishes to correct the mistaken impression of her family's aggression, and thus may be convinced that allowing the Barans through would help change the Ulduns' reputation.

 > [!info] Social
> #### Breaking the Stalemate
>
>  The party can break the stalemate in a number of ways:
>
>  - **Diplomacy (DC 20)** to convince both parties to put aside the distant past and any ancestral claims to the land, and let each other through. At the Gamemaster's discretion, any Bejak encountered in the future know that the party resolved this old conflict, and their gratitude improves their initial disposition towards the party.
> - **Diplomacy (DC 18)** to convince either party that the conflict is not worth it, and thus to turn around and engage in an extremely time-consuming back track while the opposite party continues through the depression.
> - **Diplomacy (DC 14)** to argue to Qara that Galar's ancestors being untrustworthy does not mean Galar is, and thus convince Qara to participate in a clash.
> - 5 gp to bribe either party to cede the path. The bribed family nevertheless holds an open grudge against the party, and at the Gamemaster's discretion, Bejak encountered around Lake Orial in the future may share that hostility.
> - Any party member with an animal companion may clash in Qara's stead, in which case the party member faces Galar and her yarnac in [[Participating in the Beast Clash]].
>
>  When making any of the checks listed above:
>
>  - **Knowledge: Politics**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
>  If the party convinces either side to leave without a clash, narrate their exit in accordance with the party's method of persuasion and stated reasoning.

 ### Watching the Beast Clash

 If the party convinces Qara to participate in the Beast Clash, read or paraphrase the following:

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> Qara and her yarnac Saffron march stoicly into the no-man's-land between the two barricades, while Galar and her yarnac Glory stomp forward to meet them. Galar shouts first.
>
>  > The domain is barricade-to-barricade. Victory upon retreat or surrender. Do you accept?
>
>  Qara nods with silent determination, and whistles, her yarnac crouching low in a ready stance. Then, with a sudden shout, Galar's beast charges forward. It seems as if the two hulking creatures will ram headfirst, until at the last moment, Qara's Saffron shies away. A fraction of a second later, Galar whistles, and her yarnac Glory turns and skids to a halt aside Saffron. The beasts low and moan, and at their companions' command, they settle into a rhythm of feints and charges, leaps and skids — never once colliding.
>
>  Bit by bit, Qara's Saffron loses ground, until — pinned into a corner with nowhere to run — the great beast makes a mighty leap that sends her backwards over the Barans' barricade and out of the ring. Qara turns her head away in shame, and Galar roars victoriously.
>
>  > **This** is the discipline of the Ulduns! This is who we are! Now you know it, and it's how you will tell it! Show respect, and leave the path my ancestors once walked! Go! **Go!**
>
>  Ashamed, Qara turns around, and without a word, her caravan pivots their carts, breaking the barricade and preparing to take the exhaustingly long and arduous route necessary to bypass the sloping depression. The look in her eyes is not spite, but resignation. Acceptance that — whatever is true of Galar's ancestors — the Barans can make no such claims about the Ulduns of today.

 Afterward, Galar approaches the party.

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> > Thank you for convincing that coward to finally face me, and allowing my family to clear its name. As a token of my family's gratitude, take this. May it safeguard you in your travels, as it has safeguarded our people.

 Galar gifts the party a [[Braid of Alarm]].

 ### Participating in the Beast Clash

 If a party member has an animal companion and they agree to participate in the Beast Clash in Qara's stead, Qara explains the procedure to them:

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> > A Beast Clash is a strategic game of territory and control, as well as a test of discipline and the bond you have with your companion. Each creature will attempt to intimidate and frighten the other, until they are forced out of the predetermined domain of the clash, or the opposing pair surrenders. To actually strike the other beast is an immediate forfeit, and a grave crime — a crime of which Galar's ancestors are guilty, so exercise extreme caution.
> >
> >  Predict Galar and her yarnac's movements. Anticipate her plans. Calm your animal's fear, and rein in their aggression if it gets out of hand. Do this, and you shall win.

 > [!tip] Exploration
> #### Barans and Your Beast
>
>  If a party member and their animal companion clashes in Qara's stead, the party member must succeed on at least two of the following three checks to emerge victorious. Each check includes guidance for how to narrate their victory or loss depending on the outcome of the check. After finishing all three checks, narrate the overall victory or loss with these in mind.
>
>  **Reading Your Opponent**
>
> If the character makes a successful **Awareness (DC 14)** check, they repeatedly read Galar's intentions before she acts, allowing them to predict Galar's moves. On a failure, Galar and Glory's offensives repeatedly take the party member by surprise.
>
>  - **Knowledge: Warfare**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> **Feinting Your Opponent**
>
> If the character makes a successful **Awareness (DC 14)** check, they mislead Galar about their intentions throughout the clash. On a failure, Galar anticipates the commands the party member gives their companion.
>
>  - **Knowledge: Warfare**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> **Handling Your Companion**
>
> If the character makes a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** check, they calm their companion's fear when Galar's Glory charges, and rein in their companion's aggression during charges, letting the party member masterfully execute their plan. On a failure, their companion flinches and frenzies, restricting strategic options.
>
>  - **Knowledge: Beasts**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
> - **Critical Failure**: Whether in fear or frenzy, the party member's animal companion strikes Galar's yarnac Glory. This is in an immediate loss, and both families immediately shun the party, demanding they turn and leave — see [[Avoiding the Barricade]]. At the Gamemaster's discretion, Bejak encountered around Lake Orial in the future hear of this, and have worse initial disposition.

 If the party member loses, read or paraphrase the following after the clash concludes:

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> Galar roars victoriously.
>
>  > **This** is the discipline of the Ulduns! This is who we are! Now you know it, and it's how you will tell it! Show respect, and leave the path my ancestors once walked! Go! **Go!**
>
>  Embarassed, Qara turns around, and without a word, her caravan pivots their carts, breaking the barricade and preparing to take the exhaustingly long and arduous route necessary to bypass the sloping depression. Her eyes look full of regret, and she offers you no gratitude or thanks as she leaves.

 If the party member wins, read or paraphrase the following after the clash concludes:

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> Galar turns her head away, ashamed.
>
>  > You win. On my family's honor, I cede this path to you. I hope you will remember I did so — and that Glory did not touch your beast.
>
>  With that, her caravan pivots their carts, breaking the barricade and preparing to take the exhaustingly long and arduous route necessary to bypass the sloping depression.
>
>  Qara, meanwhile, turns towards you.
>
>  > Thank you for putting Galar's ridiculous pride to rest. You and your companion took a great risk, and you deserve ample reward. May this gift safeguard you in your travels, as you have safeguarded my dear Saffron.

 Qara gifts the party member a [[Braid of Alarm]].

 ### Concluding the Event

 Once the party has either found a way to break the stalemate or decided to take the long or dangerous way around, the characters are free to continue on their journey.
