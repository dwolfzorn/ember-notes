# Traversing the Bloodwoods

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This repeatable Exploration Event can occur while traveling through the [[Bloodwoods]]. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Confront the chaotic influence of [[Primordis]] as it moves through the black aspens.
> - Potentially overcome a physical hazard.
>
> This Event can repeat, although it will not occur again until 12 hours have passed since its last occurrence. The probability of this Event occurring increases as more time passes since it last transpired.
>
> This Event takes place on the [[The Arctus Plateau]] Region Map.

> [!danger] Hazard
> #### Traversal Challenge
>
> The Bloodwoods writhe around the party as if trying to eject some foreign object from its body. The party must attempt a group challenge to overcome the mercurial magic of the Bloodwoods, with each party member rolling one of the following checks:
>
> - **Wilderness (DC 15)** to keep the black aspen vines at bay.
> - **Arcana (DC 15)** to directly counteract the magic driving the black aspens.
>
> When making any of the above checks:
>
> - **Guide Present:** The character automatically succeeds on this check if [[Maevren]] or another survival guide is accompanying the party. Only one character may benefit from the survival guide. What constitutes a survival guide in this context is up to the Gamemaster's discretion, but ideally it is an NPC with extensive experience navigating the Bloodwoods.
> - **Ruby Ingested:** The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if they are currently under the effects of [[Ruby]].
> - **Attunement: Primordis**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check if their Primordis Attunement Rank is 2 or greater.
> - **Knowledge: Tracking**: The character gains **+2 Boons** on this check.
>
> If at least half of the characters succeed, the party withstands the influence of the Bloodwoods. If over half of the characters fail, however, roll on or choose from the table below to determine what happens to the party.

|  | #### Traversing the Bloodwoods |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | #### In the Aspens' Snare  A character who failed their part of the traversal challenge becomes wholly ensnared by black aspen vines, which pull them through the underbrush and into the roots of a tree.  The ensnared character begins **Suffocating** and suffers from **Thorny Vines (Hazard 2)** at the start of each of their turns. Other characters can make a **Athletics (DC 18)** check to free the ensnared character. |
| 2 | #### Carried Away  The characters are swept away by the Bloodwoods' convulsions.  Each character suffers from **Thorny Vines (Hazard 2)**. Then, place the party token `[[/gmroll 1d4]]` hexes away in a direction opposite their intended destination; the destination must remain in the Bloodwoods. |
| 3 | #### Crimson Fever  Clots of sticky red pollen burst from the underbrush as the Bloodwoods convulse.  Each character who failed their part of the traversal challenge must make a successful **Wilderness (DC 14)** check or fall **Asleep** for `[[/gmroll 1d4]]` hours, suffering from **Horrific Dreams (Hazard 4)** of nightmarish entities sleeping beneath the surface of Ember. They cannot be woken by conventional means . |
| 4 | #### Nettled Paths  The Bloodwoods are choked by thorny black aspen vines, slowing travel speed.  Treat each Bloodwoods hex as [[Region Exploration]] for `[[/gmroll 2d3]]` days. |
| 5 | #### Unnatural Fog  In the aftermath of the Bloodwoods' convulsions, fog seeps up from the Pathways below.  The [[Region Exploration]] of all Bloodwoods hexes is reduced to 0 for `[[/gmroll 2d3]]` days. |
| 6 | #### Extreme Heat  The Bloodwoods become a humid and hot Biome. Sweat soon forms on exposed skin, and the entire forest begins to drip with condensation.  The extreme heat lasts for `[[/gmroll 2d3]]` days.  Each character without access to drinking water must make a successful **Athletics (DC 5)** check at the end of each hour or become **Staggered** and suffer **Heat Exhaustion (Hazard 2)**. The DC increases by 1 for each additional hour.  Creatures wearing Medium or Heavy armor have **-2 Banes** on the check.  Creatures that have Resistance or Immunity to Fire damage automatically succeed on the check. |

#### Primordis Attunement: Influenced by Primordis

Each character who succeeded in their part of the group challenge advances their **Attunement: Primordis (+1)** at the conclusion of the Event. Each character may gain this Attunement Point only once, no matter how many times this Event occurs.

### Concluding the Event

Once the party has completed their traversal challenge, they are free to continue on their journey. Any ongoing effects from the Traversing the Bloodwoods table remain active for their listed duration.
