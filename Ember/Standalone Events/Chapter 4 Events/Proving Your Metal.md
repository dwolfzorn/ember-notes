# Proving Your Metal

> [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
>  This Standalone Event takes place while the party travels through the section of the [[Redrak Fields]] that lies between Ordain and the imposing [[Unknown]] of the [[Burnished Hand]] located just south in [[Sarin Strand]]. This Event is significantly more likely if the party is traveling at a Fast or Reckless pace. In this Standalone Event, the characters can:
>
>  - Win over a suspicious Burnished Hand patrol using persuasion or tales of their prowess.
> - Face the patrol in a series of martial contests, earning rewards based on their success.
>
>  This Event takes place on [[The Arctus Plateau]] Region Map.

 ### Burnished Hand Patrol

 The party has been stopped by a Burnished Hand patrol made up of the characters below, who interrogate the party, eventually warming up to them and proposing contests of martial prowess.

 > [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Expected Improvisation
>
>  The patrol's exact disposition and dialogue depends on choices the party has made thus far in Ember, and those they make during this Event. Additionally, the party's Level determines which of the patrol members the party faces in the contests of martial prowess. As such, much of the dialogue and narration will be left to you to improvise. In order to do so:
>
>  - Review the characterizations of the patrol members below. The party is always initially confronted by Carver Pela Melir.
> - Follow the guidance provided throughout the Event concerning what might cause the patrol to change their disposition, and how patrol members would express that change.

 **Carver Pela Melir (Lawful Neutral, Ordani Kivahr, she/her)**

Pela Melir is an experienced commander of troops, having risen to the well-respected rank of Carver. Despite her age, she is quite physically imposing. She has spent her life studying and practicing the martial [[Oaken]] traditions of the Burnished Hand, and sees it as her duty to impart this information to the order's newest generation — and even to demonstrate this legacy to those outside of the Burnished Hand.

**Axebearer Versa Layne (Neutral, Arcturian Human, she/her)**

Versa Layne is a fully sworn member of the Burnished Hand, having quickly ascended from Initiate by demonstrating her incredible physical prowess. She is a woman of rippling muscles and few words, preferring to speak with her biceps and her axe. She desires neither leadership nor recognition — only the opportunity to hone her skills, and to demonstrate her mastery and superiority to *herself.*

**Initiate Reza Kras (Lawful Neutral, Arcturian Kivahr, they/them)**

Reza Kras is a young Kivahr with an athletic build, who recently sought membership in the Burnished Hand after one of the order's patrols saved their family's caravan from the increasingly frequent bandit attacks in the region. They have much to learn and much training to do, but what they lack in knowledge and physique, they make up for in enthusiastic determination and a willingness to fail and try again.

 ### Answering the Patrol

 This Burnished Hand patrol is responsible for protecting a swath of land between Ordain and the order's Brass Fortress just south of the Redrak Fields. Due to increasing attacks by bandits across the Plateau, the patrol demands that well-armed or quickly-traveling strangers justify their intentions. If the party has gone to lengths to hide their armaments, the experienced Carver Melir spots this effort, rousing her suspicion even more.

 The inevitable result of this conversation will always be that the patrol accepts the party's explanation — however, the party's Travel Pace, visible arms, widely-known deeds, and provided explanations all impact the patrol's initial and eventual disposition.

 > [!info] Social
> #### Sharp Suspicion
>
>  Carver Pela Melir introduces herself by rank and role — patroller and protector of this area of the Redrak Fields. She demands the party explain their intentions and justify their behavior and arms. The following factors influence how Carver Melir expresses this:
>
>  - If the party was traveling at a Fast or Reckless pace, Carver Melir is especially suspicious of why the party is making such haste, and harshly demands specific explanation.
> - The more visibly expensive the party's weapons and armor, the more wary Carver Melir is, and the more thorough or impressive an explanation of their intentions she demands.
>
>  The following factors influence how Carver Melir responds to the party's explanation, and may allow the party to proceed without any checks. If the party is ever struggling to justify their actions, she directly asks *"By what deeds should I know you?"*
>
>  - If the party completed [[Sanctuary of Death]] during [[Crumbling Sanctuary]] or worked openly and amicably with the Burnished Hand during Smoldering Cinders, the party need only speak of it, and Carver Melir instantly recognizes them by name, warming to them.
> - Carver Melir has reasonable knowledge of the broad strokes of any Main Quest milestones — though does not know the party by looks alone. Assuming the party made no widely-known evil choices during those Quests, if the party explains their role in it, she recognizes their participation by name and deed. The later the chapter of the referenced Quest, the more impressed she is, and the more she is swayed to trust them.
> - If the party convincingly indicates they are in urgent pursuit of any Quest which would aid the people of the Arctus Plateau, Carver Melir begins to warm to them.
>
>  Meanwhile, any character who truthfully argues the party's intentions are innocent and makes a successful **Diplomacy (DC 14)** check can convince Carver Melir to warm to them, by any reasonable explanation.
>
>  Any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 17)** check can convince Carver Melir that their intentions are innocent, regardless of whether they speak truthfully.
>
>  At any point, any character who makes a successful **Deception (DC 11)** check can verify that the patrol is hiding nothing — they wish only to protect the people of the Redrak Fields from danger.

 Once Carver Melir is convinced of the party's heroic record and/or good intentions, her emphasis shifts from one of duty to one of pride. She speaks with the party about the Burnished Hand, and challenges them to face a patrol member in a sporting contest of martial prowess.

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> Carver Melir removes her helmet and holds it at her side as she appraises you.
>
>  > Well, with that settled, how would you like the opportunity to **prove** your heroics? Not that I don't believe you, but rather that contests of martial prowess are a longstanding tradition of the Burnished Hand, reaching all the way back to the Oaken who founded our order. I would invite you to face us in one, and if you prove your mettle, I will grant you an artifact from the Hands' vast armory.

 ### Contests of Martial Prowess

 If the party does not wish to face these contests, they may simply proceed, ending the Event. Otherwise, Carver Melir will answer questions about them, before beginning the contests outright.

 > [!info] Social
> #### Old as Oaken
>
>  The contests Carver Melir proposes are an ancient Oaken tradition, which she will proudly tell the party more about, should they wish to know, explaining the following if so:
>
>  - The Oaken are an ancient culture who founded the Burnished Hand. They primarily reside in Oakengarde, one of the most hostile places on Ember, and passed down the techniques which kept them alive through countless generations of Burnished Hand.
> - These simple twin contests are an ancient Oaken tradition, typically undertaken to challenge another Oaken for leadership.
> - The first contest involves bending a rod of Ambral — a magical and extremely durable mineral — without any assistance, to prove one's own strength. The second contest is part race, part wrestling, in which two warriors attempt to retrieve the rod of Ambral. They are permitted the assistance of one comrade before the contest.
> - Legend says Ambral is smelted by the Oaken from the bones of an extinct magical beast. Only the Oaken know how to make it, and only the worthy may wield it.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About the Oaken?
>
> **A:**
>
> > An ancient culture, who originated and still reside today in the distant continent of Oakengarde, where mighty beasts, deadly flora, and incomparable cold make for a land so inhospitable, mere survival is a feat of heroic proportions. Long ago, Oaken warriors traveled to the Arctus Plateau and founded the Burnished Hand, lending their skills to protect the people of this land. While most modern members of our order were born and raised here on the Plateau, we continue to hone our skills using the techniques and traditions passed down to us by our Oaken forebears.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About the contests?
>
> **A:**
>
> > An old Oaken tradition dating back countless millennia. Typically, these twin contests were performed to challenge another Oaken for leadership, and were designed to be simple and able to be held anywhere, requiring only a single rod of metal and the bodies of the participants themselves. Today, we in the Burnished Hand use them as training exercises, and occasionally as proof of qualification. Interested in facing them yourselves?

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** What do the contests consist of?
>
> **A:**
>
> > Both are contests of strength and martial prowess, but the first is one of self-reliance, while the second is one of comradeship. Each contest makes use of a rod of metal. In the most traditional form of these contests …
>
>  She pulls out a metal rod the length of her forearm. The color suggests brass.
>
>  > … it uses a rod of Ambral Bronze, a metal of unparalleled durability, smelted via an ancient technique known only to the Oaken themselves. In the contest of self-reliance, both participants discard all magical items and dispel all magical aid. The more veteran participant bends the Ambral rod as far as they can. The party challenging them must then attempt to bend the rod back, until it is perfectly straight. Those who have never touched Ambral underestimate the challenge.
> >
> >  In the contest of comradeship, each participant may receive aid from a single compatriot of their choice, prior to the contest's beginning. This may be a spell to enhance their physical abilities, or, traditionally, a martial ritual to focus and channel the participant's abilities. Then, participants kneel across from each other, the rod between them, before turning and walking thirty paces away from each other. After time begins, they dash to retrieve the Ambral, and are permitted to wrestle it from each other, provided they use only their bodies — enhanced as they might be by their comrade's aid. Return thirty paces to your starting position with the Ambral in hand, and you win.

 > [!question] Q&A
> **Q:** About Ambral?
>
> **A:**
>
> > The legends say that Ambral Bronze is smelted of two metals: copper, like one would expect, and Ambral, a strange material found only in Oakengarde. They say it forms from the bones of an extinct beast, which decay not into earth, but into a metal that retains the magical toughness of the terrifying creature inside which it once grew. The Oaken safeguard it carefully, and only those who fight in their lineage — or win in it battle or fair contest — are allowed to wield it.

 After the party finishes their discussion with Carver Melir, she offers that one party member may face one of the patrol in the twin contests. Which member of the patrol the party member faces depends on the current Level of the party:

 | **Party Level** | **Contest Opponent** |
| --- | --- |
| 5+ | Carver Pela Melir |
| 3-4 | Axebearer Versa Layne |
| 1-2 | Initiate Reza Kras |

 ### Contest of Self-Reliance

 The first contest begins with the Burnished Hand patroller bending a rod of ultra-durable Ambral. To succeed, the party member facing them must bend the rod until it is fully straight once again.

 > [!tip] Exploration
> #### One Contest Alone
>
>  After removing all magical items and dispelling all magical effects on their person, the party member faces their opponent. The ensuing narration and checks depend on their opponent.
>
>  **Carver Pela Melir**
>
> > The veteran warrior grasps the Ambral with both hands, and the straining of metal can be heard as she slowly but unflinchingly bends the rod into a U-shape, her face the picture of focused determination, her breath sharp and even throughout. When finally she can push no more, she hands it to you, and raises an eyebrow appraisingly.
>
>  To accomplish the incredibly difficult feat of bending the ultra-durable Ambral straight again, the party member must make a successful **Athletics (DC 18)** check. On a failure, the degree of failure determines how far they are from bending it straight.
>
>  If they succeed, Carver Melir is **extremely** impressed. If they fail, she gives them an encouraging nod for attempting the tradition, and offers that they may face Axebearer Layne in the next contest instead — assisted by Carver Melir in the traditional way, of course.
>
> **Axebearer Versa Layne**
>
> > The muscled warrior grasps the Ambral with both hands, and her biceps bulge as she attempts to bend the Ambral into shape. After straining with her full strength for nearly thirty seconds, she has managed to bend the rod into a shallow bowl. Sweat drips from her brow, and when she finally ceases the effort, she seems frustrated with her progress — but Carver Melir grips her shoulder with pride before handing you the rod.
>
>  To accomplish the difficult feat of bending the ultra-durable Ambral straight again, the party member must make a successful **Athletics (DC 14)** check. On a failure, their degree of failure determines how far they are from bending the rod straight.
>
>  If they succeed, Carver Melir and Axebearer Layne are impressed — though the latter is frustrated with herself. If the party member fails, Carver Melir gives them an encouraging nod for attempting the tradition, and offers that they may face Initiate Kras in the next contest instead — assisted by Axebearer Layne in the traditional way, of course.
>
> **Initiate Reza Kras**
>
> > The young warrior grasps the Ambral with both hands. Their breath catches and their veins pop to the surface as they try with all their might to bend the Ambral. Sweat pours from their brow, and with a growl of frustrated effort, they manage to add the slightest curve to the metal rod. Nevertheless, Carver Melir grips her shoulder with pride before handing you the rod.
>
>  To accomplish the feat of bending the ultra-durable Ambral straight again, the party member must make a successful **Athletics (DC 12)** check. On a failure, they are unable to bend the rod in the slightest.
>
>  If they succeed, Carver Melir and Initiate Kras are impressed — and the latter looks to the party member starry-eyed. If the party member fails, Carver Melir gives them an encouraging nod, and explains that these contests are more challenging than they appear.

 ### Contest of Comradeship

 In the second contest, the participants kneel across from each other, the rod between them, before turning and walking thirty paces away from each other. After time begins, they dash to retrieve the [[Ambral Bronze]], and are permitted to wrestle it from each other, provided they use only their bodies. They must bring the rod thirty paces back to their starting position with the Ambral in hand to win.

 While participants must still forgo the aid of any magic items, for this contest, each participant may seek the aid of a single comrade prior to the contest's start.

 For the party member, this may take the form of a single ongoing buff (magical or otherwise) that a party member is capable of granting them. In the end, the party member participating in the contest must still accomplish it with only their body — no spectral weapons, invisible hands, or otherwise are permitted — but the strength and skill of their body may be enhanced by magical aid.

 > [!warning] Gamemaster
> #### Communicating the Contest
>
>  You are welcome and encouraged advise the players on the nature of the checks in [[One Contest Aided]] below, to help them translate the narrative description of the contest into game mechanics, and thus strategize on how they might offer relevant mechanical aid.

 Before the party finalizes their aid, two of the Burnished Hand conduct a ritual of their own. The Burnished Hand participant is always aided by the patroller immediately above them in rank. If Carver Melir is the participant, she is instead aided by Axebearer Layne.

 > [!quote] Read Aloud
> The two warriors of the Burnished Hand line up across from each other, crossing their fists over their chests and bowing, before each sinking back into fighting stances. In perfect synchronicity, they sweep their arms and legs in a series of mirrored martial gestures that convey uncountable hours of practice. The movements increase in speed, and begin to interlock, reaching into and out of each others' guards, before becoming quick strikes of knee and palm that the other deftly blocks. Finally, one launches a full-speed fist towards the head of your soon-to-be opponent … who catches it effortlessly in an open palm as a strange wind blows through the field. You feel a power emanating from the site of their dance, the sensation not quite like anything you have felt before.

 After the players have finished their own preparations, the participating party member is asked to kneel across from their opponent, the rod between them, before turning and walking thirty paces away. After that, the contest begins.

 > [!tip] Exploration

 ### Challenge Rewards

 No matter the outcome, Carver Melir is impressed by the party member's grit and willingness to face one of her order's ilk. Additionally:

 - If the party member bested Carver Melir herself in one or more contests, she is awestruck, and offers a hand in friendship.
- If the party member bested Axebearer Layne in one or more contests, Carver Melir is impressed, and Axebearer Layne is frustrated, but only with herself — she offers a curt but earnest and sporting congratulations.
- If the party member bested Initiate Kras in both contests, Carver Melir offers the party her congratulations, and the dispirited Kras some gentle reassurance that with training, the young initiate will continue to grow. Initiate Kras also looks to the party member for encouragement.

 Additionally, if the party member's attempts meet any of the success criteria below, Carver Melir offers the associated artifact(s) from the Burnished Hand armory. She explains the artifacts' properties (which can be found in the item description — the rarer two artifacts both require the wearer to prove themselves to the artifact in order to gain its full benefit, in the Oaken tradition).

  | **Success Criteria** | **Reward** |
| --- | --- |
| Won both contests against Carver Melir. | [[Burnished Battleaxe]] |
| Won one contest against Carver Melir or both against Axebearer Layne. | Choice of the [[Fur of the Fjord]] or [[Burnished Battleaxe]] |
| Performed any of:   - Won one contest against Axebearer Layne. - Won both contests against Initiate Kras. - Won one contest against Initiate Kras and particularly impressed or befriended Carver Melir earlier in the Event. | [[Ambral Ring]] |

 ### Concluding the Event

 Once the party has explained their intentions and chosen whether or not to compete in the contests, the characters are free to continue on their journey.
